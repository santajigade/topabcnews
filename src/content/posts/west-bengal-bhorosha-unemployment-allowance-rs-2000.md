West Bengal will pay jobless graduates **Rs 2,000 a month** and non-graduates with recognised vocational training **Rs 1,500 a month** under a new scheme called Bhorosha Karmasuchi (also reported as Bharosa). Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari announced it after a state Cabinet meeting on Monday, November 23, 2026. It is open to people aged **21 to 45**, starts in **January 2027**, and replaces the Yuva Sathi allowance run by the previous Trinamool Congress government.

Two reports, Business Today and Daily Jagran, say the payments run for **15 months**, with the first instalment on **February 1**. That works out to Rs 30,000 in total for a graduate and Rs 22,500 for a non-graduate.

## Bhorosha at a Glance

| Detail | What has been announced |
|---|---|
| Graduates | Rs 2,000 per month |
| Diploma holders (government vocational institutes and ITIs) | Rs 2,000 per month |
| Non-graduates with government-recognised vocational or technical training | Rs 1,500 per month |
| Age | 21 to 45 years |
| Duration | 15 months (Rs 30,000 or Rs 22,500 in total) |
| Scheme start | January 2027, formal launch event in February |
| First payment | February 1, by direct bank transfer |
| Payment cycle | Second week of every month |
| Limit per family | Two members |
| Not eligible | People getting Rs 3,000 a month under the Annapurna scheme |

The Cabinet also cleared **10,146 government vacancies** and dropped application fees for state government recruitment exams, according to <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/west-bengal-to-pay-unemployed-graduates-rs2000-a-month-under-new-bharosa-scheme-heres-all-you-need-to-know-569229-2026-11-24" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Today's report on the Cabinet decisions</a>. Adhikari said money saved by cutting official meeting costs would help pay for the youth relief.

## Yuva Sathi vs Bhorosha: What Changes for Applicants

The rules differ from Yuva Sathi in four ways, going by a PTI report on Yuva Sathi from February 2026 and this week's reports on Bhorosha.

| Point | Yuva Sathi (announced February 2026) | Bhorosha (announced November 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly amount | Rs 1,500 | Rs 2,000 for graduates and eligible diploma holders, Rs 1,500 for others |
| Age band | 21 to 40 | 21 to 45 |
| Minimum education | Madhyamik (Class 10) pass | Graduation, diploma from a government institute or ITI, or government-recognised vocational training |
| How long | Until a job was found, reviewed after five years | 15 months |

- **People aged 41 to 45 can apply now.** They were outside Yuva Sathi.
- **Class 10 alone may no longer be enough.** A Madhyamik pass without recognised vocational or technical training does not appear to qualify under Bhorosha. The state has not yet issued detailed rules, so this could still be clarified.
- **A graduate gets Rs 500 more each month.** Payments are fixed at 15 months, where Yuva Sathi had no set end.

## Existing Beneficiaries and New Applicants

According to the PTI report, the state has to finish verifying about **54 lakh** people enrolled under the old scheme by **December 31, 2026**. The reports do not yet say how those people will be shifted to Bhorosha.

New applicants apply through the **Apnar Sarkar Apnar Pashe** programme. Their forms go to the state Youth Welfare Department for checking. None of the reports so far mentions an online portal, a list of documents or a closing date. Dainik Jagran English advises applicants to wait for the official state notification on the procedure. Notices will appear on the <a href="https://wb.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">West Bengal government portal</a>.

Until the notification comes out, applicants can get these ready:

1. Proof of age showing you are between 21 and 45.
2. A graduation or diploma certificate, or a certificate of government-recognised vocational or technical training.
3. An active bank account in your own name, since the money will be sent by direct transfer.
4. A family check: no more than two members of one household can receive the allowance, and anyone on the Rs 3,000 Annapurna payment is left out.

## Why the Amount Is Lower Than Promised

The BJP's election manifesto had promised Rs 3,000 for graduates and Rs 2,000 for non-graduates. As <a href="https://www.indiatvnews.com/west-bengal/news-bengal-govt-to-pay-unemployed-youth-rs-2-000-monthly-allowance-from-january-check-eligibility-other-details-2026-11-23-1060197" target="_blank" rel="noopener">India TV's PTI report</a> notes, the government now plans to reach those amounts from the 2028-29 financial year. Adhikari told young people the state needed time to arrange its finances. "We will fulfil our promise," he said.

The outlay for the scheme has not been published. Nor has the state said how many people it expects to cover beyond the 54 lakh due for verification. ## Bhorosha Scheme: Common Questions

**What is the Bhorosha allowance amount in West Bengal?**
Rs 2,000 a month for graduates and for diploma holders from government vocational institutes or ITIs. Non-graduates with government-recognised vocational or technical training get Rs 1,500 a month.

**What is the age limit for the Bhorosha scheme?**
Applicants must be between 21 and 45 years old.

**When will the first Bhorosha payment come?**
The scheme starts in January 2027. Business Today and Daily Jagran report that the first instalment is due on February 1, by direct bank transfer.

**Can Yuva Sathi beneficiaries get Bhorosha?**
About 54 lakh existing beneficiaries are due to be verified by December 31, 2026. The state has not yet said how they will move to Bhorosha, so watch for the official notification.
