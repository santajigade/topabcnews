Indian stock markets ended a choppy Monday session on a positive note on November 9, 2026. The BSE Sensex closed 107.25 points, or 0.13 percent, higher at 81,106.65, while the NSE Nifty 50 gained 58.25 points, or 0.23 percent, to finish at 25,044.65. Public sector banks and pharma stocks did the heavy lifting, while information technology shares pulled the indices down for much of the day as investors booked profits after last week's sharp rally.

It was the first regular trading day after the special Diwali session on Sunday. In that one hour <a href="/muhurat-trading-2026-sensex-nifty-close-lower/">Muhurat trading session</a>, the Sensex slipped 28.62 points to 80,999.40 and the Nifty fell 43.95 points to 24,986.40, snapping a seven-day winning streak.

## How the Trading Day Played Out

The session started weak. According to PTI, as reported by ETV Bharat, the Sensex fell 93.95 points to 80,919.19 in early trade and the Nifty dipped 30 points to 24,968.80. Selling in large IT companies and profit-taking after a strong run were the main reasons.

The market stayed volatile through the afternoon. At 3 pm, the Sensex was at 81,020.95 and the Nifty was almost flat at 24,984.55. Buying in banking heavyweights in the final stretch then pushed both indices into the green, and the Nifty closed back above the 25,000 mark.

Broader markets also ended higher, with mid-sized companies doing slightly better than the main indices.

## PSU Banks and Pharma Lead the Gains

The Nifty PSU Bank index was the best performing sector of the day, rising more than 3 percent, according to <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/markets" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard's market coverage</a>. State Bank of India was among the top gainers on both the Sensex and the Nifty. The Nifty Pharma index rose 1.65 percent, and the Bank and Healthcare indices also outperformed.

Among other Sensex gainers were Trent, Larsen and Toubro, and NTPC. On the Nifty, Nestle India joined SBI and Trent at the top. Earlier in the day, private lenders HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank, along with InterGlobe Aviation, were also among the gainers, according to PTI.

## IT and Metal Stocks Weigh on the Market

Nifty IT was the weakest sector of the session. HCL Tech was among the biggest Sensex losers, while Infosys and TCS also traded lower in the morning. Ponmudi R of Enrich Money pointed to "lingering global concerns surrounding the AI trade" as a factor weighing on technology shares.

Metal stocks were also weak, with Tata Steel among the Sensex laggards at the close, alongside UltraTech Cement and Bajaj Finance.

## What Analysts Are Saying After Last Week's Rally

Monday's caution followed a big week. The Sensex gained 6,568.49 points, or 8.82 percent, last week, and the Nifty rose 1,977.25 points, or 8.57 percent, according to PTI. Our report on the <a href="/sensex-jumps-1718-points-nifty-25000/">Dhanteras rally that took the Nifty back above 25,000</a> covers part of that surge.

Vinod Nair, head of research at Geojit Investments, said the market traded in a range with a positive bias, and that intermittent profit booking after the recent rally capped gains. He said improving participation from foreign institutional investors and better than expected company earnings have raised expectations of an upgrade to the earnings outlook for FY27.

Nair also listed comfortable valuations in large caps, easing geopolitical concerns, crude prices cooling from recent peaks and the prospect of double-digit earnings growth as reasons for a constructive medium-term view. Brent crude was trading at about $98.16 a barrel in the morning, down 0.16 percent, according to PTI.

## Inflation Data Is the Next Big Trigger

Investors are now waiting for consumer price inflation numbers from both India and the United States, which will offer clues about the direction of interest rates. Ponmudi R described Thursday's CPI data as the next directional cue for the market. Crude oil prices and global risk sentiment are also expected to guide trading in the coming days. Official index data is available on the <a href="https://www.nseindia.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Stock Exchange website</a>.

Global cues on Monday were supportive. Asian markets including Japan's Nikkei 225, South Korea's Kospi, Shanghai's SSE Composite and Hong Kong's Hang Seng traded higher, and US stocks had ended mostly higher on Friday.

## What Monday's Session Tells Investors

After an almost 9 percent jump in a single week, a pause was widely expected. The way the market recovered from early losses, helped by state-run banks, suggests buyers are still active, but the drag from IT stocks shows that worries around global technology spending have not gone away. The coming inflation readings are likely to decide whether the Nifty can hold above 25,000.
