Mumbai Indians have traded all-rounder Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings, with left-arm fast bowler Arshdeep Singh moving the other way, in a high-profile swap between two franchises. Both franchises confirmed the deal on Wednesday, November 11, ahead of the IPL 2027 season and the player auction due in December. The move ends Pandya's second spell at Mumbai, where he had been captain for the last three seasons, and leaves Mumbai looking for a new leader.

## The Trade and the New Fees

According to The Federal, Pandya will earn Rs 18 crore at Punjab Kings, up from the Rs 16.35 crore he was paid at Mumbai. Arshdeep's fee at Mumbai Indians is also Rs 18 crore. <a href="https://www.espncricinfo.com/story/ipl-2027-mumbai-indians-and-punjab-kings-complete-hardik-pandya-and-arshdeep-singh-swap-1562157" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ESPNcricinfo also reported</a> the swap as complete. In a straight swap of this kind, the two franchises effectively exchange two of their most valuable Indian players at similar price tags.

In its <a href="https://www.mumbaiindians.com/news/mumbai-indians-trade-hardik-pandya-to-punjab-kings-arshdeep-singh-joins-mumbai-indians" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official announcement</a>, Mumbai Indians did not disclose financial terms. The franchise said it would evaluate its captaincy options and announce a decision later.

| Player | Moving from | Moving to | Reported IPL 2027 fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hardik Pandya | Mumbai Indians | Punjab Kings | Rs 18 crore (was Rs 16.35 crore) |
| Arshdeep Singh | Punjab Kings | Mumbai Indians | Rs 18 crore |

## What Akash Ambani and Pandya Said

MI owner Akash Ambani paid tribute to the all-rounder, saying Pandya "has shared some very special moments with Mumbai Indians" and that he holds great respect for him as a cricketer and a person. On the new signing, Ambani said Arshdeep's arrival adds an exciting dimension to the team's bowling attack.

Pandya posted a message on Instagram after the move, thanking the franchise and fans in Punjab. Willow Sports reported that his first comment referred to "my captain Shreyas, my coach Ricky," a nod to Punjab Kings skipper Shreyas Iyer and head coach Ricky Ponting. The post drew more than 3.7 million likes, The Federal said.

## Hardik Pandya's IPL Journey So Far

Pandya's career has been closely tied to Mumbai. He joined the franchise in 2015 as a young player and was part of four title-winning campaigns, in 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020. In 2022 he moved to the new Gujarat Titans for Rs 15 crore, led them to the title in their debut season and took them to the final again in 2023.

He returned to Mumbai as captain in 2024, taking over from Rohit Sharma. That change was not smooth. Team results dipped and Pandya was booed by sections of fans in his first season back, The Federal noted.

### Key numbers for Hardik Pandya

- **IPL matches:** 162
- **Runs:** 2,955 at a strike rate of 146.28
- **Wickets:** 82
- **IPL titles:** Five (four with MI, one as GT captain)

At Punjab, he will play under Iyer rather than lead the side, at least for now, which could let him focus on his batting and bowling.

## Why Arshdeep Singh Suits Mumbai Indians

Arshdeep, 27, made his IPL debut for Punjab in 2019 and spent eight seasons with the franchise. He has taken 111 wickets in 96 IPL matches, the most by any Punjab Kings bowler, including 21 wickets in 2025 and 14 in 2026. He also holds India's record for the most T20 international wickets, with 151 in 104 matches.

Mumbai already have Jasprit Bumrah, so adding an experienced left-arm seamer gives them one of the strongest Indian pace pairs in the league. For Punjab, losing their record wicket-taker is a real cost, but they gain a proven match-winner with captaincy experience in the dressing room.

## Other Big IPL 2027 Trades

The Pandya-Arshdeep swap is the headline move of a busy trade window. Other reported deals include:

- **Rishabh Pant:** Lucknow Super Giants to Delhi Capitals at a revised fee of Rs 15 crore (LSG had paid Rs 27 crore).
- **Kuldeep Yadav:** Delhi Capitals to Lucknow Super Giants at Rs 13.50 crore.
- **T Natarajan:** Delhi Capitals to Sunrisers Hyderabad at a revised Rs 5 crore.
- **Liam Livingstone:** Sunrisers Hyderabad to Chennai Super Kings at a revised Rs 6 crore.
- **Khaleel Ahmed:** Chennai Super Kings to Sunrisers Hyderabad at Rs 4.8 crore.

## Who Could Lead Mumbai Indians Next

With Pandya gone, Mumbai must name a new captain before IPL 2027. The franchise has said only that it will announce its decision later, so any names suggested now remain speculation. What is clear is that both teams have reshaped their cores ahead of the December auction: Punjab have added a big-match all-rounder, and Mumbai have built a pace attack around Bumrah and Arshdeep.
