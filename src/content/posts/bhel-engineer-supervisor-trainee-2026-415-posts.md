Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL) has started taking applications for 415 trainee posts: 200 Engineer/Executive Trainees (ET) under Advertisement 02/2026 and 215 Supervisor Trainees (ST) under Advertisement 03/2026. The online form opened on November 25, 2026 at 10 am and closes on December 28, 2026 at 11:45 pm. The computer based exam (CBE) is tentatively set for January 27, 2027. Applications are accepted only on the <a href="https://careers.bhel.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BHEL careers portal</a>.

| Detail | Engineer/Executive Trainee | Supervisor Trainee |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 200 | 215 |
| Who can apply | BE/BTech, CA/CMA, or HR postgraduates | Engineering diploma, BBA/BSW/BBM-type degree, or BCom |
| Upper age (as on Nov 1, 2026) | 27 (engineering), 29 (HR, Finance, two-year PG holders) | 27 |
| Basic pay in training | Rs 50,000 | Rs 32,000 |
| Approximate CTC | Rs 12 lakh a year | Rs 7.5 lakh a year |
| Selection | CBE plus interview | CBE plus document check |
| Last date | December 28, 2026 | December 28, 2026 |

The ET category split is 82 UR, 20 EWS, 55 OBC, 29 SC and 14 ST, and 8 posts are earmarked for persons with benchmark disabilities (PwBD). ## Who Is Eligible for Each Post

**Engineer Trainee** needs a full-time bachelor's degree in engineering or technology, a five-year integrated master's or a dual degree in the relevant branch. The ET advertisement sets no minimum percentage for engineers. **Executive Trainee (Finance)** is open to qualified CAs and CMAs. **Executive Trainee (HR)** needs a full-time bachelor's degree with 60% aggregate plus a two-year full-time postgraduate degree or diploma in HR, personnel management, industrial relations, social work or an MBA with HR, with 55% aggregate.

The Supervisor Trainee bar is higher on marks. Diploma holders in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Civil need at least 65% aggregate across all years, relaxed to 60% for SC and ST candidates. The same 65% rule applies to HR (BBA, BSW, BBM, BBS or BMS) and Finance (BCom) graduates. SC, ST or PwBD candidates who apply against unreserved posts do not get this relaxation. Final-year students can apply for both posts but must show proof of passing at document verification or interview.

Age relaxation is 5 years for SC and ST, 3 years for OBC (non-creamy layer), and 10, 13 or 15 years for PwBD candidates by category.

## ET or ST: How the Two Posts Differ

Both advertisements say ET and ST exams for the same discipline will be held on the same day, so a candidate who qualifies for both, such as an engineer who also holds a diploma or a CA with a BCom, has to pick one. The notices differ on these points.

- **Pay gap:** ET starts at Rs 50,000 basic in the Rs 50,000 to 1,60,000 scale and moves to Rs 60,000 basic after a year. ST starts at Rs 32,000 and moves to Rs 33,500 in the Rs 33,500 to 1,20,000 scale.
- **Interview:** ET merit is 75% exam and 25% interview, and the interview shortlist is five candidates per vacancy. ST has no interview. The final list is based only on CBE marks, after a document check of three candidates per vacancy.
- **Bond:** both posts need at least 3 years of service after the one-year training. Leaving early costs Rs 5 lakh for ET and Rs 3 lakh for ST, backed by an indemnity bond with a surety.
- **Posting:** both notices say selected candidates must serve at least 10 years at the unit they are allotted, with no transfer in the first 10 years. Mechanical postings listed include Trichy, Ranipet, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Haridwar, Bhopal, Visakhapatnam, Jhansi and power sector sites.
- **Cut-off:** both exams need 40% to qualify for UR and EWS and 30% for OBC, SC and ST.

Neither notice gives the exam pattern (subjects, number of questions or negative marking). Any pattern published elsewhere is unofficial until BHEL confirms it.

## Application Fee and How to Apply

The fee is the same for both posts. UR, EWS and OBC candidates pay Rs 600 exam fee plus Rs 400 processing fee plus GST, Rs 1,072 in all. SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen pay only the processing fee plus GST, Rs 472. Bank charges are extra and the fee is not refunded.

1. Go to careers.bhel.in and open the Engineer/Executive Trainee or Supervisor Trainee 2027 recruitment link.
2. Register with an email ID you will keep active for at least a year, and enter your name exactly as on your Class 10 certificate.
3. Fill in your discipline, preference for manufacturing units or power sector sites, and up to five exam centre choices.
4. Pay the fee online and confirm the payment. BHEL counts only the last submitted form, and category, post and email cannot be changed after you submit.

Keep these ready for the later stage: an OBC (NCL) certificate issued on or after January 1, 2026, an EWS certificate valid for 2026-27 based on 2025-26 income, a PwBD certificate showing at least 40% disability, and a no objection certificate if you are already employed. Candidates already working with BHEL as ET, ST or artisans cannot apply.

## BHEL Recruitment 2026: Common Questions

**What is the age limit for BHEL Supervisor Trainee?**
The upper age limit is 27 years as on November 1, 2026, so candidates born before November 1, 1999 are not eligible unless they get category relaxation.

**Is there a minimum percentage for BHEL Engineer Trainee?**
The ET advertisement sets no minimum percentage for engineering graduates. Supervisor Trainee posts need 65% (60% for SC and ST), and Executive Trainee (HR) needs 60% in graduation and 55% in the postgraduate course.

**When is the BHEL ET and ST exam?**
The CBE is tentatively set for January 27, 2027. The admit card and any change will be posted on the careers portal.
