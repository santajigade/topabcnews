The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meets from December 2 to 4, 2026, and will announce its decision on Thursday, December 4. The repo rate is 5.50% after a 25 basis point hike in October. On Monday, November 23, S&P Global Ratings said it expects the RBI to raise rates by another 25 basis points in December and once more early in 2027, which would take the repo rate to 6% by the end of March 2027.

| Item | Figure |

| S&P expected repo rate, end-March 2027 | 6.00% |

For anyone with a floating rate home loan, the gap between 5.50% and 6% is the number to watch. A worked EMI table is further down.

## Why S&P Expects More Hikes

S&P's Asia-Pacific outlook, <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/s-p-cuts-india-s-fy28-growth-forecast-to-7-sees-rbi-rate-hike-in-dec-126112301317_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">reported by Business Standard</a>, points to inflation. Retail inflation hit 6.09% in October, above the top of the RBI's 2 to 6% tolerance band. S&P expects consumer inflation to average 5.3% in 2026-27, up from 2% in 2025-26, before easing to 4.7% in 2027-28.

The agency named weak rain as one reason. It said deficient summer rainfall, lower reservoir levels and persistent El Nino conditions are likely to hurt both current farm output and the coming winter crop, and that El Nino is likely to push food prices up. It also expects companies to keep passing higher input costs on to buyers, and said "the broadening passthrough of costs" is likely to add to price pressure.

We covered the October inflation data in detail in our report on <a href="/india-retail-inflation-october-2026-six-percent/">CPI hitting 6.09% in October</a>.

## What the RBI Said in October

The October resolution already pointed this way. The MPC voted unanimously to raise the repo rate to 5.50% and changed its stance from neutral to "calibrated tightening". Two members, Dr. Nagesh Kumar and Prof. Ram Singh, wanted the stance left at neutral even though they backed the hike.

The <a href="https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63742" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RBI's October policy statement</a> said the stance change means rate cuts are off the table in the near term, and that the next move can only be a hike or a pause. The other policy rates after October:

- Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate: 5.25%
- Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate: 5.75%
- Bank Rate: 5.75%

In October the RBI projected inflation at 6.0% for the October to December quarter and 5.2% for the full year 2026-27. October's 6.09% reading is slightly above that quarterly projection, which is one reason markets are treating December as a live meeting.

## How Much a Home Loan EMI Could Rise

If your floating rate loan is linked to the repo rate, a hike passes through at the next reset date in your loan agreement. The table below uses an example starting rate of 8.5% to show the effect of a 0.25 and a 0.50 percentage point increase. These are worked examples, not quotes from any bank.

| Loan | Tenure | EMI at 8.50% | EMI at 8.75% (+25 bps) | Rise per month | EMI at 9.00% (+50 bps) | Rise per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rs 25 lakh | 20 years | Rs 21,696 | Rs 22,093 | Rs 397 | Rs 22,493 | Rs 798 |
| Rs 50 lakh | 20 years | Rs 43,391 | Rs 44,186 | Rs 794 | Rs 44,986 | Rs 1,595 |
| Rs 10 lakh (car or personal) | 5 years | Rs 20,517 | Rs 20,637 | Rs 121 | Rs 20,758 | Rs 242 |

On the Rs 50 lakh loan, a 25 basis point rise adds about Rs 1.9 lakh in total payments over 20 years if the EMI goes up. If the lender keeps the EMI at Rs 43,391 and extends the loan instead, the tenure grows by about 12 months at 8.75% and about 27 months at 9%.

Fixed rate loans, and EMIs on loans already locked at a fixed rate, do not change with the repo rate.

## What Borrowers and Savers Can Check Before December 4

- Look at your sanction letter or the bank's app to see which benchmark your loan follows and when it resets.
- Ask your lender whether it will raise the EMI or stretch the tenure after a hike, and whether you get to choose.
- If you plan to open a fixed deposit, compare rates after December 4, since banks set deposit rates themselves.

## Growth Forecast Cut for 2027-28

S&P kept its India growth forecast for 2026-27 at 7%, but cut 2027-28 by 20 basis points to 7%. It raised 2028-29 by 20 basis points to 7.2%. It said the first half of 2026-27 was driven by strong goods exports, public investment and household spending, and that growth is likely to soften between October and March as firms pass on costs and the uneven monsoon hits farming.

The agency still expects India and Vietnam to be the fastest growing economies in Asia-Pacific in 2027. For the region as a whole, it sees growth easing to 4.4% in 2027 from 4.8% in 2026. It listed a sharp slowdown in AI spending, energy prices staying high for longer and a steeper rise in global interest rates as the main risks. A bigger rise in US rates, it said, could trigger capital outflows and currency pressure and force larger rate hikes in the region.

The RBI's own October projection put 2026-27 growth at 7.1%.

## RBI December Policy: Common Questions

**When will the RBI announce its December 2026 policy?**
The MPC meets from December 2 to 4, 2026, and the decision is due on December 4.

**What is the current repo rate?**
The repo rate is 5.50%. The RBI raised it by 25 basis points at its October 5 to 7 meeting.

**Will my home loan EMI go up if the RBI hikes rates?**
If your loan is a floating rate loan linked to the repo rate, the rate rises at the next reset. Your lender may raise the EMI or extend the tenure instead.

**What does S&P expect from the RBI?**
S&P expects a 25 basis point hike in December and another early in 2027, taking the repo rate to 6% by the end of March 2027.
