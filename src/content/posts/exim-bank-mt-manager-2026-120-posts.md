The Export-Import Bank of India (Exim Bank) is recruiting 120 officers under advertisement no. HRM/MT/M/2026-27/05: 90 Management Trainees (MT) and 30 Managers in Middle Management Grade II. The online application link goes live on **November 27, 2026** and closes on **December 11, 2026**, which is also the last day to pay the fee. The written test is tentatively planned for December 2026. The fee is Rs 600 for General and OBC candidates.

| Item | Management Trainee | Manager (MM-II) |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 90 | 30 |
| Age (as on Nov 1, 2026) | 21 to 28 years | 24 to 30 years |
| Experience | Not required | Minimum 4 years post-qualification |
| Pay | Rs 65,000 a month stipend in training, then about Rs 25 lakh a year CTC | About Rs 30 lakh a year CTC |
| Service bond | Rs 3 lakh | Rs 3 lakh |
| Apply window | Nov 27 to Dec 11, 2026 | Nov 27 to Dec 11, 2026 |

## Which Degree Fits Which MT Stream

More than half of the 90 trainee posts (50) are in credit. Graduation must be a three-year full-time course with at least 60%, and a postgraduate course must be two-year full-time with 60%. Distance, online and part-time PG courses are not accepted.

| MT stream | Posts | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| Banking Operations (Credit, Loans, Advances) | 50 | Graduation plus MBA, PGDBA, PGDBM or MMS in Finance, International Business or Foreign Trade, or CA |
| Legal | 10 | LLB with 60% (integrated, or 60% in both graduation and LLB) |
| Digital Technology | 9 | B.E. or B.Tech in CS, IT or Electronics and Communication, or graduation plus MCA |
| Internal Audit | 5 | Same as credit (finance PG or CA) |
| Treasury and Accounts | 3 | Same as credit (finance PG or CA) |
| Risk Management | 2 | Same as credit (finance PG or CA) |
| Compliance | 2 | ACS membership plus full-time graduation |
| Cyber Security | 2 | B.E., B.Tech, M.E., M.Tech or MCA in CS, IT or E&C |
| HR Management | 2 | PG in HRM or Personnel Management |
| Administration | 2 | PG in Hotel and Hospitality or Facilities Management |
| International Relations | 1 | Finance or International Business PG, or MA Economics |
| ESG | 1 | PG in Management or Environmental Science |
| Corporate Communications | 1 | Master's or PG diploma in mass communication, journalism, advertising or related |

Final-year students can sit for the written test if their exams end by May 2027, but they must submit their documents by the joining date, which the bank says will not be later than June 30, 2027.

### Manager posts need four years of banking work

The 30 Manager posts are spread across 12 areas, led by Credit Appraisal and Trade Finance with 4 each. Each needs at least four years of post-qualification experience in a bank, financial institution or similar firm. Some posts list extra requirements, for example working knowledge of Finacle for Documentary Credit and a CISA or equivalent certificate for Information Security audit.

Age is counted as on November 1, 2026. Working from the limits in the notice, a General category MT applicant should have been born no earlier than November 2, 1997 and no later than November 1, 2005. For a General category Manager applicant, the range is November 2, 1995 to November 1, 2002.

## How to Apply for Exim Bank MT and Manager 2026

1. Go to the <a href="https://www.eximbankindia.in/careers" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Exim Bank careers page</a> from November 27 and open the link for this drive.
2. Register with your name, phone number and email, and note the provisional registration number and password.
3. Upload your photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the sizes the form asks for.
4. Fill in your education, stream and experience details. You can apply for only one post; if you register more than once, the bank keeps the latest valid application and the fees paid for the others are lost.
5. Pay the fee online by December 11 and print the e-receipt and the form. Registration is not complete until the fee is paid.

Selection is through a written test followed by a personal interview. The written test will be held in Chennai, Guwahati, Kolkata, Mumbai and New Delhi, and interviews in Mumbai and/or New Delhi. Full details are in the <a href="https://www.eximbankindia.in/sites/default/files/2026-11/MT%20%20M%20Recruitment%20Advertisement%2012-11-2026%201.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official advertisement PDF</a>.

Applicants preparing for other bank officer exams should note that the <a href="/sidbi-grade-a-b-2026-68-posts-apply-december-11/">SIDBI Grade A and B recruitment</a> also closes on December 11.

## Exim Bank Recruitment 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date to apply for Exim Bank MT 2026?**
December 11, 2026. The application link opens on November 27, 2026, and the fee must also be paid by December 11.

**What is the Exim Bank MT stipend and salary?**
Trainees get Rs 65,000 a month during the one-year training. After absorption as Deputy Manager, the bank puts the current annual CTC at about Rs 25 lakh. For Managers, it is about Rs 30 lakh.

**What is the application fee?**
Rs 600 for General and OBC candidates. SC, ST, PwBD, EWS and female candidates pay no application fee and are charged Rs 100 as intimation charges. The fee is not refundable.

**Is there a bond for Exim Bank Management Trainees?**
Yes. Trainees sign a Rs 3 lakh bond to serve the one-year training and at least five years as Deputy Manager after absorption.
