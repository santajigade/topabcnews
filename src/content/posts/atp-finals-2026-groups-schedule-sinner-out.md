The men's tennis season reaches its finale on Sunday, November 15, when the Nitto ATP Finals begin at the Inalpi Arena in Turin, Italy. The top eight singles players of the year will compete for the title until Sunday, November 22, but it starts without the man who won it last year. Defending champion Jannik Sinner is out with a knee problem, leaving world No. 1 Alexander Zverev and Carlos Alcaraz as the biggest names in the field.

## Why Defending Champion Jannik Sinner Is Missing

Sinner's team announced on October 6 that he would not play again in 2026. He has been out since winning Wimbledon in July because of inflammation in his knee, and his camp said the treatment would give him "the necessary time to recover" before preparing for 2027, as reported by <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/10/6/tennis-world-number-one-sinner-will-not-play-in-2026-due-to-knee-injury" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera</a>.

The Italian won the title in Turin last year, beating Alcaraz 7-6(4), 7-5 in the final. His absence also means he misses the Davis Cup Final 8, where Italy are three-time defending champions. Alex de Minaur and Taylor Fritz are the two alternates for this year's ATP Finals.

## ATP Finals 2026 Groups Revealed

The draw was held on Thursday, November 12. The eight singles players are split into two groups of four, named after former champions.

| John McEnroe Group | Ivan Lendl Group |
|---|---|
| Alexander Zverev (1) | Carlos Alcaraz (2) |
| Ben Shelton (3) | Daniil Medvedev (4) |
| Arthur Fils (6) | Flavio Cobolli (5) |
| Rafael Jodar (8) | Frances Tiafoe (7) |

The top two from each group reach the semi-finals, where group winners face the runners-up from the other group.

### Storylines to follow

- **Zverev vs Shelton rematch:** The two met in the US Open final in September, which Zverev won 6-3, 7-6(2), 5-7, 6-2. Zverev has won all six of their meetings. The German, a champion in 2018 and 2021, is chasing a third title.
- **Alcaraz's missing trophy:** This is Alcaraz's fourth appearance, and he has never won the event. He lost last year's final, but arrives in form after titles in Paris, Vienna and Tokyo since returning from injury at the US Open.
- **Medvedev's return to the big stage:** The Russian won the title in 2020 and is in the same group as Alcaraz.
- **Four debutants:** Cobolli, Fils, Tiafoe and Jodar are all playing the ATP Finals for the first time. Fils is the first Frenchman in the event since Gael Monfils in 2016 and called his qualification "a childhood dream". Jodar sealed the last spot at the Paris Masters.
- **A home player still in action:** With Sinner out, Cobolli carries Italian hopes in singles, while Simone Bolelli and Andrea Vavassori play doubles.

## ATP Finals 2026 Schedule in Indian Time

Round-robin matches run from Sunday, November 15 to Friday, November 20. Singles matches are played in an afternoon session and an evening session each day. Italy is four and a half hours behind India in November, so the evening singles match will finish well after midnight IST.

## Doubles Groups and Format

The doubles field also has two groups of four teams:

- **Todd Woodbridge Group:** Harri Heliovaara and Henry Patten (1), Marcelo Arevalo and Mate Pavic (4), Theo Arribage and Albano Olivetti (6), Orlando Luz and Rafael Matos (8)
- **Mark Woodforde Group:** Christian Harrison and Neal Skupski (2), Marcel Granollers and Horacio Zeballos (3), Simone Bolelli and Andrea Vavassori (5), Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz (7)

Heliovaara and Patten are the defending doubles champions. Singles matches are best of three sets with tie-breaks, while doubles matches are two sets with a match tie-break deciding a third.

## History of the Season Finale

The event began in 1970. Novak Djokovic holds the record with seven singles titles and is also the oldest champion, having won at 36 in 2023. John McEnroe is the youngest winner at 19 in 1978, and Roger Federer has the most match wins with 59. ## An Open Field Without Sinner

With the champion missing, the 2026 ATP Finals look more open than they have in a while. Zverev has the ranking, Alcaraz has the momentum, and four first-timers have nothing to lose. The opening match between Zverev and Jodar on Sunday evening, Indian time, will give the first clue about who is ready for the week.
