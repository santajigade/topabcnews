Johnny Depp's return to a big studio film has started on top. *Ebenezer*, Paramount's dark retelling of Charles Dickens' *A Christmas Carol* with Depp as Ebenezer Scrooge, took the No. 1 spot at the North American box office on Friday, November 13, earning about $6.7 million including Thursday previews. Paramount expects a domestic opening weekend of around $20 million, with roughly the same amount coming from overseas markets.

That is a solid if not spectacular start for a film that cost about $38 million to make. It is also Depp's first major studio feature since *Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald* in 2018, which is why the opening has drawn close attention in Hollywood.

## How Ebenezer Performed on Opening Day

*Ebenezer* opened in 3,535 theatres across the US and Canada on Friday. Previews that began at 2 pm on Thursday brought in about $1.5 million, which was folded into the Friday figure.

Industry trackers put the weekend range at $18 million to $21 million. Final Sunday estimates and actual numbers are due later, and studios usually confirm official weekend tallies on Monday through services such as <a href="https://www.boxofficemojo.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Box Office Mojo</a>.

## Audiences Warmer Than Critics

Reviews for *Ebenezer* have been mixed. The film holds about 53% approval from critics on Rotten Tomatoes, but audiences have been far kinder. Its audience score there is about 90%, it earned an A- grade from CinemaScore exit polls, and PostTrak audience surveys gave it 4.5 out of 5 stars.

PostTrak also found that about half of the viewers bought tickets mainly because of Depp, and 62% of Thursday night's audience was over 30. Good word of mouth matters for a Christmas film, since it can keep selling tickets through the holiday season if viewers recommend it.

For comparison, two earlier Depp-led films opened in a similar range. *Black Mass* opened to $22.6 million in 2015, and *Public Enemies* debuted with $25.2 million in 2009.

## Who Made Ebenezer

The film is directed by Ti West and written by Nathaniel Halpern. It is produced by Emma Watts and released by <a href="https://www.paramount.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Paramount Pictures</a>. The cast includes:

- Johnny Depp as Ebenezer Scrooge
- Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley
- Rupert Grint as Bob Cratchit
- Andrea Riseborough as the Ghost of Christmas Past
- Tramell Tillman as the Ghost of Christmas Present
- Sam Claflin as Fred
- Daisy Ridley in a supporting role

Filming began in January 2026 in and around London and Dublin, including at The Historic Dockyard Chatham in Kent. The film is the first major project given the green light under Paramount's new leadership following its merger with Skydance.

## How the Rest of the Weekend Is Shaping Up

The other new wide releases had a tougher Friday.

- ***How to Rob a Bank***: Amazon MGM's R-rated heist comedy, directed by David Leitch and starring Nicholas Hoult, Zoe Kravitz and Pete Davidson, made about $2.2 million on Friday from 2,810 theatres. It is tracking towards an opening of about $4.7 million. Critics gave it 33% on Rotten Tomatoes and audiences a B- CinemaScore.
- ***Ghost Soldier***: Sony's release led by Jim Caviezel took about $1.5 million on Friday from 2,558 theatres and is tracking near $4.9 million for three days.
- ***Godzilla Minus Zero***: Last week's No. 1 film earned about $2 million on Friday. It is heading for around $7.5 million in its second weekend, a drop of about 75%, and has made about $48 million in North America so far. We covered its record start in our report on the <a href="/godzilla-minus-zero-box-office-opening-record/">Godzilla Minus Zero opening</a>.

Overall ticket sales for all films are estimated at about $80 million for the weekend, which would be up about 8% on the same weekend last year.

## Will Ebenezer Release in India?

The film opened in the United States and the United Kingdom on November 13, and in Australia and Brazil on November 12. An Indian release date has not been announced in the sources we checked. Hollywood titles often reach Indian screens on staggered dates, so fans should watch for an official update from the distributor.

## What Comes Next for Ebenezer

The real test for *Ebenezer* will be how well it holds over the coming weeks. Next weekend, Lionsgate's *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* arrives, and some forecasts suggest it could open at around $100 million. If strong audience scores translate into steady holiday sales, *Ebenezer* could still earn back its budget comfortably as Christmas approaches.
