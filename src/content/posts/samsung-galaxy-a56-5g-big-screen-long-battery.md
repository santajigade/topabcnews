The Samsung Galaxy A56 5G went on sale in India in March 2025 at Rs 41,999 for the 8GB RAM and 128GB storage model. It has a 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED screen, Samsung's Exynos 1580 chip, a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging, IP67 water and dust resistance, and a promise of six Android upgrades and six years of security updates. More than a year and a half later, its price has moved and a successor is out, so here is where the phone stands now.

## Update (20 November 2026)

- **Price changes:** Samsung later raised the A56's price to Rs 44,999, according to Digit. During the Amazon Great Indian Festival in October 2026, the 8GB and 128GB model sold for Rs 31,999, its lowest price so far, with a Rs 959 cashback on the Amazon Pay ICICI Bank credit card.
- **Successor launched:** Samsung launched the Galaxy A57 5G in India in March 2026 at Rs 56,999 for 8GB and 256GB, with sales from 10 April.
- **Software support:** Samsung's India product page now lists security updates for the A56 until 31 March 2032.

## Display, Build and Battery

The screen is a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED panel (1080 x 2340) with a 120Hz refresh rate. Samsung's spec page lists up to 1,200 nits in high brightness mode; launch reports quoted a peak of 1,900 nits. It is covered by Gorilla Glass Victus+, and the IP67 rating means it can handle dust and water exposure. The phone weighs 198g and is 7.4mm thick.

The 5,000mAh battery is rated by Samsung for up to 29 hours of video playback. It supports 45W wired charging.

## Chip, Cameras and Software

The Exynos 1580 is a 4nm chip paired with an AMD Xclipse 540 GPU. You get 8GB or 12GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage.

On the back are a 50MP main camera, a 12MP ultrawide and a 5MP macro. The selfie camera is 12MP. The phone shipped with One UI 7 on Android 15. The six-generation upgrade promise matters more in this price band than any single spec, because it means a phone bought in 2025 is still supported well into the next decade.

What this means for buyers in India:

- **On a budget:** The A56 at around Rs 32,000 in a sale costs about the same as the A36 did at launch, and it gets the 12MP ultrawide and the faster 4nm chip.
- **Want the latest:** The A57 is thinner and lighter, adds IP68 protection and a newer chip, but it starts at Rs 56,999, which is Rs 15,000 more than the A56's launch price.
- **Same battery on all three:** Every model uses a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging, so battery size is not a reason to pay more.

## Galaxy A56: Common Questions

**What is the Samsung Galaxy A56 price in India?**
It launched at Rs 41,999 for 8GB and 128GB, and Samsung later raised it to Rs 44,999. In the October 2026 Amazon sale the same model sold for Rs 31,999.

**How many years of updates does the Galaxy A56 get?**
Samsung promises six generations of Android upgrades and six years of security updates. Its India page lists security support until 31 March 2032.

**Is the Galaxy A56 waterproof?**
It has an IP67 rating for dust and water resistance. The newer A57 moves up to IP68.

**Does the Galaxy A56 support fast charging?**
Yes, it supports 45W wired charging for its 5,000mAh battery.
