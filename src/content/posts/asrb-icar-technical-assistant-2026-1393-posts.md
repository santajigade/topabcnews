The Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board (ASRB) is hiring for 1,393 technical posts through its Centralized Combined Examination-2026. Of these, 1,254 are Technical Assistant (T-3) posts at <a href="https://icar.org.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ICAR institutes</a> and 139 are Senior Technical Assistant (T-4) posts at ICAR's Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs). The notification, F. No. 2(2)/2025-Exam, is dated November 11, 2026. Online forms opened on November 18 and close on December 17, 2026 at 11:59 pm, and the preliminary exam is set for February 2027. Applications are taken only on the <a href="https://asrb.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ASRB website</a>.

| Detail | T-3 Technical Assistant | T-4 Senior Technical Assistant |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 1,254 | 139 |
| Where | ICAR institutes | ICAR KVKs |
| Pay | Level 5 (Rs 29,200 to Rs 92,300) | Level 6 (Rs 35,400 to Rs 1,12,400) |
| Age | 21 to 30 years on December 17, 2026 | 21 to 30 years on December 17, 2026 |
| Last date (form and fee) | December 17, 2026, 11:59 pm | December 17, 2026, 11:59 pm |

The board calls the vacancy numbers tentative. Corrections to submitted forms are allowed from December 18 to 20, 2026, with any fee for changes paid in that window.

## Who Can Apply: Degrees by Group

You need the degree for your group by the closing date.

- **Agriculture:** B.Sc. in Agriculture, Horticulture, Agricultural Engineering, Agro-forestry, Food Science, Biotechnology, Environmental Science, Home Science, Community Science or Natural Farming.
- **Animal Science:** B.V.Sc. & A.H.
- **Fisheries Science:** B.F.Sc.
- **Home Science / Social Science:** Bachelor's in Home Science or Community Science.
- **Workshop groups:** B.E./B.Tech or a three-year diploma in the matching branch (Civil, Agricultural or Mechanical, Electrical, or Electronics and Instrumentation).
- **Library Science:** Bachelor's in Library Science or Library and Information Science.
- **Information Technology:** BCA, a bachelor's in Computer Science or IT, or B.E./B.Tech in IT, CS or ECE.

The upper age limit is relaxed by 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (non-creamy layer), 10 years for PwBD and 3 years for ex-servicemen after their service is deducted. In-service ICAR technical staff in the UR category can apply up to 40 years of age.

### Steps to Apply

1. Open asrb.gov.in, find the Centralized Combined Examination-2026 link and register with a working email ID and mobile number.
2. Choose T-3, T-4 or both, and pick one functional group. If you choose both posts, rank them in order of preference. You cannot change that order later.
3. Upload your photo, signature and documents.
4. Pay the fee, if any, and submit before 11:59 pm on December 17. Only one form per candidate is accepted.

## Exam Pattern: Prelims and Mains

The Prelims is a one-hour computer based test of 100 multiple choice questions for 100 marks, in Hindi and English. It has four sections of 25 marks each: General Knowledge, General English, Quantitative Aptitude and General Agriculture. A wrong answer costs 0.25 marks. To qualify, UR candidates need 30%, EWS and OBC candidates 25%, and SC, ST and PwBD candidates 20%. Prelims marks are used only for shortlisting.

The Mains is a two-hour test of 100 questions worth 300 marks on your functional group, and final selection rests on it. There is no interview. The Prelims will be held in 62 cities and the Mains in 31 (tentative lists).
