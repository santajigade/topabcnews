India are champions of Asia for a third year running. On Thursday, November 5, 2026, the men's team came from a goal down to beat Japan 4-1 in the <a href="https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/hockey/india-vs-japan-asian-champions-trophy-2026-match-report" target="_blank" rel="noopener">final of the Asian Champions Trophy</a> at the Olympian Balbir Singh Senior International Hockey Stadium in Mohali. It is India's sixth title, the most by any team, and no side had won the event three times in a row before. India won all seven of their matches.

| Final | Details |
|---|---|
| Result | India 4, Japan 1 |
| Half time | Japan 1, India 0 |
| Venue | Mohali, Punjab |
| India's record | 7 played, 7 won |
| Titles | 6th overall, 3rd in a row (2023, 2024, 2026) |

## Japan Strike First, India Turn It Around After the Break

Japan scored in the third minute. Kazumasa Matsumoto got past the Indian defence inside the circle and finished with a reverse flick from a tight angle. Japan then sat deep and kept that lead until half time.

India broke through in the 42nd minute. After a run of penalty corners kept Japan pinned back, Aditya Lalage slipped a reverse pass to Shilanand Lakra, who scored from close range. The last quarter then swung the match:

| Minute | Team | Scorer | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Japan | Kazumasa Matsumoto | Reverse flick in the circle |
| 42 | India | Shilanand Lakra | Close-range finish from Aditya Lalage's pass |
| 48 | India | Dilpreet Singh | Turned and flicked after a Hardik Singh assist |
| 53 | India | Jugraj Singh | Penalty corner drag flick |
| 54 | India | Vivek Sagar Prasad | Low half-volley from a rebound |

Reports differ by a minute on the last three goals; the times above follow Outlook India and DT Next. Three goals in about six minutes ended Japan's resistance. Captain Harmanpreet Singh said afterwards that the focus was "to stay focused for the entire 60 minutes."

## Seven Matches, Seven Wins

India opened in Jalandhar at the Olympian Surjit Singh Hockey Stadium and moved to Mohali for the knockouts.

| Stage | Opponent | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| League | Bangladesh | 13-0 | Opening match, October 27 |
| League | China | 5-2 | Sukhjeet Singh and Harmanpreet scored twice each |
| League | Japan | 4-1 | October 30, Jalandhar |
| League | Malaysia | 4-2 | Harmanpreet hat-trick, November 1 |
| League | South Korea | 8-0 | India top the league with 15 points |
| Semi-final | South Korea | 8-1 | Lakra and Dilpreet scored twice each |
| Final | Japan | 4-1 | November 5, Mohali |

In the other semi-final, Japan and Malaysia drew 0-0 and Japan won the shootout 5-3. Malaysia beat South Korea 5-2 to take bronze. For Japan it was a third lost final, after defeats in 2013 and 2021, and they had already lost 4-1 to India in the league stage.

## India's Six Asian Champions Trophy Titles

The 2026 tournament was the ninth edition of the event.

| Year | Result for India |
|---|---|
| 2011 | Champions |
| 2016 | Champions |
| 2018 | Joint champions (title shared) |
| 2023 | Champions |
| 2024 | Champions |
| 2026 | Champions, beat Japan 4-1 |

Three of the six titles have come in the last four years, which shows how settled this squad has become against Asian opposition.

## How This Fits India's 2026 Season

The title caps a mixed year. At the FIH World Cup in Belgium and the Netherlands, India finished eighth after losing to Belgium in a shootout on August 28, following a 3-3 draw. Defeats to the Netherlands and Argentina in the second round had ended their medal hopes.

Asia has been a different story. On October 3, India beat Malaysia 5-1 in the Asian Games final in Japan to keep their gold and book a direct place at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Less than five weeks later, they added the Asian Champions Trophy, beating Malaysia again on the way.

## What Comes Next for the Men's Team

The next big test is the 2026-27 FIH Pro League, which runs from December 8, 2026 to June 27, 2027. India's men play 16 matches and start with their first eight at home in February. According to <a href="https://www.hockey.org.au/news/kookaburras-and-hockeyroos-discover-2026-27-fih-pro-league-locations-and-dates" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Hockey Australia's published schedule</a>, the India stage runs from February 13 to 28, 2027, with Argentina, Germany, Great Britain and Spain also playing there. Venues have not been named yet. The same schedule lists India in further stages in Belgium, Germany and the Netherlands in June 2027.

These games against top European and South American sides will be a sterner test than Asian opposition, and a fairer measure of whether the team has moved on from its World Cup finish.
