The regular application window for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 shuts at 11:50 PM today, Sunday, November 22, 2026. The National Testing Agency (NTA) will then keep the form open for four more days, November 23 to 26, for anyone willing to pay an extra Rs 500. Session 1 exams are on January 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28, 2027.

Today's 11:50 PM cutoff applies to the form and the fee together. NTA has not given a separate, later date for payment, so a form saved without a successful fee transaction will not count.

## JEE Main 2027 Session 1 Calendar From Tonight to the Exam

All dates below come from NTA's <a href="https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JEE Main website</a> and its public notice of October 24, 2026. Every deadline closes at 11:50 PM on the date shown.

| Stage | Date | What you need to do |
|---|---|---|
| Regular form and fee deadline | November 22, 2026 | Submit form and pay normal fee |
| Late fee window | November 23 to 26, 2026 | Submit form, pay normal fee plus Rs 500 |
| Correction window | November 27 to 29, 2026 | Edit only the fields NTA opens |
| City intimation slip | December 30, 2026 | Check your allotted exam city |
| Admit card | January 13, 2027 | Download and print |
| Session 1 exams | January 20 to 24 and 28, 2027 | Appear in your allotted shift |

Only certain fields will be editable in the correction window. NTA will publish that list when the window opens.

## Missed Tonight? How the Late Fee Route Works

These steps follow NTA's information bulletin and notices.

1. Log in at jeemain.nta.nic.in between November 23 and 26. NTA's October 28 advisory asks candidates to sign in with their own DigiLocker, JanParichay or e-Pramaan account through Meri Pehchaan, and to do Re-KYC on DigiLocker before filling the form.
2. Check that your name and date of birth match your Class 10 certificate. The advisory specifically warns about this.
3. Upload your photo and signature in the sizes listed in the table further down.
4. Pay online by net banking, credit card, debit card or UPI. The bulletin allows no offline payment. You pay the normal fee, the Rs 500 late fee, plus processing charges and GST.
5. Download the confirmation page and keep it safe.

### Made a mistake in an earlier form?

NTA's October 30 notice says a candidate can submit a fresh form even after paying for an earlier one. Only the last form with a successful payment and confirmation page will be considered. The earlier fee will not be refunded or adjusted, and filing more than one form does not give you an extra attempt in the same session. A fresh form must be finished by tonight, or by November 26 with the late fee.

## Paper Pattern and Shift Timings

Paper 1 is a computer-based test of 75 questions for 300 marks. Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry get 25 questions each. In every subject, Section A has 20 multiple choice questions and Section B has 5 numerical answer questions. A correct answer earns 4 marks, a wrong one costs 1 mark, and a skipped question scores zero.

Paper 1 runs for 3 hours, in two shifts: 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon and 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM IST. Candidates taking Paper 2A and 2B together sit for 3 hours 30 minutes. NTA asks students to report two hours early. Gates close 30 minutes before the start.

Paper 2A for B.Arch has 77 questions and a pen and paper drawing test, for 400 marks. Paper 2B for B.Planning has 100 questions, also for 400 marks. The exam is offered in 13 languages, including English and Hindi.

## Session 2 Dates to Keep in Mind

The bulletin also gives a tentative Session 2 schedule. Applications open January 5 and close February 3, 2027. Exams follow on April 2 to 7, 2027. NTA will issue a separate notice for Session 2. If you registered for both sessions in your Session 1 form, the same application number carries over.

## JEE Main 2027: Common Questions

**What is the last date for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 registration?**
The regular last date is November 22, 2026, at 11:50 PM. With a late fee of Rs 500, you can apply until November 26, 2026.

**Is there an age limit for JEE Main 2027?**
NTA sets no upper age limit. You must have passed Class 12 in 2025 or 2026, or be appearing in 2027. Students who passed in 2024 or earlier cannot apply. Individual institutes may have their own age rules.

**When will the JEE Main 2027 correction window open?**
The window runs from November 27 to 29, 2026. Only the fields NTA lists can be changed.

**When will the JEE Main 2027 admit card be released?**
NTA plans to release the Session 1 admit card on January 13, 2027. The city intimation slip comes earlier, on December 30, 2026.
