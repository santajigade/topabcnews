A large Microsoft outage on Thursday, November 12, left many users unable to open their Outlook inboxes or send email, and also disrupted Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 apps. Problem reports for Outlook on the tracking site Downdetector climbed to about 130,000 at the peak, most of them from the Americas. Microsoft said a large number of its servers had unexpectedly gone offline, pushing extra load onto the rest of its systems, and it began moving traffic around to restore service.

## How the Outage Unfolded

Trouble began to build early on Thursday afternoon US Eastern Time. According to a Reuters report carried by <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/microsoft-outage-hits-tens-of-thousands-of-users" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera</a>, outage reports peaked shortly after 1pm ET (18:00 GMT, or about 11:30pm IST). By just before 3pm ET, the number of Outlook reports had fallen to around 77,000, and later updates from Tom's Guide showed it still above 25,000 as recovery continued.

Users mainly described three problems: they could not reach their mailboxes, they could not send or receive messages, and some email arrived late. Euronews reported that the problems were especially widespread across the Americas, although Microsoft said users in any region could be affected.

### Key highlights

- **Date:** Thursday, November 12, 2026 (late Thursday night in India)
- **Main service hit:** Outlook email, through Exchange Online
- **Also affected:** Microsoft Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 tools
- **Peak reports:** About 130,000 for Outlook on Downdetector
- **Microsoft 365 reports:** More than 46,000, according to Euronews
- **Teams reports:** Around 3,000
- **Incident IDs:** EX1550543 and MO1550821 in the Microsoft 365 admin centre

## What Microsoft Said Caused the Problem

In its status updates, Microsoft first said users in the Americas could not access Exchange Online mailboxes through several connection methods. It later explained that parts of its infrastructure were handling heavier than normal traffic.

The company said servers became unavailable and then failed automatic health checks. That put extra pressure on the servers still running, which led to connection errors for users. Microsoft also said it had not found a recent change that lined up with the start of the problem, and was looking at whether a component that processes server health signals was involved.

To fix it, Microsoft said it was testing a configuration change to spread traffic more evenly and restarting the affected services. "We're working to safely return them to service," the company said, according to Reuters.

## Was the Outage Fully Fixed?

Microsoft said it had resolved the Teams issues in North America, and some users reported that Outlook was working again. However, the company did not give a firm time for full recovery, and Tom's Guide noted the issue was only partly resolved for some users at its last update. Microsoft did not give an estimate of how many people were affected in total.

Downdetector numbers are based on reports sent by users. They show the scale and timing of a problem, but they do not equal the actual number of people affected, which could be higher or lower.

## Why Microsoft 365 Outages Hit So Hard

Outlook, Teams and the wider <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Microsoft 365</a> suite sit at the centre of daily work for companies, government offices, schools and universities around the world. When email and chat stop working together, meetings, approvals and customer communication can grind to a halt within minutes.

Copilot, Microsoft's AI assistant, is also now built into many of these apps, so an outage on the core services can knock out AI features as well. Tom's Guide noted that earlier Outlook outages had lasted far longer, with one in July of last year running 19 to 21 hours and another in August stretching to 67 hours.

The disruption came on a busy day for big tech news, with markets already reacting to <a href="/jensen-huang-resigns-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-interim/">Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO</a>.

## What Users in India Should Know

Because the outage peaked late on Thursday night IST, most Indian offices were closed when the worst of it hit, and reports were concentrated in the Americas. Indian teams working night shifts for clients in the Americas were the ones most likely to run into the problems during their working hours.

If Outlook or Teams misbehaves during an outage like this, a few simple steps can help:

1. Check the service health page in the Microsoft 365 admin centre (for IT admins) or your company's IT notices.
2. Try the web version of Outlook or Teams as well as the desktop app, since different connection methods can behave differently.
3. Avoid repeatedly deleting and re-adding your account, which does not fix a server side problem.
4. Keep an alternate channel, such as phone or another messaging tool, for urgent work communication.

## The Outage Raises Questions About Cloud Resilience

Microsoft has not yet published a full root cause report for the November 12 incident. Its early explanation, servers dropping out and overloading the rest of the system, shows how a fault in one part of a huge cloud platform can spread quickly to a very large number of users. Businesses that depend on a single provider for email, chat and AI tools will be watching for Microsoft's final review and any steps it takes to prevent a repeat.
