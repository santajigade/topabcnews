India's wholesale inflation rose to 12.29% in October 2026, up from 11.39% in September, according to provisional data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry on Monday, November 16. Fuel and power did most of the lifting: prices in that group were 32.84% higher than a year earlier. The reading comes four days after retail inflation crossed the Reserve Bank of India's 6% upper limit, and about two weeks before the RBI's next policy meeting, so it adds to the pressure on interest rates and on the prices shoppers pay in the months ahead.

## Where Wholesale Prices Rose the Most

The all India Wholesale Price Index, which now uses 2022-23 as its base year, stood at 113.3 in October against 112.5 in September. The rise was not even across the three main groups.

| WPI group | September 2026 | October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel and power | 25.85% | 32.84% |
| Primary articles | 8.67% | 9.24% |
| Manufactured products | 9.76% | 9.54% |
| WPI Food Index | 8.66% | 8.58% |
| **Headline WPI** | **11.39%** | **12.29%** |

Inside fuel and power, crude petroleum and natural gas prices were up 70.80% from a year ago, compared with 57.32% in September. Mineral oils, which cover refined products, rose 45.57%. Electricity turned from a small fall of 0.43% in September to a rise of 8.06% in October, while coal and lignite went up 10.49%.

Manufacturing costs stayed high even though the group's rate eased a little. Chemicals and chemical products rose 17.53%, basic metals 17.21%, rubber and plastic products 13.90%, and textiles 13.67%. Among primary articles, minerals rose 12.64% and non-food articles 11.69%.

Food was the one area with slight relief. The WPI Food Index, which carries close to a quarter of the index weight, eased to 8.58%, although food articles on their own edged up to 8.26%.

## A Steady Climb Since the Middle of the Year

October's figure is not a one month jump. Wholesale inflation has risen every month in the data published since July:

| Month (2026) | WPI inflation | Fuel and power |
|---|---|---|
| July | 9.78% | Not reported in our sources |
| August | 9.92% | 22.93% |
| September | 11.39% | 25.85% |
| October | 12.29% | 32.84% |

For April to October 2026-27, cumulative wholesale inflation was 10.14%, compared with minus 0.30% in the same period of 2025-26, according to DD India. In August, Outlook Business linked the steady rise in wholesale prices to the West Asia war and the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, which pushed up global crude and fertiliser costs. ## Why a 12% WPI and a 6% CPI Can Both Be True

Many readers ask why wholesale inflation is double the retail figure. The two indices measure different things.

| | Wholesale Price Index (WPI) | Consumer Price Index (CPI) |
|---|---|---|
| Released by | Ministry of Commerce and Industry | Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI) |
| Prices tracked | First bulk sale of goods, at factory or market gate | Prices paid by households in shops |
| Covers services | No | Yes (rent, health, education, transport) |
| October 2026 reading | 12.29% | 6.09% |
| Used by RBI for its target | No | Yes |

Three points explain the gap. First, WPI gives a much larger weight to fuel, metals and chemicals, exactly the items now rising fastest. Second, CPI includes services such as rent and school fees, which move slowly. Third, retail fuel prices are partly controlled, so a 70% rise in crude does not show up fully or quickly at petrol pumps or in cooking gas bills.

That third point is also the risk. Wholesale prices are costs for businesses. When companies pay much more for fuel, chemicals, plastics and metal, they usually try to pass some of it on to buyers over the following months. A large and widening gap between WPI and CPI is often read as a sign that retail inflation has more room to rise. City gas firms have already started; see our report on the <a href="/mumbai-cng-price-hike-rs-91-50-november-15/">Mumbai CNG price hike</a>.

## What It Could Mean for Loans and Household Budgets

The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% on October 7 and shifted its stance to "calibrated tightening", according to the <a href="https://rbi.org.in/scriptS/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63742" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official RBI policy resolution</a>. In that statement it projected retail inflation at 6.0% for the October to December quarter and 5.2% for the full year. October's 6.09% CPI is already above that quarterly forecast, and the wholesale data shows cost pressure building further up the supply chain.

The next MPC meeting runs from December 2 to 4, 2026. For households, the practical points are:

- **Borrowers:** Floating rate home, car and personal loans linked to the repo rate would get costlier if the RBI raises rates again. Banks usually pass on changes over the following weeks.
- **Savers:** Higher policy rates have generally been followed by better fixed deposit rates, though banks set their own timing.
- **Shoppers:** Goods with a high share of fuel, plastic or chemical inputs, such as packaged products, detergents and transport services, are the ones most exposed to price increases if companies pass on costs.
- **Small businesses:** Firms buying raw material in bulk feel wholesale prices directly, well before they show up in retail data.

## Next Data Points to Watch

The Commerce Ministry said the provisional October figure was based on a weighted response rate of 81.1%, so it can be revised. The final August number was kept at 9.92%. Detailed tables are available on the <a href="https://eaindustry.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Office of the Economic Adviser website</a>. The November WPI, along with the Output and Input Producer Price Indices, is due on December 14, 2026, which is after the RBI's December decision. Until then, global crude prices and any change in retail fuel rates will be the clearest early signals of where wholesale inflation goes next.
