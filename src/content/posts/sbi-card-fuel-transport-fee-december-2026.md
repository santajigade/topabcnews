From **Tuesday, December 1, 2026**, SBI Card will charge a 1% fee on two kinds of large credit card payments: fuel, once a billing cycle's fuel spend crosses **Rs 50,000**, and any single transport payment above **Rs 30,000**. The card issuer announced the change in a customer notice dated October 27, according to <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/sbi-card-to-levy-1-fee-on-transport-payments-above-rs30000-fuel-spends-over-rs50000-from-december-1-564529-2026-10-30" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Today</a>. Outlook Money and Upstox report that the fee applies across SBI credit card variants, and that applicable taxes come on top.

How the two rules compare:

| | Fuel | Transport |
|---|---|---|
| Fee | 1% | 1% |
| Trigger | Total fuel spend in a billing cycle above Rs 50,000 | A single transaction above Rs 30,000 |
| Charged on | The whole cycle's fuel spend, not just the excess | That transaction |
| Merchant codes (MCC) | 5172, 5541, 5542, 5983 | 4111, 4784, 4131 |
| Effective | December 1, 2026 | December 1, 2026 |

## The Fuel Rule Has a Cliff at Rs 50,000

The fuel fee is calculated on the billing cycle total. Stay at Rs 50,000 or below and nothing is charged. Go above it and SBI Card charges 1% on the entire amount, starting from the first rupee. The notice says the fee will be charged on the total fuel spends, Business Today reported.

The four fuel codes cover petroleum products, service stations, automated fuel dispensers and fuel dealers, according to Upstox and CardInsider.

## Which Transport Payments Count

The transport fee works per transaction. Spending across the cycle is not added up. A payment of Rs 35,000 attracts the fee, while two payments of Rs 20,000 each do not, even though they total Rs 40,000, Outlook Money explained.

The three transport codes cover local and suburban commuter transport (4111), tolls and bridge fees (4784) and bus lines (4131), according to Outlook Money. SBI Card has said that card networks assign these codes and may change them without telling customers.

## What the Fee Will Cost You: Worked Examples

These figures apply the published rules to common spending levels and exclude taxes, which are charged on top.

| Spend in one billing cycle | Fee on fuel | Fee on transport (if it is one payment) |
|---|---|---|
| Rs 20,000 | Nil | Nil |
| Rs 30,000 | Nil | Nil |
| Rs 35,000 | Nil | Rs 350 |
| Rs 50,000 | Nil | Rs 500 |
| Rs 52,000 | Rs 520 | Rs 520 |
| Rs 60,000 | Rs 600 | Rs 600 |
| Rs 75,000 | Rs 750 | Rs 750 |
| Rs 1,00,000 | Rs 1,000 | Rs 1,000 |

In practice:

- **Watch the Rs 50,000 line on fuel.** At Rs 50,000 you pay nothing; at Rs 52,000 you pay Rs 520. A cardholder near the limit late in a billing cycle may be better off paying for the next fill by another method.
- **Fuel spend is counted per billing cycle, not per calendar month.** Check your statement date: the cycle runs from one statement to the next.
- **Small, regular transport payments are untouched.** Bus tickets, metro top-ups and toll payments of Rs 30,000 or less carry no fee, however many you make.
- **Big one-off transport bills are the target.** The fee falls on a single large payment coded as commuter transport, tolls or bus lines.

Heavy fuel or transport spenders and business users are the ones who should check recent statements.

## How to Check If Your Spending Will Be Hit

1. Open your last two or three SBI Card statements in the SBI Card app or on the website.
2. Add up all fuel transactions in each billing cycle. If any cycle crossed Rs 50,000, the new fee would have applied to the whole fuel total.
3. Look for any single transport payment above Rs 30,000.
4. If you are unsure whether a merchant falls under these codes, ask SBI Card customer care for the MCC of that transaction.
5. Read the original notice on <a href="https://www.sbicard.com/en/customer-notices.page" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SBI Card's customer notices page</a> before you change how you pay.

## SBI Card Fuel and Transport Fee: Common Questions

**Does the 1% fuel fee apply on spends below Rs 50,000?**
No. If your eligible fuel spend in a billing cycle is Rs 50,000 or less, no fee is charged.

**If I spend Rs 60,000 on fuel, is the fee only on Rs 10,000?**
No. Once the cycle total crosses Rs 50,000, the 1% is charged on the full amount, so Rs 60,000 of fuel means a Rs 600 fee plus taxes.

**Are several small transport payments added together?**
No. The Rs 30,000 limit applies to each transaction separately, so smaller payments are not combined.

**Which SBI cards are covered?**
Reports by Outlook Money and Upstox say all SBI credit card variants are covered, and GoodReturns names co-branded cards such as BPCL SBI Card OCTANE and Flipkart SBI Credit Card among them.
