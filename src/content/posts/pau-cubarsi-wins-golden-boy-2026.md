Barcelona centre-back Pau Cubarsi has been named the winner of the 2026 Golden Boy award, the prize given each year by Italian sports daily Tuttosport to the best young footballer playing in Europe. The result was announced on Monday, November 9. Cubarsi, 19, becomes the fifth Barcelona player to win the award, following Lionel Messi, Pedri, Gavi and Lamine Yamal, and he will receive the trophy at a gala in Turin on December 14.

## How the Golden Boy Winner Was Chosen

The Golden Boy recognises the best under-21 player in European football. Tuttosport first drew up a list of 100 candidates, which was later cut to 25 finalists. The shortlist was published on October 5, and Spain had the most names on it, with four players.

According to the <a href="https://rfef.es/es/noticias/cuatro-jugadores-espanoles-optan-al-golden-boy-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Royal Spanish Football Federation</a>, those four were Cubarsi, his Barcelona teammates Marc Bernal and Xavi Espart, and Real Sociedad defender Jon Martin. Espart made the list through one of five special wild card invitations.

A jury of 50 journalists from leading European media outlets then voted to pick the winner. Cubarsi finished ahead of a strong group of contenders, including Real Madrid's Endrick and Yan Diomande, Chelsea's Estevao and Paris Saint-Germain's Warren Zaire-Emery. Detailed vote totals have not been published.

## Cubarsi's Breakthrough Year for Club and Country

Cubarsi had been seen as the favourite for months, and his year explains why. He helped Barcelona win La Liga and the Spanish Super Cup, and then carried that form into the summer with Spain. The Spanish federation said he was voted best young player of the 2026 World Cup, and Goal reported that he started in the tournament final.

His rise has also been recognised at the biggest awards. Cubarsi finished 12th in the 2026 Ballon d'Or rankings, an event in which England captain Harry Kane took the main prize. In the men's Young Talent of the Year category, he came second with 94 points, just behind his clubmate Lamine Yamal on 96.

This season, Goal reported, he has made 13 appearances in all competitions and provided two assists. He also played in Barcelona's 2-1 win over Atletico Madrid, a result that lifted the Catalan club to 33 points, three clear of second-placed Real Madrid in La Liga. The Standard noted that Cubarsi won the award two months before his 20th birthday.

## Barcelona's Record in Golden Boy History

The award adds to Barcelona's strong record with young talent from its La Masia academy. Barcelona players have now won the Golden Boy five times, more than any other European club, according to Barca Universal. Manchester United are next on that list with three winners.

### Barcelona's Golden Boy winners

| Year | Player | Position |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | Lionel Messi | Forward |
| 2021 | Pedri | Midfielder |
| 2022 | Gavi | Midfielder |
| 2024 | Lamine Yamal | Forward |
| 2026 | Pau Cubarsi | Defender |

Barcelona players have taken four of the last six editions. The two winners from other clubs in that period were Jude Bellingham in 2023 and Desire Doue in 2025. Other famous names on the all-time list include Wayne Rooney, Sergio Aguero and Mario Gotze.

Cubarsi is also the only defender among Barcelona's five winners, which makes his win stand out. The Golden Boy rules do not allow repeat winners, which is why Lamine Yamal, the 2024 winner, was not on this year's shortlist, as <a href="https://www.fcbarcelona.es/es/futbol/primer-equipo/noticias/4586995/cubarsi-bernal-y-xavi-espart-entre-los-25-finalistas-al-golden-boy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">FC Barcelona noted</a> when the finalists were named. The award is run by Tuttosport together with Transfermarkt.

Barcelona's success with the award follows a pattern seen in other sports too, where a strong youth system feeds the senior team. India's hockey teams have shown similar depth recently, as seen in the [Asian Champions Trophy title win](/india-win-asian-champions-trophy-2026/).

- **Shortlist:** 25 finalists, cut from 100 candidates

## What Lies Ahead for Cubarsi

With the gala in Turin set for mid-December, Cubarsi's immediate focus will be helping Barcelona stay ahead of Real Madrid at the top of La Liga. Past winners have shown that the Golden Boy can be the start of a great career, and Messi remains the only winner on Barca Universal's list who has gone on to lift the Ballon d'Or. Barcelona will hope their young defender can follow that path.
