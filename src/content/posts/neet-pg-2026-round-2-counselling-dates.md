The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) declared the NEET PG 2026 round 2 seat allotment result on November 20, three days later than planned. The result was due on November 17, but MCC stopped processing it that evening after a Madras High Court interim order. Allotted candidates had to report by November 25, and All India Quota (AIQ) round 3 is next.

## Update (25 November 2026)

| What | Status as of November 25 |
|---|---|
| Round 2 result (original date) | November 17 |
| Result put on hold | November 17, after a Madras High Court interim order |
| Court clarifies stay | November 19, limited to one Puducherry institute |
| Round 2 result declared | November 20, on mcc.nic.in |
| AIQ round 3 (tentative) | November 26 to December 4 |
| AIQ stray vacancy round (tentative) | December 16 to 24 |
| Last date of joining (tentative) | December 31 |

**Why the result stopped.** On November 17 the Madras High Court passed an interim order in a petition filed by Sri Venkateshwaraa Medical College Hospital and Research Centre against the Government of India (WP No. 46350 of 2026). The order stayed further admission proceedings and the joining of students through MCC and CENTAC, the Puducherry counselling body. MCC then issued a notice saying seat processing for the round 2 result had been "kept on hold", as Medical Dialogues reported.

The college had challenged a November 11 Puducherry government order that asked MCC to counsel its MD and MS seats for 2026-27. On November 19 the court clarified that the stay applies only to Sri Venkateshwaraa Deemed University and does not cover counselling for other institutions. MCC released the round 2 allotment the next day.

**Reporting.** The original schedule gave reporting from November 18 to 24 and MCC verification of joined candidates on November 25. After the delay, Careers360 reported that allotted candidates had to report on or before November 25. Reporting dates were listed differently during the hold, so check the latest notice on the official <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MCC website</a>.

**Round 3.** A tentative MCC schedule dated November 16, reported by Careers360, places AIQ round 3 between November 26 and December 4, state counselling from December 4 to 12, the AIQ stray vacancy round from December 16 to 24, the state stray round from December 22 to 25, and December 31 as the last date of joining. As of November 25 we could not find a detailed round 3 notice with registration, choice filling and result dates, so we are not listing them.

## How Round 2 Was Planned

Round 2 registration was first due on November 6. MCC pushed it to November 7 after the <a href="https://www.nmc.org.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Medical Commission</a> sent a seat matrix of newly approved postgraduate seats on November 3. Institutes had to verify it after applying the reservation roster. MCC's revised schedule:

| Activity | Date and time |
|---|---|
| Institutes verify round 2 seat matrix | By 11 am, November 9 |
| Resign round 1 seat without losing security deposit | By 6 pm, November 9 |
| Round 2 registration | November 7 (1 pm) to November 14 (3 pm) |
| Edit or reset registration | November 7 (3 pm) to November 14 (1 pm) |
| Fee payment | By 6 pm, November 14 |
| Choice filling | November 10 (1 pm) to November 15 (11:55 pm) |
| Choice locking | November 14 (4 pm) to November 15 (11:55 pm) |
| Seat processing and provisional result | November 16 |
| Final result (original) | November 17 |
| Reporting (original) | November 18 to 24 |
| MCC verifies joined candidates (original) | November 25 |

## Round 2 Rules on Re-registration and Resigning a Seat

These were the round 2 rules for candidates already in the process:

- Candidates who registered in round 1 and did not get a seat did not have to register again for round 2. They did have to fill and lock a fresh list of choices, because round 1 choices did not carry over.
- Candidates holding a round 1 seat could resign without losing the security deposit only until 6 pm on November 9.
- The way to resign depended on the option chosen. A "freeze" candidate had to go to the allotted institute in person. A "float" candidate could resign online on the MCC portal.

Round 3 terms will come in MCC's round 3 notice, so do not assume the round 2 rules carry over.

## How to Register, Fill Choices and Check Results on mcc.nic.in

These are the steps MCC used for round 2 registration:

1. Go to mcc.nic.in and open the "PG Medical Counselling" section.
2. Under "Candidate Activity Board", click the new registration link for NEET PG Counselling 2026.
3. Enter your NEET PG details and personal information.
4. Create login credentials and sign in.
5. Complete the registration form and pay the counselling fee online.
6. Submit the form and save the confirmation page.

Once choice filling opens, log in again and add colleges and MD or MS courses in order of preference, then lock them inside the locking window. Results are posted in the same PG Medical Counselling section of mcc.nic.in.

The round 2 result moved by three days after a court order, so check the MCC notices page daily until you have reported to your college.
