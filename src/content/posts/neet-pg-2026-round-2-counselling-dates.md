The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has started registration for round 2 of NEET PG 2026 counselling for MD, MS and PG diploma seats. The window opened on Saturday, November 7, at 1 pm and will close on November 14 at 3 pm. Choice filling begins on November 10 and the final round 2 result is scheduled for November 17. The round was pushed back by a day so that newly approved postgraduate seats could be verified and added to the pool.

## Why MCC Delayed Round 2 by One Day

Round 2 registration was first planned for November 6. According to Medical Dialogues, the delay came after the <a href="https://www.nmc.org.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Medical Commission</a> released the seat matrix for newly approved PG seats on November 3. Medical colleges then had to verify that matrix after applying the reservation roster before the round could move ahead.

This matters for candidates because the fresh seats are expected to be part of round 2. Careers360 reported that the NMC's revised 2026 to 27 seat matrix lists 68,305 approved broad speciality seats, with seats in private institutes up by 13 percent. Maharashtra alone added 1,066 seats, taking its total MD and MS seats to 7,615.

The full timeline, based on the MCC schedule as reported by Careers360, is below:

## Do Round 1 Candidates Need to Register Again?

No. Candidates who registered in round 1 but did not get a seat do not need to register again. However, they must fill and lock a fresh set of choices for round 2, because round 1 choices do not carry over.

Students who already hold a round 1 seat have until 6 pm on November 9 to resign without forfeiting their security deposit. The method depends on the option they chose:

- **Freeze option:** visit the allotted institute in person to resign.
- **Float option:** resign online through the MCC portal.

## How to Register for NEET PG Round 2 at mcc.nic.in

New candidates can register on the official <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MCC website</a> by following these steps:

1. Go to mcc.nic.in and open the "PG Medical Counselling" section.
2. Under "Candidate Activity Board", click the new registration link for NEET PG Counselling 2026.
3. Enter your NEET PG details and personal information.
4. Create login credentials and sign in.
5. Complete the registration form and pay the counselling fee online.
6. Submit the form and save the confirmation page for your records.

Once choice filling opens on November 10, log in again to select colleges and MD or MS specialisations in order of preference.

## Tips Before You Lock Your Choices

Choice locking runs only from November 14 to November 15, so candidates should prepare their list early. A few practical points help:

- Check the updated seat matrix after November 9, since new seats may change your options.
- Order choices by genuine preference, not by what you think you will get.
- Make sure the registration fee is paid by 6 pm on November 14, which is the payment deadline.
- Keep an eye on the MCC notices page, as dates have already shifted once this round.
