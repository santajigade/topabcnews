The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) opened online applications on November 20, 2026 for 365 posts of Civil Judge (Junior Division) under the UP Judicial Service Civil Judge Examination 2026, better known as PCS J. The last date to apply and pay the fee is **Monday, December 21, 2026**, which leaves exactly 30 days from today. Corrections to a submitted form are allowed until December 28. Candidates must be between 22 and 35 years old on July 1, 2027.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Post | Civil Judge (Junior Division) |
| Vacancies | 365 (provisional, can change) |
| Advertisement no. | A-6/E-1/2026 |
| Applications open | November 20, 2026 |
| Last date to apply and pay fee | December 21, 2026 |
| Correction window ends | December 28, 2026 |
| Age limit | 22 to 35 years as on July 1, 2027 |
| Prelims date | Not announced yet |
| Where to apply | <a href="https://uppsc.up.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">uppsc.up.nic.in</a> |

The commission has said the vacancy count is provisional. The short notice released with the advertisement does not give a category-wise split of the 365 posts, so candidates should check the detailed advertisement on the UPPSC site for reserved seats.

## Am I Eligible? Check Your Date of Birth

The age is counted on July 1, 2027, not on the date you apply. A candidate must be born between **July 2, 1992 and July 1, 2005**, both dates included, according to the date window reported by CareerIndia and Testbook from the notice.

| Your date of birth | Status for PCS J 2026 (general rule) |
|---|---|
| On or before July 1, 1992 | Over the upper limit, unless a category relaxation applies |
| July 2, 1992 to July 1, 2005 | Within the age band |
| On or after July 2, 2005 | Under 22 on July 1, 2027, not eligible |

Reserved category candidates of Uttar Pradesh and candidates with disabilities get upper age relaxation as per state rules. The news reports do not list the exact years of relaxation, so check the detailed advertisement before ruling yourself out. Candidates from other states apply in the general category.

On qualification, <a href="https://www.amarujala.com/jobs/uppsc-pcs-j-recruitment-2026-notification-out-for-365-civil-judge-posts-apply-from-november-20-2026-11-20" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Amar Ujala reports</a> that a law degree (LLB) from a recognised university is required. Testbook's summary of the notice adds that advocates enrolled under the Advocates Act, 1961 are also covered. Neither the news reports nor the summaries say whether final year LLB students can apply, so treat that as unconfirmed until you read the full advertisement.

## Application Fee by Category

Both Amar Ujala and Times Now Navbharat report the same fee table:

| Category | Fee |
|---|---|
| General, OBC, EWS | Rs 125 |
| SC, ST | Rs 65 |
| Persons with disabilities | Rs 25 |

The fee is paid online by debit card, credit card or net banking, and the payment deadline is the same as the application deadline, December 21. Amar Ujala advises keeping the payment receipt along with a copy of the submitted form.

## How to Fill the PCS J Form, Step by Step

1. Open uppsc.up.nic.in and find the link for the Civil Judge (Junior Division) Examination 2026.
2. Register with your basic details. A registration number and password are sent to your mobile number. Testbook reports that UPPSC uses One Time Registration (OTR), so candidates who already have an OTR number should use it.
3. Log in and fill in your personal, educational and category details.
4. Upload your scanned photograph and signature in the size and format the form asks for.
5. Pay the fee online.
6. Check every entry, submit the form and download a printout.

If you spot a mistake after submitting, you can fix it until December 28, 2026. After that date the form is locked.

## How Selection Works

The selection has three stages: a preliminary exam, a main exam and an interview with document verification. Testbook's reading of the notice says the prelims has two objective papers, General Knowledge and Law, and the mains has six written papers covering General Knowledge, English, Hindi and Law.

UPPSC has not yet announced the prelims date or the admit card date. Both will be posted on the commission's website. ## UP PCS J 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date to apply for UP PCS J 2026?**
December 21, 2026 is the last date to submit the online form and pay the fee. Corrections are allowed until December 28.

**What is the age limit for UP PCS J 2026?**
22 to 35 years as on July 1, 2027. That works out to a date of birth between July 2, 1992 and July 1, 2005. UP reserved categories and candidates with disabilities get relaxation as per rules.

**How much is the PCS J application fee?**
Rs 125 for General, OBC and EWS candidates, Rs 65 for SC and ST candidates, and Rs 25 for candidates with disabilities.

**When is the UP PCS J prelims exam?**
The date has not been announced. UPPSC will publish it on uppsc.up.nic.in.

The dates and figures above come from news reports on the UPPSC notice. Read the detailed advertisement on the official site before you apply, since it is the final word on eligibility, reservation and relaxation.
