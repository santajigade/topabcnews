CBSE board exams for 2027 start on Tuesday, February 16, for both classes. Class 10 finishes on March 9 and Class 12 on April 9. Every paper starts at 10:30 am. Most run three hours, to 1:30 pm, and some skill and language papers end at 12:30 pm. The board put out the date sheet on November 6, 2026, and the full PDF is on the <a href="https://www.cbse.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official CBSE website</a>.

| Detail | Class 10 | Class 12 |
|---|---|---|
| First paper | Feb 16, 2027 | Feb 16, 2027 (Physics) |
| Last paper | Mar 9, 2027 | Apr 9, 2027 |
| Subjects on offer | 83 | 121 |
| Students expected | Over 26.5 lakh | Over 19.8 lakh |
| Start time | 10:30 am | 10:30 am |

According to the <a href="https://newsonair.gov.in/cbse-releases-2027-board-exam-date-sheet-exams-begin-16th-february/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">All India Radio report</a> on the release, CBSE built the schedule from more than 40,000 subject combinations so that no student has two of their own subjects on the same day.

## Days Off Before Each Paper, Stream by Stream

The date sheet gives dates, not the gaps between them, and the gaps decide how revision time gets split. We counted the full days with no exam between papers for common subject sets.

**Class 12 Science with Maths (PCM)**

| Paper | Date | Days off before it |
|---|---|---|
| Physics | Feb 16 | First paper |
| Chemistry | Feb 27 | 10 |
| English Core | Mar 5 | 5 |
| Mathematics | Mar 9 | 3 |
| Computer Science (if taken) | Mar 31 | 21 |

**Class 12 Science with Biology (PCB)**

Physics, Chemistry and English fall on the same dates as for PCM. Biology then comes on March 25, after 19 free days.

**Class 12 Commerce**

| Paper | Date | Days off before it |
|---|---|---|
| Accountancy | Feb 23 | First paper |
| English Core | Mar 5 | 9 |
| Applied Maths or Maths (if taken) | Mar 9 | 3 |
| Economics | Mar 16 | 6 |
| Business Studies | Apr 1 | 15 |

Without a Maths paper, a commerce student gets 10 days between English and Economics.

**Class 12 Humanities (one common set)**

Geography (Feb 25), Psychology (Mar 2), English (Mar 5), Political Science (Mar 19), History (Mar 27) and Sociology (Mar 30). The gaps are 4, 2, 13, 7 and 2 days. History to Sociology is the tightest stretch.

**Class 10 core subjects**

Maths (Feb 17), Science (Feb 22), English (Feb 26), Hindi (Mar 3) and Social Science (Mar 8) have 4, 3, 4 and 4 days between them. A student taking Sanskrit sits it on March 1, which cuts the English to Hindi gap into two short breaks of 2 and 1 days.

## Practical Exam Windows

Practicals, projects and internal assessment come before the theory papers, and the window depends on where the school is:

- **Winter-bound schools:** November 9 to December 19, 2026, so these schools are already in their practical season.
- **All other schools in India and abroad:** January 1 to February 13, 2027.

The <a href="https://newsonair.gov.in/cbse-releases-date-sheet-for-class-10th-and-12th-board-examinations-2027/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIR report</a> says detailed instructions for practicals will follow. The CBSE notice also says no communication device is allowed inside an exam centre and asks students to read the instructions printed on the admit card. The board has not announced a reporting time.

## How to Download the Official PDF

1. Go to cbse.gov.in.
2. Find the Class 10 and Class 12 date sheet 2027 notice dated November 6, 2026.
3. Download the PDF and check the subject codes against your registration, since the date sheet lists papers by code.

Copies on other sites can carry errors, as the Class 12 Maths date shows, so check any doubtful date in the CBSE PDF itself. For how other national exams are being run this season, see our report on <a href="/nta-exam-observers-pool-centre-empanelment/">NTA's new exam observer pool</a>.

## CBSE Board Exam 2027: Common Questions

**When does the CBSE Class 12 exam 2027 start?**
On February 16, 2027, with Physics. The last Class 12 paper is on April 9, 2027.

**What is the CBSE Class 10 Maths exam date for 2027?**
Wednesday, February 17, 2027, for both Mathematics Standard and Mathematics Basic, from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm.

**When are CBSE practical exams for 2027?**
From January 1 to February 13, 2027, for most schools. Winter-bound schools hold them from November 9 to December 19, 2026.
