Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana Part One has crossed Rs 100 crore net in India within two days of release. The big budget mythological film, starring Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram and Yash as Ravana, opened in theatres on Thursday, November 5, 2026. Early tracking data from Sacnilk shows the film earned about Rs 48.05 crore net on Friday, November 6, taking its two-day India net total to roughly Rs 112.90 crore across all languages. The numbers came even as the Dhanteras festival kept many families away from cinemas.

We had covered the film's <a href="/ramayana-part-one-box-office-day-1/">record opening day</a> earlier. Here is how the second day went and what the coming Diwali week could bring.

## How Much Ramayana Earned on Friday

According to Sacnilk's day wise data, Ramayana collected Rs 64.85 crore net on Thursday and Rs 48.05 crore net on Friday. That is a fall of about 26 percent from the opening day. Average occupancy also slipped from 36 percent on Day 1 to around 28.9 percent on Day 2, with the number of shows coming down slightly from over 25,000 to about 23,300.

A drop on the second day is not unusual for a film that opens on a Thursday, especially in a festival week. Trade observers pointed out that Dhanteras shopping and family gatherings took a share of the audience on Friday.

| Day | India net (all languages) | India gross | Occupancy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 (Thursday, Nov 5) | Rs 64.85 crore | Rs 77.50 crore | 36.0% |
| Day 2 (Friday, Nov 6) | Rs 48.05 crore | Rs 57.30 crore | 28.9% |
| Total (2 days) | Rs 112.90 crore | Rs 134.80 crore | |

*Figures are early estimates from Sacnilk and may be revised.*

## Hindi Version Leads, South Languages Add Strength

The Hindi version is doing most of the heavy lifting. Sacnilk puts the two-day Hindi net at about Rs 83 crore. Among the dubbed versions, Kannada has done especially well with around Rs 12.40 crore, which is not surprising since Yash is one of the biggest stars of Kannada cinema. Telugu has added about Rs 10.75 crore, Tamil about Rs 4.10 crore and Malayalam about Rs 2.65 crore.

Trade website Bollywood Hungama has slightly different Hindi numbers. In its <a href="https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/box-office-special-features/ramayana-day-2-hindi-box-office-estimate-ranbir-kapoor-and-yash-starrer-collects-rs-37-crore-on-friday/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Day 2 Hindi estimate</a>, it said the film opened at Rs 45.46 crore and earned between Rs 36 crore and Rs 38 crore on Friday, for a two-day Hindi total of about Rs 82 crore. Such small gaps between trackers are common in the first few days, and official producer figures may differ again.

## Worldwide Total Crosses Rs 220 Crore

The film is also pulling strong numbers abroad. Sacnilk estimates the overseas gross at about Rs 86.25 crore after two days. Combined with the India gross of Rs 134.80 crore, Ramayana's worldwide gross stands at roughly Rs 221.05 crore. Overseas markets account for close to 39 percent of the global total, a big share for an Indian film in its first two days.

## Where Ramayana Stands Among Big Openers

As reported by <a href="https://www.theweek.in/news/entertainment/2026/11/06/ramayana-day-1-box-office-collection-record-ranbir-kapoor-film-beats-border-2-mirzapur.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Week</a>, Ramayana's Day 1 Hindi net of about Rs 47.50 crore made it the third biggest Bollywood opening, behind Dhurandhar 2: The Revenge and Drishyam 3: The Conclusion. It moved ahead of Border 2 and Mirzapur: The Movie on that list.

It also set a new record for a pre-Diwali Hindi release. The previous best in that window was Tiger 3, which opened at about Rs 43 crore in 2023. Older pre-Diwali releases such as Krrish 3, Housefull 4 and Ae Dil Hai Mushkil opened far lower.

## What the Diwali Week Could Mean for Collections

The real test begins over the weekend and the Diwali holidays. Bollywood Hungama expects the pre-Diwali slowdown to ease from Monday, when festival holidays bring families back to theatres. It has projected that the film's Hindi version could reach around Rs 164 crore in four days and that the first week could go past Rs 400 crore if the trend holds. These are trade projections, not confirmed figures, and actual numbers will depend on how strong the word of mouth stays.

Early audience reactions have been largely positive, with viewers praising the visuals and performances. If that word of mouth holds through Diwali, the film is in a good position to post one of the biggest first weeks for a Hindi film this year.

For now, Ramayana has delivered a strong start in a tricky release window. The coming four days, with the Diwali holidays in full swing, will decide whether it can challenge the top grossers of 2026.
