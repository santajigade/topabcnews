England ended Spain's 43-match unbeaten run with a 3-2 win in Madrid on Sunday, November 15, in the final round of the UEFA Nations League group stage. Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White put Thomas Tuchel's side three goals up before a red card for Elliot Anderson let the world champions back into the game. England held on to top their group and will be seeded for the quarter-finals.

## How England Built a 3-0 Lead

Both coaches made notable selection calls. Tuchel handed Trafford the gloves and brought White and Konsa into the side, Arab News reported. Spain coach Luis de la Fuente was able to start Yamal, who was fit again after missing the midweek win over the Czech Republic. Two of England's three goals would come from set pieces, a penalty and a corner.

The first breakthrough came right on half-time. Spain defender Pau Cubarsi fouled Kane in the area and the England captain scored the penalty in the 45th minute. According to the AP, it was Kane's 93rd goal for England, extending his national record.

England then struck twice in four minutes after the break. Konsa headed in a Declan Rice corner in the 53rd minute, and White finished a counter-attack led by Jude Bellingham in the 57th minute to make it 3-0 at the Metropolitano, Atletico Madrid's home ground.

## The Red Card That Changed the Game

Spain's comeback began in the 64th minute when Anderson was sent off for fouling Lamine Yamal inside the box. Mikel Oyarzabal scored the penalty a minute later. Nico Williams, on as a substitute, then cut the gap to 3-2 in the 75th minute, his shot going in off the post after a pass from Yamal.

With 10 men for the final quarter of an hour, England had to defend deep. Goalkeeper James Trafford, picked ahead of Jordan Pickford, made two close-range saves as Spain pushed for an equaliser, <a href="https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50493712/england-spain-43-game-unbeaten-run" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ESPN reported</a>. Rice later told ITV the red card "changed the course of the game". Spain goalkeeper Unai Simon admitted England were "superior" on the night, according to the AP.

## Spain's Long Unbeaten Run Comes to an End

Spain had not lost in 43 matches, according to Opta data, a run that lasted 968 days and was the longest among European national teams, Goal.com reported. During that run they won a European Championship, a Nations League title and the World Cup. Their last defeat had been a friendly against Colombia in March 2024, and they had not lost at home in more than four years.

For England, the result also settled old scores. Spain had knocked them out of Euro 2024 and beaten them 3-2 at Wembley in the Nations League. Young defender Cubarsi, who conceded the first penalty, was recently in the news for winning the <a href="/pau-cubarsi-wins-golden-boy-2026/">Golden Boy award for 2026</a>.

### Key highlights

- Final score: Spain 2, England 3, at the Metropolitano in Madrid.
- Scorers: Kane (45, pen), Konsa (53), White (57) for England; Oyarzabal (65, pen), Williams (75) for Spain.
- Elliot Anderson sent off in the 64th minute.
- England finish top of their group; both teams were already through to the quarter-finals.

## What Comes Next in the Nations League

Both England and Spain had already qualified for the knockout stage before kick-off, so the result was about seeding and pride. As group winners, England will be seeded when the quarter-finals are played in March 2027. Fixtures and the draw will be published on the official <a href="https://www.uefa.com/uefanationsleague/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">UEFA Nations League website</a>.

Elsewhere on Sunday, Denmark beat Portugal 4-1 away from home, with Rasmus Hojlund scoring twice in Portugal's first match since Cristiano Ronaldo retired from international football, Anadolu Agency reported. France beat Turkey 2-1 to top their group. Norway beat Wales 3-1, with Erling Haaland among the scorers, according to Arab News.

## Why This Win Matters for Tuchel

Tuchel made bold choices, starting Trafford in goal and bringing in White and Konsa, and all three played key roles. Rice told ITV the players went in believing they would win. Ending the run of the world champions on their own soil, even while finishing with 10 men, gives England a clear marker of where they stand as the Nations League moves into its knockout rounds next year.
