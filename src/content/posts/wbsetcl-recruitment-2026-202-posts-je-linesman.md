West Bengal State Electricity Transmission Company Ltd (WBSETCL) opened online applications on **November 25, 2026** for **202 posts** under Employment Notification No. REC/2026/04. The **last date is December 15, 2026, 11:55 pm**. There is **no application fee**, the age limit is **18 to 37 years as on January 1, 2026**, and candidates must hold West Bengal domicile and be able to read, speak and write Bengali or Nepali.

| Detail | What the notice says |
|---|---|
| Total posts | 202 |
| Online form | Nov 25, 2026 (11:00 am) to Dec 15, 2026 (11:55 pm) |
| Fee | Nil for all categories |
| Age | 18 to 37 years on 01.01.2026 |
| Selection | CBT (85 marks) + interview (15 marks) |
| Exam date | Not announced yet |

## Post-Wise Vacancies, Pay and Qualification

Junior Engineer accounts for 100 of the 202 posts. The pay shown is basic pay; the notice says other allowances are paid on top as per company rules.

| Post | Vacancies | Pay level and scale | Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junior Engineer (Elect.) Gr.-II | 100 | Level-6, Rs 36,800 to 1,06,700 | Full-time 3-year diploma in Electrical Engineering |
| Special Technical Assistant | 40 | Level-6, Rs 36,800 to 1,06,700 | Full-time 3-year diploma in Electronics and Tele-Communication, Radio or Wireless Engineering |
| Linesman | 40 | Level-3, Rs 23,400 to 68,900 | Madhyamik plus 2-year NCVT ITI certificate in Wireman or Electrician |
| Office Executive | 20 | Level-4, Rs 29,000 to 84,500 | Graduation with 50% (or 50% at HS level) plus a computer qualification such as NIELIT O Level |
| Junior Surveyor | 2 | Level-6, Rs 36,800 to 1,06,700 | Full-time 3-year diploma in Survey Engineering |

For Office Executive, BBA, BCA, MCA, honours in Statistics or Computer Science, or a degree with a computer paper are also accepted in place of the separate computer certificate, still subject to the 50% rule. Degrees earned through open, distance, part-time or sandwich mode are not accepted, except for existing WBSETCL employees. Students in their final semester can sit the CBT but must show their result at the interview.

The notice also splits each post's vacancies by category (UR, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, ex-servicemen, PwBD and exempted category).

## Age Relaxation Rules

The upper limit of 37 years is relaxed by 5 years for SC and ST candidates and by 3 years for OBC candidates. Reserved category certificates from outside West Bengal are treated as General, with no relaxation. PwBD candidates, exempted category candidates and WBSETCL employees can apply up to 45 years. Ex-servicemen get relaxation as per rules.

## How the CBT Differs for Office Executive and Technical Posts

Every candidate takes an 85-mark computer-based test of 90 minutes, with **0.33 marks cut for each wrong answer**. The paper is in English except the language section. What goes into those 85 marks depends on the post.

| Section | JE, STA, Linesman, Jr Surveyor | Office Executive |
|---|---|---|
| Domain knowledge | 50 marks (must score 20) | Not included |
| Bengali or Nepali | 10 marks (must score 4) | 10 marks (must score 4) |
| General English | 10 marks | 10 marks |
| General Aptitude | 15 marks | Not included |
| Arithmetic | Not included | 20 marks |
| Reasoning | Not included | 15 marks |
| General Knowledge | Not included | 10 marks |
| MS Office proficiency | Not included | 20 marks (must score 8) |

On top of the section minimums, candidates need an overall CBT score of at least 40% (UR and EWS), 35% (OBC and SC) or 30% (ST and PwBD). Those who clear are shortlisted for a 15-mark interview at three times the vacancies in each category. The final list is based on CBT plus interview marks, followed by a medical test and police and certificate checks. The CBT and interview will be held in Kolkata, though WBSETCL may add other cities in the state. A provisional answer key will be released with a 3 to 5 day window to raise objections.

## Bond, Training and Probation

Selected candidates for JE, STA, Junior Surveyor and Office Executive serve one year of probation and sign a bond of **Rs 1,00,000 for four years**. Linesman recruits first go through three months of on-job training at extra high voltage sub-stations, getting only basic pay and DA during that time. They then take a written test set by WBSETCL before being absorbed, sign a Rs 1,00,000 bond at absorption and serve one year of probation.

Job seekers in the state may also want to track the new <a href="/west-bengal-bhorosha-unemployment-allowance-rs-2000/">Bhorosha unemployment allowance</a>, which starts in January 2027. Diploma holders looking beyond Bengal can compare this notice with the <a href="/dsssb-mts-je-electrical-2026-1260-posts/">DSSSB JE Electrical vacancies</a>.

## How to Apply for WBSETCL Recruitment 2026

1. Go to the <a href="https://wbsetcl.in/index.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">WBSETCL website</a> and open the Career section.
2. Find notification REC/2026/04 and read the <a href="https://www.wbsetcl.in/career/REC_2026_04_Empl._Notification_2026_11_23.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official notice PDF</a> in full.
3. Click the online application link under REC/2026/04 and register.
4. Fill in the form and check it before submitting. The notice says a submitted form cannot be edited.
5. Do not upload date of birth or education documents at this stage, as the notice asks. Keep originals ready for the interview, including the domicile certificate.
6. Submit before 11:55 pm on December 15, 2026.
