Chinese EV giant BYD has brought its luxury brand Denza to India, and it has started at the very top of the market. The Denza Z9 GT, an all-electric shooting brake with more than 1,150 PS, was launched on November 13 at an ex-showroom price of Rs 1.57 crore. A second model, the seven-seat Denza D9 electric MPV, has been priced at Rs 90 lakh. Bookings for both are open with a token amount of Rs 10 lakh. Denza is the first dedicated Chinese luxury car brand to enter India, and its arrival puts it up against established German names in the premium electric space.

## Denza Z9 GT Price and Bookings

The Z9 GT comes in a single, fully loaded specification. Approximate on-road prices reported by CarWale for major cities are below.

| City | Approx. on-road price |
|---|---|
| Ex-showroom (all India) | Rs 1.57 crore |
| Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Chandigarh | Rs 1.65 crore |
| Ahmedabad | Rs 1.67 crore |
| Delhi, Bengaluru | Rs 1.81 crore |

Buyers who reserve the car get a free 11 kW home charger with installation and six years of roadside assistance, according to <a href="https://www.topgearmag.in/news/cars/denza-z9-gt-launched-in-india-at-rs157-crore" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TopGear India's launch report</a>. The vehicle carries a warranty of six years or 1,50,000 km, while the high-voltage powertrain is covered for eight years or 1,60,000 km.

Denza cars will be sold through separate Denza showrooms rather than regular BYD outlets. The brand is starting in six cities: Mumbai, Gurugram, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Ahmedabad.

## Tri-Motor Power and 670 km Range

The headline number is performance. The Z9 GT uses three electric motors: one at the front and two at the rear. Together they make 850 kW, which is 1,156 PS, and 1,210 Nm of torque. Denza claims 0 to 100 km/h in 2.7 seconds and a top speed of 270 km/h, figures that put it in supercar territory despite its estate-like body.

Power comes from a second-generation BYD Blade battery of about 122 kWh, with a claimed range of up to 670 km on the NEDC cycle. Real-world range is usually lower than NEDC figures. The car supports 11 kW AC charging at home and vehicle-to-load, which lets owners power external devices from the battery.

### Clever chassis tricks

The Z9 GT is built on BYD's e3 platform with independent control of the rear wheels. That enables party pieces such as a "crab walk" sideways movement for tight parking, a 360-degree spot turn and a drift mode. It also gets rear-wheel steering, DiSus-A air suspension and a 5.35 m turning radius.

## Cabin Features and Safety Kit

Inside, the five-seater is loaded with luxury and tech:

- 17.3-inch central touchscreen plus a 13.2-inch screen for the front passenger
- 13.2-inch digital driver display and an augmented reality head-up display
- 20-speaker Devialet sound system and four-zone climate control
- Front and rear seats with heating, ventilation and massage
- Onboard refrigerator and 50W wireless charging front and rear
- Apple CarPlay, Android Auto and over-the-air updates

Safety equipment includes multiple airbags, a 360-degree camera, tyre pressure monitoring and a Level 2 ADAS suite with adaptive cruise control, automatic emergency braking, blind spot detection, lane keeping assist and automated parking.

## Denza D9 Electric MPV at Rs 90 Lakh

The second launch, the Denza D9, is aimed at families and chauffeur-driven buyers. Priced at an introductory Rs 90 lakh ex-showroom, it seats seven in a 2+2+3 layout. A single front motor produces 462 hp and 365 Nm, and a 115 kWh Blade battery gives a claimed NEDC range of 730 km. The D9 sprints from 0 to 100 km/h in 8.5 seconds.

## What Denza Means for BYD in India

Denza was set up in 2010 as a joint venture between BYD and the company now called Mercedes-Benz Group, and BYD later took greater control of the brand, <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/auto/story/byds-denza-is-coming-to-india-with-premium-evs-what-buyers-need-to-know-559390-2026-10-04" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Today reported</a> ahead of the launch. In India it will sit above BYD models such as the Atto 3, Seal and Sealion 7.

The launch comes as BYD's wider India business finds its footing. According to Mint, BYD has completed Indian certification through the Automotive Research Association of India for three of its four models, which removes a cap of 2,500 imported units per model each year that applies to cars with only international approval. BYD's Indian retail sales rose 54% to 5,361 electric cars in FY26, Mint reported, citing dealer body FADA data.

Denza models, however, are not yet homologated in India. A BYD India executive told Mint the company would first study the response from customers and the media before deciding on local certification. Until then, volumes are likely to stay small, which suits a car priced above Rs 1.5 crore.

## Where the Z9 GT Fits Among Luxury EVs

At Rs 1.57 crore, the Z9 GT is priced against high-end electric cars from established European brands. Mercedes-Benz recently expanded its own line-up with the <a href="/mercedes-glc-electric-launched-india/">GLC Electric launch in India</a>, showing how crowded the luxury EV segment is becoming. Denza's challenge will be convincing Indian buyers to pay luxury prices for a brand they are only now meeting, and building service confidence through a small six-city network.
