Prime Minister Narendra Modi is marking Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas on Sunday, November 15, 2026, by launching 246 development projects for tribal communities worth around Rs 3,000 crore. He is joining a state level programme in Gadchiroli, Maharashtra, by video conference from 11 am. The day marks the 151st birth anniversary of tribal freedom fighter Dharti Aaba Bhagwan Birsa Munda and closes a two week national campaign to take government schemes to tribal families.

The projects cover new Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS), community centres under the PM-JANMAN mission, marketing centres for tribal produce, a research institute and a museum.

## What Is in the Rs 3,000 Crore Package

According to <a href="https://ddnews.gov.in/en/pm-modi-to-participate-in-janjatiya-gaurav-diwas-programme-on-november-15/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DD News</a> and All India Radio, the 246 projects are split into 173 inaugurations and 73 foundation stones.

| Type | Number | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| Inaugurations | 173 | 38 EMRS, 100 Multi-Purpose Centres under PM-JANMAN, a Tribal Research Institute building in Itanagar, and the Ropuiliani Tribal Freedom Fighter Museum in Mizoram |
| Foundation stones | 73 | 19 new EMRS, 12 Tribal Multipurpose Marketing Centres, and 42 other projects under PM-JANMAN and DAJGUA |
| **Total** | **246** | Worth around Rs 3,000 crore |

Taken together, that means 57 Eklavya schools are either opening or getting a foundation stone today.

## The Schemes Behind the Projects

Three central programmes are at the heart of the day's launches:

- **Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS):** Residential schools for tribal students and a key part of the Ministry of Tribal Affairs' work. About 200 EMRS students met the President at Rashtrapati Bhavan on November 12 as part of this year's celebrations.
- **PM-JANMAN (Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan):** A mission focused on Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs). Its Multi-Purpose Centres were used this year for health, nutrition and scheme camps for PVTG families.
- **DAJGUA (Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan):** A village focused tribal development programme, under which part of today's foundation stone projects fall.

The new Tribal Research Institute building in Itanagar and the Ropuiliani museum in Mizoram, named after a tribal freedom fighter, add to the network of institutions that record tribal history and culture.

## Why November 15 Is Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas

The central government designated November 15, Birsa Munda's birth anniversary, as Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas in 2021. Earlier national events were held in Ranchi in 2021, Khunti in 2022 and 2023, Jamui in 2024 and Dediapada in 2025, according to the <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2328976&reg=1&lang=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ministry of Tribal Affairs</a>.

The year from 2024 to 2025 was observed as Janjatiya Gaurav Varsh for Birsa Munda's 150th birth anniversary. The ministry says it featured more than 28 national events, over 2 lakh activities and more than 3.8 crore participants.

## Highlights of the Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada

This year the ministry ran a Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada from November 1 to 15, which it described as a time bound drive to speed up delivery of its flagship schemes. Key highlights:

- A National Tribal Literary Festival at India Habitat Centre, New Delhi, on November 12 and 13
- A Rani Durgavati Adivasi Mahila Sammelan on November 14 with 500 tribal women entrepreneurs from 30 states and union territories
- A tribal healers' conclave in Hyderabad to train about 1,500 healers, part of a plan to train and certify 1 lakh
- Scholarship Access Camps in 73 districts to help students apply and link Aadhaar
- Training of about 1.54 lakh Adi Karmayogi volunteers across 76,963 villages under the Dharti Aaba Janbhagidari Abhiyan
- New digital platforms, including the Jan-HIT tribal health data portal built with ICMR and a National FRA Digital Platform for forest rights claims

## Maharashtra's Birsa Munda Gaurav Week

Maharashtra is holding its own Bhagwan Birsa Munda Gaurav Week from November 11 to 18, according to PTI. State tribal development minister Ashok Uike announced the Gadchiroli event to reporters in Mumbai, and state minister Ashish Shelar outlined the week's plans, which include camps on November 16 to help people get Aadhaar cards and other documents, health and nutrition awareness programmes on November 17, and a Tribal Economic, Educational and Social Empowerment Day on November 18.

Shelar also said more than 3,000 teachers have been hired on contract for tribal residential schools, and competitive exam coaching centres for tribal students have been set up in all districts.

## How Tribal Families Can Benefit

For families in tribal areas, the most practical parts of this campaign are the document, scholarship and health camps, which let people apply for benefits close to home. Students in tribal districts can check with their local EMRS or the district tribal development office about admission and scholarship support.
