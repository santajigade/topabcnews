Spanish singer Rosalia was the biggest name at the 27th Latin Grammy Awards, held on Thursday night, November 12, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas. Her album *Lux* won Album of the Year and her track "La Perla" took Song of the Year, making her the night's headline story. Argentine artist Milo J also left with multiple trophies, while Daddy Yankee was celebrated as the Latin Recording Academy's Person of the Year. For viewers in India, the results landed early on Friday morning IST.

## Rosalia Wins Album of the Year for the Third Time

The Album of the Year win for *Lux* is Rosalia's third in the top category. She first won it with *El Mal Querer* in 2018 and again with *Motomami* in 2022. Ricky Martin presented the award.

"La Perla", recorded with Yahritza y Su Esencia, was named Song of the Year, a songwriting award shared with co-writers including Noah Goldstein, David Rodriguez, Ryan Tedder and Dylan Wiggins. The same track also won Best Pop Song, and *Lux (Complete Works)* was named Best Alternative Music Album.

On stage, a visibly emotional Rosalia told the audience she was "seriously in shock" and thanked the people who support her music, according to a Cover Media report. She also opened the ceremony with an operatic performance.

Going into the night, she was among the most nominated artists, with eight nods in the <a href="https://www.grammy.com/news/2026-latin-grammys-nominations-winners-nominees-full-list/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official 2026 Latin Grammys nominations list</a> announced in September.

## Milo J, Karol G and Jorge Drexler Add to Their Collections

Milo J, one of the night's most talked about young artists, won Best Urban Performance for "Gil" with Trueno. His album *La Vida Era Mas Corta* was also honoured in the folk category, according to reports from Hola and Yahoo.

Colombian star Karol G won Best Urban Music Album for *Tropicoqueta*, and she also performed during the show. Uruguayan songwriter Jorge Drexler, another heavy nominee, picked up Best Singer-Songwriter Album for *Taraca* and Best Alternative Song for "Ante la duda, baila".

Argentine duo CA7RIEL and Paco Amoroso won Best Pop/Rock Song for a track featuring Sting. It was the British musician's first Latin Grammy. Paco Amoroso credited Sting for helping the duo through the whole process.

Among the other names on the winners list, singer Bobby Pulido won in the Tejano album category just days after winning a seat in the US House of Representatives in the midterm elections.

## Daddy Yankee Honoured as Person of the Year

Puerto Rican rapper Daddy Yankee received the Latin Recording Academy's 2026 Person of the Year honour, one of the most prestigious recognitions in Latin music. He also performed a medley of his hits, including "Gasolina" and "Despacito", and shared the stage with Bad Gyal and with American rapper LL Cool J.

Accepting the honour, he spoke proudly about his home island, saying in Spanish that Puerto Rico is not small, only the map is. Besides the honour, he also shared the Best Urban Song award with Argentine producer Bizarrap.

## Tributes, Hosts and Performances

The ceremony was hosted by Puerto Rican actor and singer Roselyn Sanchez and aired live on Univision. Presenters included Camila Cabello, Pablo Alboran, Howie D of the Backstreet Boys, Greeicy, Ryan Castro, and Mexican football goalkeeper Memo Ochoa.

Around 40 artists performed, including Anitta, Feid, Mon Laferte and Jorge Drexler. The show paid tribute to salsa legend Willie Colon, who died in February, and marked the birth centenary of Mexican songwriter Jose Alfredo Jimenez with a special segment.

Brazilian star Anitta, who performed on the night, won Best Portuguese Language Contemporary Pop Album for *Equilibrivm*. Full category results are available on the <a href="https://www.latingrammy.com/en/awards/27th-annual-latin-grammy-awards/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Latin Recording Academy's awards page</a>.

## Rosalia's Big Night Sets the Tone for Awards Season

With Album and Song of the Year in hand, Rosalia has now matched a rare level of consistency at the Latin Grammys, winning the top album prize with three different records in under a decade. Daddy Yankee's honour and Milo J's wins showed how the Academy is celebrating both veterans and a new generation. Fans will now watch whether these winners carry the same momentum into the rest of the awards season.
