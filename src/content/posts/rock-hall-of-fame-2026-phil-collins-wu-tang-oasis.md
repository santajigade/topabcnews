The Rock and Roll Hall of Fame welcomed 18 new inductees on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. Phil Collins, Wu-Tang Clan, Oasis, Sade, Iron Maiden, Billy Idol, Luther Vandross and Joy Division/New Order led the performer class, while pioneers such as Celia Cruz and Fela Kuti were honoured for their early influence. The ceremony, which included a surprise opening set by U2, will be shown on ABC on December 8.

## The Full List of 2026 Inductees

The <a href="https://www.rockhall.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Rock and Roll Hall of Fame</a> honoured artists across four categories this year:

| Category | Inductees |
|---|---|
| Performers | Phil Collins, Billy Idol, Iron Maiden, Joy Division/New Order, Oasis, Sade, Luther Vandross, Wu-Tang Clan |
| Early Influence | Celia Cruz, Fela Kuti, Queen Latifah, MC Lyte, Gram Parsons |
| Musical Excellence | Linda Creed, Arif Mardin, Jimmy Miller, Rick Rubin |
| Ahmet Ertegun Award | Ed Sullivan |

Two of the honours carried historic weight. Celia Cruz became the first inductee who sang mainly in Spanish. Fela Kuti became the first African-born solo artist to enter the Hall.

## Phil Collins Joins the Two-Time Club

Phil Collins was already in the Hall as a member of Genesis, which was inducted in 2010. This year's honour recognises his solo career and makes him a two-time inductee. He was inducted by drummer Travis Barker, who then played the famous drum fill from "In the Air Tonight" while actor and singer Cynthia Erivo sang the song.

In his speech, Collins said he had grown up "just wanting to play the drums" and called the rest of his career a happy accident. He also hinted that he was not finished, telling the crowd, "You haven't heard the last of me."

## Wu-Tang Clan, Queen Latifah and the Hip-Hop Moments

Hip-hop had a strong presence on the night. Comedian Chris Rock introduced Wu-Tang Clan, whose members performed a medley backed by a jazz band, including "C.R.E.A.M." and "Shimmy Shimmy Ya", the latter as a tribute to late member Ol' Dirty Bastard.

Queen Latifah, inducted by Megan Thee Stallion, and MC Lyte, inducted by Remy Ma, both accepted their awards in person and performed together, including Latifah's anthem "U.N.I.T.Y.".

Oprah Winfrey inducted the late soul singer Luther Vandross, and Usher sang a medley of his songs, including "Here and Now" and "So Amazing".

## Who Did Not Turn Up

Several big names were missing from the stage, according to the Associated Press and CelebrityAccess:

- **Oasis:** Neither Liam nor Noel Gallagher attended. Actor Bob Odenkirk introduced the band, and singer Stephen Wilson Jr. played an acoustic "Wonderwall".
- **Sade:** The singer did not appear. Andra Day inducted her, and Sienna Spiro performed "Smooth Operator".
- **Iron Maiden:** The heavy metal band was touring Australia. A tribute featured Jack Black, St. Vincent and musicians linked with Metallica and Rage Against the Machine.

For Joy Division/New Order, Trent Reznor gave the introduction and Peter Hook accepted the honour.

## Other Highlights From the Night

- **U2** opened the show as a surprise act, playing "Silencio" and "Pride (In the Name of Love)".
- **Billy Idol** was inducted by Gwen Stefani and Tony Kanal, played several hits and sang "Eyes Without a Face" with Stefani, while the audience joined in on "White Wedding".
- **Red Hot Chili Peppers** paid tribute to producer Rick Rubin.
- **Aerosmith and Run-DMC** reunited to close the night with "Walk This Way".
- **Gram Parsons** and **Ed Sullivan** were honoured with video montages. A separate Parsons tribute was held earlier in the week at the Troubadour club with Emmylou Harris, Sheryl Crow and Elvis Costello.

## How to Watch the Ceremony

The induction will be broadcast as a three-hour special on <a href="https://abc.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ABC</a> on Tuesday, December 8, 2026, from 8 p.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern Time, according to CelebrityAccess. It will then stream on Disney+ and Hulu from Wednesday, December 9. For viewers in India, the US broadcast slot falls on the morning of December 9 IST.

To be eligible for the Hall, an artist's first commercial recording must be at least 25 years old. Phil Collins, Wu-Tang Clan and Luther Vandross were all first-time nominees this year, according to Parade.

## Rock Hall Heads Back to Cleveland in 2027

After this year's Los Angeles show, the ceremony will return in 2027 to Cleveland, the city where the Hall of Fame museum is based. The 2026 class, which ranges from heavy metal and hip-hop to salsa and Afrobeat pioneers, is one of the most varied the Hall has honoured. The night came during a busy awards season that also includes the run-up to the <a href="/grammy-nominations-2027-date-time-ist/">2027 Grammy nominations</a>.
