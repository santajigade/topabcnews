The Rs 8,981 crore public issue of Mahanadi Coalfields Ltd (MCL), the Odisha-based arm of Coal India, closes on Monday, November 23. Shares are priced between Rs 129 and Rs 136, one lot is 110 shares, and a retail bid at the top of the band costs Rs 14,960. With the weekend in between, Monday is the only working day left to bid. By the end of day two on November 20, retail investors had bid for only about a quarter of the shares kept for them.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Price band | Rs 129 to Rs 136 (face value Rs 2) |
| Lot size | 110 shares |
| One lot at upper band | Rs 14,960 |
| Retail maximum | 13 lots, 1,430 shares, Rs 1,94,480 |
| Issue size | Rs 8,981 crore, all offer for sale |
| Seller | Coal India, up to 66.18 crore shares (10% stake) |

| Allotment | November 25 (tentative) |
| Refunds and demat credit | November 26 |
| Listing | NSE and BSE, likely November 27 |

## Where the Money Goes and Who Has Bought So Far

The issue is a pure offer for sale. Coal India is selling up to 66.18 crore shares, about 10% of MCL, so the proceeds go to the parent and MCL itself receives nothing, as <a href="https://theprint.in/economy/mahanadi-coalfields-raises-rs-2295-crore-from-anchor-investors-ahead-of-ipo/3102585/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PTI reported via ThePrint</a>. At the upper band the company is valued at a little over Rs 90,000 crore. Reports tie the sale to Coal India's plan to unlock value from its subsidiaries through the stock market.

Before the issue opened, MCL raised Rs 2,295 crore from 53 anchor investors, who took 16,87,68,255 shares at Rs 136. LIC was the largest at Rs 568 crore. The Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, Invesco Mutual Fund and HDFC Mutual Fund were among the others. Domestic mutual funds took 53% of the anchor allocation.

SBI Capital Markets, Axis Capital, BOB Capital Markets, IDBI Capital Markets & Securities and IIFL Capital Services are the lead managers. KFin Technologies is the registrar, and allotment status will be on its site and the exchange sites.

## Subscription After Two Days

On day one, November 19, the issue was 38% subscribed overall according to NSE data reported by ETV Bharat. Qualified institutional buyers bid 1.26 times their quota, while non-institutional investors were at 8% and retail at 13%.

By 5:06 pm on November 20, figures compiled by Chittorgarh showed total subscription at 0.49 times. The QIB portion was at 1.37 times, non-institutional at 0.18 times, retail at 0.25 times and the employee portion at 0.09 times.

## What a Retail Bid Looks Like, Step by Step

1. Choose how many lots to buy. Each lot is 110 shares. At Rs 136, one lot costs Rs 14,960, two lots Rs 29,920 and the retail cap of 13 lots Rs 1,94,480. A bid above that falls in the non-institutional category, which starts at 14 lots (Rs 2,09,440).
2. Tick the cut-off price option. You accept whatever final price is fixed, and your bank blocks the amount at Rs 136 a share.
3. Place the bid through your broker app with UPI, or through net banking with ASBA, before your bank or broker's cut-off time on Monday.
4. Approve the UPI mandate on your phone. A bid without an approved mandate is not counted.
5. Check allotment on November 25 on the registrar or exchange sites. If you get no shares, the blocked money is released, with refunds due from November 26.

Two special quotas exist. Eligible MCL employees get a discount of Rs 6 per share. Coal India shareholders have a separate reserved portion for bids of up to Rs 2 lakh, but according to JM Financial they must have held at least one Coal India share on the record date in the offer documents, and shares of other group companies do not count.

On valuation, Upstox put the issue at about 8.4 times FY26 earnings and 5.8 times FY26 EV/EBITDA. Univest's data showed Coal India itself trading at a trailing P/E of 7.21 on November 18, with its share at Rs 388. That puts the MCL issue at a slightly higher earnings multiple than the parent that owns it.

## The Business Behind the Price

MCL runs 17 mines in the Talcher and Ib Valley coalfields of Odisha, 14 opencast and 3 underground. It produced 218.31 million tonnes of coal in FY26, about 22.4% of India's non-coking coal output according to a CRISIL report cited in the <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/ipo-corner/story/coal-india-arm-mahanadi-coalfields-filed-drhp-to-launch-its-ipo-check-all-key-details-552844-2026-09-02" target="_blank" rel="noopener">draft papers covered by Business Today</a>. Audited reserves of 9,840.31 million tonnes would last about 45 years at that rate. The company accounts for over 28% of Coal India's output.

| Year | Revenue from operations | Net profit |
|---|---|---|
| FY24 | Rs 31,634 crore | Rs 11,842 crore |
| FY25 | Rs 31,353 crore | Rs 10,825 crore |
| FY26 | Rs 30,550 crore | Rs 10,698 crore |

Net profit has fallen in each of the last two years. In the June 2026 quarter, revenue from operations rose to Rs 8,033.7 crore from Rs 7,548.3 crore a year earlier, while net profit eased to Rs 2,398.7 crore from Rs 2,448.3 crore.

Univest's summary of the offer document's risks lists customer concentration (the top 10 buyers give 55.94% of revenue) and about Rs 2,432 crore owed by Andhra Pradesh power utilities. Power companies bought about 68.6% of FY26 sales.

## MCL IPO: Common Questions

**What is the last date to apply for the MCL IPO?**
Monday, November 23, 2026. Banks and brokers may close UPI bids earlier in the day.

**What is the minimum investment in the Mahanadi Coalfields IPO?**
One lot of 110 shares, which costs Rs 14,960 at the upper price of Rs 136.

**When will MCL IPO allotment be announced?**
Allotment is expected on November 25, with shares in demat accounts on November 26 and listing likely on November 27.

**Is there a discount for Coal India shareholders?**
No discount has been reported for them. They get a separate reserved portion for bids of up to Rs 2 lakh. Only eligible MCL employees get the Rs 6 per share discount.
