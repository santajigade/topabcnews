Candidates who were racing to apply for the Chhattisgarh power company jobs tonight now have another week. Chhattisgarh State Power Transmission Company Ltd (CSPTCL) has moved the last date for online applications from **November 26, 2026** to **December 3, 2026 (Thursday), 11:55 pm**. It did this through a notice dated November 25, signed by the Chief Engineer (HR), Raipur. Every other condition of the original advertisement stays the same.

The recruitment covers **1,501 posts** across the three state power companies: CSPDCL (distribution), CSPGCL (generation) and CSPTCL itself. The main notice, No. 01-01/HR/Recruitment/739106, is dated November 5, 2026. Applications opened at 8 pm on November 6.

| Detail | What the notice says |
|---|---|
| Total posts | 1,501 (Class II: 195, Class III and IV: 1,306) |
| Advertisements | 1/2026 (Class II) and 2/2026 (Class III and IV) |
| Apply online | From 8 pm, Nov 6, 2026 |
| Last date (extended) | 11:55 pm, Dec 3, 2026 |
| Age counted on | January 1, 2026 |
| Exam | Online CBT, 100 questions, 2 hours |
| Website | cspc.co.in |

One point the extension leaves open: the original notice set the fee payment deadline at 11:55 pm on November 26, the same time as the form. The extension notice talks only about the online application date. The safe option is to pay the fee when you submit the form.

## Where the 1,501 Posts Sit: Company by Company

Several posts ask you to choose a company preference, so the split matters. Adding up the vacancy tables in the official notice gives this picture:

| Company | Class II | Class III and IV | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| CSPDCL (distribution) | 69 | 581 | 650 |
| CSPGCL (generation) | 70 | 502 | 572 |
| CSPTCL (transmission) | 6 | 96 | 102 |
| Common to all three | 50 | 127 | 177 |
| **Total** | **195** | **1,306** | **1,501** |

The single biggest block is **Data Entry Operator: 609 posts** (450 in CSPDCL, 95 in CSPGCL, 64 in CSPTCL). Next comes **Attendant (Plant) Grade III with 300 posts** in CSPGCL, spread across Fitter (84), Turner (36), Electrician (90), Instrument Mechanic (45) and Electronics (45). There are 45 Fire Attendant Grade II posts in CSPGCL as well.

Engineers have 152 Assistant Engineer posts, including 60 in CSPGCL and 62 AE (T&D) Electrical in CSPDCL, and 216 Junior Engineer posts, of which 124 are JE (T&D) Electrical in CSPDCL. The notice says vacancy numbers may change at CSPTCL's discretion.

## Domicile Rule: Who Can Apply for Which Posts

- **Class III and IV posts** (JE, DEO, Attendant, Fire Attendant, Stenographer and the medical support posts) are open **only to candidates with Chhattisgarh domicile**.
- **Class II posts** are open to candidates from other states too, but with a tighter age limit: **21 to 30 years** for non-Chhattisgarh candidates.
- Reserved seats for SC, ST, OBC, PwD and sports quota go only to Chhattisgarh domicile candidates. The 30% horizontal reservation for women also applies only to women with Chhattisgarh domicile.

## Age Limit and Application Fee

Age is counted as on **January 1, 2026**, and the notice says the maximum, including all relaxations, is 45 years.

| Post group | Minimum age | Max: UR male | Max: UR female, SC, ST, OBC |

| DEO, Stenographer, Attendant (Plant), Fire Attendant, medical support posts | Rs 400 | Rs 300 | Rs 200 |

## How the CBT Works and How to Apply

The online computer based test lasts 2 hours and has 100 multiple choice questions of 1 mark each. **80 questions cover the professional subject**. The other 20 cover general knowledge, verbal ability in English and Hindi, reasoning and quantitative ability. **A quarter mark (0.25) is cut for each wrong answer**, and questions you leave blank cost nothing.

After the CBT, AE, Accounts Officer, Medical Officer, Programmer and Shift Chemist candidates are shortlisted at three to five times the vacancies for document verification and a 10 mark interview. Stenographers and Data Entry Operators take typing tests. The exam date has not been announced.

To apply:

1. Open the <a href="https://cspc.co.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Chhattisgarh State Power Companies website</a> and go to the recruitment section for advertisement 739106.
2. Register and fill in personal and education details. Choose your post and, where asked, your company preference.
3. Upload your photo, signature and documents.
4. Pay the fee online and submit before 11:55 pm on December 3.
5. Save the acknowledgement. Do not register twice for the same post.

CSPDCL also lists recruitment notices on its <a href="https://web.cspdcl.co.in/#/FrmViewRecruitment" target="_blank" rel="noopener">recruitment page</a>. Check both for any corrigendum before you apply.

## CSPTCL Recruitment 2026: Common Questions

**What is the new last date for CSPTCL recruitment 2026?**
The online application deadline is now 11:55 pm on December 3, 2026. The original date was November 26, 2026.

**Can candidates from other states apply?**
Only for Class II posts such as Assistant Engineer, with an age limit of 21 to 30 years. Class III and IV posts are for Chhattisgarh domicile candidates only.

**What is the fee for JE and DEO posts?**
JE is in the higher group: Rs 800 for UR, Rs 700 for OBC and Rs 600 for SC/ST. DEO is in the lower group: Rs 400, Rs 300 and Rs 200.

**Is there negative marking in the CBT?**
Yes. Each wrong answer loses 0.25 marks, and each correct answer gets 1 mark.
