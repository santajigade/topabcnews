## How to Download the CAT Hall Ticket

The admit card is online only. Use the same login you created at registration.

1. Open iimcat.ac.in.
2. Click "Registered Candidate Login" on the home page.
3. Enter your CAT User ID and password.
4. On your dashboard, open the CAT 2026 admit card link.
5. Check your name, photograph, registration details, exam date, allotted slot, reporting time and test centre address.
6. Download the PDF and print a clear copy.

If anything on the card is wrong, report it to the CAT authorities through the contact options on the official website. Do not use links forwarded on WhatsApp or social media. ## Inside the Paper: Section Order, Time and Marking

This is the part many first-time takers get wrong. CAT is not one open two-hour paper. Each section is locked to its own clock.

| Section | Time allowed |
|---|---|
| Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC) | 40 minutes |
| Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR) | 40 minutes |
| Quantitative Ability (QA) | 40 minutes |
| **Total** | **120 minutes (160 minutes for eligible PwBD candidates)** |

What this means in practice:

- **Fixed order.** The sections come one after another. You cannot go back to VARC once the DILR clock starts, and spare minutes do not carry over.
- **Two question types.** There are multiple choice questions (MCQs) and non-MCQ questions where you type the answer.
- **Marking.** A correct MCQ gives +3 and a wrong MCQ costs 1 mark. Non-MCQs carry no negative marking, so it is worth attempting every one you have time for.

The official mock test is the best place to practise this rhythm. Try it at the same time of day as your slot, so the 40-minute blocks feel normal by November 29.

## What to Carry and What Gets Checked

- A printed copy of the CAT 2026 admit card.
- A valid government-issued photo ID in original.
- Any other document your admit card instructions ask for.

Entry can be refused if you cannot show these. Expect a face match check at the gate, and do not plan on using a phone inside: some centres will have jammers. After the exam, keep your admit card safe until the admission process ends, because IIMs and other B-schools may ask for it later.

## CAT 2026 Admit Card: Common Questions

### What is the last date to download the CAT 2026 admit card?

The admit card stays available on iimcat.ac.in until November 29, 2026, which is the exam day itself. It is safer to download and print it well before.

### What are the CAT 2026 slot timings?

Slot 1 runs from 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM, slot 2 from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM and slot 3 from 4:30 PM to 6:30 PM, all IST. Your slot is on your admit card.

### When will the CAT 2026 result be declared?

The result is expected in the first week of January 2027. The score is valid until December 31, 2027.

### Is there negative marking in CAT 2026?

Yes, but only for MCQs, where a wrong answer costs 1 mark and a correct one earns 3. Non-MCQ (type-in) questions have no negative marking.
