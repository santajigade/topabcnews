MBA aspirants across India are waiting for one link today. The Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Indore, which is conducting the Common Admission Test (CAT) 2026, rescheduled the admit card release from November 4 to Friday, November 6, 2026. Once live, candidates can download the hall ticket from the official website, iimcat.ac.in. The exam itself will be held on November 29, 2026, in three slots.

The admit card is the most important document for exam day. Without a printed copy and a valid photo ID, no candidate will be allowed inside the test centre.

## CAT 2026 key dates at a glance

| Event | Details |
|---|---|
| Conducting institute | IIM Indore |
| Admit card release | November 6, 2026 (moved from November 4) |
| Official website | iimcat.ac.in |
| Exam date | November 29, 2026 |
| Exam mode | Computer based test |
| Number of slots | 3 |
| Result | Expected late December 2026 or early January 2027 (tentative) |

Coaching websites tracking the release listed noon as a tentative time, but IIM Indore has not confirmed an exact hour. Candidates should keep checking the official website through the day.

## How to download the CAT 2026 admit card

The admit card will be available only online. It will not be sent by post. Follow these steps:

1. Visit the official website, iimcat.ac.in.
2. Click on the registered candidate login link.
3. Enter your CAT User ID and password.
4. Open the CAT 2026 admit card link on your dashboard.
5. Check all the details carefully.
6. Download the PDF and take a clear printout on A4 size paper.

If you have forgotten your login details, use the "Forgot User ID" or "Forgot Password" option on the login page. The download link will be available only on the official website, so avoid links shared on social media.

## Details printed on the admit card

After downloading, check that every detail is correct:

- Candidate name, registration number, photograph and signature
- Date of birth and category
- Exam date and allotted slot
- Reporting time
- Name and address of the test centre
- Important exam day instructions

If you find any mistake in your name, photo or other details, contact the CAT helpdesk at once. Only use contact details given on the official website. Please note that requests to change the test centre, city or slot are not accepted once admit cards are issued.

Your slot and centre will be mentioned on your admit card. Reach early, because entry will close well before the exam begins.

## Documents and exam day rules

- **Printed admit card:** carry a hard copy on A4 paper. A copy on a phone screen will not be accepted.
- **Original photo ID:** such as Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
- **Pens:** black or blue ballpoint pens are allowed.
- **Not allowed:** mobile phones, smart watches, other electronic devices, study material and bags.

Keep a second printout of the admit card at home. It may be useful later during IIM admission rounds.

## Mock test and preparation tips

A mock test on the official website is also expected around the same time as the admit card. Taking it helps you get used to the real exam screen, the on screen calculator and the way sections are timed.

With about three weeks left before the exam, here are a few practical tips:

- Take full length mock tests at the same time as your allotted slot.
- Revise formulas and shortcuts for quantitative aptitude.
- Read daily to keep your reading speed and comprehension sharp.
- Visit your test centre a day before if it is in an unfamiliar area.
- Sleep well the night before the exam.
