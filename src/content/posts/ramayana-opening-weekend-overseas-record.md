Ramayana Part One has crossed Rs 375 crore at the worldwide box office in its first three days, and its overseas run is now setting records. The Ranbir Kapoor and Yash film, which opened on Thursday, November 5, 2026, earned about Rs 375.50 crore gross globally by Saturday, according to trade tracker Sacnilk as cited by PTI. Trade estimates published by Pinkvilla say its Saturday overseas collection was the biggest single day abroad for any Indian film so far, ahead of Baahubali: The Conclusion.

We have tracked the film since release, from its <a href="/ramayana-part-one-box-office-day-1/">record first day</a> to the <a href="/ramayana-box-office-day-2-collection/">second day numbers</a>. This update looks at the full opening weekend and the film's strong showing outside India.

## Worldwide Total After Three Days

A <a href="https://www.deccanchronicle.com/entertainment/ramayana-rakes-in-rs-375-crore-globally-on-opening-weekend-2002766" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PTI report carried by Deccan Chronicle</a> put the film's global gross at Rs 375.50 crore, with Rs 218.25 crore of that coming from India. Pinkvilla's own estimate is close: about Rs 214 crore gross in India and about Rs 164 crore overseas, which together come to roughly Rs 378 crore.

Small differences between trackers are normal in the first week, since each uses its own data on screens, ticket sales and exchange rates. Pinkvilla itself describes its figures as trade estimates rather than certified accounts. PTI's report also noted that the global opening weekend had comfortably passed the Rs 300 crore mark.

One more detail is worth keeping in mind when comparing numbers. Pinkvilla converted Canadian earnings into US dollars for its total. Without that conversion, its overseas figure reads 18.15 million dollars instead of 17.45 million dollars.

| Measure (three days) | Sacnilk via PTI | Pinkvilla estimate |
|---|---|---|
| India gross | Rs 218.25 crore | About Rs 214 crore |
| Overseas gross | Not given separately | About Rs 164 crore (17.45 million dollars) |
| Worldwide gross | Rs 375.50 crore | About Rs 378 crore |

## The Saturday That Beat Baahubali 2

The standout number came from overseas markets on Saturday, November 7. Pinkvilla estimates the film earned about 7.30 million dollars abroad that day, roughly Rs 69 crore. That is higher than the just under 7 million dollars that Baahubali: The Conclusion managed on its biggest Saturday, making it the best single overseas day ever for an Indian release by that estimate.

Pinkvilla also listed several country records:

- **United Kingdom:** about 750,000 pounds on Saturday, around 15 percent above the previous single-day record for an Indian film.
- **United States:** the biggest single day for a Bollywood release, passing Dhurandhar: The Revenge.
- **Canada and Australia:** the second-biggest single days for an Indian film, behind only Dhurandhar.
- **Middle East:** a strong Saturday after a slower start.

The film is also expanding in Asia. Pinkvilla said Thailand was heading for about 400,000 dollars over its opening weekend, Vietnam was set to cross 100,000 dollars, and Indonesia will get the film next week.

## Cast, Music and Release Details

Ramayana Part One is the first of a two-part adaptation of the epic. Ranbir Kapoor plays Lord Ram, Sai Pallavi plays Sita and Yash plays Ravana. The cast also includes Sunny Deol, Anupam Kher, Kajal Aggarwal, Rakul Preet Singh, Vivek Oberoi, Arun Govil, Kunal Kapoor and Lara Dutta, among others.

The music is by Hans Zimmer and A R Rahman, and the action was handled by Terry Notary and Guy Norris. The film is produced by Namit Malhotra's Prime Focus Studios with DNEG and Yash's Monster Mind Creations. It released in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam.

## Diwali Sunday and the Road to Rs 500 Crore

Sunday, November 8 was Diwali weekend, and trade watchers expected another big day. Pinkvilla projected worldwide earnings of about Rs 490 crore after four days, with overseas collections near 23 million dollars. Those are projections, not final figures, and actual numbers will depend on how Sunday and the first weekdays hold up.

If Pinkvilla's four-day projection holds, the film would be within touching distance of Rs 500 crore worldwide. The weekday numbers that follow the festival will show whether the film has the staying power to match its huge start. Box office figures quoted here are trade estimates and may change once official numbers are released.
