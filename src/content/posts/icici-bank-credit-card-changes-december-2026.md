ICICI Bank is changing fees and benefits on several of its credit cards and on NRI savings accounts in four steps between November 25 and December 15, 2026. A higher currency conversion fee on select business and corporate cards starts on **November 25**, NRI cash charges rise on **December 1**, the card replacement fee goes up from Rs 200 to **Rs 300 plus GST** on **December 11**, and free air accident insurance and INOX movie benefits end on several cards on **December 15**.

## The Four Dates to Mark

| Effective date | Change | Old | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 25, 2026 | DCC fee on select business and corporate credit cards | 1% | 3.5% |
| Dec 1, 2026 | NRI savings account cash deposits and withdrawals above free limits | Rs 3.50 per Rs 1,000 | Rs 4 per Rs 1,000 or Rs 150, whichever is higher |
| Dec 11, 2026 | Credit card replacement fee | Rs 200 + GST | Rs 300 + GST |
| Dec 11, 2026 | Priority Pass card replacement | Free | Rs 300 + GST |
| Dec 15, 2026 | Complimentary air accident and related insurance on premium cards | Included | Withdrawn |
| Dec 15, 2026 | INOX movie benefit on select cards | Included | Withdrawn |

The dates above come from <a href="https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/free-insurance-movie-benefits-to-go-what-icici-bank-credit-card-users-need-to-know" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Outlook Money's card-by-card breakdown</a> and the December 1 NRI date from Business Standard.

## Which Card Loses What

Outlook Money and <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/icici-bank-changes-from-december-credit-card-benefits-fees-to-be-revised-126112300401_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard</a> list these cards by name:

| Card | Air accident cover ends | CFAR cover ends | INOX benefit ends | New Priority Pass replacement fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emeralde Private Metal | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| Times Black | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| Emeralde | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| Emeralde Private | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| Diamant | Yes | No | No | No |
| Sapphiro | Yes | No | No | Yes |
| Rubyx | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| Coral | No | No | Yes | No |
| Expressions | No | No | Yes | No |
| Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card | No | No | Yes | No |

CFAR stands for Cancellation For Any Reason, a travel cover that pays out when a trip is cancelled. Outlook Money also reports that the personal and air accident covers linked to the Parakram Select and Parakram cards are being discontinued, and that the Priority Pass fee applies to some co-branded cards that it does not name.

The higher Rs 300 replacement fee does not apply to three cards: Emeralde Private Metal, 1838 Reserve and Times Black. Holders of those cards keep the existing replacement terms, though the first and third will pay the new Priority Pass replacement charge.

Neither report gives the rupee value of the insurance covers being withdrawn, so ask ICICI Bank what cover your card carries today before deciding whether you need a replacement policy.

## What the New Charges Cost in Rupees

These examples use the rates in the table above, before GST unless stated.

**DCC fee on a business card.** On a Rs 50,000 transaction where the dynamic currency conversion fee applies, the charge goes from Rs 500 at 1% to Rs 1,750 at 3.5%, an extra Rs 1,250. The change applies only to select business and corporate credit cards, according to Outlook Money.

**Lost card.** Replacing a lost or damaged card goes from Rs 200 to Rs 300. With 18% GST, that is Rs 354 instead of Rs 236. A Priority Pass card that is free to replace today will also cost Rs 354 after GST.

**NRI cash transactions.** Business Standard reports the new rate is Rs 4 per Rs 1,000 or Rs 150, whichever is higher. So a Rs 20,000 deposit beyond the free limit costs Rs 150 (the minimum, since Rs 80 is lower), and a Rs 1 lakh deposit beyond the limit costs Rs 400, up from Rs 350 at the old rate.

## NRI Account Free Limits Stay the Same

Only the charge above the limit is going up. Regular eligible NRI savings accounts keep three free cash deposits and three free cash withdrawals a month, each capped at a monthly value of Rs 1 lakh. Private banking and premium customers who meet the bank's relationship value criteria keep up to 15 free cumulative transactions and a Rs 15 lakh monthly value limit each for deposits and withdrawals.

## Higher ECS Bounce Charges

Business Standard also reports revised charges when an ECS debit is returned for financial reasons, such as low balance, more than once on the same mandate in a month: Rs 500 for the first return and Rs 550 each for the second and third. Recovery is capped at three instances a month per mandate, with taxes extra. The report does not give the old amounts or a start date. Our earlier explainer on [RBI's 2026 money transfer limits](/rbi-domestic-money-transfer-rules-2026-limits/) covers related rules for cash and NEFT.

## What Cardholders Can Do Before December 15

1. Check which card you hold against the table above.
2. If you relied on the card's air accident cover for flights, look at a standalone travel or accident policy before trips after December 15.
3. Book any INOX tickets you planned on Rubyx, Coral, Expressions or CSK cards before December 15 if you want the benefit.
4. Report a lost card promptly, but note that replacements requested from December 11 cost Rs 300 plus GST.

## ICICI Card Changes: Common Questions

**When does the ICICI credit card replacement fee increase?**
From December 11, 2026, the fee rises from Rs 200 to Rs 300 plus GST. Emeralde Private Metal, 1838 Reserve and Times Black cards are excluded.

**Which ICICI cards lose the free INOX movie benefit?**
Rubyx, Coral, Expressions and the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card lose it from December 15, 2026, per Outlook Money.

**Is the free air accident insurance on ICICI Sapphiro and Rubyx ending?**
Yes. Complimentary air accident and related cover ends on December 15, 2026 for Sapphiro, Rubyx, Diamant, Emeralde, Emeralde Private, Emeralde Private Metal and Times Black.

**What is the new ICICI NRI cash deposit charge?**
From December 1, 2026, cash deposits and withdrawals above the free limits cost Rs 4 per Rs 1,000 or Rs 150, whichever is higher, up from Rs 3.50 per Rs 1,000.
