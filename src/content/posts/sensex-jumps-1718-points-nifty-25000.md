Indian stock markets gave investors a big Dhanteras gift on Friday, November 6, 2026. The BSE Sensex rose 1,718.01 points, or 2.17%, to close at 81,028.02, while the NSE Nifty 50 gained 493.75 points, or 2.01%, to end at 25,030.35. The rally came on a day when markets in the US and across Asia saw heavy selling, which makes the move by Indian shares stand out even more. Banks led the charge, and foreign investors were the main buyers behind the surge.

## How the Day Unfolded on Dalal Street

The morning did not hint at such a strong finish. Overnight, Wall Street had suffered one of its worst sessions in a long time. According to Business Standard, the S&P 500 fell 4.4%, the Dow lost 3.4% and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.5%. In Asia, Japan's Nikkei fell as much as 8% and South Korea's Kospi dropped so sharply that a circuit breaker was triggered.

Against that backdrop, Indian markets opened almost flat. ETV Bharat reported that the Sensex started the day at 79,413.66, up just about 104 points, while the Nifty was nearly unchanged at 24,538.20. Buying then picked up through the session, especially in banking stocks, and by the closing bell both indices were up more than 2%.

The Nifty has now risen for seven sessions in a row, its longest winning run in 14 months, as per HDFC Sky. For the Sensex, it is the longest such streak in 26 months. Over the week, the Sensex has gained about 8.8% and the Nifty about 8.6%, their best weekly performance since February 2021.

## Why Indian Shares Rose While Global Markets Fell

Market reports pointed to a few clear reasons for the rally:

- **Strong foreign buying:** Foreign institutional investors bought shares worth about Rs 15,000 crore on Thursday, their biggest single day net purchase in nearly six years, according to HDFC Sky.
- **Rotation away from AI stocks:** The global sell-off was centred on technology and artificial intelligence linked companies. Geojit's VK Vijayakumar told ETV Bharat that India could be among the least affected markets, since the slowdown in the AI trade could benefit India.
- **Crude oil below the $100 mark:** Brent crude had slipped below $100 a barrel earlier in the week, which is good news for a big oil importer like India. Kotak Neo noted that Brent January futures were trading near $97.62 a barrel on Friday.
- **Festive mood:** Dhanteras buying sentiment and hopes ahead of the special Diwali trading session added to the positive mood.

The Nifty Private Bank index rose about 3.2%. Kotak Neo reported that Nifty Metal, Oil and Gas, PSU Bank and IT indices also ended in the green. Energy, media and realty stocks were among the laggards.

The broader market told a different story. The Nifty Midcap 100 closed almost flat, down 0.02%, and the Nifty Smallcap 100 fell 0.82%. On the exchanges, 1,681 stocks advanced while 2,400 declined, which shows that the rally was led by a small group of big names rather than the whole market.

## Market Value Crosses Rs 500 Lakh Crore

One milestone from the day was the total value of all companies listed on the <a href="https://www.bseindia.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Bombay Stock Exchange</a>. Kotak Neo reported that the combined market capitalisation of BSE listed firms crossed Rs 500 lakh crore for the first time, helped by the rally and new listings.

Gold and silver moved in the opposite direction. MCX gold December futures were down 1.19% at Rs 1,42,379 per 10 grams, and silver was down 1.05% at Rs 1,85,177 per kg. ## What Investors Should Watch Next

Despite this week's strong run, Indian shares are still behind many global peers this year. HDFC Sky noted that the Nifty is down about 4.2% and the Sensex about 4.9% so far in 2026, while the MSCI Emerging Markets index is up around 7%.

Markets will hold the special Muhurat trading session on Sunday for Diwali, which is usually a short, symbolic session. Investors will also keep an eye on:

- Whether foreign investors continue buying after their record purchase
- Crude oil prices, as reports of fresh tanker attacks in West Asia kept oil volatile
- Second quarter results, with companies such as Hindalco, Siemens, Voltas and Bosch scheduled to report around this time
- Further swings in US technology stocks

Official index data and circulars are available on the <a href="https://www.nseindia.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Stock Exchange website</a>.

## A Strong Week With a Note of Caution

Friday's surge capped a remarkable week for Indian equities, powered by foreign money, softer oil and a shift away from global AI stocks. However, weak market breadth and the year to date gap with other emerging markets suggest that investors should stay selective rather than chase every rally.
