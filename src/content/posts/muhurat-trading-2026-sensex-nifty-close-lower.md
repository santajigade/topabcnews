Indian stock markets began the new Hindu calendar year Samvat 2083 on a slightly weak note on Sunday, November 8, 2026. In the special one hour Muhurat trading session held for Diwali, the BSE Sensex ended 28.62 points, or 0.04%, lower at 80,999.40, while the NSE Nifty 50 lost 43.95 points, or 0.18%, to finish at 24,986.40, just under the 25,000 mark. The headline indices gave up an early gain as banking, IT and realty shares came under pressure, but the broader market stayed firmly positive.

The session ran from 2:20 pm to 3:30 pm, as announced by both exchanges. If you missed the details of how the session works, read our <a href="/muhurat-trading-2026-timings-samvat-2083/">Muhurat trading 2026 timings guide</a>.

## How the Muhurat Session Played Out

Buying was strong at the start. According to Kotak Neo market data, the Sensex was up 394.49 points at 81,422.51 at 2:22 pm, and the Nifty had climbed 94.90 points to 25,125.25. The mood cooled as the hour went on. By the pre-close period the Sensex was still marginally in the green, but the Nifty had already slipped into the red, and both indices finished lower after the closing auction.

The small dip comes after a strong run. The Muhurat session followed a seven day winning streak for the benchmarks, one of their longest in several months. Part of that rally came on Dhanteras, when the <a href="/sensex-jumps-1718-points-nifty-25000/">Sensex jumped 1,718 points and the Nifty reclaimed 25,000</a>. Some profit booking in heavyweight stocks during a thin, short session is common after such a rally.

## Broader Market Beats the Headline Numbers

The index figures hide how positive the day was for most stocks. Market breadth was heavily in favour of buyers, with 3,136 stocks advancing and only 913 declining at the close, according to Kotak Neo. The Nifty Smallcap 100 index rose 0.94% and the Nifty Midcap 100 gained 0.13%.

Several companies touched fresh 52-week highs during the session, Upstox reported, including Adani Ports, Ather Energy, Divi's Laboratories, Emcure Pharmaceuticals and International Gemological Institute.

## Pharma Leads, Banks and IT Drag

Pharma stocks were the clear favourites as investors moved toward defensive names. Sun Pharma was among the best performers in the Nifty 50, rising 3.24% to Rs 1,920.20 at pre-close. The Nifty Pharma index gained 1.44%, and Upstox said some pharma shares rallied as much as 8% during the hour. The Nifty Media index was the top sectoral gainer at 1.88%.

On the other side, the Bank Nifty slipped 0.27% and the Nifty Realty index fell 0.45%.

### Key highlights of the session

- **Top Nifty gainers:** Tata Steel, Sun Pharma, Adani Ports, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles and ONGC, with Maruti Suzuki, BEL, UltraTech Cement and Power Grid also in the green.
- **Top Nifty laggards:** SBI Life, IndiGo, Bharti Airtel, Infosys, Trent, HDFC Life, Reliance Industries, Asian Paints and Grasim.
- **Volatility:** India VIX, the market's fear gauge, rose 5.32% to 18.40 at pre-close, pointing to higher expected swings in the coming days.
- **Breadth:** Over three stocks rose for every one that fell.

## Earnings Drive Individual Stock Moves

Quarterly results, which are still flowing in, shaped some of the sharpest moves. Tata Steel touched an intraday high of Rs 192.19, up about 3%, after reporting that its net profit for the September quarter of FY27 rose more than 16% from a year earlier to about Rs 3,700 crore, Upstox reported.

Aster DM Healthcare rose 8.25% to an intraday high of Rs 771.95 after its normalised profit after tax for the September quarter grew 34% year on year. Finolex Cables was among the biggest movers in the broader market, trading over 15% higher on the NSE during the session.

## What the Muhurat Result Means for Investors

Muhurat trading is largely symbolic. Many families and traders place small token orders to mark the start of their financial year, and volumes are usually lower than on a normal day. Because the session lasts only an hour, its direction is not a reliable signal for how markets will move over the coming year.

Still, a few points stand out from Sunday's session:

1. **Broad based buying continues.** The strong advance-decline ratio shows retail and domestic interest in smaller stocks remains healthy.
2. **Heavyweights saw mild selling.** Banks, IT majors and Reliance kept the Sensex and Nifty in check after their recent rally.
3. **Volatility is creeping up.** The rise in India VIX suggests traders are bracing for bigger moves as results season and global cues play out.
4. **Defensive sectors are in focus.** The rush into pharma points to some caution among investors after the sharp gains of the past week.

## Looking Ahead to the First Full Week of Samvat 2083

Regular trading will resume after the Diwali holidays, and attention will shift back to corporate earnings, foreign investor flows and global factors such as US interest rate expectations. The Nifty's position just below 25,000 will be watched closely, since the index slipped below that round number during the session.

For long term investors, a flat Muhurat session changes little. Decisions are better based on company fundamentals, valuations and personal goals rather than the mood of a single festive hour.
