## What "ACG Silent Start" Actually Means

ACG stands for Alternating Current Generator. Most older scooters start with a separate starter motor that meshes a small gear into the engine, which makes a mechanical noise when you press the button. On the Dio, the generator that charges the battery also turns the engine over, so there is no gear meshing and the start is nearly silent.

This matters most because of a second feature built on top of it: the idling stop system. At a red light, the engine switches off by itself, and it restarts when you twist the throttle. A gear-type starter would make that noisy and add wear every time. With ACG, the restart is smooth enough to use at every signal, which is how the Dio saves fuel in stop-start city traffic. Both Dio variants get it, according to the <a href="https://www.honda2wheelersindia.com/dio110/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Honda Dio 110 page</a>.

At 106 kg and a 765 mm seat, the Dio is light and low enough for shorter riders and new learners. The 5.3-litre tank is small, so expect to refuel more often than on bigger 125cc scooters. Practical touches include a side-stand engine cut-off, a passing switch, an external fuel filler with a double lid and a retractable pillion footrest.

## Standard or DLX: Which One to Buy

The Standard makes sense if you want the Dio's looks and quiet start at the lowest price and plan to ride mostly in daylight. Its LCD shows the basics, and you can buy Honda's charging socket separately.

The DLX is worth the extra Rs 12,000 or so if you ride after dark often or use your phone for navigation. The LED headlamp and the built-in USB-C port make a daily difference, and the colour TFT is easier to read. Note that the TFT has no Bluetooth, so do not expect call or navigation alerts on it.

Neither variant gets a front disc brake or ABS, so riders who want stronger braking will need to look elsewhere.

## Honda Dio 110: Common Questions

**What is the price of Honda Dio 110 in 2026?**
The Dio costs Rs 70,309 (Standard) and Rs 82,362 (DLX) ex-showroom Delhi. On-road in Delhi it is about Rs 78,395 to Rs 93,481, and on-road prices vary by city.

**What is ACG in Honda Dio?**
ACG is the Alternating Current Generator starter. It starts the engine through the generator instead of a geared starter motor, so the start is silent and supports the idling stop system.

**Does Honda Dio have ABS or a disc brake?**
No. Both variants use drum brakes at the front and rear, and there is no ABS.

**What warranty does Honda give on the Dio?**
Honda gives a standard 3-year or 36,000 km warranty, with optional extended warranty that can take total cover up to 10 years.
