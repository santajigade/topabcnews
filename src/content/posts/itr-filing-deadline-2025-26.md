The income tax return deadline for Assessment Year (AY) 2025-26, which covers income earned in FY 2024-25, was first moved from 31 July 2025 to 15 September 2025. On the night of 15 September, the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) pushed it one more day, to **16 September 2025**, after heavy traffic slowed the e-filing portal. By then more than 7 crore returns had been filed.

| Item | Date or rule |
|---|---|
| Original due date (non-audit) | 31 July 2025 |
| First extension | 15 September 2025 |
| Final extension | 16 September 2025 |
| Returns filed by 16 Sept | Over 7 crore |
| Late fee (Section 234F) | Rs 1,000 if income up to Rs 5 lakh, Rs 5,000 above that |

## Update (21 November 2026)

The AY 2025-26 window has closed. The next cycle, AY 2026-27 (income of FY 2025-26), follows split deadlines. Salaried people and pensioners filing ITR-1 or ITR-2 without audit had to file by 31 July 2026. Non-audit business and professional income, including presumptive taxpayers under Sections 44AD, 44ADA and 44AE, got time till 31 August 2026. Audit cases were scheduled for 31 October 2026 and transfer pricing cases for 30 November 2026, as reported by <a href="https://businesstoday.in/personal-finance/tax/story/july-31-deadline-is-over-is-your-itr-filing-due-now-on-aug-31-oct-31-or-nov-30-heres-who-files-when-546655-2026-08-01" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Today</a>. If you missed your date, a belated return for AY 2026-27 can still be filed till 31 December 2026.

## Why the 2025 Deadline Moved Twice

The first extension to 15 September was announced early in the season. The second one came at the last minute. According to the <a href="https://incometaxindia.gov.in/news/press-release-cbdt-further-extends-the-due-date-of-filing-of-itrs-which-were-due-for-filing-by-31-july-2025.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CBDT press release of 15 September 2025</a>, returns that were due on 31 July and had already been extended to 15 September could be filed till 16 September. A formal notification followed separately.

News On AIR reported that technical glitches had disrupted filings on 15 September. Business Standard added that the portal was under maintenance between midnight and 2.30 am on 16 September, and that a large group of taxpayers, estimated by ClearTax at 1.5 to 2 crore people, had not filed when the rush peaked.

## What Late Filers Paid

Anyone who missed 16 September could still file a belated return, but with costs:

- **Late fee under Section 234F:** Rs 1,000 for total income up to Rs 5 lakh, and Rs 5,000 above Rs 5 lakh.
- **Interest under Section 234A:** 1 percent per month on unpaid tax, counted from the day after the due date until filing.
- **Regime choice:** people with business or professional income who wanted the old tax regime had to file Form 10-IEA before the due date. Miss it and the new regime, the default since AY 2024-25, applies.
- **E-verification:** a return is treated as filed only after it is verified, and this must be done within 30 days.

## What Changes From AY 2026-27

Some rules got tighter or looser for the current cycle, based on Business Today's reading of the Finance Act, 2026:

| Rule | Earlier | From AY 2026-27 |
|---|---|---|
| Revised return deadline | 31 December | 31 March 2027 |
| Fee for revising after 31 Dec | None | Rs 1,000 (income up to Rs 5 lakh) or Rs 5,000 (Section 234I) |
| Updated return (ITR-U) window | 24 months | 48 months after the AY ends |
| Late tax audit report | Older penalty rules | Rs 75,000 up to one month late, Rs 1.5 lakh beyond |

The practical lesson: revising a return in January to March 2027 is now possible, but it costs money. If your original return was also late, both the 234F and 234I fees can apply together.

## A Simple Filing Checklist for Households

From what went wrong in September 2025, a few habits save stress:

1. **File in the first half of July.** The portal slowed only in the last days.
2. **Match Form 26AS and AIS** before you file, so the department's records agree with yours.
3. **Keep your PAN details handy.** If you need a copy, see our guide on how to <a href="/download-pan-card-from-digilocker/">download your PAN card from DigiLocker</a>.
4. **E-verify the same day** using Aadhaar OTP or net banking.
5. **Business owners:** decide old or new regime early and file Form 10-IEA on time if needed.

## ITR Deadline: Common Questions

**What was the last date to file ITR for AY 2025-26?**
After two extensions, the last date without a late fee for non-audit taxpayers was 16 September 2025.

**What is the late fee for filing ITR after the due date?**
Under Section 234F it is Rs 1,000 if total income is up to Rs 5 lakh and Rs 5,000 if it is higher, plus 1 percent monthly interest on any unpaid tax.

**What is the ITR due date for AY 2026-27?**
31 July 2026 for salaried and other non-audit ITR-1 and ITR-2 filers, 31 August 2026 for non-audit business and professional income, and 31 October 2026 for audit cases.

**Can I still file a belated return for AY 2026-27?**
Yes, a belated return can be filed till 31 December 2026, with the applicable late fee and interest.
