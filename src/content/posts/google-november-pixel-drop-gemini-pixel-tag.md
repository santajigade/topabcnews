Google has started rolling out its November 2026 Pixel Drop, a feature update that brings its Gemini Intelligence tools to the Pixel 10 phones and launches Pixel Tag, the company's first Bluetooth item tracker. The update began reaching devices on November 10 and will continue over the coming weeks. Pixel Tag went on sale on November 11 in the US at $29.99. For Indian users, the most useful additions are AI multi-step tasks and Hindi support for conversation summaries, while Google has not yet announced Pixel Tag pricing for India.

## Gemini Intelligence Reaches the Pixel 10 Series

Gemini Intelligence first arrived with the Pixel 11 series. With this drop, Google is extending it to the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold, according to <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/google-november-2026-pixel-drop-gemini-intelligence-pixel-tag-features-126111200342_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard</a>. The package includes several tools:

- **Rambler voice typing:** You speak naturally, and it strips out filler words like "um" to turn your speech into clean text that you can review and send.
- **Multi-step tasks:** Gemini can complete chores that need several steps across apps, such as ordering groceries, booking a ride, ordering takeout or finding a restaurant table. Google says this is now available in Japan, the UK and India.
- **Proactive suggestions:** The phone surfaces timely actions, such as sharing your arrival time with one tap or finding a place for dinner. This needs a Pixel 10 (excluding the 10a) or newer, the Gemini app and users aged 18 and above, in select countries.

## Custom Widgets You Describe in Words

One of the more unusual features is AI generated widgets. Instead of picking from a fixed list, you describe what you want and Gemini builds it. Google's examples include a monthly spending tracker, a game day widget in your favourite team's colours and a trip countdown that pulls in flight and booking details. It works on the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 families in select markets, and Google says it is coming to Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5. For more on Google's AI models, read our report on <a href="/google-gemini-4-carbon-rollout-pricing-swarm/">the Gemini 4 Carbon rollout</a>.

## Pixel Tag: Price, Battery and How It Works

Pixel Tag is Google's answer to item trackers that help you find keys, wallets and luggage. In its <a href="https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/november-2026-pixel-drop/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Pixel Drop announcement</a>, Google said the tag is slim enough to slip into a wallet and has a battery that lasts more than a year.

| Pixel Tag detail | What Google says |
|---|---|
| Price | $29.99 for one, $99.99 for a four-pack |
| On sale from | November 11, 2026 |
| Battery life | More than a year |
| Network | Find Hub, using over 1 billion Android devices |
| Close range finding | Ultra Wideband (UWB) direction and distance guidance |
| Phone support | Android 9 and above |
| India price | Not announced yet |

The tracker relies on Google's Find Hub network, which uses nearby Android phones to relay the location of a lost item even when it is miles away. When you are close, UWB shows direction and distance on screen.

## Smaller Upgrades Across Older Pixel Phones

Not every feature needs a new phone. Several additions reach devices going back several years:

- **Pixel search:** Lets you search photos, emails, messages, calendar events and settings from one place. Available on Pixel 6a and newer in all regions.
- **Custom icon styles:** More styles that apply to every app icon on the home screen, on Pixel 6a and newer.
- **Calculator unit conversion:** Pixel 6a and newer can convert measurements and show live currency rates, Business Standard reported.
- **Conversation summaries:** Condenses long chats, with support for Hindi along with German, French, Spanish, Italian and Traditional Chinese.
- **Pixel Watch workouts:** Step-by-step workout guidance with rep and weight logging on Pixel Watch 3 and newer.

## Features Limited to the Pixel 11 or the US

Some tools are restricted. The upgraded Call Screen, where Gemini chats with unknown callers to find out why they are calling and shows a live transcript, works only on the Pixel 11 series in the US. Pixel Solver, which uses generative AI to solve maths problems inside the Calculator app, is limited to the Pixel 11 series. HiLight for Google Messages, which makes the phone glow for messages from favourite contacts when it is face down, is on the Pixel 11 Pro models. Google Fi network switching is a US only feature.

## What This Means for Pixel Owners in India

For Pixel 10 owners in India, this drop is a meaningful upgrade, since multi-step Gemini tasks and Hindi conversation summaries are listed as available here. Older Pixel phones from the 6a onward also pick up useful extras like unified search and new icon styles. The big question for Indian buyers is Pixel Tag, which Google has priced only for the US so far. Updates roll out in phases, so if your phone has not received the November Pixel Drop yet, it should arrive over the next few weeks.
