India all-rounder Deepti Sharma has been named the ICC Women's Player of the Month for October 2026, the International Cricket Council announced on Friday, November 13. The award recognises her match-winning form in India's home series against Zimbabwe, where she scored an unbeaten century and picked up wickets in both formats. South Africa's Marco Jansen won the men's award for his role in a Test series win over Australia.

For Deepti, this is the second time she has won the monthly honour. Her first came in December 2023, so the new award arrives almost three years later and shows how consistent she has remained as one of India's most important players.

## How Deepti Sharma Dominated the Zimbabwe Series

India hosted Zimbabwe for a white-ball series in October, with the one-day matches counting towards the ICC Women's Championship. India won the ODI leg 3-0, and Deepti was at the centre of it.

She batted only once in the ODIs but made it count with an unbeaten 106. With the ball, she took five wickets and kept the scoring tight, conceding runs at an economy rate of just 3.42. In a format where batters look to rotate strike freely, that level of control from an off-spinner is a big advantage for any captain.

In the T20 internationals, she again got just one chance with the bat and scored 49 off 37 balls. She also took two wickets with her off-spin.

Across both formats, that adds up to 155 runs and seven wickets, a strong return given how few innings she had.

| Format | Batting | Bowling |
|---|---|---|
| ODIs (India won 3-0) | 106 not out in one innings | 5 wickets, economy 3.42 |
| T20Is | 49 off 37 balls in one innings | 2 wickets |
| Total | 155 runs | 7 wickets |

## Who Else Was in the Running

The ICC shortlist for the women's award also included Bangladesh's Sobhana Mostary and Australia's Annabel Sutherland, which made it a three-way contest between players from three different countries.

Deepti thanked the voting panel, the fans, her teammates and the coaching staff after the announcement.

## What Deepti Said About Her Century

Speaking after the award, Deepti said she was pleased to win and proud of how the Indian team played in the series, especially the younger players who got chances. She described her hundred as "particularly special" because it helped India win, <a href="https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/deepti-sharma-bags-second-icc-163200173.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">according to a report on Yahoo Sports</a>.

Her comments put the focus on the team result rather than personal numbers. Still, an unbeaten century from her only ODI innings, together with five wickets, shows how much she can shape a match even when she gets limited time at the crease.

## Marco Jansen Wins the Men's Award After Australia Series

On the men's side, the <a href="https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/icc-men-s-player-of-the-month-for-october-confirmed" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ICC confirmed</a> that South Africa's Marco Jansen is the ICC Men's Player of the Month for October 2026. It is the first time the 26-year-old left-arm fast bowler has won the award.

South Africa played a three-Test series against Australia as part of the ICC World Test Championship. They lost the opening Test but came back to win the series 2-1. Jansen scored 198 runs and took 13 wickets across the series.

### The Gqeberha Test That Turned the Series

The second Test in Gqeberha was his standout match. He scored 85 in South Africa's first innings and returned figures of 5 for 33 and 1 for 34, as South Africa won by an innings and 31 runs to level the series at 1-1. In the deciding third Test, he added another half-century and three more wickets.

Jansen called the award a great honour and described the contest as "one of the most memorable series of my career." He also credited his teammates and support staff.

### Top of the Test Rankings

His October form lifted him to No. 1 in both the ICC Test bowling and Test all-rounder rankings, <a href="https://www.aninews.in/news/sports/cricket/marco-jansen-deepti-sharma-named-icc-mens-womens-players-of-the-month-for-october20261113161430/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ANI reported</a>. He is the first South African bowler to top the Test bowling list since Kagiso Rabada in 2024, and the first South African all-rounder at No. 1 since Vernon Philander in August 2014.

The series win also moved South Africa back to second place in the World Test Championship standings, with a points percentage of 71.43 from seven Tests. The other men's nominees were Pakistan's Arafat Minhas and West Indies white-ball captain Shai Hope.

## Key Highlights

- Deepti Sharma wins ICC Women's Player of the Month for October 2026, her second such award after December 2023.
- She scored 106 not out and took 5 wickets in the ODIs against Zimbabwe, which India won 3-0.
- In the T20Is she made 49 off 37 balls and took 2 wickets.
- Marco Jansen wins the men's award for the first time after 198 runs and 13 wickets against Australia.
- Jansen is now No. 1 in the ICC Test bowling and all-rounder rankings.

## Why This Matters for Indian Cricket

For India's women's team, Deepti's award is a reminder of how much depth her all-round skills give the side. A player who can bat in the middle order and bowl a full quota of economical overs makes team selection simpler, especially in ICC Women's Championship games where every point counts.
