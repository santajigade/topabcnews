The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has changed its KYC rules for investors who open accounts with brokers, depository participants and other market intermediaries. The circular, issued on November 25, 2026, takes effect on **December 24, 2026**, unless a specific clause says otherwise. You will repeat less paperwork when you open a new account, and intermediaries get fixed deadlines to act when you close one.

The changes amend SEBI's KYC Master Circular of October 12, 2023, according to Kotak Neo, and they also revise how KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs) manage and flag records.

## The Key Dates and Deadlines in One Table

| Rule | Deadline or date |
|---|---|
| New rules take effect | December 24, 2026 |
| Intermediary uploads CKYC ID, DIGIPIN and document expiry date to KRA | Within 3 working days of finishing your first KYC |
| Broker or other intermediary tells KRA you closed your account | Within 5 working days of closure |
| KRA delinks the closed account from your record | Within 2 working days |
| KYC record downloaded but no account opened | Intermediary informs KRA within 5 working days after 21 working days pass |
| KRAs publish a standard operating procedure | Within 30 days of the circular |

Mutual funds are treated separately. They are not covered by the 5-day closure rule. Instead, a fund house or its registrar must inform the KRA within 5 working days once a folio has gone six months without holdings. The Tribune describes this as six months of zero balance, while Outlook Money describes it as no transactions or holdings for six months.

## What Changes for You, Case by Case

The circular is addressed to intermediaries. This is how its main clauses apply to an investor in common situations, going by reports of the circular.

**Opening a second demat or trading account.** Details such as income range, occupation, tax residency information and whether you are a politically exposed person will now sit with the KRA. LiveLaw reports this is Part II of the account opening form. You should not have to fill it in again for every new broker, though the broker can still ask what services you want.

**Your mobile number is already linked to Aadhaar.** If the KRA record already tags your number as Aadhaar seeded and verified, a fresh mobile check becomes optional for the broker. In the circular's words, quoted by The Tribune, it "shall be optional for the intermediary accessing the information from the KRA database."

**You moved house.** For KYC portability, either your current address or your permanent address can be verified against official databases. Kotak Neo says PAN and Aadhaar linkage is one of the conditions. Records that pass are marked as validated, and SEBI wants KRAs to tag each attribute separately as validated or not. If your record is not validated, an intermediary may ask you for more information.

**You changed your name after marriage.** No separate proof is needed if the new name already appears on both your PAN and Aadhaar. If it does not, Outlook Money reports that a state government marriage certificate, a passport showing the spouse's name, or a gazette notification will work. A name change for any other reason needs a gazette notification. Outlook adds that extra documents are allowed in Karnataka and Punjab, where gazette notifications are not issued for private individuals.

**You are an OCI cardholder or a foreign national living in India.** Overseas address proof becomes optional. You must give proof of an Indian residential address, which the intermediary will verify. Outlook Money says residence here follows Section 6 of the Income Tax Act, 2025.

**You closed an old broker account.** The broker has 5 working days to inform the KRA, and the KRA then has 2 working days to delink the closed account from your record.

## What Intermediaries Must Now Upload

Along with the core KYC, intermediaries must upload your Central KYC (CKYC) identifier, your DIGIPIN and the expiry date of the officially valid document you submitted, where these are available. The Central KYC registry is run by CERSAI and can be accessed at <a href="https://www.ckycindia.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ckycindia.in</a>. Intermediaries must also refresh your record using information from the KRA and the central registry, and you must be given the option to confirm those updates.

KRAs, for their part, must flag which records they have independently verified and validated, LiveLaw reports. Persons resident outside India from FATF-compliant countries also get several of the relaxed provisions, including the ones on supplementary information, mobile verification and account closure, according to Outlook Money.

## How to Check Your KYC Before December 24

The rules apply on their own, but these checks can prevent delays when you open your next account:

1. Make sure your mobile number is linked to Aadhaar. You can check this on the <a href="https://uidai.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">UIDAI website</a>.
2. Confirm that your PAN is linked to your Aadhaar, because linkage is one of the conditions for address validation.
3. If you have changed your name, see whether the new name appears on both PAN and Aadhaar. If not, keep a marriage certificate, passport or gazette notification ready.

## SEBI KYC Rules: Common Questions

**When do the new SEBI KYC rules start?**
They apply from December 24, 2026, unless a specific clause in the November 25 circular gives a different date.

**Is mobile number verification still required for opening a demat account?**
It becomes optional for the intermediary if the KRA record already shows your mobile number as Aadhaar seeded and verified. Otherwise the usual verification continues.

**Do I need to submit proof of name change after marriage?**
Not if your new name is already on both PAN and Aadhaar. If it is not, a marriage certificate, a passport showing the spouse's name, or a gazette notification is accepted.

**How fast will my KYC be delinked after I close a broker account?**
The broker must inform the KRA within 5 working days, and the KRA must delink the record within 2 working days after that.
