India have made three changes to their plans for the two-Test series in New Zealand that begins on November 19. The BCCI's selection committee has added fast bowler Gurnoor Brar to the Test squad, released wrist spinner Kuldeep Yadav so he can play domestic cricket at home, and confirmed that wicketkeeper-batter Rishabh Pant is fit and has joined the team in Wellington. The updates were announced on November 14, ahead of the final ODI of the tour.

## Why Gurnoor Brar Got the Call

Brar has been the standout bowler of India's ODI series in New Zealand. According to the <a href="https://icc-cricket.com/tournaments/world-test-championship/news/india-release-spinner-for-pacer-in-test-squad-to-face-kiwis" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ICC's report on the squad change</a>, he leads the wicket-takers in the series with nine wickets.

The 26-year-old has been a regular for India A and has 71 wickets in 22 first-class matches.

The move means Brar will leave the ODI squad early. The BCCI said he "will not be available for the fifth ODI," and that no replacement will be named for that match.

## Kuldeep Yadav Sent Home for Domestic Cricket

To make room, India have released Kuldeep Yadav, who was part of the original Test squad. The BCCI said the left-arm wrist spinner is being released "to play the ongoing domestic cricket back in India." The board did not give any other reason.

The swap of a spinner for a pacer reflects what India expect in New Zealand. With Ravindra Jadeja and Washington Sundar still in the squad, India keep two spin-bowling all-rounders while adding a fifth specialist seamer option.

## Rishabh Pant Cleared After Side Strain

The biggest relief for India is Pant's fitness. His selection had been subject to clearance when the squad was first named. Pant suffered a side strain while captaining Rest of India in the Irani Cup in Srinagar from October 1 to 4, Public TV reported. He had been working on his recovery at the BCCI Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru since October 23.

His rehab was extended, so he missed the India A matches against New Zealand A. The BCCI medical team has now cleared him, and he has linked up with the Test squad in Wellington. Earlier this month we covered how [New Zealand named their Test squad under Tom Latham](/new-zealand-test-squad-india-ravindra-conway-return/), so the stage is now set on both sides.

## India's Updated Test Squad

Shubman Gill leads the 16-player squad, with KL Rahul as vice-captain.

| Role | Players |

| Batters | Shubman Gill (captain), Yashasvi Jaiswal, KL Rahul (vice-captain), Devdutt Padikkal, Sai Sudharsan |
| Wicketkeepers | Rishabh Pant, Dhruv Jurel |
| All-rounders | Ravindra Jadeja, Washington Sundar, Nitish Kumar Reddy |
| Fast bowlers | Jasprit Bumrah, Mohammed Siraj, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep, Anshul Kamboj, Gurnoor Brar |

## Test Series Schedule and the WTC Race

The series is part of the World Test Championship. According to <a href="https://www.nzc.nz/news-items/historic-india-mega-tour-headlines-international-home-schedule/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">New Zealand Cricket's tour schedule</a>, the dates are:

- **1st Test:** Starts Thursday, November 19, at the Basin Reserve, Wellington
- **2nd Test:** Starts Friday, November 27, in Christchurch

India are fourth in the WTC standings and need several wins to stay in the race for a place in the final, the ICC said. That makes these two Tests in New Zealand important for Gill's side, especially away from home.

## The Fifth ODI Before the Tests

Before the red-ball cricket starts, the five-match ODI series ends on Sunday, with the last two games of the series scheduled at Bay Oval in Tauranga. India lead 2-1 going into the final match after the third game in Hamilton was [washed out without a ball bowled](/india-vs-new-zealand-3rd-odi-hamilton-washed-out/), so they cannot lose the series. Shreyas Iyer will captain India in the fifth ODI, with Ishan Kishan as vice-captain, according to Public TV.

### Key highlights

- Pacer Gurnoor Brar added to India's Test squad after nine wickets in the ODI series.
- Kuldeep Yadav released to play domestic cricket in India.
- Rishabh Pant passed fit after a side strain and has joined the squad in Wellington.
- First Test starts November 19 in Wellington; second Test from November 27 in Christchurch.
- India are fourth in the World Test Championship table.

## A Pace-Heavy Plan for New Zealand

India's changes point to a clear plan: back pace in New Zealand and give a bowler in form a chance at Test level. With Bumrah and Siraj leading the attack and Pant back behind the stumps, the visitors will hope to start the series strongly in Wellington and improve their WTC position before the second Test in Christchurch.
