Glenn Close finally has an Oscar. The actor, who has been nominated eight times without a competitive win, received an honorary Academy Award at the 17th Governors Awards in Los Angeles on Sunday night, November 15, 2026. Director Ridley Scott and pioneering Disney animator Floyd Norman were also given honorary Oscars, while producers Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler of Killer Films received the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award.

## Who Was Honoured at the 2026 Governors Awards

The ceremony was held at the Ray Dolby Ballroom at Ovation Hollywood, the same complex that hosts the main Oscars show. Unlike the Academy Awards, the honorees are picked by the Academy's Board of Governors through a selection committee rather than by a vote of all members. The event has been a separate, non-televised evening since 2009.

| Honoree | Award | Age | Known for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glenn Close | Honorary Oscar | 79 | Fatal Attraction, The World According to Garp, Hillbilly Elegy |
| Ridley Scott | Honorary Oscar | 88 | Alien, Blade Runner, Gladiator, Thelma & Louise |
| Floyd Norman | Honorary Oscar | 91 | Sleeping Beauty, 101 Dalmatians, The Jungle Book |
| Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler | Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award | Not stated | Killer Films: Hedwig and the Angry Inch, May December, Past Lives |

In its announcement on Instagram, the Academy said the honorary Oscars recognise "exceptional contributions to cinema."

## Glenn Close and Her Long Wait for an Oscar

Close's honour closes one of Hollywood's best known Oscar gaps. According to the Associated Press, her eight nominations without a win tie her with Peter O'Toole for the most by any actor who never won a competitive Oscar. Her first nomination came for The World According to Garp in 1983, and later nods included Fatal Attraction in 1987 and Hillbilly Elegy in 2020.

Outside film, she has won three Emmys, three Tonys and three Golden Globes over a career of five decades. Speaking to AP before the ceremony, she reflected on how long the road had been: "I got my first Oscar nomination 43 years ago." WION reported that she walked the red carpet with her daughter, Annie Stark.

## Ridley Scott Accepts by Video

Ridley Scott, 88, is the director behind films such as Alien, Blade Runner and Gladiator. He has four Oscar nominations: three for best director, for Thelma & Louise, Gladiator and Black Hawk Down, and one for best picture for The Martian, WION reported.

Scott did not attend the ceremony. WION said he stayed away for health reasons and accepted the award through a recorded video message.

## Floyd Norman, Disney's First Black Animator

Floyd Norman, 91, began his career in 1956 as the first Black animator at Walt Disney Animation Studios. Over 65 years he has worked on classics including Sleeping Beauty, 101 Dalmatians and The Jungle Book, and later on Mulan, Toy Story 2 and Monsters, Inc., according to AP. Speaking to AP, he described the honour as hard to believe after such a long career.

## Killer Films Founders Make Thalberg History

The Thalberg award goes to a creative producer whose body of work shows consistently high quality. Vachon and Koffler founded Killer Films in New York in 1995. AP reported that they are the first female producing team and the first independent producers to receive the award. Their credits include Hedwig and the Angry Inch, One Hour Photo and May December, and both were nominated for best picture for Past Lives.

## Stars on the Red Carpet and the Road to the Oscars

The Governors Awards have become the unofficial start of Hollywood's awards season, and AP noted that the night often draws younger stars campaigning for their films. WION reported that guests included Zendaya, Tom Holland, Andrew Garfield and Pedro Pascal. Last year's honorees included Tom Cruise, another big name who had never won a competitive Oscar.

More details on the honorees are on the <a href="https://www.oscars.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences website</a>, and the <a href="https://apnews.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Associated Press</a> has covered the evening and the wider awards race.

For Close, Scott and Norman, the night was less about the race ahead and more about long careers finally marked with a gold statuette.
