First-year college students who scored well in Class 12 have one week left to apply for the PM-USP Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS). The <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in/ViewMoreAnnouncement" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Scholarship Portal announcement</a> gives **November 30, 2026** as the last date for fresh applications. The scheme pays **Rs 12,000 a year** for the first three years of a degree and **Rs 20,000 a year** at postgraduate level. Family income must be **Rs 4.5 lakh a year or less**.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fresh application last date | November 30, 2026 (on NSP) |
| Renewal application last date | October 31, 2026 (closed) |
| Fresh scholarships per year | Up to 82,000 |
| Share for girls | 50% |
| UG, years 1 to 3 | Rs 12,000 a year |
| PG, and years 4 and 5 of 5-year courses | Rs 20,000 a year |
| Family income limit | Rs 4.5 lakh a year (gross) |
| Merit condition | Above the 80th percentile in your stream and board, Class 12 |
| Maximum duration | 5 years |

## Who Can Apply This Year

Under the Ministry of Education guidelines in force since 2022-23, you need to meet all of these for a fresh award:

- You passed Class 12 in the last academic year and are now in the first year of a regular degree course.
- Your Class 12 result is above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in your stream (Science, Commerce or Humanities) on your board. The FAQ describes this as the top 20th percentile list of the board, whether that is a state board, CBSE or CISCE.
- Gross parental or family income is up to Rs 4.5 lakh a year. The 2019 version of the rules allowed up to Rs 8 lakh, so older articles quoting that figure are out of date.
- The course is a regular degree at a recognised college. Diploma courses, correspondence courses and distance mode do not qualify.
- You are not taking any other scholarship, fee waiver or fee reimbursement, including state schemes.

The NSP announcement adds a rule that is new for 2026-27: a student may now apply for one merit-based scheme plus one or more welfare-based schemes. CSSS is a merit-based scheme, so you cannot pair it with another merit scholarship.

## How the 82,000 Seats Are Shared

Seats are not open to the whole country on one merit list. Each state board gets a quota based on the state's population aged 18 to 25, after CBSE and ICSE shares are set aside. Inside each board's quota, seats are split between Science, Commerce and Humanities, and half go to girls. Central reservation applies: 15% for SC, 7.5% for ST, 27% for OBC, plus 5% horizontal reservation for students with disabilities across all categories. Since 2022-23, unfilled slots can be moved between categories, gender and states.

So the same marks can clear the cut-off on one board and miss it on another. What counts is where you rank among your own board's successful students in your stream.

## Your One-Week Checklist Before November 30

1. **Get your OTR.** NSP has required One Time Registration since 2024-25. It is a single number that stays with you for your whole academic career on the portal.
2. **Match your name exactly.** Your name, father's name and roll number must match the Class 12 marksheet letter for letter. The FAQ gives the example of an application rejected because the father's name was abbreviated. Your Aadhaar name must also match the marksheet.
3. **Pick the scheme correctly.** After personal details, choose "Post-Matric" and then the Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students.
4. **Choose your college by AISHE code**, not by name. Many colleges have similar names, and a wrong pick after final submission leads to non-verification.
5. **Keep these ready:** a bank account in your own name with Aadhaar seeded, Aadhaar number, parental income certificate, mobile number, email, and caste or disability certificate if it applies. Students with disabilities must first give consent on the UDID portal, or NSP will not accept the form.
6. **Follow up with your college.** No documents are uploaded at the application stage, but your institute verifies the form first and the state higher education department checks it next. An application not verified at both levels is treated as invalid.

If you do not have internet access at home, the NSP page says Common Service Centres now offer scholarship services for a fixed fee of Rs 30.

## Keeping the Scholarship After Year One

The award is renewed every year, not paid automatically. To renew, you need at least 50% marks in the annual exam and at least 75% attendance. Any finding of ragging or indiscipline ends the scholarship. If you miss a renewal deadline, you lose that year's payment but may apply again the following year. For 2026-27, renewal students had to apply by October 31 and colleges had to verify by November 15, with the second level of checks due by November 30.

Payment goes by Direct Benefit Transfer to your Aadhaar-seeded bank account. You can track it on the PFMS "Know Your Payment" page using your Aadhaar, account number or NSP application ID. ## PM-USP Scholarship: Common Questions

### What is the last date for the PM-USP CSSS scholarship 2026-27?
Fresh applications on the National Scholarship Portal close on November 30, 2026. Renewal applications closed on October 31, 2026.

### What is the income limit for the Central Sector Scholarship?
Gross parental or family income must be up to Rs 4.5 lakh a year. An income certificate is needed only for fresh applicants.

### How much money does the scholarship give?
Rs 12,000 a year for the first three years of graduation, and Rs 20,000 a year for postgraduation and for the fourth and fifth years of five-year or integrated courses. B.Tech and B.E. students get Rs 12,000 for three years and Rs 20,000 in the fourth year.

### Can diploma or distance learning students apply?
No. The <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in/public/schemeGuidelines/FAQ_DOHE_CSSS.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ministry of Education FAQ</a> limits the scheme to regular degree courses, so diploma, correspondence and distance mode students are not eligible.

For scheme queries, the FAQ lists the National Scholarship Division at 011-20862360. Technical problems go to the NSP helpdesk, and questions on whether you made the merit list go to your state education board.
