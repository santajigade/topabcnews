There is no Maruti Alto 800 2025. Maruti Suzuki stopped the Alto 800 in 2023, when BS6 Phase 2 emission rules took effect in April, and it has not brought the car back. The Alto you can buy today is the Alto K10, with a 998cc engine, six airbags and ex-showroom prices from Rs 3,69,900 to Rs 5,44,900 on Maruti Suzuki's official price list.

## Update (22 November 2026)

When this article was first published in October 2025, it described a new Alto 800 for 2025 with dual airbags, a touchscreen, keyless entry and an expected price of Rs 3.8 lakh to Rs 5.5 lakh. That was wrong. Maruti Suzuki did not launch an Alto 800 in 2025, and the features and prices in the earlier version were not from the company. We have removed them.

Here is what happened. In September 2022, Autocar India reported that Maruti would retire its 796cc three-cylinder F8D engine, which first appeared in the Maruti 800 in 1983, by the end of FY2023. The Alto 800 was the only car still using it. DNA India reported on 31 March 2023 that the Alto 800 would be phased out as BS6 Phase 2 rules arrived on 1 April, and that it was not viable to upgrade the car. At that time, Maruti's website listed the Alto 800 at Rs 3.54 lakh to Rs 5.13 lakh ex-showroom in New Delhi.

## Why Maruti Dropped the 796cc Engine

The reports give three reasons:

1. **Emissions.** The 2023 rules would have meant reworking an engine that dated back four decades.
2. **Falling demand.** DNA India said the entry hatchback segment sold over 4.5 lakh cars in FY16, but volumes for FY23 were projected at about 2.5 lakh.
3. **Overlap with the Alto K10.** Autocar India noted that the new K10, launched in August 2022, sat in almost the same price band, so Maruti kept one Alto instead of two.

The Alto 800 sold in big numbers right to the end. DNA India put sales at about 17 lakh units from 2010 onwards.

## What Alto 800 Owners Should Know

If you own an Alto 800 and are thinking about your next car, these points follow from the facts above:

- The 796cc engine was retired in 2023, and none of the sources we checked mentions a replacement 800cc engine.
- The closest replacement is the Alto K10, with a bigger 998cc engine and six airbags listed on the official page.
- The price gap is small. The last Alto 800 started at Rs 3.54 lakh in New Delhi in 2023. The K10 Std (O) now starts at Rs 3,69,900, about Rs 16,000 more.
- The K10 is sold with factory CNG from Rs 4,84,400 and with an AGS automatic from Rs 4,97,400.

None of the sources we checked covers spare parts or service support for the Alto 800, so ask your Maruti service centre directly before planning major repairs.

## Maruti Alto 800: Common Questions

**Is the Maruti Alto 800 still available in 2025 or 2026?**
No. Maruti Suzuki stopped making the Alto 800 in 2023, when BS6 Phase 2 rules took effect, and no new Alto 800 has been launched since.

**What was the last price of the Alto 800?**
In March 2023, Maruti's website listed it at Rs 3.54 lakh to Rs 5.13 lakh ex-showroom in New Delhi, according to DNA India.

**What is the Alto K10 price now?**
Maruti Suzuki's official price list shows Rs 3,69,900 for the Std (O) up to Rs 5,44,900 for the VXi+ (O) AGS, ex-showroom.

**Does the Alto K10 come with CNG?**
Yes. The LXi (O) CNG costs Rs 4,84,400 and the VXi (O) CNG costs Rs 5,34,400 ex-showroom.
