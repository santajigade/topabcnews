The International Cricket Council (ICC) has fixed the dates for all its major men's tournaments from 2028 to 2035, and India has two big events on the list. The ICC Board, meeting in Bali, Indonesia, approved the windows on Sunday, November 15, 2026. India will host the Champions Trophy in October 2029, its first time as host of that event since 2006, and will co-host the 50-over World Cup with Bangladesh in October and November 2031.

## The Full ICC Men's Calendar From 2028 to 2035

The approved plan covers 12 tournaments over eight years: four Men's T20 World Cups, two Men's Cricket World Cups (ODI), two Champions Trophies and four World Test Championship (WTC) Finals. Here is the schedule released on the <a href="https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/icc-approves-window-for-major-men-s-tournaments-till-2035" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official ICC website</a>:

| Year | Tournament | Host(s) | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2028 | T20 World Cup | Australia and New Zealand | Oct 12 to Nov 12 |
| 2029 | WTC Final | England | June 8 to 12 |
| 2029 | Champions Trophy | India | Oct 9 to 28 |
| 2030 | T20 World Cup | England, Ireland and Scotland | July 4 to Aug 4 |
| 2031 | WTC Final | England | June 12 to 16 |
| 2031 | ODI World Cup | India and Bangladesh | Oct 9 to Nov 23 |
| 2032 | T20 World Cup | To be confirmed | Oct 14 to Nov 14 |
| 2033 | WTC Final | To be confirmed | June 9 to 13 |
| 2033 | Champions Trophy | To be confirmed | Oct 2 to 23 |
| 2034 | T20 World Cup | To be confirmed | July 6 to Aug 6 |
| 2035 | WTC Final | To be confirmed | June 7 to 11 |
| 2035 | ODI World Cup | To be confirmed | Oct 2 to Nov 18 |

Hosts for all events from 2032 onwards have not been named yet. Only their dates are public for now.

## What India's Two Hosting Rights Mean for Fans

For Indian fans, the 2029 Champions Trophy is the headline. The country last staged the tournament in 2006, so a whole generation of supporters will get their first chance to see it at home. The event is scheduled for October 9 to 28, 2029.

Two years later, India and Bangladesh will jointly host the ODI World Cup from October 9 to November 23, 2031. India last hosted the 50-over World Cup in 2023, also in an October and November window. The ICC's next ODI World Cup, in 2027, will be held in South Africa in the same part of the year, according to a PTI report carried by The Tribune.

India go into this new cycle as holders of two ICC men's trophies. The ICC lists India as the current champions of both the Men's T20 World Cup and the Champions Trophy, while Australia hold the ODI World Cup and South Africa hold the WTC mace. The men's team is also in good form in white-ball cricket, having just <a href="/india-beat-new-zealand-5th-odi-series-3-1/">won the ODI series in New Zealand 3-1</a>.

## WTC Final Stays a June Fixture in England

The World Test Championship Final will remain a two-yearly event played in June right through to 2035. England has been confirmed as the host for both 2029 and 2031, while hosts for the 2033 and 2035 finals are still to be decided.

Keeping the final in a fixed June slot helps boards plan their home and away Test series in the two-year cycle leading up to each final.

## T20 World Cups Shift Between Two Windows

The T20 World Cup will alternate between two parts of the year:

- **2028:** Australia and New Zealand, October 12 to November 12
- **2030:** England, Ireland and Scotland, July 4 to August 4
- **2032:** host to be confirmed, October 14 to November 14
- **2034:** host to be confirmed, July 6 to August 6

The 2030 edition is notable for Ireland and Scotland, who will share hosting duties with England. So two editions fall in the July and August period and two in the October and November period.

## What the ICC Said About the Schedule

ICC Chief Executive Sanjog Gupta said the tournaments were "pinnacle moments for fans and players alike". He called the events portfolio and schedule "critical components of cricket's global architecture".

Gupta also said the ICC recognised the value of giving players and member boards long-term visibility of these events. He linked this to better planning of player pathways, high-performance programmes and structured international calendars.

The decision came at the end of a round of <a href="https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/icc-board-and-committee-meetings-commence-in-bali" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ICC Board and committee meetings in Bali</a>. The same week also saw India's under-19 side beat Pakistan by one wicket in the Under-19 Asia Cup final in Muscat to win the title for a ninth time.

### Key highlights

- ICC Board approved men's event windows from 2028 to 2035 on November 15, 2026.
- India hosts the Champions Trophy from October 9 to 28, 2029, its first since 2006.
- India and Bangladesh co-host the ODI World Cup from October 9 to November 23, 2031.
- England hosts the WTC Final in June 2029 and June 2031.
- Hosts for 2032 to 2035 events are yet to be announced.

## Why Fixed Windows Help Boards and Players

With dates locked in nearly a decade ahead, national boards can now build their bilateral tours, domestic seasons and franchise league schedules around the ICC events. Venues for India's 2029 Champions Trophy and the split of 2031 World Cup matches with Bangladesh have not been announced yet, and the hosts for the tournaments from 2032 to 2035 are still to be named.
