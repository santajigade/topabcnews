When this article was first written in September 2025, the question was whether Lionel Messi would play the 2026 FIFA World Cup at all. He did. At 39, he scored 8 goals and made 4 assists in eight matches, took Argentina to the final, and lost it 1-0 to Spain after extra time on 19 July 2026. On 31 August 2026 he retired from the national team.

| Messi at the 2026 World Cup | |
|---|---|
| Matches | 8 |
| Goals | 8 (second in the Golden Boot race) |
| Assists | 4 |
| Award | Silver Ball (Rodri won the Golden Ball) |
| Final | Spain 1-0 Argentina (aet), 19 July 2026, East Rutherford, New Jersey |
| Argentina retirement | Announced 31 August 2026 |

## Update (20 November 2026)

Argentina's run to the final leaned on Messi late in games. In the round of 16 against Egypt he came on late and helped them recover from 2-0 down. In the semi-final against England he set up both late goals in a comeback win, Fox Sports reported.

The final at the New York New Jersey Stadium was a different story. Spain had 20 shots to Argentina's 2, and Argentina did not land one on target, <a href="https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/49402253/world-cup-spain-win-lionel-messi-argentina-reaction-analysis-ferran-torres" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ESPN reported</a>. Messi touched the ball 54 times and never once in Spain's penalty box. Argentina went down to 10 men when Enzo Fernandez got a second yellow card just before the end of normal time. Ferran Torres scored in the 106th minute, 37 seconds into the second half of extra time, according to <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/7/19/spain-battle-past-10-man-argentina-1-0-in-extra-time-to-win-2026-world-cup" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera</a>. It was Spain's second World Cup title, after 2010. Spain's young defender Pau Cubarsi was named best young player of the tournament, which we covered in our story on <a href="/pau-cubarsi-wins-golden-boy-2026/">Cubarsi winning the Golden Boy</a>.

The next day Messi posted on Instagram that the pain was immense and that it would take time to heal. He also congratulated Spain.

Kylian Mbappe won the Golden Boot with 10 goals, two more than Messi. Rodri took the Golden Ball, with Messi second and Mbappe third.

On 31 August, Messi announced on social media that he was leaving the national team after 21 years. He wrote that he had "nothing left to give" and that younger players deserved their place. He said the death of his father and long-time agent, Jorge Messi, had made him more sure of the decision. He continues to play for Inter Miami.

On 16 November 2026, Argentina coach Lionel Scaloni, still in charge, told a press conference that Messi would be welcome on his coaching staff whenever he stops playing, Mexican sports daily Record reported.

## The Night in Buenos Aires That Started the Question

On 4 September 2025, Argentina beat Venezuela 3-0 at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires in a World Cup qualifier. Messi scored in the 39th and 80th minutes, and Lautaro Martinez added a goal in the 76th. A sellout crowd treated it as a farewell, since it was Messi's last home qualifier. Argentina had already qualified and were sure to finish top of the South American table.

Afterwards Messi said he was excited about 2026 but would take it day by day and see how his body felt. If he was not in good shape, he said, "I'd rather not be there."

## What Messi Said in 2025 and What Happened in 2026

| September 2025 | July and August 2026 |
|---|---|
| Unsure if he would play at 39 | Played all the way to the final |
| Would only go if he felt good | Scored 8 goals, the second highest total of the tournament |
| Wanted to take it day by day | Came off the bench late against Egypt to help overturn a 2-0 deficit |
| Kept the door open on his future | Retired from Argentina on 31 August 2026 |

Looking back, his caution in 2025 was honest rather than a hint at retirement. He went, he was one of the best players at the tournament, and the Silver Ball shows it. The final was the one match where he could not change the result.

He leaves as Argentina's record holder for appearances, goals and assists.

## What Comes Next for Argentina

Scaloni's contract runs to December 2026, and after the final he hinted he might step down at the end of the year, the AP reported. Argentina scheduled friendlies for September, October and November, and their next major tournament is the 2028 Copa America.

The question of who wears the No. 10 shirt is open. Nico Paz and Thiago Almada are the names most often mentioned for the playmaker role, but neither has taken Messi's place yet. Argentina will also host one of the centenary matches of the 2030 World Cup, which Spain, Portugal and Morocco will co-host with single games in Argentina, Uruguay and Paraguay.

His World Cup story now has a clear shape: four tournaments without a title, the win in Qatar in his fifth, and a sixth that ended one goal short of a second. Spain are now the team everyone chases, although their 43-match unbeaten run has since ended, as we reported in <a href="/england-beat-spain-end-43-match-unbeaten-run/">England's 3-2 win in Madrid</a>.
