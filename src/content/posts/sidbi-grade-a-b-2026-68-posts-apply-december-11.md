Applications opened on November 21, 2026 and close on December 11, 2026. The online exam is tentatively set for December 27, with interviews in February 2027. The fee is Rs 1,100 for General, OBC and EWS candidates and Rs 175 for SC, ST and PwBD candidates.

The exam calendar is also tighter. Last year the two phases were a month apart. This year both run on the same day, which leaves about 16 days between the application deadline and the exam.

## Eligibility in Brief

**Grade A General stream:** a graduation degree in Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Business Administration or Engineering with 60% marks (50% for eligible SC, ST and PwBD candidates), or CA, CS, CMA or CFA, or a two-year full-time MBA or PGDM. The notice asks for two years of officer-level experience, or three years at clerk level. CA holders and two-year full-time MBA or PGDM holders are exempt from the experience condition, and final-year MBA or PGDM students may apply.

**Grade A Legal:** an LLB with 50% marks (45% for reserved categories), enrolment with the Bar Council of India and two years of experience as a law officer.

**Grade A Systems/IT:** a B.E. or B.Tech in any discipline, or MCA, with 60% marks (55% for reserved categories), and two years of IT experience.

**Grade B posts:** the specialist qualification for the stream plus five years of post-qualification experience.

Age relaxation is 5 years for SC and ST, 3 years for OBC (non-creamy layer), and 10 to 15 years for PwBD candidates depending on category. Qualification and experience are counted up to December 11, 2026. Each candidate can apply for only one post. Employees of government bodies, PSUs, banks or financial institutions must bring a no objection certificate at the interview stage.

## Steps to Apply Before December 11

1. Go to the Careers and Recruitment section of the SIDBI website and open the Grade A and B 2026 advertisement.
2. Pick one post and check that your degree, marks and experience match it as on December 11, 2026.
3. Register with your mobile number and email ID, then fill in the form.
4. Upload your photograph, signature and other documents in the format the notice asks for.
5. Pay the fee online: Rs 1,100 (Rs 925 fee plus Rs 175 intimation charge) for General, OBC and EWS, or Rs 175 for SC, ST and PwBD. The fee includes GST and is not refunded.
6. Save the final form and payment receipt.

Selection has three stages. Phase I is an objective screening test, Phase II is a mix of descriptive English and stream-specific papers, and only Phase I qualifiers have their Phase II answers checked. Phase III is a 100-mark interview. ## SIDBI Grade A and B 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date for SIDBI Grade A 2026?**
The online application and fee payment both close on December 11, 2026.

**What is the age limit for SIDBI Grade A and Grade B?**
Grade A applicants must be 21 to 30 years old and Grade B applicants 25 to 33 years old on November 21, 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

**When is the SIDBI Grade A exam 2026?**
Phase I and Phase II are tentatively scheduled together on December 27, 2026. Interviews are expected in February 2027.

**What is the SIDBI Grade A salary in 2026?**
The notice gives an approximate monthly gross of Rs 1,20,000 for Grade A and Rs 1,45,000 for Grade B, with indicative CTC of Rs 30 lakh to Rs 34 lakh and Rs 40 lakh to Rs 43 lakh.

Dates, posts and pay can change through a corrigendum, so confirm the details in the official SIDBI notice before you apply.
