Online applications for 1,265 Station Master posts in Indian Railways close at 11:59 pm on Wednesday, November 25, 2026. The Railway Recruitment Boards opened the form under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) 08/2026 on October 27, and the <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2329071&reg=48&lang=2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ministry of Railways release</a> lists RRB Mumbai (177 posts), Jammu-Srinagar (122) and Chennai (119) as the boards with the most openings. No extension had been announced as of November 20.

Any graduate can apply. The post sits at Pay Level 6 of the 7th Pay Commission matrix.

| Step | Date | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Form submission closes | November 25, 2026, 11:59 pm | Form must be submitted on rrbapply.gov.in |
| Fee payment closes | November 27, 2026 | Online only: net banking, card or UPI |
| Correction window | November 28 to December 7, 2026 | Rs 250 for each change, not refunded |
| Age counted as on | January 1, 2027 | Limits and relaxations in Para 8 of the CEN |
| CBT date | Not announced | City slip and schedule to come by SMS, email and RRB sites |

## Who Can Apply and What the Job Pays

The board asks for a bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognised university. The official FAQ is strict about timing: you must already hold the degree on November 25. Final-year students and anyone still waiting for a result on that date are not eligible.

The post is at Pay Level 6. Adda247 puts the starting basic pay at Rs 35,400 a month, with allowances on top.

Job portals do not agree on the age limit. Careerindia gives 18 to 33 years, Adda247 20 to 33, and Careers360 20 to 30. The board's FAQ only says age is counted as on January 1, 2027 and sends candidates to Para 8 of the notice for limits and relaxations. Check that paragraph in the official PDF on the <a href="https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RRB application portal</a> before you pay, and do not go by a figure on a job site.

## Five Rules That Can Cost You the Application

The CEN 08/2026 FAQ sets out these rules:

1. Apply to only one RRB. If you apply to more than one board for the same pay level, all your applications are rejected. Once you submit, the RRB you picked cannot be changed, not even in the correction window.
2. Some fields are locked. During November 28 to December 7 you can correct most details. The fields entered while creating the account (name, date of birth, email, mobile number) cannot be changed. Each correction costs Rs 250, and that money does not come back.
3. Station Master is a safety post with medical standard A-2. The FAQ says candidates who have had LASIK or any other refractive eye surgery are not eligible for A-2 posts. If you have had such surgery, do not pay the fee.
4. Shortlisted candidates must bring a vision certificate in the format given in Annexure VI of the notice (VI-A for ex-servicemen). They also need a self-declaration on medical fitness, signed by a general physician and an eye specialist, on the day of the test.
5. The signature must be a JPG or JPEG file of 30 to 49 KB, at least 100 DPI, and at least 140 x 60 pixels. A caste certificate goes as a PDF of up to 400 KB.

The FAQ also says the exam city and date given to you are final. There is no request process to change them.

## Fee and Refund Before You Pay

Careerindia gives the fee as Rs 500 for most candidates and Rs 250 for women, transgender candidates, ex-servicemen, SC, ST, minority and economically backward class candidates. The official FAQ names the same groups for the lower fee but leaves the amounts to the notice.

The fee comes back only if you sit for the computer based test (CBT), minus bank charges. Adda247 says Rs 400 of the Rs 500 is refunded. Correction fees, rejected forms and incomplete forms get nothing back. The last day to pay is November 27.

## How Selection Works for Station Master

Careers360 notes that Station Master is no longer part of the RRB NTPC graduate notice and is recruited separately. Selection has four stages:

- **Single-stage CBT.** Adda247 gives the pattern as 100 questions for 100 marks in 120 minutes, covering basic science and mathematics, data analysis and interpretation, reading comprehension and mental reasoning. The FAQ confirms that one-third of a mark is cut for each wrong answer and that the paper comes in 15 languages along with English.
- **Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT).** According to Adda247, up to eight times the vacancies of each RRB are called. There is no negative marking, and you must score at least a T-score of 42 in every test battery.
- **Final merit.** Adda247 says the CBT carries 70% weight and the CBAT 30%.
- **Document verification and medical examination** for those on the merit list.

## RRB Station Master 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date to apply for RRB Station Master 2026?**
The online form closes at 11:59 pm on November 25, 2026. You can pay the fee until November 27.

**Can I apply for RRB Station Master in the final year of graduation?**
No. The FAQ says you must hold the degree on November 25, 2026. If your result is still pending on that date, you are not eligible.

**Can I change my RRB after submitting the form?**
No. The chosen RRB cannot be changed, even during the November 28 to December 7 correction window.

**Is there negative marking in the Station Master exam?**
Yes. In the CBT, one-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer. The aptitude test has no negative marking.

For queries, the RRB helpline listed in the FAQ is rrb.help@csc.gov.in, or 9592001188 and 0172-5653333, from 10 am to 5 pm on working days.
