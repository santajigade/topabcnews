Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) ran its 2025 apprentice drive for **537 posts** in the Pipelines Division from 29 August to 18 September 2025. That window closed more than a year ago. The Pipelines Division has since issued a new notice for **about 500 apprentices**, open from **6 November to 5 December 2026**, with an age limit of 18 to 24 years as on 30 November 2026.

## Update (20 November 2026)

The current notice is Advertisement No. PL/HR/ESTB/APPR (2026), dated 6 November 2026, published on the <a href="https://iocl.com/admin/img/Apprenticeships/Files/c1ee9de273284203b8546936356b9787.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IOCL apprenticeships page</a>. The basics:

| Detail | 2026 Pipelines notice |
|---|---|
| Posts | About 500 |
| Apply from | 6 November 2026 |
| Last date | 5 December 2026 |
| Age | 18 to 24 years as on 30 November 2026 |
| Minimum marks | 50% aggregate (45% for SC, ST and PwBD reserved posts) |
| Training period | 12 months |
| Selection | Merit list on qualifying marks, no written test or interview |

You have to apply on two portals within these dates: the national apprenticeship portal (NATS or NAPS, depending on the trade) and the IOCL Pipelines portal. The steps are further down.

## Seats by Region: 2025 Against 2026

The Pipelines Division splits its seats across five regional pipelines. This table puts the 2025 count (from the earlier notice, as reported by the Free Press Journal) next to the 2026 notice.

| Region | 2025 seats | 2026 seats |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ERPL) | 156 | 116 |
| Western (WRPL) | 152 | 149 |
| Northern (NRPL) | 97 | 110 |
| South Eastern (SERPL) | 85 | 79 |
| Southern (SRPL) | 47 | 46 |
| **Total** | **537** | **about 500** |

Western Region still has the most seats. Northern Region is the only one with more seats than last year, up from 97 to 110. Eastern Region fell the most, from 156 to 116.

## Which Trades You Can Apply For

The 2026 notice lists four groups:

- **Technician Apprentice** in Mechanical, Electrical, and Telecommunication & Instrumentation. You need a three-year full-time engineering diploma in that discipline. The notice also mentions lateral entry options.
- **Trade Apprentice (Assistant-Human Resource)** needs a full-time bachelor's degree.
- **Trade Apprentice (Accountant)** needs a full-time bachelor's degree in Commerce.
- **Data Entry Operator (Fresher Apprentice)** needs at least Class 12 and must be below graduate level. The **Domestic Data Entry Operator** post also needs a specified skill certificate.

Graduates and diploma holders need 50% aggregate marks, and 45% for SC, ST and PwBD candidates on reserved posts. Your qualification must be complete, with results declared, by 30 November 2026. You can apply for one trade code only.

### Age relaxation

The upper age limit of 24 goes up by 5 years for SC and ST candidates, 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) and 10 years for PwBD candidates. PwBD candidates who are also SC, ST or OBC (NCL) get a further relaxation.

## How to Apply for the 2026 Pipelines Apprenticeship

1. Pick your trade code from the notice. Each applicant can choose only one.
2. If you are applying for a Technician, Assistant-HR or Accountant post, register on the <a href="https://nats.education.gov.in/student_register.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NATS portal of the Ministry of Education</a>.
3. If you are applying for a Data Entry Operator trade, register on the <a href="https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Apprenticeship India (NAPS) portal</a>.
4. Then fill in the application on the IOCL Pipelines portal, plapps.indianoilpipelines.in, before 5 December 2026.
5. Keep your NATS or NAPS registration number handy, along with scanned copies of your certificates, photo and signature.

The notice says it will reject incomplete applications, applications sent any other way and applications filed after the deadline.

## How Selection Works

IOCL makes a merit panel from your percentage of marks in the essential qualification. There is no written test and no interview. If two candidates have the same marks, the older one ranks higher, and if that is also equal, the one with higher Class 10 marks goes ahead. Shortlisted candidates go through document verification and a medical fitness check before they join.

The stipend is set under the Apprentices Act and Rules and IOCL guidelines. The notice does not print an amount, and it says apprentices get no accommodation, HRA, conveyance or medical benefits beyond the stipend.

## What the 2025 Drive Looked Like

The 2025 notice offered 537 seats in three groups: Technician Apprentice (diploma), Trade Apprentice (graduate) and Data Entry Operator (Class 12). The age limit was 18 to 24 as on 31 August 2025, and training ran for 12 months. Selection was on merit. Patrika reported that IOCL ranked applicants by the average of their marks across all years or semesters. There was no exam.

IOCL runs separate apprentice drives outside the Pipelines Division too. In February 2026 it advertised 637 seats at refinery and marketing locations, open from 7 to 20 February, CareerIndia reported. In March 2026 India TV reported a 405-seat drive for northern states including Delhi, Punjab, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh, which closed on 26 March. Those windows have closed, but they show IOCL opens new batches several times a year. If you have an engineering degree and want a regular job rather than an apprenticeship, see our earlier report on <a href="/indian-oil-recruitment-2025/">Indian Oil's graduate engineer recruitment</a>.

## IOCL Apprentice Recruitment: Common Questions

**What is the last date for the IOCL Pipelines apprentice 2026 application?**
5 December 2026. Applications opened on 6 November 2026, and you must finish both the NATS or NAPS step and the IOCL Pipelines portal form by the last date.

**What is the age limit for the IOCL apprentice 2026 drive?**
18 to 24 years as on 30 November 2026. SC and ST candidates get 5 extra years, OBC (NCL) candidates get 3 and PwBD candidates get 10.

**Is there an exam for IOCL apprentice selection?**
No. Selection is on merit from your qualifying marks, then document verification and a medical check.

**Can B.Com graduates apply?**
Yes. The Trade Apprentice (Accountant) post asks for a full-time bachelor's degree in Commerce with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC, ST and PwBD reserved posts).
