Mercedes-Benz India has launched the new GLC Electric, an all electric version of its popular GLC SUV, at an ex-showroom price of Rs 78.5 lakh. The launch took place this week, around November 4, 2026. The electric SUV comes in a single GLC 400 4Matic variant and promises a WLTP certified range of up to 711 km on a full charge. It is assembled locally at the company's Chakan plant near Pune, and it now becomes the most affordable way into the Mercedes electric SUV family in India.

## Price and how it compares with the petrol and diesel GLC

The biggest surprise is the pricing. According to Autocar India, the GLC Electric costs only about Rs 1.5 lakh more than the petrol GLC and around Rs 50,000 more than the diesel version. That narrow gap makes it a serious option for buyers who were already looking at a regular GLC. Before launch, Carlelo had expected a price between Rs 80 lakh and Rs 90 lakh, so the final price came in lower than many expected.

Local assembly is a key reason behind the pricing. Rushlane reports that the electric GLC is built on the same flexible production line at Chakan that makes the company's petrol and diesel models, and the first locally assembled unit has already rolled out.

## Battery, motors and performance

The GLC Electric uses a 94 kWh battery and two electric motors, one at the front and one at the rear, which give it all wheel drive. It sits on a new dedicated EV platform with an 800 volt electrical system.

Autocar India notes that the motors use a 2 speed gearbox, which is unusual for an electric car. With 490 hp and 800 Nm, this is a quick SUV for its size, even though its kerb weight is close to 2.5 tonnes.

## Charging time

Thanks to the 800 volt system, the SUV can accept DC charging at up to 330 kW. Mercedes claims that on a fast enough charger, about 300 km of range can be added in just 10 minutes. Rushlane points out that the real figure will depend on the charger, battery temperature and charge level. A 22 kW AC charger comes as standard for home and office charging. Buyers can also choose MB.CHARGE public charging credits in place of a free AC wallbox, Rushlane says.

## Hyperscreen and cabin features

Inside, the headline feature is a 39.1 inch MBUX Hyperscreen that stretches across the dashboard. Autocar India describes it as the largest single piece infotainment screen in any vehicle sold in India. It runs on the new MB.OS software, which supports over the air updates, and there are very few physical buttons in the cabin, even for the air conditioning.

Key features include:

- **Illuminated grille** with hundreds of tiny pixel lights and star shaped LED lamps
- **Panoramic glass roof** with illuminated stars and electrically adjustable tint
- **15 speaker Burmester 3D** sound system
- **Heated front seats with massage** function (no ventilation)
- **Two wireless chargers**, ambient lighting and a powered tailgate
- **Level 2 ADAS** with eight cameras and five radars
- **360 degree camera**, auto parking assist and nine airbags
- **20 inch alloy wheels** and AMG Line styling as standard

There are a few things missing at this price. Autocar India points out that the SUV does not get air suspension or rear wheel steering, and the glass roof has no sunshade.

Compared with the regular GLC, the electric model is 140 mm longer, 23 mm wider and 12 mm taller, with an 84 mm longer wheelbase. That should mean more space for rear passengers.

## Bookings, deliveries and rivals

Demand looks strong. Autocar India reports more than 1,000 bookings already, with the SUV sold out until March 2027. Rushlane says deliveries are planned to begin by mid November 2026. The GLC name has a big following in India, with nearly 22,000 customers since the combustion GLC arrived in 2016, according to Rushlane.

For now, the GLC Electric has no direct rival in its price band. The BMW iX3 is expected to reach India next year, as per Autocar India.

Mercedes-Benz India has also committed an additional Rs 200 crore investment in 2026, which takes its total investment in India to Rs 3,200 crore, Rushlane reported.

## Why the GLC Electric Matters for Luxury EV Buyers

The Mercedes GLC Electric brings a long range, quick charging and a large Hyperscreen at a price very close to the petrol and diesel GLC. That makes the switch to electric a much easier choice for luxury SUV buyers. With bookings already stretching into next year, it is likely to be one of the most important luxury EV launches in India this year.
