Mercedes-Benz India launched the all electric GLC on 4 November 2026 at an introductory price of **Rs 78.5 lakh (ex-showroom, all India)**. It comes in one version, the GLC 400 4Matic, with a 94 kWh battery, two motors making 360 kW (489 hp) and 800 Nm, and a WLTP range of up to **711 km** as certified by ARAI. The SUV is assembled at the company's Chakan plant near Pune, and the first locally built unit rolled out on launch day, <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/auto/story/mercedes-benz-electric-glc-launched-at-rs78-50-lakh-deliveries-to-begin-in-november-565777-2026-11-05" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Today reported</a>.

Deliveries were scheduled to start in mid November 2026. Demand is already ahead of supply: the order book has crossed 1,000 cars and is full until March 2027.

## Electric GLC vs Petrol and Diesel GLC: The Price Gap

The headline here is how close the EV sits to the regular GLC. Autocar India lists the petrol and diesel GLC at roughly Rs 77 lakh to Rs 78 lakh ex-showroom, so the electric model costs only about Rs 50,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh more. For that small premium, you get close to double the power of the petrol car.

| | GLC Electric (GLC 400 4Matic) | GLC petrol | GLC diesel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ex-showroom price | Rs 78.5 lakh | Rs 77 lakh to Rs 78 lakh range (both fuels) | Rs 77 lakh to Rs 78 lakh range (both fuels) |
| Engine / motor | Dual electric motors | 2.0 litre, mild hybrid | 2.0 litre, mild hybrid |
| Power | 360 kW (489 hp) | 258 hp | 197 hp |
| Torque | 800 Nm | 400 Nm | 440 Nm |
| Drive | All wheel drive | 4Matic AWD | 4Matic AWD |

On rivals, evo India names the Audi Q6 e-tron and the long wheelbase BMW iX3, which is due in India in 2027. Volvo's EX90 costs Rs 1.26 crore, which puts it a full price class above the Mercedes. BYD's luxury brand also arrived recently, with the [Denza D9 MPV at Rs 90 lakh](/denza-z9-gt-d9-launched-india-price/).

## Battery, Motors and Size

The GLC Electric sits on Mercedes' MB.EA-M platform and is the first locally made Mercedes in India built on it. It uses an 800 volt electrical system, which is what allows very fast DC charging. The rear axle gets a two speed gearbox, unusual in an EV, which shifts on its own depending on the drive mode, according to the <a href="https://www.evoindia.com/article/mercedes-glc-400-electric-review-india-first-drive-587714" target="_blank" rel="noopener">evo India first drive</a>. Top speed is limited to 210 km/h.

The EV is bigger than the petrol and diesel GLC:

- Length 4,845 mm, width 1,913 mm, height 1,649 mm
- Wheelbase 2,972 mm, which is 84 mm longer than the combustion GLC
- 23 mm wider and 9 mm taller than the combustion model
- Ground clearance of 148 mm, which rises by 25 mm when the air suspension is raised
- Boot of 570 litres (50 litres less than the outgoing GLC) plus a 128 litre front trunk

The longer wheelbase should free up more legroom in the back seat, though the boot is slightly smaller.

## What Charging Will Look Like for an Indian Owner

Mercedes says the GLC Electric can take DC charging at up to 330 kW. On a charger that can deliver that much power, and in ideal conditions, 10 minutes adds up to 303 km of range. evo India quotes about 22 minutes to go from 10% to 80%. In practice, the speed you get depends on the charger, the battery temperature and how full the battery is.

Buyers get a choice at delivery: a free AC wall box for home charging, or MB.CHARGE credits worth 2,500 kWh of public charging, valid for 24 months. Here is what those credits roughly translate to, using only the published figures:

| Measure | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Full charges from empty | 2,500 kWh / 94 kWh | About 26 |
| Distance at WLTP efficiency | 711 km / 94 kWh = 7.6 km per kWh | About 18,900 km |
| Distance at evo India's real world estimate | About 500 km per charge, so 5.3 km per kWh | About 13,300 km |

These are estimates, not company figures. Charging losses mean the real distance will be a little lower. Still, someone who drives around 12,000 km a year and lacks home parking may find the credits more useful than a wall box.

## Hyperscreen and Cabin

The dashboard is covered by a 39.1 inch MBUX Hyperscreen running Mercedes' own MB.OS software. It handles navigation, driver assistance and personal settings, and it can be updated over the air. Outside, the grille carries 942 illuminated dots around a glowing Mercedes roundel, and the headlamps and tail lamps use star shaped LED graphics.

Other features mentioned at launch include:

- 20 inch alloy wheels and AMG Line styling as standard
- SKY CONTROL panoramic glass roof with illuminated stars
- Flush door handles that pop out when the car is unlocked
- A full width gloss black band across the tail
- Recycled and low CO2 materials inside the cabin

## Bookings, Deliveries and the Waiting Period

Mercedes-Benz India received more than 550 expressions of interest within 36 hours of opening bookings, Business Today reported. By launch day, bookings had crossed 1,000, Mercedes-Benz India's Santosh Iyer said, according to <a href="https://www.autocarpro.in/news/mercedes-glc-ev-launched-from-rs-785-lakh-bookings-cross-1000-in-record-time-135715" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Autocar Professional</a>. Those orders carry an indicative retail value above Rs 785 crore, and the order book is full through March 2027. The company is asking its parent for more assembly kits to build more cars locally and cut waiting times. It believes EVs could make up 25% to 30% of its India sales if supply allows.

Mercedes-Benz India is also investing an extra Rs 200 crore in 2026, taking its total investment in the country to Rs 3,200 crore. Pune is now one of the global production sites for the electric GLC, alongside Bremen in Germany.

## Mercedes GLC Electric: Common Questions

### What is the price of the Mercedes GLC Electric in India?
It costs Rs 78.5 lakh ex-showroom across India. This is an introductory price for the only version on sale, the GLC 400 4Matic.

### What is the range of the GLC Electric?
Mercedes claims up to 711 km on the WLTP cycle, certified by ARAI, from a 94 kWh battery. evo India's reviewer felt about 500 km in real driving is a fair expectation.

### How fast does the GLC Electric charge?
On a 330 kW DC charger in ideal conditions, 10 minutes adds up to 303 km of range, and 10% to 80% takes about 22 minutes. Buyers can choose either a free AC wall box or 2,500 kWh of public charging credits.

### What is the waiting period for the GLC Electric?
Mercedes-Benz India said bookings crossed 1,000 at launch and the order book is full until March 2027, so new buyers should expect to wait several months.
