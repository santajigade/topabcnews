Jensen Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and ran it for 33 years, resigned as president, chief executive and board member on Thursday, November 12, with immediate effect. The company named its chief financial officer, Colette Kress, as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor. The exit of the best known figure in the artificial intelligence chip boom sent Nvidia shares sharply lower and raised fresh questions about the company that supplies most of the world's advanced AI processors.

## What Nvidia Announced on Thursday

In a press release filed with the <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">US Securities and Exchange Commission</a>, Nvidia set out three changes at the top:

- **Jensen Huang** has stepped down as president, CEO and director, effective immediately.
- **Colette Kress**, executive vice president and CFO, becomes interim CEO and will keep her finance role until a permanent chief is appointed.
- **Harvey C. Jones**, a former CEO and executive chairman of chip design software firm Synopsys, becomes Nvidia's first ever board chair.

The board said it will look at both internal and external candidates. It also said it expects operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap to continue without interruption. Nvidia did not give a reason for Huang's departure in the release.

## Why Huang Is Leaving

According to Al Jazeera, the resignation followed days of media reports about Huang's personal conduct. Huang publicly admitted on Sunday that he had been unfaithful to his wife and apologised to his family and employees. Reuters had reported that the board was considering removing him, and the board had earlier hired a law firm to carry out an independent review after reports that he had been targeted by Chinese intelligence.

These remain allegations under review. Al Jazeera noted that there is no established evidence that Huang knowingly helped Beijing or that company secrets were compromised, and Nvidia's board has said it has no reason to believe national security was affected. US President Donald Trump, a close ally of Huang, said Huang told him he had "absolutely no idea" about the alleged intelligence link. US lawmakers have said their own inquiries will continue.

## How the Market Reacted

The news hit one of the most valuable companies on the planet. Live market coverage on Yahoo Finance showed Nvidia shares falling as much as 13% at the open before recovering part of the loss, with the market value slipping below $4 trillion and touching about $3.75 trillion at one point. The stock closed more than 4% lower, according to CNBC and South Korea's SBS. Nvidia had been the world's largest company at about $5 trillion but dropped to third place behind Apple and Microsoft during the session.

Analysts were divided on the long term impact. Oppenheimer downgraded the stock, pointing to "massive shoes to fill" and the risk of senior staff leaving. Bernstein said it did not expect near term damage to the business but saw considerable uncertainty. Yorkville's Dan Ives cut his price target from $300 to $225.

## Who Is Colette Kress

Kress joined Nvidia in September 2013 and had spent nearly 25 years in finance roles at Cisco and Microsoft before that, according to the company. Al Jazeera reported that she also worked at Texas Instruments, and that during her time as CFO Nvidia's annual revenue grew from $4.13 billion in fiscal 2014 to $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026.

Kress said she is committed to building on the foundation Huang created. President Trump has publicly suggested former Apple CEO Tim Cook as a replacement, but there is no sign that Cook is interested, and the board has not named any candidates.

## What It Means for the AI Chip Industry

Nvidia's chips power most large AI systems, and Al Jazeera estimates it controls about 90% of the advanced AI chip market. Huang was the public face of that dominance and a key voice in Washington's debate over selling advanced chips to China. His exit comes at a busy moment: Nvidia is preparing a large investment linked to the planned stock market listing of AI firm Anthropic, which we covered in our report on the <a href="/anthropic-ipo-price-range-70-78-nasdaq/">Anthropic IPO price range</a>, and it supplies cloud companies such as CoreWeave, whose <a href="/coreweave-q3-2026-revenue-168-percent-backlog/">latest quarterly results</a> showed how much demand still depends on Nvidia hardware.

For Indian investors who hold US technology stocks or global funds, the sharp swing in Nvidia is a reminder of how much a single company now weighs on major indices such as the Nasdaq, which fell 0.6% in early trade on Thursday according to Al Jazeera.

## What to Watch Next Week

The first big test for the new leadership comes on Tuesday, November 17, when Nvidia reports results for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 at about 1:20 pm Pacific Time (2:50 am IST on Wednesday), followed by an earnings call. Investors will look for any change in guidance, signs of senior staff departures and an update on the CEO search. Until then, Kress and new chair Harvey Jones will have to convince customers and markets that Nvidia's roadmap does not depend on one person.
