The third ODI between India and New Zealand at Seddon Park in Hamilton was abandoned on Tuesday, November 10, without a single ball being bowled. Steady rain delayed the toss several times, and although Shubman Gill eventually won it and chose to bowl, the weather returned before play could start. The match was called off at about 11:15 am IST. India keep their 2-0 lead in the five-match series, which means they can no longer lose it.

## How the Rain Ruined the Hamilton Game

The match was scheduled for a 3 pm local start, which is 7:30 am in India. Forecasts had warned of heavy rain through the afternoon and evening, with chances of 77 to 86 percent at key hours. The toss was pushed back again and again, and at one stage the game was cut to 40 overs a side with a limit of eight overs per bowler.

When the toss finally took place, Gill called correctly and opted to field first. That decision never mattered. The rain came back, the umpires kept checking the outfield, and the conditions never improved enough for play to begin. New Zealand news site Stuff summed it up simply: rain had the final say.

India named an unchanged playing XI. New Zealand made one change, bringing in Matt Fisher for fast bowler Blair Tickner, who was on a seven-day stand-down after taking a blow to the helmet.

## Where the Series Stands After Three Games

India won both completed matches comfortably before the washout. Our full report on the <a href="/india-beat-new-zealand-2nd-odi-dls-wellington/">four-run DLS win in Wellington</a> covers the second game in detail.

| Match | Venue | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1st ODI | Eden Park, Auckland | India won by 5 wickets after bowling New Zealand out for 185 |
| 2nd ODI | Wellington | India won by 4 runs (DLS) after New Zealand were all out for 134 |
| 3rd ODI | Seddon Park, Hamilton | Abandoned without a ball bowled |
| 4th ODI | Bay Oval, Tauranga | Friday, November 13 |
| 5th ODI | Bay Oval, Tauranga | Sunday, November 15 |

With two games left, the worst India can do is draw the series 2-2. One more win in Tauranga would seal it. New Zealand must win both remaining matches just to level the contest.

## Key Performers So Far

India's bowling has set up both wins. In the opening game, Arshdeep Singh took four wickets and Anshul Kamboj picked up two as New Zealand were dismissed for 185. Virat Kohli then made 74 to guide the chase.

In Wellington, young pacer Gurnoor Brar returned career-best figures of 4 for 30 as the hosts fell for 134. India's batting wobbled in that game, but they stayed ahead of the DLS par score when rain ended play.

Key highlights of the series so far:

- India have bowled New Zealand out in both completed matches.
- Gurnoor Brar's 4 for 30 is the best bowling return of the series.
- Kohli's 74 in Auckland is the standout innings.
- Two of the three games have now been hit by rain.

## A Lucky Escape at a Tough Venue for India

The washout is not all bad news for the visitors. Seddon Park has been a fortress for New Zealand. According to match previews, the hosts had not lost a completed ODI there since February 2017, a run of 13 straight wins. India had won only one of their eight ODIs at the ground before this game. Avoiding a defeat there keeps India's grip on the series firm.

## New Zealand's Injury Problems

The hosts are also struggling for fitness. Lockie Ferguson tore his right calf and will miss the last two ODIs, while Tickner's stand-down rules him out for now. Kristian Clarke has joined the squad as cover. Several of New Zealand's leading players are also set to switch to Test preparations after the one-day games.

## Timing for the Tauranga Matches

Both remaining games will be played at Bay Oval in Tauranga, near Mount Maunganui. The 4th ODI on Friday, November 13 and the 5th ODI on Sunday, November 15 both start at 3 pm local time, which is 7:30 am IST. ## India One Win Away From the Series

A frustrating day in Hamilton has left India exactly where they started it: two wins up and needing one more. If the weather in Tauranga is kinder, India will get their chance to close out the series on Friday, with Sunday's game as a second opportunity if needed.
