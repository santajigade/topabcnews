Telangana opens applications today, Thursday, November 19, 2026, for the Indiramma Family Life Insurance Scheme (Indiramma Kutumba Jeevitha Bima). It pays Rs 5 lakh to a family if its insured earning member dies. Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy is launching it at a public meeting in Siddipet, on the birth anniversary of Indira Gandhi, and enrolment camps will run in villages and urban wards until December 18.

The state wants to reach about one crore families. It also plans to fold several existing department-run insurance schemes into this one, so families already covered elsewhere should read the details below carefully.

## Who Is Eligible for the Rs 5 Lakh Cover

According to the enrolment rules reported by [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/telangana-to-launch-rs-5-lakh-life-insurance-scheme-for-over-one-crore-families-from-november-19-enn26111802776), the key conditions are:

- **One member per family.** Only one person can be insured, and the family decides who. Officials have described this as the earning member.
- **Age 18 to 59.** In practice, the person's date of birth must fall between November 19, 1967 and November 18, 2008.
- **Aadhaar is the age proof.** The date of birth on Aadhaar is what officials will check.
- **Ration card helps but is not compulsory.** Families without a ration card can still apply through a separate form.

If the insured person crosses the upper age limit after enrolling, cover continues for at least one more year.

## How to Enrol at a Camp, Step by Step

There is no online portal mentioned in official briefings so far. Enrolment happens at camps run by Panchayat Secretaries in villages and Ward Officers in towns and cities. Announcements, including dandora (drum) announcements, have to go out two days before a camp is held in an area.

1. **Wait for the camp notice** in your village or ward, or ask your Panchayat Secretary or Ward Officer for the date.
2. **Decide who in the family will be insured.** Check that this person's Aadhaar birth date falls inside the window above.
3. **Carry Aadhaar** for the chosen member, your ration card if you have one, and the names and details of the nominee or nominees.
4. **If your family is in the food security (ration card) database,** officials pull your data from it, and your ration card number becomes your application number.
5. **If you have no ration card or are not in the database,** you fill a separate form and get a unique code instead.
6. **If your ration card is from another district,** you can still register at the camp nearest to where you live now.
7. **Name nominees with care.** If you list more than one, officials will explain at the camp how the payout of up to Rs 5 lakh is split.

The government says a state-level help desk and district helplines will be set up. District Collectors, and the commissioners of the Hyderabad, Malkajgiri and Cyberabad municipal corporations, are in charge of the drive. Siddipet Collector Rahul Sharma has asked field staff to block duplicate applications and make sure Aadhaar authentication and nominee details are correct, [The Hans India reported](https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/rs-5-lakh-life-insurance-scheme-roll-out-today-1143508).

## What Happens to Rythu Bima and Other Existing Schemes

This is the part many families will ask about. According to The Hans India, the state plans to scrap and merge three community schemes into Indiramma Bima:

| Existing scheme | Covers | Enrolled now (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Rythu Bima | Farmers | Over 42 lakh |
| Netanna Bima | Handloom weavers | Over 80,000 |
| Geetha Karmikula Bima | Toddy tappers | About 1 lakh |

The Chief Minister has told officials that nobody already covered by a government insurance scheme should be left out during the merger. Deccan Chronicle reported that department schemes paying Rs 2 lakh in life cover will move up to Rs 5 lakh, while accident benefits of up to Rs 10 lakh under those schemes stay as they are. The exact switchover process for current policyholders has not been explained yet, so existing Rythu Bima members should confirm at their camp whether they need to enrol again.

## How It Compares With the Central PMJJBY Scheme

Many Telangana families may already hold the Centre's Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana through a bank account. The two are different products:

| Feature | Indiramma Family Insurance | PMJJBY (central) |
|---|---|---|
| Life cover | Rs 5 lakh | Rs 2 lakh |
| Who pays premium | State government (full cost) | Subscriber, Rs 436 a year by auto-debit |
| Entry age | 18 to 59 | 18 to 50 |
| Cover per family | One member | Each account holder can join |
| How to join | Village or ward camp | Bank or post office account |

PMJJBY figures are from the [Department of Financial Services](https://financialservices.gov.in/pmjjby). Nothing announced so far says that holding PMJJBY stops a person from joining the state scheme, but families should ask at the camp if they are unsure.

## The Cost to the State

The State Planning Board estimates the scheme will cost close to Rs 3,000 crore a year if it covers one crore families, The Hans India reported. The government will pay the full premium through yearly budget provisions. Officials said the premium per person will depend on the final age limit, and the insurer has not been named in reports so far. Chief Secretary Sanjay Jaju has asked for enrolment centres in every gram panchayat and municipal ward, and wide publicity so that rural job guarantee workers and other poor households are not missed.

At the same Siddipet meeting, the Chief Minister is also starting distribution of Indiramma saris to women. For a similar state health cover story, see our report on [Delhi's Rs 10 lakh PM-JAY cover for transgender persons](/delhi-transgender-health-cover-rs-10-lakh/).

The enrolment window closes on December 18, which gives families about a month. Check the birth date on Aadhaar before the camp reaches your area, since that single detail decides who in the family can be insured.
