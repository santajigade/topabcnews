New Zealand on Wednesday, November 11, named a 14-player squad for the two-match Test series against India, with Tom Latham as captain. All-rounder Rachin Ravindra and opener Devon Conway have been included even though both are still recovering from injuries, as has wicketkeeper Tom Blundell. The first Test starts on November 19 at the Basin Reserve in Wellington, days after India took the ODI series 2-0.

## New Zealand's 14-Player Test Squad

The squad was named on the morning of November 11 (Indian time). Here is the full list:

- Tom Latham (captain)
- Tom Blundell (wicketkeeper)
- Devon Conway
- Jacob Duffy
- Matt Henry
- Kyle Jamieson
- Daryl Mitchell
- Henry Nicholls
- Glenn Phillips
- Rachin Ravindra
- Mitchell Santner
- Nathan Smith
- Blair Tickner
- Will Young

Jacob Duffy returns after missing the England tour earlier this year on paternity leave. Kane Williamson is not part of the squad. According to CricTracker, he retired midway through the England series in June 2026.

## Injury Watch: Ravindra, Conway and Blundell

Three key names are in the squad but still working towards full fitness for the opening Test:

| Player | Role | Injury update |
|---|---|---|
| Rachin Ravindra | All-rounder | Recovering from a shoulder injury |
| Devon Conway | Opener | Recovering from a concussion |
| Tom Blundell | Wicketkeeper | Managing a quadriceps injury |

Several fast bowlers are unavailable. Will O'Rourke is on a return-to-play programme, Zak Foulkes has a hip injury and Ben Sears has an ankle injury. That leaves Matt Henry, Kyle Jamieson, Jacob Duffy, Blair Tickner and Nathan Smith to carry the pace attack, with Mitchell Santner and Glenn Phillips offering spin.

The absence of O'Rourke, Foulkes and Sears means New Zealand will lean heavily on experienced seamers Henry and Jamieson. Duffy's return adds another option, and Tickner and Smith give the selectors cover if workloads need to be managed across two Tests played less than a week apart.

Head coach Rob Walter said the squad is largely the same group that played in England earlier this year. Former wicketkeeper BJ Watling has also joined the support staff as the new batting coach.

## India vs New Zealand Test Series Schedule

| Match | Dates | Venue |
|---|---|---|
| 1st Test | November 19 to 24, 2026 | Basin Reserve, Wellington |
| 2nd Test | November 27 to December 2, 2026 | Hagley Oval, Christchurch |

Both matches carry World Test Championship points. For New Zealand, the India series opens a busy home summer of ten Tests before March, which also includes four Tests against Australia in December and January and two against Sri Lanka in February. New Zealand beat England 2-1 in a Test series earlier in 2026.

## India's Test Squad Led by Shubman Gill

India named its Test squad for the tour on October 16. According to the <a href="https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/indias-odi-and-test-squads-for-new-zealand-tour-announced" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BCCI announcement</a>, Shubman Gill is captain and KL Rahul vice-captain. Rishabh Pant's place depends on a fitness clearance, and Dhruv Jurel is the second wicketkeeper in the group. The 16-member squad is:

Shubman Gill (C), KL Rahul (VC), Yashasvi Jaiswal, Devdutt Padikkal, Rishabh Pant (WK, subject to fitness clearance), Dhruv Jurel (WK), Ravindra Jadeja, Anshul Kamboj, Jasprit Bumrah, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep, Sai Sudharsan, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Washington Sundar, Kuldeep Yadav and Mohammed Siraj.

With Bumrah, Siraj, Prasidh Krishna and Akash Deep, India have a deep fast bowling group, while Jadeja, Sundar and Kuldeep give spin and batting depth.

## A Fitness Race Before Wellington

New Zealand's biggest worry in the next week is fitness. If Ravindra, Conway and Blundell are cleared, the hosts will have close to their best available batting line-up, though their pace attack is thinner than usual. India, fresh from an ODI series win, will look to make the most of that and collect valuable World Test Championship points.
