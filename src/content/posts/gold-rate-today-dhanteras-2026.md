Gold got cheaper on Dhanteras, Friday 6 November 2026. The India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA) rate stood at **Rs 14,102 a gram for 24 carat** and **Rs 12,918 a gram for 22 carat**, the purity most jewellery is made in. On MCX, gold futures traded at Rs 1,42,264 per 10 grams, down 1.27%, according to Business Today.

In the Delhi bullion market, 99.9% purity gold fell Rs 2,500 to Rs 1,46,300 per 10 grams (inclusive of taxes), PTI reported, citing the All India Sarafa Association. That is still about Rs 13,900 more than on Dhanteras 2025 (18 October), when the same gold cost Rs 1,32,400.

## Update (23 November 2026)

The Dhanteras dip turned into a much bigger fall. By the morning of 19 November, Business Today put 24 carat gold in Delhi at Rs 1,29,340 per 10 grams and 22 carat at Rs 1,18,562. MCX gold was at Rs 1,29,221.

Globally, spot gold slipped to about $3,723 an ounce on 19 November, near its lowest level since September 2025, <a href="https://www.khaleejtimes.com/gold-holds-near-14-month-low-as-fed-hike-bets-weigh" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Reuters reported</a>. Minutes of the US Federal Reserve's 27 and 28 October meeting showed officials worried they might need to do more on inflation, and the CME FedWatch tool priced a 90% chance of a quarter point rate hike in December. A strong dollar added to the pressure. Our report on [gold hitting a 14-month low after the Fed minutes](/gold-price-14-month-low-fed-minutes/) has the full background.

## Why buyers held back this Dhanteras

PTI reported weak demand in Delhi despite the festival. Jewellers saw little or no improvement in sales over last year, and customers were choosing lighter pieces because of high prices. In the international market, spot gold fell 0.5% to $4,068.71 an ounce that day.

This fits a wider trend. The World Gold Council found India's jewellery demand fell 20% by volume in the September quarter, even as its value rose 16% from a year earlier. In short, Indians are spending more money on gold but taking home fewer grams.

## How Your Jewellery Bill Is Actually Calculated

The rate in the newspaper is only the starting point. A shop bill has three parts: the gold value, the making charge and GST.

1. **Gold value** = weight in grams x the shop's 22 carat rate for the day.
2. **Making charge** = set by each jeweller, usually as a percentage of the gold value or a flat amount per gram. This is where bills differ most between shops.
3. **GST at 3%** on the total. The <a href="https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/sectoral-fq-gems-jewellery.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GST Council's jewellery FAQ</a> says 3% applies to the full transaction value of the jewellery, whether or not the making charge is shown separately. It is not 3% on gold plus 5% on making.

**Worked example.** A 10 gram 22 carat chain at the Dhanteras IBJA rate of Rs 12,918 a gram, with a making charge of 10% (an assumed figure for illustration; ask your jeweller for theirs):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gold value (10 g x Rs 12,918) | Rs 1,29,180 |
| Making charge (10%) | Rs 12,918 |
| Taxable value | Rs 1,42,098 |
| GST at 3% | Rs 4,263 |
| **Final bill** | **Rs 1,46,361** |

Each extra 1% of making charge adds about Rs 1,330 to this bill once GST is included. On a 10 gram piece, haggling the making charge down from 12% to 8% saves over Rs 5,300.

### Check the hallmark before you pay

According to the <a href="https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/hallmarking-faqs/hallmarking-faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Bureau of Indian Standards</a>, every hallmark since 1 July 2021 has three marks: the BIS logo, the purity (22K916 for 22 carat) and a six digit alphanumeric HUID code. You can check the HUID with the "Verify HUID" option in the BIS Care app. BIS charges jewellers Rs 45 per article for hallmarking gold, so it should not add much to your bill. Keep the itemised invoice; BIS says you will need it for any complaint.

## Gold Rate: Common Questions

### What was the gold rate on Dhanteras 2026?
The IBJA rate on 6 November 2026 was Rs 14,102 a gram for 24 carat and Rs 12,918 for 22 carat. In Delhi's bullion market, 99.9% gold closed at Rs 1,46,300 per 10 grams including taxes.

### Is GST on gold jewellery 3% or 5%?
For a jewellery purchase it is 3% on the whole value, including the making charge, as per the GST Council FAQ. The 5% rate applies to job work charges between a jeweller and a job worker, not to your retail bill.

### How do I check if my gold is genuine?
Look for the BIS logo, the purity mark (such as 22K916) and the six digit HUID on the piece. Enter the HUID in the BIS Care app under "Verify HUID" to confirm its details.

### Has gold become cheaper after Dhanteras?
Yes. By 19 November, 24 carat gold in Delhi was about Rs 12,910 per 10 grams cheaper than on Dhanteras, after the Fed minutes raised expectations of a US rate hike.
