Gold buyers got a pleasant surprise on Dhanteras this year. On Friday, November 6, 2026, gold prices in India fell sharply in morning trade, just as families headed out to jewellery shops for the festival. The price of 24 carat gold dropped to Rs 14,346 per gram, about 1.8% lower than the previous day, after a four day rally earlier in the week. The fall came from both the domestic market and global markets, where gold also traded lower.

Dhanteras is considered one of the most auspicious days of the year to buy gold, silver and utensils in India, so any change in price on this day is closely watched by households.

## Gold rate today in India (November 6, 2026)

Here are the national gold rates as reported on the morning of Dhanteras:

| Purity | Price per gram | Price per 10 grams | Change (10 grams) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 carat | Rs 14,346 | Rs 1,43,460 | Down Rs 2,560 |
| 22 carat | Rs 13,150 | Rs 1,31,500 | Down Rs 2,350 |
| 18 carat | Rs 10,759 | Rs 1,07,590 | Down Rs 1,930 |

For bulk buyers, 100 grams of 24 carat gold became cheaper by Rs 25,600, while 100 grams of 22 carat gold fell by Rs 23,500.

Most jewellery in India is made in 22 carat gold, so that is the rate most buyers should look at. Please remember that the final bill at a shop also includes making charges and GST, so what you pay will be higher than these base rates.

## Why gold prices fell today

The drop came after a strong run in the first week of November. 24 carat gold had climbed from Rs 14,288 per gram on November 1 to Rs 14,602 per gram on November 5. Some of that gain was given up on Dhanteras morning. Reports did not point to one single cause, but described the move as a correction after a sudden jump in prices on Thursday.

The futures market showed the same trend. On the Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX), gold for December delivery fell by up to Rs 1,900 to around Rs 1,42,150 per 10 grams. In the international market, Comex gold slipped 1.31% to about $4,059.90 per ounce.

Even after this fall, gold is still about 3.3% higher for the month so far, so prices remain close to record levels.

## Should you buy gold this Dhanteras?

For many families, buying a small amount of gold on Dhanteras is a tradition rather than an investment decision. A lower price on the day simply means a little more value for the same budget. Some market reports described today's dip as a chance for festive buyers.

If you are buying, keep these simple points in mind:

- **Check the hallmark.** Always buy BIS hallmarked jewellery and ask for the HUID code on the bill.
- **Compare making charges.** These can vary a lot between shops and can add a big amount to the final price.
- **Think about digital options.** If you only want gold as savings, options like gold ETFs or digital gold avoid making charges.
- **Do not overspend.** Buy within your budget, even if prices look attractive.

Traders should note that brokerage views are short term. One brokerage suggested a sell on rise strategy for MCX gold today, which shows that prices may stay volatile in the coming days.
