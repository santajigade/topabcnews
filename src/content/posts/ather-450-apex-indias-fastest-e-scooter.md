<h4>Ather 450 Apex: India’s Fastest E-Scooter Gets Even Smarter</h4>		<p>In <a href="https://topabcnews.com/category/automobile/">automobile</a> sector, Ather Energy reaffirmed its reputation for innovation with updates to the <strong>450 Apex</strong>, its high performance electric scooter. Alongside the original design features, </p><p><a href="https://www.atherenergy.com/apex" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ather</a> has rolled out a sophisticated cruise control system named <strong>Infinite Cruise </strong>that increases everyday usability without sacrificing the thrills for which the Apex is known.</p>			<h4>Pricing &amp; Availability</h4>		<ul><li><p>Originally launched at an ex-showroom price of approximately <strong>₹1.90 lakh</strong>, the Apex remains one of Ather’s most premium offerings.</p></li><li><p>Delhi on-road pricing is around <strong>₹1.96 lakh</strong>.</p></li><li><p>Bangalore listings show ex-showroom prices ranging between <strong>₹1.84 lakh to ₹1.89 lakh</strong>, depending on the source</p></li></ul>

										<h4>Ather 450 Apex Performance</h4>		<ul><li><p>The 450 Apex boasts a <strong>7 kW electric motor</strong> coupled with a <strong>3.7 kWh battery</strong>, enabling a <strong>top speed of 100 km/h, </strong>a notch above the 90 km/h of the 450X.</p></li><li><p>Acceleration is exhilarating: <strong>0–40 km/h</strong> in just <strong>2.9 seconds</strong>, thanks to enhanced drive-controller algorithms.</p></li><li><p>Ather claims an IDC-certified range of <strong>157 km</strong> per full charge, with Warp Plus mode sustaining real-world ranges of approx. <strong>75 km</strong>, while <strong>Smart Eco</strong> mode can stretch that to around <strong>110 km</strong>.</p></li><li><p>Charging takes <strong>5 hours 45 minutes</strong> at home and about <strong>1 km/min</strong> at Ather’s Grid fast-charging stations</p></li></ul><p><br />The standout new feature, <strong>Infinite Cruise</strong>, now brings cruise control across <strong>all speed ranges</strong>, kicking in from as low as <strong>10 km/h</strong>. Impressively, it automatically <strong>pauses in stop-and-go traffic</strong> and restarts when you start moving again. Additionally included are <strong>Hill Control</strong> (for climbs and descents) and <strong>Crawl Control</strong>, allowing steady cruising even off-road at speeds up to <strong>10 km/h</strong></p>													<img loading="lazy" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Ather-Apex-450-Price-1024x683.webp" alt="Ather 450 Apex" />																<h4>Design &amp;
