Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Part 1 has crossed the Rs 1,000 crore mark at the worldwide box office in just eight days. The Ranbir Kapoor led epic collected Rs 53.50 crore net in India on its eighth day, Thursday, November 12, taking its worldwide gross to Rs 1,033.55 crore, according to trade tracker Sacnilk figures reported by The Week and Business Today. The film now heads into its second weekend as one of the fastest Indian releases to reach the milestone.

## Ramayana Day 8 Collection in Detail

The second Thursday figure of Rs 53.50 crore net came from 24,042 shows across India. The Hindi version once again did most of the work, while the dubbed South Indian versions added a steady share.

| Language | Day 8 net (Rs crore) | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Hindi | 43.00 | 19,464 |
| Kannada | 3.25 | 922 |
| Malayalam | 2.80 | 977 |
| Telugu | 2.30 | 1,783 |
| Tamil | 2.15 | 896 |
| **Total** | **53.50** | **24,042** |

Overseas markets added about Rs 25 crore on the same day, The Week reported.

## Hindi and South Indian Versions Split

Across eight days, the Hindi version has collected Rs 437 crore net. The four dubbed versions together have crossed Rs 100 crore net in the first week, a strong sign for a Hindi film in southern markets.

- Hindi: Rs 437.00 crore
- Kannada: Rs 48.95 crore
- Telugu: Rs 38.05 crore
- Tamil: Rs 22.15 crore
- Malayalam: Rs 18.45 crore

The Kannada version is the strongest of the dubbed releases. The Week also reported that Ramayana has become the first Bollywood film to cross Rs 20 crore gross in Kerala.

## How Ramayana Compares With Pushpa 2 and Dhurandhar 2

Ramayana is only the fourth Indian film to cross Rs 500 crore net in India, and it took seven days to get there. Pushpa 2: The Rule did it in five days and Dhurandhar 2: The Revenge in six, while Baahubali 2: The Conclusion also took seven.

| Film | First week India net | Day 8 net |
|---|---|---|
| Ramayana: Part 1 | Rs 511.10 crore | Rs 53.50 crore |
| Pushpa 2: The Rule | Rs 688.35 crore (with paid previews) | Rs 37.45 crore |
| Dhurandhar 2: The Revenge | Rs 624.47 crore | Rs 49.70 crore |
| Baahubali 2: The Conclusion | Rs 539 crore | Not reported |

While the first-week totals of Pushpa 2 and Dhurandhar 2 are higher, Ramayana's day 8 figure is better than both, which points to stronger holding power. The Week noted that it also did not have the advantage of an earlier hit instalment or big paid preview numbers, unlike those two sequels. Its overseas earnings over eight days are also ahead of both films.

On the worldwide chart, Business Today reported that only ten other Indian films have crossed Rs 1,000 crore. Ramayana is still behind the lifetime totals of Kalki 2898 AD (Rs 1,042.25 crore) and Pathaan (Rs 1,055 crore), both of which also reached the mark faster, and it is now less than Rs 22 crore short of the Pathaan figure.

## Second Weekend Outlook for Ramayana

Advance bookings for day 9, the second Friday, stood at Rs 23.45 crore gross (Rs 17.98 crore without block seats), according to Sacnilk data cited by Business Today. Maharashtra led with Rs 3.77 crore, followed by Karnataka at Rs 2.93 crore and Delhi at Rs 2.69 crore. Among cities, NCR, Bengaluru and Mumbai were the top three.

The film stars Ranbir Kapoor, with Sai Pallavi as Sita and Yash in a key role. ## A Strong Hold Into Week Two

Crossing Rs 1,000 crore worldwide in eight days puts Ramayana: Part 1 among the biggest Indian releases of all time. With bookings for the second weekend looking solid, the next question is how far it can climb up the all-time list in the coming days.
