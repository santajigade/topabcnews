Indian vehicle dealers sold 31,72,466 vehicles in October 2026, down 23.8% from 41,63,555 in October 2025, according to retail data released by the Federation of Automobile Dealers Associations (FADA) on 6 November. Against September 2026, though, sales rose 25.05%. The main reason for the yearly fall is the festival calendar. In 2025, Navratri, Dhanteras and Diwali all fell in October. This year Navratri stayed in October, but Dhanteras (6 November) and Diwali (8 November) moved into November.

Electric vehicles went the other way. About 3.55 lakh EVs were sold, an all-time high and 44.88% more than a year earlier, which took the EV share of all vehicle retail to about 11.5% from roughly 6%.

## Update (24 November 2026)

FADA has not yet released November 2026 figures. Last year the November numbers were reported on 8 December 2025, so this year's data should arrive in early December. Early festive signals came from carmakers. Maruti Suzuki said it had retailed over 4 lakh vehicles between 14 September and 6 November, against 3.46 lakh in the same period last year, Deccan Herald reported. Tata Motors expected about 12,000 deliveries on Dhanteras and Hyundai about 16,000.

## Segment-Wise Retail Sales in October 2026

Every segment grew over September. Year on year, two-wheelers and cars fell while commercial vehicles, three-wheelers, tractors and construction equipment grew. Figures are from the <a href="https://www.fada.in/images/press-release/16aed49cca8e14FADA_Press_Release_Oct_26.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">FADA October 2026 retail data release</a>.

| Segment | October 2026 | October 2025 | Change (YoY) | Change (vs Sept) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-wheelers | 23,21,419 | 32,39,949 | -28.35% | +29.67% |
| Three-wheelers | 1,41,420 | 1,36,025 | +3.97% | +6.68% |
| Passenger vehicles | 5,07,768 | 5,92,032 | -14.23% | +18.86% |
| Commercial vehicles | 1,15,140 | 1,12,561 | +2.29% | +11.19% |
| Tractors | 79,285 | 76,898 | +3.10% | +3.09% |
| Construction equipment | 7,434 | 6,090 | +22.07% | +14.62% |
| **Total** | **31,72,466** | **41,63,555** | **-23.80%** | **+25.05%** |

FADA President Sai Giridhar said the yearly decline is "almost entirely optical". By FADA's count this was still the second highest October ever for total retail. Commercial vehicles, three-wheelers and tractors each had their best October on record, according to <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/auto/auto-retail-sales-fall-23-8-in-october-to-3-172-million-units-fada-126110600528_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard's report on the FADA data</a>. Inside commercial vehicles, heavy trucks rose 28.95% and medium trucks 21.16%, while light commercial vehicles fell 9.57%.

## Why Rural Two-Wheeler Buyers Waited

Two-wheelers make up almost three quarters of the market, so their 28% drop pulled the total down. The fall was steeper in villages: rural two-wheeler sales dropped about 34% and urban sales about 21%. FADA's reading is that rural families held their purchases for Dhanteras and Diwali, the traditional days to bring home a new vehicle, and it expects rural and northern markets to lead November. Dealers also mentioned local rural stress linked to weak rainfall and a poor kharif season in some areas, and said last year's GST price advantage has faded after manufacturers raised prices.

## Who Sold the Most Cars

Passenger vehicle retail fell 14.23% to 5,07,768 units, which FADA called the third best October on record. Utility vehicles held up demand, while entry level cars were weaker. Market shares, as reported by <a href="https://www.autocarpro.in/analysis-sales/passenger-vehicle-retail-sales-fall-1423-percent-to-508-lakh-in-october-fada-135776" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Autocar Professional</a>:

| Tata Motors | 73,449 | 14.47% | 13.52% |
| Mahindra & Mahindra | 64,982 | 12.80% | 12.46% |

Tata and Mahindra gained share, and Toyota lost the most. Among smaller brands, VinFast retailed 2,571 cars against 142 a year earlier.

The fuel mix in cars is shifting. Petrol's share fell to 41.60% from 48.96%. CNG rose to 24.59%, hybrids to 9.94% and electric to 7.30%, together 41.83% of car sales, just ahead of petrol. Diesel slipped to 16.57%.

## EVs: Demand Ahead of Supply

Electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, cars and commercial vehicles all set records in October. The EV share by segment:

- Three-wheelers: 62.63%
- Two-wheelers: 9.65%
- Passenger vehicles: 7.30% (about 37,000 cars, up from about 21,000)
- Commercial vehicles: 4.29%

Giridhar said availability, not demand, limited EV sales during the month. For recent EV launches, see our reports on the [Mercedes GLC Electric](/mercedes-glc-electric-launched-india/) and the [Ather 450 Apex](/ather-450-apex-indias-fastest-e-scooter/).

## What November Has to Show

The cleanest test of FADA's calendar argument is to compare the two festive months together. Here is the arithmetic using reported figures:

| | Vehicles retailed |
|---|---|
| October 2025 | 41,63,555 |
| November 2025 | Just over 33 lakh (up 2.14% YoY) |
| October plus November 2025 | About 74.6 lakh |
| October 2026 | 31,72,466 |
| November 2026 needed to match last year's two months | Roughly 43 lakh |

So November 2026 would have to beat last November by around 10 lakh vehicles, close to 30%, for the 2026 festive season to equal 2025's. Anything less would suggest some demand really was lost, not just delayed. The 2025 base was also lifted by the GST rate cuts that took effect on 22 September 2025.

Dealers are cautiously hopeful. In FADA's survey, 51.65% expect growth in November, 34.71% expect flat sales and 13.64% expect a decline. For November 2026 to January 2027, 50.41% expect growth. About 31% said the first repo rate increase in over three years is affecting buyers' loan costs.

Car stock at dealers stayed at 43 to 45 days, unchanged from September even as factory dispatches hit records. Giridhar called inventory the "pressing concern" and expects it to fall to about 30 days by the end of December. For buyers, high stock can mean year-end offers on some cars, while popular EVs may carry waiting periods.

## FADA Vehicle Sales: Common Questions

### How many vehicles were sold in India in October 2026?
Dealers retailed 31,72,466 vehicles, FADA data shows. That is 23.8% lower than October 2025 but 25.05% higher than September 2026.

### Why did vehicle sales fall in October 2026?
Mostly because Dhanteras and Diwali moved to November this year, while in 2025 they fell in October. Last October was also lifted by demand after the GST rate cuts of September 2025.

### Which company sold the most cars in October 2026?
Maruti Suzuki, with 2,11,996 retail units and a 41.75% share. Tata Motors was second with 73,449 units.

### When will FADA release November 2026 sales data?
FADA has not given a date. Last year's November figures were reported on 8 December 2025, so early December is likely.
