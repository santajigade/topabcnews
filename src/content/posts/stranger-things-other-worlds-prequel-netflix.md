Netflix has confirmed that the world of Stranger Things is not finished yet. The streamer has announced Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, a live-action prequel series set in 1955 in the small desert town of Marion, Nevada, nearly three decades before the original show. The news came on November 6, the date fans celebrate as Stranger Things Day, along with a short teaser. The series is set to premiere in 2028.

The project brings back creators Matt and Ross Duffer, with longtime series writer Kate Trefry leading the show and filmmaker Joe Wright directing its opening episodes.

## What the Other Worlds Teaser Shows

The first teaser runs for just over half a minute and gives away very little. According to ABC News, it opens on a bicycle lying on its side, with a radio on the handlebars playing the song "Sh-Boom" by The Crew Cuts. The camera then tilts up to a cloudy night sky, which is torn open by a sudden flash of light and an explosion.

The screen cuts to black, and a title card reads "April 19, 1955, Marion, Nevada." A slowed-down version of the song plays as the clip ends. TheWrap noted that the teaser hints at what may be another version of the Upside Down, the dark parallel world at the heart of the original series.

## Who Is Making the Stranger Things Prequel

The creative team mixes familiar names from the franchise with new talent.

| Role | Name |
|---|---|
| Creators | Matt and Ross Duffer, Kate Trefry |
| Showrunner | Kate Trefry |
| Director (first three episodes) | Joe Wright |
| Executive producers | The Duffer Brothers (Upside Down Pictures), Shawn Levy (21 Laps) |
| Filming location | New Mexico |
| Setting | Marion, Nevada, April 1955 |
| Release | 2028 on Netflix |

Trefry was a writer on the original series and also wrote Stranger Things: The First Shadow, the stage play that explores the franchise's backstory. Wright is known for films such as Atonement, Darkest Hour and Pride & Prejudice, and is a BAFTA and Golden Globe winner, according to ABC News.

In a statement, the Duffer Brothers said, "We've been dreaming of 'Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure' for many years now." They added that casting is the next step.

## Will Eleven or Vecna Appear in Other Worlds?

No cast members have been announced yet. Executive producer Shawn Levy has said the show will have "all-new characters, themes and mythology," according to TheWrap. Netflix executive Jinny Howe has described the series as bringing new heroes to the 1950s.

That suggests fans should not expect a return of Eleven, Mike, Dustin or the rest of the Hawkins gang. Gizmodo has suggested the show could explore events before the villain Vecna gained his powers, a backstory also covered in The First Shadow, but Netflix has not confirmed any connection to that character.

## How Other Worlds Fits Into the Stranger Things Franchise

Stranger Things first arrived on Netflix in 2016 and grew into one of the streamer's biggest hits. TheWrap reported that Seasons 1 to 4 have crossed 1.2 billion views, and that the fifth and final season delivered Netflix's biggest premiere week ever for an English-language title.

Since the main story ended, Netflix has been expanding the brand in several directions:

- **Stranger Things: Tales From '85**, an animated series
- **Stranger Things: The First Shadow**, a Tony Award-winning stage production
- Live fan experiences at Netflix House venues
- A range of tie-in books

Gizmodo counts Other Worlds as the franchise's fourth spin-off. Stranger Things Day itself falls on November 6 because that is the date in 1983 when the character Will Byers disappeared in the very first episode.

The news also shows how studios are leaning on proven names to draw audiences. ## A Long Wait Before the Return to the Upside Down

With filming planned in New Mexico and casting yet to begin, fans face a wait of more than a year before the prequel arrives. For now, the teaser's single date, April 19, 1955, is the main clue to what lies ahead, and casting news will be the next big moment for fans to watch.
