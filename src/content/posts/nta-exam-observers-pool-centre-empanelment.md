The National Testing Agency (NTA), which conducts NEET UG, JEE Main, CUET and UGC NET, is setting up a permanent, pre-verified pool of independent observers for its exams. It has also invited schools, colleges and other institutions to apply to become regular exam centres. Both moves were made through two Expressions of Interest (EOIs), with applications open from November 7 to December 6, 2026, as reported by PTI on November 12. The overhaul follows the NEET UG question paper leak earlier this year, which led to nationwide student protests.

## Why NTA Is Changing How Exams Are Watched

Until now, observers were arranged exam by exam. Under the new system, NTA wants a ready list of checked individuals so that deployment orders can be issued well in advance. Each observer will act as NTA's own representative at a centre, separate from the City Coordinator and the Centre Superintendent, and will report directly to the agency.

According to a PTI report, the NEET UG leak also led to the resignation of the then Union education minister Dharmendra Pradhan. A day later, Prime Minister Narendra Modi set up a high-powered task force headed by Nandan Nilekani to recommend ways to leak-proof exams. That task force has not yet submitted its report. ## Who Can Become an NTA Observer

Applications are made online on the <a href="https://observermanagement.nta.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NTA observer management portal</a>. As reported by Careers360, applicants fall into three groups:

| Category | Who is eligible | Age limit |
|---|---|---|
| A | Retired Central or state government officers above Pay Level 8 (7th CPC) | 60 to 65 years |
| B | Retired armed forces and CAPF personnel of Subedar Major rank or equivalent and above | Up to 65 years |
| C | Serving or retired teachers and professors of government schools, colleges and universities | Retired: 60 to 65; serving: no upper limit, NOC needed |

All applicants must be physically fit, willing to travel outside their headquarters, and free of pending disciplinary or vigilance proceedings. Empanelment is valid for two years, subject to satisfactory conduct, and being on the panel does not guarantee duty in every exam cycle.

For centres outside India, NTA will generally pick Indian nationals living in the same city, such as serving or retired officials of Indian missions and retired Ministry of External Affairs officers.

### Observer Pay and Duties

Observers will get Rs 3,000 for the briefing day (Rs 2,000 fee plus Rs 1,000 conveyance) and Rs 4,000 for the exam day (Rs 3,000 fee plus Rs 1,000 conveyance). Travel and stay for outstation duty are reimbursed separately.

Their duties include attending a compulsory briefing a day before the exam and checking identity verification, frisking, seating, handling of question papers, CCTV and webcasting, and power backup. Any lapse or malpractice must be reported straight to NTA.

## New Rules for Exam Centres

The second EOI invites government, aided and private schools, colleges, universities, institutes of national importance, polytechnics, ITIs, government skill centres and testing service providers to apply as centres. Applications go through the EOI portal linked on the <a href="https://nta.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NTA website</a>.

- **Computer based test centres:** 285 working computers per shift, including buffer, arranged in at least four separate labs of 30 workstations each. Smaller centres may apply but will be considered only where supply is limited.
- **Pen and paper centres:** 250 seats per shift, including buffer, in at least six halls of 40 candidates each or an equivalent layout.
- **Shift length:** Centres must be able to run a single three-hour shift.
- **Ownership:** The applicant must own or operate the facility or hold legally enforceable rights to use it.

Meeting these minimums does not guarantee selection.

## Key Dates and Facts

- Applications opened: November 7, 2026
- Last date: December 6, 2026
- Exams covered: NEET UG, JEE Main, CUET UG and PG, UGC NET, CSIR NET and others
- Observer panel validity: two years

## What It Means for Students

For candidates, the changes will not alter the syllabus or exam pattern. What they aim to change is the security on exam day, with trained observers who answer only to NTA and centres that meet fixed standards. Careers360 noted that the notice does not confirm whether NEET UG 2027 will be held online or on paper, as a proposed shift to computer based testing is still under review. Students should follow official NTA notices for final exam rules.
