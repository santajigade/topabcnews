India's retail inflation climbed to 6.09% in October 2026, up from 5.54% in September, according to provisional data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) on Thursday, November 12. The reading is the highest in about two years and pushes inflation past the Reserve Bank of India's upper tolerance limit of 6%. Food prices did most of the damage, with food inflation rising to 8.94%. The figure arrives just weeks before the RBI's next policy meeting, and several economists now expect a second interest rate hike in December.

## How Much Prices Rose in October

The headline Consumer Price Index (CPI), which uses 2024 as its base year, rose 55 basis points in a single month. According to an ANI report carried by The Tribune, this is the highest level since October 2024, when inflation stood at 6.21%.

Both villages and cities felt the pressure, but rural India saw a sharper rise.

| Measure | September 2026 | October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| All India CPI inflation | 5.54% | 6.09% |
| Rural CPI inflation | 6.08% | 6.63% |
| Urban CPI inflation | 4.88% | 5.41% |
| Food inflation (all India) | 7.74% | 8.94% |
| Rural food inflation | 8.06% | 9.09% |
| Urban food inflation | 7.18% | 8.68% |

September figures are from the official <a href="https://www.mospi.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MoSPI CPI release</a> issued in October. October figures are provisional and may be revised next month.

According to Xinhua, prices were collected from 1,407 urban markets, including online markets, and 1,465 villages across all states, with National Sample Survey Office field staff visiting on a weekly schedule.

## Onion, Garlic and Ginger Drive the Food Bill Higher

The kitchen basket was the biggest driver. Onion prices were 116.22% higher than a year ago in October, up from 108.13% in September. Garlic inflation rose to 60.74% from 54.62%, while ginger (48.32%) and lemon (47.30%) also posted steep annual increases.

The broader food and beverages category rose 8.51% nationally. The RBI has pointed to supply problems behind the jump, including depleted stocks of stored onion and uneven rainfall that has affected the arrival of fresh kharif crops in markets.

Food is not the only pressure point. Two service-heavy groups also ran well above the headline rate:

- Personal care, social protection and miscellaneous goods and services: 10.14%
- Restaurants and accommodation services: 10.06%

For a household, this means the rise is showing up both in the vegetable market and in eating out or staying at a hotel.

## Why the RBI December Meeting Now Matters More

The RBI targets CPI inflation of 4%, with a band of 2% to 6%. A reading above 6% puts the central bank outside its comfort zone.

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) already raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% at its October 7 review, in a unanimous decision, and moved to a stance described as "calibrated tightening". The next MPC meeting is scheduled for December 2 to 4, 2026.

Economists quoted by Argus mostly read the October data as support for another hike:

- **Radhika Rao, DBS Bank:** Said inflation has not yet peaked, with an unfavourable base effect adding pressure, and expects a second successive rate hike in December along with a higher RBI inflation projection.
- **Tanay Dalal, Axis Bank:** Said the data raises the risk that policy rates may need to go above the estimated neutral rate of about 6%.
- **Anil Gupta, PHDCCI:** Said the food component needs close monitoring because of its heavy weight in the consumption basket.

The global setting also points in the same direction. The US Federal Reserve raised its benchmark rate by 25 basis points in September, and US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October. ## What Higher Inflation Means for Your Loans and Savings

If the RBI raises the repo rate again in December, banks usually pass on the higher cost over the following weeks and months. Borrowers with floating rate home loans, car loans and personal loans linked to an external benchmark could see their EMIs or loan tenures go up.

On the other side, a tighter rate cycle often brings better fixed deposit rates for savers. Senior citizens and others who depend on interest income may find new FD offers more attractive than in the last few years.

For everyday budgets, the bigger concern is food. With onion prices more than double their level of a year ago, families may feel the pinch most in daily cooking costs until fresh crop arrivals improve supply. Readers can track official data and policy statements on the <a href="https://www.rbi.org.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Reserve Bank of India website</a>.

## Key Highlights

- CPI inflation for October 2026: 6.09%, up from 5.54% in September
- Highest since October 2024 and above the RBI's 6% upper limit
- Food inflation: 8.94%, up from 7.74%
- Rural inflation 6.63%, urban 5.41%
- Onion prices up 116.22% year on year
- Repo rate now 5.50% after an October hike; next MPC meeting on December 2 to 4

## What to Watch Before December

MoSPI releases CPI data on the 12th of each month, so the November number will come after the RBI's policy meeting, so the October data is the last full reading the MPC will see before it decides. Kharif crop arrivals, onion supply and global energy prices will shape whether inflation cools in the coming months or keeps climbing. For now, the data has turned the December meeting into one of the most closely watched policy decisions of the year.
