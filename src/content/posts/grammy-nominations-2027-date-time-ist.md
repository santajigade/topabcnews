The race for music's biggest prize enters its next stage on Monday. The Recording Academy will reveal the full list of nominees for the 69th Grammy Awards on November 16, 2026, in a live presentation from Times Square in New York. For fans in India, the announcement begins in the early evening on Monday, with the complete list going online about an hour and a half later. The winners will be named at the ceremony in Los Angeles on February 7, 2027, which falls on the morning of February 8 in India.

## Grammy Nominations Time in IST

The Academy has given approximate start times in US Eastern Time. Here is how they convert for Indian viewers.

| Event | US Eastern Time | India (IST) |
|---|---|---|
| Live nominations presentation begins | 7:45 am, Mon Nov 16 | 6:15 pm, Mon Nov 16 |
| Full nominations list published and wrap-up show | 9:00 am, Mon Nov 16 | 7:30 pm, Mon Nov 16 |
| 69th Grammy Awards ceremony | 7:00 pm, Sun Feb 7, 2027 | 5:30 am, Mon Feb 8, 2027 |

The times are approximate, so it is worth tuning in a few minutes early.

## Who Will Announce the Nominees

This year's reveal moves to The Square in Times Square, the first time the nominations presentation has been broadcast live from that spot, according to the <a href="https://www.grammy.com/news/grammy-nominees-to-be-unveiled-monday-nov-16/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Recording Academy's announcement</a>. Grammys CEO Harvey Mason jr. and Recording Academy Board of Trustees Chair Dr. Chelsey Green will host it.

A wide mix of artists from different genres will read out the categories:

- Olivia Dean, Kehlani and Pharrell Williams
- David Guetta, Juanes and YUNGBLUD
- Herbie Hancock, Alan Menken and Renee Fleming
- Carly Pearce, Linda Perry, Amy Allen and Ziggy Marley
- Israel Houghton, FYUTCH and Aura V, and Meghan Foley and Michelle Holme

## How to Watch From India

The presentation will stream live on Grammy.com and the official Grammys YouTube channel, both of which can be accessed from India. In the US, parts of the morning will also air on ABC's Good Morning America. That tie-up reflects a bigger change: from 2027 the Grammy Awards ceremony moves to ABC, with streaming on Disney+ and Hulu. It is the first time the show has aired on ABC since 1972.

### Key 2027 Grammy dates

- Eligibility period: August 31, 2025 to August 28, 2026
- First-round voting: October 12 to 22, 2026
- Nominations announced: November 16, 2026
- Final-round voting: December 10, 2026 to January 7, 2027
- Ceremony: February 7, 2027, at Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

The full schedule is set out in the <a href="https://www.grammy.com/news/2027-grammys-show-air-date-nominations-announced/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official 2027 Grammys timeline</a>. Only music released within the eligibility window can compete, which is why some big releases from late 2026 will have to wait for the 2028 awards.

## Big Names in the Running

Nominations are decided by the Academy's voting members, who are music professionals. Before that, labels and artists submit their work for consideration. Submission is not the same as a nomination, but it shows who is in the race. According to a Forbes round-up of submissions in the main General Field categories, entries include:

- Bruno Mars with the album *The Romantic* and the single "I Just Might"
- Olivia Rodrigo with *you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love* and "the cure"
- Charli XCX with *Music, Fashion, Film*
- Lady Gaga with "RUNWAY", featuring Doechii
- Kacey Musgraves, Noah Kahan, Mitski, Phoebe Bridgers and Karol G with their latest projects
- Ella Langley and Don Toliver, who were also entered for Best New Artist
- KATSEYE with "Animal"

## Indian Names Among the Submissions

There is some interest for Indian listeners too. Forbes listed violin maestro Dr. L. Subramaniam among Record of the Year submissions with "Shanti Priya - Ballet (Live)". Other entries with clear Indian links include the album *Festivo*, submitted for Album of the Year and Best Global Music Album with references to Diwali and Onam, and songs such as "Dil Jale" in the Best Asian Pop Music Performance category. Whether any of these turn into nominations will only be clear on Monday evening.

## What to Expect on Monday Evening

The live presentation will be followed by the full list across all categories, from the major General Field awards such as Album of the Year, Record of the Year, Song of the Year and Best New Artist to the genre awards. Expect social media to light up quickly with reactions to the top nominees, surprise snubs and first-time nominees.

Award season has already begun elsewhere. Spanish star Rosalia took Album of the Year at the Latin Grammys this week, as covered in our report on the <a href="/latin-grammys-2026-rosalia-lux-album-of-year/">Latin Grammys 2026 winners</a>. Monday's announcement will show whether any of those Latin music stars, and the global pop names above, carry their momentum into the 69th Grammy Awards.
