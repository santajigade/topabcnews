The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) is filling 1,260 posts under Advertisement No. 06/2026: 1,075 Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) jobs across nine Delhi government offices and 185 Junior Engineer (Electrical) jobs in five bodies, including NDMC, MCD and Delhi Transco. The online form opened on November 12 and closes at 11:59 PM on December 11, 2026. The MTS posts need only a Class 10 pass.

| Qualification | Matriculation (Class 10) | Degree or diploma in Electrical Engineering |

The board issued the notice on November 6. Age, qualification and experience are counted as on December 11, 2026, the closing date. DSSSB calls the vacancy numbers "tentative", so they could still change.

These numbers include horizontal reservation. The general duty posts include 30 seats for persons with benchmark disabilities (PwBD), 76 for ex-servicemen and 38 for meritorious sports persons. The Aaya posts include 8 PwBD, 24 ex-servicemen and 12 sports seats.

No work experience is needed for any MTS post. Most departments ask for Class 10 or an equivalent pass. DUSIB also accepts an ITI pass, and its recruits must finish an induction training of at least two weeks. Departmental candidates in several offices, including the Directorate of Education, can be up to 40 years old.

Freshers with an electrical diploma should look at DTL and DUSIB first, because they ask for no experience. DTL also pays more than the other four bodies: Level 8, Rs 52,000 to Rs 1,51,100, against Level 6 at NDMC, MCD and DUSIB. NDMC's upper age limit of 32 is the highest among the JE posts. Government servants at MCD and DUSIB get up to five years of age relaxation, and contract workers in the same department can get a one-time relaxation for the time they have served, up to five years.

## Fee, Exam Pattern and Qualifying Marks

The fee is Rs 100, paid only through SBI e-pay, and it is not refundable. Women, SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen candidates pay nothing. Ex-servicemen who already hold a regular civil job after using the ex-servicemen quota do not get the waiver.

The MTS exam is a single tier: 200 multiple choice questions for 200 marks in two hours. It has five sections of 40 marks each: general awareness, reasoning, arithmetic and numerical ability, Hindi, and English.

The JE exam has two tiers. Tier I has 200 questions in two hours: 100 on the same five general sections and 100 on electrical engineering. It is used for shortlisting. Tier II has 150 questions for 200 marks in two and a half hours.

Wrong answers cost 0.25 marks on a 1-mark question and 0.50 marks on a 2-mark question. To qualify, General and EWS candidates need 40%, OBC (Delhi) candidates need 35%, and SC, ST and PwBD candidates need 30%. Ex-servicemen get a further 5% relaxation within their category, but cannot go below 30%. Exam dates have not been announced yet. The board says it will post them on its website.

## How to Apply on dsssbonline.nic.in

1. Go to <a href="https://dsssbonline.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the DSSSB online application portal</a> and register. Registration is done once, and the same user ID and password work for later DSSSB exams.
2. Log in, choose Advertisement No. 06/2026 and the post code: 810/26 for MTS or 809/26 for JE.
3. Fill in your education and category details as they stand on December 11, 2026.
4. Upload your photo and signature as the portal asks.
5. Pay Rs 100 through SBI e-pay if you are not exempt, then submit and save a copy.

Only one application per candidate is allowed, and DSSSB says duplicate registrations will be cancelled. You choose your preferred departments later, at the e-dossier stage, and those choices cannot be changed afterwards. ## DSSSB MTS 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date to apply for DSSSB MTS 2026?**
December 11, 2026, until 11:59 PM. The link is disabled after that.

**What is the age limit for DSSSB MTS?**
18 to 27 years as on December 11, 2026. Departmental candidates in several offices can apply up to 40 years.

**Can a 10th pass apply for DSSSB MTS?**
Yes. Most MTS posts need only Class 10 or an equivalent pass, with no work experience.

**How much is the DSSSB MTS application fee?**
Rs 100. Women, SC, ST, PwBD and eligible ex-servicemen candidates pay no fee.
