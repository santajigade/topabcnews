Milk has become costlier for millions of households in Delhi and the National Capital Region from today, Thursday, November 12, 2026. Mother Dairy, one of the largest milk suppliers in the region, has raised the price of its liquid milk by Rs 2 to Rs 4 per litre across variants. The company has blamed higher fodder, cattle feed, packaging and transport costs. This is its second price revision in six months, after a Rs 2 per litre hike on May 14.

The change was announced on Wednesday and applies to pouched and bulk vended milk sold in Delhi-NCR. Full cream milk sees the steepest jump, while toned and double toned milk go up by Rs 2 a litre.

## Mother Dairy New Milk Rates From November 12

Here are the old and new prices per litre, as reported by news agencies ANI and PTI.

| Variant | Old price (Rs per litre) | New price (Rs per litre) | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full cream milk | 72 | 76 | Rs 4 |
| Pro Milk | 72 | 76 | Rs 4 |
| Cow milk | 62 | 65 | Rs 3 |
| Toned milk (pouch) | 60 | 62 | Rs 2 |
| Bulk vended (token) milk | 58 | 60 | Rs 2 |
| Double toned milk (Live Lite) | 54 | 56 | Rs 2 |

In percentage terms, full cream milk is up about 5.6% and toned milk about 3.3%. Buyers of half litre pouches should check the printed MRP from today, as the reports listed rates per litre.

## Why Mother Dairy Says Prices Had to Rise

According to the company, an uneven monsoon has hurt the supply of fodder for dairy animals. Green and dry fodder prices have climbed by up to 20%, and cattle feed has become around 10% more expensive over the past year. The company also said packaging, transport and other operating costs have risen by as much as 25%.

A Mother Dairy spokesperson said the company "remains committed to balancing the interests of both farmers and consumers." The company says it passes nearly 75 to 80% of what it earns from milk sales back to farmers as procurement payments. PTI described the revision as only a partial transfer of the higher costs to buyers.

## Second Hike Since May: How Much More Families Now Pay

The latest increase comes on top of the May 14 revision, when <a href="https://www.motherdairy.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Mother Dairy</a> raised all liquid milk variants by Rs 2 a litre, citing a roughly 6% rise in farmer procurement costs. Amul also raised its fresh pouch milk prices by Rs 2 a litre across India on the same day in May.

Put together, the two revisions have pushed full cream milk from Rs 70 to Rs 76 a litre in Delhi-NCR within six months. Toned milk has gone from Rs 58 to Rs 62.

### What it means for a typical household

For a family buying one litre of full cream milk every day, the November change adds about Rs 120 a month (Rs 4 multiplied by 30 days). Compared with prices before May, the extra cost is now about Rs 180 a month. A household using one litre of toned milk daily will pay about Rs 60 more a month from today.

The impact can be larger for tea stalls, sweet shops and small eateries, which buy milk in bulk. Some of them may choose to pass the extra cost on to customers through higher prices for tea, sweets and other milk based items.

## Key Highlights of the Milk Price Revision

- Effective date: Thursday, November 12, 2026
- Region: Delhi-NCR
- Increase: Rs 2 to Rs 4 per litre depending on the variant
- Full cream and Pro Milk: Rs 72 to Rs 76
- Cow milk: Rs 62 to Rs 65
- Toned, double toned and token milk: up Rs 2 each
- Reasons given: costlier fodder, cattle feed, packaging and transport
- Previous revision: May 14, 2026 (Rs 2 per litre)

## Dairy Costs and India's Food Inflation Picture

Milk is one of the most widely consumed food items in India, so its price closely affects family budgets. The timing is notable because food inflation had already picked up before this hike. According to the <a href="https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestreleasesfiles/1791801549227-Press%20Release%20for%20the%20month%20of%20September,%202026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official CPI data from the Ministry of Statistics</a>, all India retail inflation rose to 5.54% in September 2026 from 4.83% in August, while food inflation climbed to 7.74% from 5.97%.

The ministry is scheduled to release the October CPI numbers today, November 12. The November milk revision will only show up in data for later months. Other dairy products have also become costlier in recent weeks. PTI reported that Amul raised the price of its 1 kg ghee carton from Rs 630 to Rs 680 earlier this month.

## Will Other Dairy Brands Follow?

So far, the confirmed revision is only from Mother Dairy and only for Delhi-NCR. There is no official word yet from Amul or other cooperatives about a fresh milk price change in November. In the past, brands have often moved close together, as seen in May when both Amul and Mother Dairy raised prices on the same day. Consumers in other cities should watch for official announcements from their local dairy brands rather than rely on social media messages.

With fodder and feed costs high after an uneven monsoon, dairy companies say they are under pressure from both farmers and buyers. For families in Delhi-NCR, the immediate effect is simple: the daily milk packet now costs Rs 2 to Rs 4 more per litre than it did yesterday.
