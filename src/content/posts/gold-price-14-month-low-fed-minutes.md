Gold has slipped to its weakest level in more than a year. On Wednesday, November 18, spot gold dropped 3.2% to $3,719.55 an ounce, the lowest since September 2025, after minutes of the US Federal Reserve's October meeting showed that almost all officials expect more interest rate hikes. In India, gold and silver futures on MCX had already fallen sharply in Wednesday morning trade, and the overnight slide abroad adds fresh pressure as markets open on Thursday.

For Indian households, the bigger picture matters more than one day's move. Gold is now far below the record levels it touched earlier in 2026, and also below where it stood on Dhanteras less than two weeks ago.

## What the Fed Minutes Said

The Fed held rates steady at its October meeting after raising them in September for the first time since July 2023. The minutes, released at 19:00 GMT on November 18 (12:30 am IST on November 19), showed that the pause was not an easy decision.

- Some officials described the decision to hold as "a close call".
- Some members wanted a 25 basis point hike in October itself, arguing that policy was not tight enough and inflation was still too high.
- Others warned that waiting too long could let high inflation settle in, or force sharper hikes later.

October consumer price data had also come in hotter than expected. You can read our earlier report on [US inflation in October and rising Fed hike odds](/us-cpi-october-2026-inflation-fed-hike/) for those numbers.

After the minutes, the CME FedWatch tool put the probability of a December hike to a 4.00% to 4.25% range at 91.7%, according to InvestmentNews. Several Fed officials had already signalled more tightening before the minutes came out. FXEmpire reported that Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan called for at least two more quarter point increases.

## Why Rate Hikes Hurt Gold

Gold does not pay interest. When the Fed raises rates, bonds and deposits pay more, so holding gold becomes more costly in terms of income given up. Higher US rates also tend to lift the dollar, and since gold is priced in dollars, a stronger dollar makes it more expensive for buyers using other currencies, including the rupee.

Both forces were at work this week. Finimize noted that the dollar strengthened and Treasury yields rose after the minutes, and that silver, platinum and palladium also fell as traders treated precious metals as one rate sensitive trade. Once gold broke lower, stop loss orders and trend following funds may have added to the selling.

## What This Means for Indian Buyers and Investors

**Wedding and festive shoppers.** For families buying jewellery for weddings, lower prices mean a fixed budget now buys more gold than it did in early November. Retail jewellery rates follow the futures market but also include making charges and GST, so check the bill carefully and always ask for BIS hallmarking with an HUID code. Our [Dhanteras gold rate report](/gold-rate-today-dhanteras-2026/) shows what per gram rates looked like on November 6 for comparison.

**Investors in gold ETFs and funds.** Anyone who bought near the January peak is sitting on a large paper loss. Analysts are divided on what comes next. A brokerage quoted by Business Standard said gold looks oversold but could still slip to $3,750, a level spot gold has already broken. FXEmpire's analyst described the broader trend as bearish and listed $3,707.82 as the next downside level to watch.

**Traders.** Kedia Advisory, quoted by Business Standard, said falling open interest in MCX gold pointed to investors exiting long positions. Short term moves are likely to stay sharp around US data and Fed comments.

## Fed Dates to Watch in IST

The next big trigger is the Fed's December meeting. According to the <a href="https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/2026-december.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Federal Reserve's official calendar</a>, the schedule is:

| Event | US time (ET) | India time (IST) |
|---|---|---|
| FOMC meeting | December 8 and 9 | December 8 to 10 |
| Rate decision | December 9, 2:00 pm | December 10, 12:30 am |
| Chair's press conference | December 9, 2:30 pm | December 10, 1:00 am |
| December meeting minutes | December 30, 2:00 pm | December 31, 12:30 am |

Because these fall after midnight in India, the first reaction will show up in MCX gold when trading resumes on the morning of December 10.

## Where Gold Goes From Here

Gold's slide is being driven by one main question: how far the Fed will go to bring down inflation. With markets pricing a December hike as almost certain, much of that news is already reflected in prices, but any sign of more hikes into 2027 could keep pressure on the metal. Softer US inflation data in the coming weeks would ease that pressure. For most Indian families, buying gold for weddings or long term savings in small, regular amounts remains a calmer approach than trying to time a volatile market.
