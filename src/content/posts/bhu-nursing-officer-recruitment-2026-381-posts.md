Banaras Hindu University (BHU) has opened applications for non-teaching jobs in its hospital and medical sector under Advertisement No. 06/2026-27, dated November 16, 2026. The largest share is for nurses: **381 Nursing Officer posts** at Pay Level 7. The notice also covers Medical Officers, a Deputy Medical Superintendent, therapists, technical assistants and laboratory attendants. Applications are online only, and the **last date is December 15, 2026, 5:00 pm**.

| Detail | What the notice says |
|---|---|
| Advertisement | No. 06/2026-27, dated 16.11.2026 |
| Nursing Officer posts | 381 (Female 233, Male 65, Female NCA 67, Male NCA 16) |
| Medical Officer posts | 25 (plus 2 NCA and 2 Specialist) |
| Last date to apply and pay fee | December 15, 2026, 5:00 pm |
| Eligibility counted as on | December 15, 2026 |
| Fee (UR, EWS, OBC) | Rs 1,000 for Group A, Rs 500 for Group B and C |
| Fee (SC, ST, PwD, women) | Nil |
| Where to apply | BHU Samarth portal |

## Nursing Officer Vacancies Split by Post Code

BHU has listed the nursing posts under four separate codes. Female, male and NCA (new creation) posts are counted separately, so apply under the code that fits you. All four sit at Pay Level 7 with an upper age limit of 35 years.

| Post code | Post | Vacancies | Of which PwBD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26009 | Nursing Officer (Female) | 233 | 16 |
| 26010 | Nursing Officer (Male) | 65 | 3 |
| 26011 | Nursing Officer (Female), NCA | 67 | 3 |
| 26012 | Nursing Officer (Male), NCA | 16 | 1 |

The qualification has two routes. A candidate needs either a GNM diploma with five years of experience, or a B.Sc. or Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing with 55% marks and three years of experience. In both cases the experience must be as a Nursing Officer in a hospital of at least 100 beds, either a reputed private hospital or a government or corporate one. Registration as a nurse and midwife with a State Nursing Council is also required.

So the posts are not open to freshers. A recent B.Sc. Nursing graduate with less than three years of hospital work will not meet the condition on December 15.

The Laboratory Attendant (OT-cum-Anaesthesia) post needs Class 12 in Science plus a diploma in OT or Anaesthesia Technology. The general Laboratory Attendant post asks for a B.Sc. or I.Sc. with three years of experience.

The notice has 41 post codes in all, many with a single vacancy, such as Dietician and Dental Hygienist. Job portals quote different grand totals, so check your own post code in the official PDF.

## How to Apply on the BHU Samarth Portal

1. Download and read the <a href="https://www.bhu.ac.in/Images/files/Advt%20No_%2006-2026-27%20hospital%20group%20A%20B%20%26%20C%202026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BHU advertisement PDF</a> and note the post code you are applying for.
2. Go to the <a href="https://bhunt.samarth.edu.in/index.php/site/login" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BHU non-teaching Samarth portal</a> and register with your email and mobile number.
3. Fill in personal, education and experience details. For nursing posts, keep your State Nursing Council registration and experience certificates ready, as the experience condition is strict.
4. Pay the fee online by internet banking, debit or credit card, or UPI. The fee is not refundable.
5. Submit before 5:00 pm on December 15, 2026, and save the final form.

No hard copy is needed. Candidates already employed must apply through proper channel or submit a no objection certificate.

## Selection Process and Age Relaxation

For Group B and C posts, which include all Nursing Officer jobs, selection is through a written test, followed by a skill test for shortlisted candidates and then document verification. For Group A posts, BHU may hold a written test for shortlisting, followed by an interaction or presentation and an interview. The notice says meeting the minimum eligibility does not guarantee a call for interview. Exam dates have not been announced yet.

Age relaxation for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD candidates follows Government of India rules, and ex-servicemen get relaxation as per GoI norms. Permanent BHU employees have no upper age limit. For Group B and C posts, earlier service in a BHU project, scheme, contract or daily-wage role can be counted for relaxation if one stretch lasted more than six months.

Posts under the older Advertisement 06/2018-2019 stand cancelled, and fee refunds can be sought at ar.rac@bhu.ac.in. For other queries, BHU lists recruitment@bhu.ac.in and 0542-2368781.

## BHU Recruitment 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date for BHU Nursing Officer recruitment 2026?**
The online form and fee payment close on December 15, 2026, at 5:00 pm.

**What is the age limit for BHU Nursing Officer?**
The upper age limit is 35 years, counted as on December 15, 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India rules.

**Can freshers apply for BHU Nursing Officer posts?**
No. GNM holders need five years and B.Sc. Nursing holders need three years of experience as a Nursing Officer in a hospital of at least 100 beds.

**What is the BHU application fee for women candidates?**
Women candidates pay no fee, and neither do SC, ST and PwD candidates.
