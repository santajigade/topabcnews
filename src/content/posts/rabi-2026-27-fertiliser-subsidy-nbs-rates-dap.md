The Union Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, on Wednesday, November 11, approved new Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) rates for phosphatic and potassic (P&K) fertilisers for the Rabi 2026-27 season. The tentative outlay is about Rs 62,370 crore, roughly 64% more than last year. The government said the higher support is needed because global prices of fertilisers and raw materials have risen, and it wants to keep farm inputs affordable for farmers during the winter sowing season.

## How Big Is the Rabi 2026-27 Subsidy

According to the Cabinet decision reported by <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/cabinet-approves-62-370-crore-fertiliser-subsidy-for-rabi-2026-27-season-126111100867_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard</a>, the budget requirement for this Rabi season is Rs 62,370.02 crore. For Rabi 2025-26, the figure was Rs 37,952.29 crore. That means an increase of about Rs 24,417.73 crore.

The new rates apply for six months, from October 1, 2026, to March 31, 2027.

| Item | Rabi 2025-26 | Rabi 2026-27 |
|---|---|---|
| Subsidy outlay | Rs 37,952.29 crore | Rs 62,370.02 crore |
| Increase | | About Rs 24,418 crore (64%) |
| Period | | Oct 1, 2026 to Mar 31, 2027 |
| Grades covered | | 28 P&K grades, including DAP and NPKS |

## New Per Kg NBS Rates for N, P, K and S

Under the NBS scheme, the government pays companies a fixed subsidy for each kilogram of nutrient in a fertiliser. The Tribune and ETV Bharat reported the following rates for Rabi 2026-27:

| Nutrient | Subsidy (Rs per kg) |
|---|---|
| Nitrogen (N) | 94.21 |
| Phosphorus (P) | 66.11 |
| Potash (K) | 2.38 |
| Sulphur (S) | 10.42 |

These figures are the subsidy paid to fertiliser makers and importers, not the price farmers pay at the shop. The idea is that companies pass on the benefit so that the retail price stays low.

## What It Means for DAP Prices for Farmers

Di-ammonium phosphate, or DAP, is one of the P&K fertilisers covered by the scheme. Special provisions on top of the NBS subsidy will keep DAP available at Rs 1,350 per 50 kg bag, according to The Tribune. ETV Bharat reported that the same fixed-price support covers imported and domestic Triple Superphosphate (TSP) and imported Monoammonium Phosphate (MAP).

Other support measures that continue include:

- Freight subsidy for Single Superphosphate (SSP)
- Subsidy for imported and domestic Ammonium Sulphate
- Support for domestic sulphur and sulphuric acid makers equal to 20% of the actual procurement cost
- Floor prices of USD 800 per tonne for sulphur and USD 266.67 per tonne for sulphuric acid

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw, briefing reporters after the Cabinet meeting, said domestic manufacturing will grow and farmers "will not face any additional burden", <a href="https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/agricultural-policy/cabinet-approves-rs-62370-crore-pk-fertiliser-subsidy-for-rabi-2026-27-up-64-from-last-year" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Tribune reported</a>.

## Why the Government Raised the Subsidy

The Cabinet said the rates were set after looking at recent trends in international prices of urea, DAP, muriate of potash (MOP) and sulphur. Its stated aim is to rationalise the subsidy in line with these price trends so that fertilisers reach farmers at subsidised and affordable prices.

The Tribune noted that the jump could strain the fertiliser budget for 2026-27 and may need additional allocations later in the year.

## Fertiliser Stocks Ahead of Rabi Sowing

The government also pointed to healthy stock levels. Figures reported by ETV Bharat show stocks are much higher than a year ago.

| Product | Nov 10, 2025 (lakh tonnes) | Nov 10, 2026 (lakh tonnes) |
|---|---|---|
| Urea | 72.14 | 103.12 |
| DAP | 24.86 | 28.65 |
| NPKs | 42.44 | 60.87 |
| MOP | 10.78 | 20.08 |
| SSP | 23.24 | 22.49 |
| Total | 173.46 | 235.21 |

Vaishnaw put the estimated fertiliser requirement for the Rabi season at about 35 million tonnes, The Tribune said.

## Tips for Farmers This Season

Farmers can buy all 28 subsidised P&K grades through authorised dealers of manufacturers and importers. Advisories reported by ETV Bharat suggest applying nutrients in balanced amounts based on a soil test, and point to SSP as an option in place of DAP, and Ammonium Sulphate as an option alongside urea, depending on crop needs. The NBS scheme has run since April 1, 2010.

At the same Cabinet meeting, the government also extended another major scheme, which we covered in our report on <a href="/stand-up-india-extended-2031-cgfmu-mudra/">Stand-Up India being extended to 2031</a>.

The bigger subsidy is meant to shield farmers from global price swings during the key winter sowing window. Whether retail prices stay steady across states will depend on supply reaching local dealers on time.
