Young wrestler Taniya has given India a first ever Youth Olympics medal in beach wrestling, winning silver in the women's 75kg event at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games in Senegal. She lost a tight final 2-1 to China's Zhao Min on Saturday, November 7, at the Saly Beach West venue. Her medal takes India's tally at Dakar 2026 to seven, made up of three silver and four bronze medals, with the search for a first gold still on.

## How Taniya Reached the Beach Wrestling Final

Taniya was unbeaten on the sand until the very last bout. According to ETV Bharat, her run in the women's 75kg category went like this:

- **Group B, bout 1:** Beat Germany's Mia Mauer 3-1
- **Group B, bout 2:** Beat Greece's Vasiliki Karavanou 3-1
- **Quarterfinal:** Beat Estonia's Merily Vaester 4-1
- **Semifinal:** Beat Matilda Ann Hruby of the USA 3-1
- **Final:** Lost 1-2 to China's Zhao Min

She even took a 1-0 lead early in the final before Zhao, the 2025 U17 world champion in the women's 69kg category, scored twice to take gold. You can read the official report of her medal on <a href="https://olympics.com/en/news/youth-olympics-2026-taniya-beach-wrestling-silver-medal" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Olympics.com</a>.

## Beach Wrestling Makes Its Youth Olympics Debut

Dakar 2026 is the first Youth Olympic Games to include beach wrestling, which means Taniya's name goes into the record books as India's first medallist in the sport at this level. The format is quick and explosive. Each bout lasts three minutes inside a seven metre circle of sand. A wrestler wins by being the first to reach three points, or by putting the opponent's back on the sand with a decisive throw, as ETV Bharat explained. That is why so many of Taniya's wins ended with a 3-1 scoreline.

Here are all seven Indian medals so far, as listed by Yahoo Sports and Khel Now:

| Bronze | Dhinidhi Desinghu | Swimming | Women's 200m freestyle |

### Standout performances

- **Tanvi Patri** reached the women's singles badminton final on November 5 and came home with silver.
- **Dhinidhi Desinghu** is the only Indian with two medals at these Games, both bronzes in freestyle swimming. The swimming programme ended on November 6.
- **Tirthank Pegu** added a third swimming medal in the men's 200m butterfly, making the pool one of India's most productive venues in Dakar.
- **Rajkumari Lanchenbi Chanu** won a historic bronze in wushu, a sport in which India had not medalled at the Youth Olympics before, according to Yahoo Sports.

ETV Bharat placed India 36th on the medal table after Taniya's silver.

## A Small Contingent Punching Above Its Weight

India's results look even better when you consider the size of the team. Khel Now reported that India sent 17 athletes across 11 sports to Dakar, the country's smallest contingent in the history of the Youth Olympics. For comparison, India had sent 46 athletes to the 2018 edition in Buenos Aires. Seven medals from 17 athletes is a strong return, and every one of the medals except Tirthank Pegu's has been won by a female athlete.

The Games are historic for another reason as well. Running from October 31 to November 13, 2026, this is the first time the Youth Olympics have been held in an African nation. For many young athletes, the Youth Olympics are an early test on the international stage before senior competition.

## What Is Left for India at the Dakar Youth Olympics

With five days of competition remaining, India still has chances to add to the tally. According to the Yahoo Sports schedule, athletics runs from November 8 to 10, fencing from November 8 to 13, sailing from November 8 to 12 and beach handball from November 9 to 13. Indian gymnasts were also in action on November 8, and India's fencers were set to begin their foil pool bouts.

India has not yet won an athletics medal at these Games, so the track events will be closely watched. A first gold would be the perfect way to finish what is already a memorable campaign.

Indian hockey has also had a good month at senior level, with the men's team winning the <a href="/india-win-asian-champions-trophy-2026/">Asian Champions Trophy for a sixth time</a>. In Dakar, though, it is the next generation of individual athletes, led by young women like Taniya, Tanvi and Dhinidhi, who are setting the pace.

## Why Taniya's Silver Matters for Indian Wrestling

Beach wrestling is a new and different discipline with shorter bouts and fewer chances to recover from a mistake. By winning four bouts in a row and pushing a reigning age group world champion close in the final, Taniya has shown that Indian wrestlers can adapt quickly to the format. Her silver gives the sport a strong start in India's Youth Olympic story.
