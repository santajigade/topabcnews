OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, has taken the first formal step towards a stock market listing but will not go public in 2026. It filed confidential IPO paperwork with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 8 June 2026, and in September CEO Sam Altman said a listing would not happen this year. Most reports now point to 2027. Meanwhile its private value keeps climbing: Bloomberg reported on 6 November that OpenAI had raised $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, up from $852 billion in March.

## Update (22 November 2026)

This article first appeared on 30 October 2025, when Reuters reported that OpenAI was preparing for an IPO that could value it at up to $1 trillion. Here is where things stand now.

| Question | Latest verified answer |
|---|---|
| Has OpenAI filed for an IPO? | Yes, a confidential draft filing with the SEC, announced 8 June 2026 |
| Listing date | None set. Altman said "not 2026"; 2027 is widely expected |
| Latest reported valuation | $1.25 trillion (Bloomberg, 6 November 2026, round not yet closed) |
| Last closed round | $122 billion at $852 billion, completed 31 March 2026 |
| Company structure | OpenAI Group PBC, a public benefit corporation controlled by the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation |
| Can retail investors buy shares? | Not on an exchange. OpenAI did raise over $3 billion from individuals through bank channels in March |

In the original October 2025 report, Reuters said OpenAI was weighing a filing as soon as the second half of 2026 and an initial raise of at least $60 billion. CFO Sarah Friar had reportedly told associates that 2027 was the target. At the time an OpenAI spokesperson said an IPO was "not our focus, so we could not possibly have set a date." The filing did come in 2026, but the listing itself has slipped.

## Why Altman Ruled Out a 2026 Listing

In a Fortune interview reported by Reuters on 12 September 2026, Altman said "right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public". He tied the delay to AI safety. On X, Altman also said he agreed with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei on slowing the pace of frontier AI, and Fortune reported he hinted that leading AI firms could be close to an agreement to slow development.

OpenAI's own wording at the time of the June filing was cautious too. The company said it had not decided on timing and that some things it wanted to do were likely easier as a private company. According to the AP, OpenAI has not disclosed its profits or said when it expects to become profitable.

## The Path From Nonprofit to Possible Public Company

The structure question had to be settled before any IPO talk could be serious. On 28 October 2025, OpenAI completed a recapitalization after nearly a year of talks with the Attorneys General of California and Delaware. The for-profit became a public benefit corporation called OpenAI Group PBC, and the nonprofit was renamed the OpenAI Foundation. According to <a href="https://openai.com/index/built-to-benefit-everyone" target="_blank" rel="noopener">OpenAI's announcement of the new structure</a>, the Foundation's equity was then worth about $130 billion, with more ownership due if the company hits a valuation milestone. Reuters put the Foundation's stake at 26%.

Microsoft came out of the same deal with roughly 27% on an as-converted diluted basis, valued at about $135 billion, down from 32.5% before OpenAI's recent funding rounds. As part of the <a href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/10/28/the-next-chapter-of-the-microsoft-openai-partnership/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">revised Microsoft partnership</a>, OpenAI also contracted to buy an extra $250 billion of Azure cloud services.

One more obstacle was removed in May 2026, when a jury found that Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI was filed too late and the judge dismissed the case, the AP reported.

The November figure falls short of the $1.4 trillion target reported in September. TechCrunch noted wider market jitters around AI after the US midterm elections, along with strongly negative voter sentiment about data centres.

## How OpenAI's Numbers Have Changed in a Year

The business behind the valuation has grown fast. In October 2025, Reuters expected OpenAI's annualised revenue to reach about $20 billion by the end of that year. By March 2026, OpenAI said it was generating about $2 billion a month, with enterprise customers making up more than 40% of revenue. It also reported more than 900 million weekly ChatGPT users and over 50 million subscribers.

By late September 2026, Axios reported that the annualised revenue run rate was nearing $70 billion.

## Anthropic Went First

Rival Anthropic has already listed. It priced its shares at $83 and raised about $81.3 billion, the largest US IPO on record, and the stock first traded at $111.50 on 19 November, <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/19/anthropic-starts-trading-in-the-us-after-blockbuster-ipo" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera reported</a>. We covered the run-up in our report on the <a href="/anthropic-ipo-price-range-70-78-nasdaq/">Anthropic IPO price range</a>.

That debut gives investors a public benchmark for the next big AI listing. For OpenAI the open questions are timing, and whether it can close its current round near the valuations reported this autumn. On the product side, OpenAI keeps adding paid features, such as the <a href="/chatgpt-wallet-launch-ai-agents-purchases/">ChatGPT Wallet for AI agent purchases</a>.

For readers in India and elsewhere, the practical point is simple: OpenAI is still a private company, and its shares do not trade on any stock exchange. It has not announced a listing date or a share price range.
