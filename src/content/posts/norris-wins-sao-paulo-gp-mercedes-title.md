Lando Norris won the Sao Paulo Grand Prix at Interlagos on Sunday, November 8, 2026, in a race shaped by changing weather from start to finish. The McLaren driver started from pole, lost the lead on the opening lap and then took it back during the pit stops, crossing the line about 14.6 seconds ahead of Max Verstappen. Red Bull's Isack Hadjar completed the podium. Behind them, Mercedes did enough to clinch the 2026 Constructors' Championship with three rounds still to go.

We had covered <a href="/sao-paulo-grand-prix-2026-norris-pole/">Norris taking pole position</a> in a rain-hit qualifying on Saturday. Here is how the race unfolded.

## How Norris Turned a Slow Start Into a Win

Norris did not get away cleanly. Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli, who started alongside him on the front row, took the lead into the first corner, and Liam Lawson of Racing Bulls also moved ahead of the McLaren for a short time. The track was damp at the start, so the whole field began on intermediate tyres.

As the surface dried, teams started switching to slick tyres. Lewis Hamilton was the first to make that call, at the end of lap 14. Norris came in on lap 16 and Antonelli, still leading, pitted one lap later. The Mercedes rejoined only about a second in front, and then spun, letting Norris through. Antonelli also lost places to both Red Bulls after the mistake.

From there Norris controlled the race. According to <a href="https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13609694/sao-paulo-gp-lando-norris-wins-wet-race-for-mclaren-as-mercedes-seal-2026-f1-constructors-championship-in-brazil" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sky Sports' race report</a>, his winning time was 1 hour 35 minutes and 23.504 seconds. Rain returned in the closing stages, and the final laps were run on wet weather rubber, but he kept the car on the road. After the race he said he had kept it cool and "didn't make any mistakes".

It was his third win of the season, putting him level with Verstappen and George Russell on that count.

## Verstappen's Charge From 21st on the Grid

Verstappen produced the drive of the day. Grid penalties linked to engine part allowances left him 21st at the start, with Hamilton just ahead in 20th. The Dutch driver moved steadily through the order and was third by lap 24 after passing Antonelli.

Red Bull later asked Hadjar to let his team-mate through on lap 38, which put Verstappen into second. He admitted that once he reached second place he could not close the gap to Norris. The result extended his run to eight straight podium finishes.

## Mercedes Clinch Their Ninth Constructors' Crown

Fourth for Russell and fifth for Antonelli gave Mercedes an unbeatable lead over Ferrari. It is the team's ninth constructors' title and the first since their run of eight in a row between 2014 and 2021. The tally moves them level with Williams for third on the all-time list. Team boss Toto Wolff simply called it "amazing".

The title caps a strong season for Mercedes. A week earlier, Antonelli became the youngest world champion in Formula 1 history by finishing third in Mexico City. <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/11/2/kimi-antonelli-becomes-formula-ones-youngest-world-champion-in-mexico-city" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera reported</a> that he was 20 years and 68 days old, beating Sebastian Vettel's 2010 record of 23 years and 134 days, and that he is Italy's first champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953.

## Other Talking Points From the Race

- **Russell spin:** Russell spun in the pit lane after switching to slicks, costing him time.
- **Hamilton's opening lap:** He gained nine places at the start but had to hand one back to Oliver Bearman after gaining it off the track.
- **Debut for Camara:** Rookie Rafael Camara finished 20th on his Grand Prix debut for Haas.
- **Retirements:** Lance Stroll and Valtteri Bottas did not finish.
- **Antonelli probe:** Motorsport Week reported that Antonelli was due to be investigated after the race for an unsafe pit release.

## Battle for Third Heads to Las Vegas

With both titles now settled, attention turns to the fight among the chasing teams. Red Bull and McLaren each scored 33 points in Brazil, so McLaren remain 17 points behind Red Bull for third in the constructors' standings. The next round is the Las Vegas Grand Prix from November 20 to 22.

Norris will head there with momentum, while Verstappen's recovery drive in Sao Paulo led Sky Sports to name him a likely favourite for next year's title. For Mercedes, the last three races are now a celebration lap for a season in which they won both championships.
