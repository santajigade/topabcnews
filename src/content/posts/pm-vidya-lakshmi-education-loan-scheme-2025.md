<p>This article is related to PM Vidya Lakshmi Education Loan <a href="https://topabcnews.com/category/yojana/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Scheme</a>. The Government of India has launched the <strong>Pradhan Mantri Vidya Lakshmi Education Loan Scheme</strong>, a one-stop digital platform designed to simplify education loans for students across the country. </p><p>Announced under the Union Budget 2024–25, the scheme aims to make higher education more accessible by offering collateral free, guarantor free loans with flexible repayment options.</p>

					<h4>What Is the Vidya Lakshmi Scheme?</h4>		<p>The Vidya Lakshmi scheme provides a <strong>single online window</strong> where students can apply for education loans from multiple banks. Instead of approaching individual banks, applicants can use the dedicated <a href="https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in/Index.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com" target="_new" rel="noopener">Vidya Lakshmi portal</a> to fill in details, upload documents and track the status of their applications.</p><p>According to the <strong>Education Ministry</strong>, the scheme is expected to benefit nearly <strong>22 lakh students annually</strong>, covering both technical and non-technical higher education courses.</p>										<figure>

										<h4>Who Can Apply for PM Vidya Lakshmi Education Loan Scheme?</h4>		<p>All Indian students admitted to <strong>Quality Higher Education Institutions (QHEIs)</strong> are eligible. This includes:</p><ul><li><p>Top 100 institutions under the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).</p></li><li><p>Central government-run institutions.</p></li><li><p>State institutions ranked between 101 and 200 in NIRF.</p></li></ul><p><br />There is no income bar for eligibility, although subsidies are available for students from families with annual incomes below specific thresholds.</p>			<h4>Key Benefits</h4>		<p>The PM Vidya Lakshmi scheme offers multiple advantages for students:</p><ul><li><p><strong>No collateral or guarantor required</strong> for loans.</p></li><li><p><strong>Full interest subsidy</strong> on loans up to ₹10 lakh for students from families with annual income up to ₹4.5 lakh, pursuing technical or professional courses.</p></li><li><p><strong>3% subsidy</strong> for students from families with annual income up to ₹8 lakh, applicable for all courses.</p></li><li><p><strong>Credit guarantee support</strong> of 75% on loans up to ₹7.5 lakh, reducing the risk for banks.</p></li><li><p><strong>Flexible repayment tenure</strong> of up to 15 years, with a one-year moratorium after the completion of the course.</p></li></ul><p>Detailed guidelines can be accessed on the <a href="https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/document-reports/PM_Vidyalaxmi_Scheme_Guidelines.pdf" target="_new" rel="noopener">Education Ministry’s official website</a>.</p>								<a href="https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/document-reports/PM_Vidyalaxmi_Scheme_Guidelines.pdf">

									<h4>Application Process</h4>		<p>Students can apply through the official PM <a href="https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in/Index.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com" target="_new" rel="noopener">Vidya Lakshmi portal</a>. The process involves:</p><ol><li><p>Registering on the portal and filling out the common education loan application form.</p></li><li><p>Selecting up to three preferred banks.</p></li><li><p>Uploading necessary documents, including admission proof, ID, and income certificates.</p></li><li><p>Tracking loan status online, from submission to sanction and disbursement.</p></li></ol>													<img loading="lazy" width="1024" height="576" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-Vidya-Lakshmi-1024x576.webp" alt="PM Vidya Lakshmi" />																<h4>Latest Updates</h4>		<p>Banks are beginning to adopt the scheme aggressively. Recently, <strong>Punjab National Bank (PNB)</strong> announced a <strong>20 basis point cut in interest rates</strong> for loans disbursed under the Vidya Lakshmi scheme, making borrowing even more affordable for students.</p>			<h4>Why It Matters</h4>		<p>With rising education costs in India and abroad, the PM Vidya Lakshmi scheme is important in ensuring that deserving students are not denied higher education due to financial constraints. </p><p>By reducing paperwork, offering interest subsidies and ensuring government-backed credit guarantees, the scheme marks a significant step toward making education loans transparent and student-friendly.</p>
