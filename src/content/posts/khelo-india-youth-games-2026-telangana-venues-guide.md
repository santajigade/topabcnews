The 8th Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG) will be held across six cities in Telangana from December 11 to 22, 2026, with less than four weeks to go. Union Sports Minister Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya and Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy unveiled the official logo and the mascot, a bird named Neelamani, on November 15. It is the first time Telangana is hosting a Khelo India event, and the programme includes 27 medal sports, with softball counted as a medal sport for the first time.

Most reports so far list the host cities but not which sport goes where. This guide puts that together for athletes, parents and fans planning to watch.

## Which Sport Is Being Played in Which City

Hyderabad hosts most of the Games. Five sports have been given to district cities, according to Telangana Today and ETV Bharat, which both report the same split.

| City | Sports | Venues reported |
|---|---|---|
| Hyderabad | 22 medal sports, including badminton, basketball, boxing, cycling, fencing, football, gymnastics, hockey, shooting, swimming, softball, tennis, volleyball, weightlifting, wrestling, yogasana, rowing and the traditional sports | Gachibowli Sports Complex and Lal Bahadur Stadium are the main hubs; rowing at Hussain Sagar |
| Hanumakonda | Athletics | Not yet announced |
| Khammam | Archery | Not yet announced |
| Karimnagar | Judo | Not yet announced |
| Nizamabad | Table tennis | Not yet announced |
| Mahabubnagar | Kabaddi | Not yet announced |

ETV Bharat reports that the opening ceremony is planned at Gachibowli Stadium and the closing ceremony at the tennis complex in Lal Bahadur Stadium. Other Hyderabad sites named in its report include Saroor Nagar, JNTUH, Secunderabad Indoor Stadium and Kotla Vijayabhaskar Reddy Stadium in Yousufguda. The full day by day schedule for each sport has not been published in the sources we checked, so fans should watch the official Khelo India channels for timings.

### The full sports list

According to the <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2336512&reg=48&lang=2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sports Ministry's release on PIB</a>, the 27 competitive sports are: archery, athletics, badminton, basketball, boxing, cycling, fencing, football, gatka, gymnastics, hockey, judo, kabaddi, kalaripayattu, kho-kho, mallakhamb, rowing, shooting, swimming, softball, table tennis, tennis, thang-ta, volleyball, weightlifting, wrestling and yogasana. Sailing and Karra Samu are demonstration events, so no medals will count towards the table.

## What Is New in 2026

Two changes to the sports programme stand out.

1. **Softball's debut as a medal sport.** It joins the competitive programme for the first time, which gives school and state softball players a national youth stage.
2. **Two demonstration sports.** Sailing and Karra Samu make their KIYG debut. Karra Samu is a traditional Dravidian art form, and coaches already run classes for children in parts of Hyderabad such as Yousufguda, Kukatpally and Uppal. Mandaviya said he was pleased sailors would take part and help promote their sport.
The Games are for athletes under 18, ETV Bharat reports. The PIB release puts the expected turnout at about 8,000 athletes and participants. ETV Bharat, citing official sources, says the Centre has released Rs 22 crore for the event and that the organising committee is headed by the Chief Minister.

## Bihar 2025 vs Telangana 2026 at a Glance

| Detail | 7th edition, 2025 | 8th edition, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Host | Bihar (closing in Patna) | Telangana, six cities |
| Dates | May 2025, ended May 15 | December 11 to 22 |
| Overall champion | Maharashtra, 158 medals (58 gold, 47 silver, 53 bronze) | To be decided |
| Runner up | Haryana, 117 medals (39 gold) | To be decided |
| Third | Rajasthan, 60 medals (24 gold) | To be decided |

The 2025 medal figures come from <a href="https://www.newsonair.gov.in/khelo-india-youth-games-2025-ends-maharashtra-tops-with-158-medals" target="_blank" rel="noopener">All India Radio News</a>. Maharashtra goes into Telangana as the team to beat. Telangana Today reports that the 2025 win was its fifth title and third in a row, and that 26 records fell in Bihar, including five national youth weightlifting records.

## Meet Neelamani and the Logo

The mascot Neelamani is a bird with blue and turquoise feathers. The ministry says it stands for energy, agility and determination, with four qualities listed as vibrant, courageous, agile and pride.

The logo mixes Telangana landmarks with sport. It carries the saffron and green Khelo India running figure, the Kakatiya Kala Thoranam, the Buddha statue at Hussain Sagar, the proposed Gandhi Tower at Bapu Ghat and the Tangedu flower linked to Bathukamma. Seven sporting silhouettes form a skyline beside them. The tagline is "Khel Ka Junoon, Telangana Ki Jaan Aur Pehchaan."

Revanth Reddy linked the Games to the goal of a drug free youth, encouraging young people to stay away from substance abuse.

## Coming Straight After the Asian Games

The Games come straight after the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, and Mandaviya said he expects quality performances as a result. Families planning to watch district events in Hanumakonda, Khammam or Karimnagar will need the sport wise timetables, which have not been released yet.
