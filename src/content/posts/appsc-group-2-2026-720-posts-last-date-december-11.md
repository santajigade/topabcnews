The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) is accepting online applications for 720 Group 2 posts until 11:59 pm on Friday, December 11, 2026. The window opened on November 10. The screening test, an offline OMR exam, is fixed for Sunday, February 21, 2027. Of the 720 posts, 471 are executive and 249 non-executive. The largest single block is 203 Deputy Tahsildar posts.

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Online applications open | November 10, 2026 |
| Last date to apply | December 11, 2026, 11:59 pm |
| Screening (prelims) test, offline OMR | February 21, 2027 |
| Main exam, computer based | To be announced |

As of November 20, applicants have three weeks left.

## What Changed From the October Figures

Reports in October, based on a provisional count, put Group 2 at 686 posts with applications closing on November 30. The November notification raised the total to 720 and moved the deadline to December 11. Comparing CareerIndia's October post-wise table with the Hans India figures:

| Post | October report | November notification | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total vacancies | 686 | 720 | +34 |
| Deputy Tahsildar | 200 | 203 | +3 |
| Senior Accountant | 90 | 91 | +1 |
| DMPDO | 176 | 176 | None |
| GST Officer | 40 | 40 | None |
| Prohibition and Excise SI | 29 | 29 | None |
| ASO (all departments) | 80 | 80 | None |
| Last date | November 30 | December 11 | 11 more days |

The October table also listed Municipal Commissioner Grade III (6 posts), Sub-Registrar Grade II (7) and Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies (10). The November reports do not name these posts, so check the final notification to see where they stand.

## Who Can Apply

A bachelor's degree from a recognised university is the basic qualification. In the October listing, Accounts and Legal posts needed subject-specific degrees, Senior and Junior Accountant posts carried a computer qualification requirement, and Prohibition and Excise Sub-Inspector candidates must meet physical standards.

The minimum age is 18. The reports give different upper limits: CareerIndia said 42 in October, while Sakshi Post says 44 for most posts in the November notice. Reserved categories get age relaxation. Read the age clause for your post in the official notification before you pay the fee.

## How to Apply on psc.ap.gov.in

1. Go to the <a href="https://psc.ap.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">APPSC website</a>.
2. Complete One Time Profile Registration (OTPR) if you do not have an OTPR ID.
3. Log in with your OTPR details and open the Group 2 application.
4. Fill in the form, choose your posts and upload the documents asked for.
5. Pay the application fee online and submit before 11:59 pm on December 11.
6. Download and save the filled form and the payment receipt.

The selection has three stages: the screening test, a computer-based main exam, and a Computer Proficiency Test. Only those who clear the screening test move to the mains, which <a href="https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/appsc-issues-notification-for-720-group-ii-posts-2003285" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Deccan Chronicle reports</a> will be scheduled later. If you are also applying for central jobs this season, the <a href="/rrb-station-master-2026-last-date-november-25/">RRB Station Master form closes on November 25</a>, before this one.

## APPSC Group 2 2026: Common Questions

**What is the last date for APPSC Group 2 2026?**
Applications close at 11:59 pm on December 11, 2026. Earlier reports gave November 30, but the November notification moved it.

**When is the APPSC Group 2 prelims exam?**
The screening test will be held offline on OMR sheets on February 21, 2027. The main exam date has not been announced.

**How many Deputy Tahsildar posts are in APPSC Group 2 2026?**
There are 203 Deputy Tahsildar posts, the most of any single post in this recruitment.
