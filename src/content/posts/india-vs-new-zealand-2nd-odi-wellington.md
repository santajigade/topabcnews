India will try to go 2-0 up in their five match ODI series against New Zealand when the two sides meet in the second game at Hnry Stadium, Wellington, on Saturday, November 7, 2026. The match starts at 7:30 AM IST. India arrive with confidence after a five wicket win in the rain hit opener in Auckland, while New Zealand have been hit by an injury blow, with opener Devon Conway ruled out of the rest of the series because of concussion.

## How India Won the Rain Hit Series Opener

The first ODI was played at Eden Park, Auckland, on Wednesday, November 4. India captain Shubman Gill won the toss and chose to bowl, and the decision paid off quickly. New Zealand were bowled out for 185 in 40.2 overs.

Rain then cut short India's chase. Under the DLS method, the target was revised to 143 runs in 32 overs. India reached it in 22.4 overs with five wickets to spare, and with 56 balls left.

Virat Kohli was the star with the bat. He made 74 off just 51 balls, hitting 13 fours and a six, at a strike rate above 145. Gill added 37, while Rohit Sharma was out for a duck. New Zealand pacer Jacob Duffy briefly gave his side hope by taking two wickets in three balls, but by then the chase was well under control.

## Seamers Set Up the Win

India's fast bowlers did most of the damage in Auckland. Here is how the key bowlers performed:

| Bowler | Figures |
|---|---|
| Arshdeep Singh | 10 overs, 4 for 70 |
| Gurnoor Brar | 3 wickets |
| Anshul Kamboj | 10 overs, 2 for 22 |
| Axar Patel | 4 overs, 0 for 9 |

Anshul Kamboj was named Player of the Match. His 10 overs cost only 22 runs, and he bowled 46 dot balls, which kept the New Zealand batters under constant pressure. Arshdeep picked up the most wickets, and Brar chipped in with three.

The performance matters because there were doubts before the series about India's relatively inexperienced pace attack. One good game does not settle the question, but it gives the team management something to build on.

## Conway Ruled Out, Henry Cleared

New Zealand's preparation for the second match has been disturbed by injuries. According to <a href="https://aninews.in/news/sports/cricket/new-zealand-suffer-injury-blow-as-devon-conway-ruled-out-of-india-odi-series-with-concussion-matt-henry-cleared-for-second-odi20261106082930" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ANI</a>, Devon Conway suffered a concussion after a collision while fielding in a training session before the first ODI. Fast bowler Matt Henry was involved in the same incident, and both players missed the Auckland game.

The update for New Zealand is mixed:

- **Devon Conway:** Ruled out of the remaining ODIs against India.
- **Matt Henry:** Cleared of concussion and available for the second ODI.
- **Nick Kelly:** The left handed batter, who was originally picked for the fourth and fifth ODIs, has been called up early and joins the squad in Wellington. He has been withdrawn from the New Zealand A squad.
- **Dale Phillips:** The Auckland batter replaces Kelly in the New Zealand A squad for the four day match against India A.

Henry's return should strengthen the home side's bowling, but losing Conway, an experienced top order batter, is a real setback for captain Mitchell Santner.

## Can India Tighten Their Grip on the Series

India hold the early advantage. Kohli is in fine form, the seamers have made a strong start, and New Zealand must reshuffle their top order without Conway. A win in Wellington would put India 2-0 up with three games left and put serious pressure on the hosts. For New Zealand, the return of Matt Henry and a full 50 over game without rain could help them level the series and set up a close contest in Hamilton.
