A depression over the Bay of Bengal is expected to strengthen into Cyclone Akvan by Monday evening, November 16, according to the India Meteorological Department (IMD), and could grow into a very severe cyclonic storm before it nears the Andhra Pradesh coast later this week. Heavy rain is forecast for coastal Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha from November 18, and fishermen have been told to stay off the sea. Akvan would be the third cyclone to threaten India's east coast in under a month.

## Where the Depression Is and How Fast It Is Growing

In its update reported on Saturday afternoon, the IMD said the depression was centred about 1,130 km south-southeast of Gopalpur in Odisha and 1,010 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam, and was intensifying gradually. A later position reported by the Deccan Chronicle put it about 960 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam, 800 km east-southeast of Chennai and 620 km east-northeast of Trincomalee in Sri Lanka, moving north-northwest.

The expected timeline, based on IMD guidance reported by OdishaTV and the Deccan Chronicle, looks like this:

| Date | Expected stage |
|---|---|
| November 16 | Becomes cyclonic storm Akvan over the central Bay of Bengal |
| November 17 | Strengthens into a severe cyclonic storm |
| November 18 and 19 | Moves north-northwest towards the Andhra Pradesh coast |
| By November 19 | May become a very severe cyclonic storm, winds 120 to 130 kmph, gusts to 145 kmph |
| Around November 20 | Possible crossing of the Andhra coast, location not yet fixed |

The IMD has not yet named a landfall point. The Deccan Chronicle reported that the storm could cross somewhere between Visakhapatnam and Bapatla around November 20, but forecasters stress the track will become clearer only once the cyclone has fully formed. Official bulletins are published on the <a href="https://mausam.imd.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IMD weather website</a>.

## Rain and Wind Forecast for Andhra Pradesh

IMD scientist Dr S. Karunasagar told the Deccan Chronicle that the system could bring heavy rain and strong winds to several parts of coastal Andhra Pradesh and some areas of Rayalaseema. Weather models suggest two to three days of heavy to very heavy rain along the coast.

Districts expected to see heavy to very heavy rain on November 19 and 20 include Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Parvathipuram Manyam, Visakhapatnam, Alluri Sitarama Raju, Anakapalli, Kakinada, Konaseema, East Godavari, West Godavari, Eluru, Krishna and Bapatla. Nellore, Tirupati and Chittoor may also get heavy rain in the same period. The report added that strong winds could further damage standing crops.

## What Odisha Should Expect

Manorama Mohanty, director of the Regional Meteorological Centre, said Odisha should stay mostly dry until rain begins on November 18, OdishaTV reported. The forecast for the state is:

- **November 18:** Light to moderate rain in coastal districts, with heavy rain at isolated places in Puri, Ganjam and Gajapati, plus thunderstorms and gusty winds of 40 to 50 kmph.
- **November 19:** Heavy to very heavy rain likely in Puri, Ganjam and Gajapati, with isolated heavy rain in districts including Khordha, Nayagarh, Jagatsinghpur, Kandhamal, Rayagada, Koraput and Malkangiri.

The Federal reported that an orange alert covers some Odisha districts.

## Fishermen Warning and Andaman Preparations

The IMD has advised fishermen not to venture into the sea from November 18 to 21 and told those already out to return to the coast at once. Rough seas and squally winds are also expected over the Andaman Sea and around the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

Andaman and Nicobar Lieutenant Governor Dinesh Sharma said the administration was ready for any emergency, with disaster response teams prepared and the Army, Navy and Air Force on high alert, according to India TV. General cyclone safety advice for households is available from the <a href="https://ndma.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Disaster Management Authority</a>.

## Will Chennai Get Rain From Akvan?

Probably not much. VR Durai, head of the Regional Meteorological Centre in Chennai, told The Federal that the system could pull winds from the Arabian Sea and change wind direction over Chennai, bringing drier weather. If the storm keeps tracking towards Andhra Pradesh, Chennai may see only isolated light showers, while light to moderate rain is expected over southern Tamil Nadu and the Western Ghats until November 19.

## Third Storm After Arnab and Murasu

Akvan follows two cyclones that hit Andhra Pradesh in quick succession. Cyclone Arnab made landfall near Narasapur on October 28 as a very severe cyclonic storm, with winds of 125 to 135 kmph, and killed at least six people. Cyclone Murasu crossed the southern Andhra coast near Ongole on November 3 as a severe cyclonic storm, followed by very heavy rain in coastal Andhra and parts of Telangana.

The name Akvan was suggested by Iran under the regional naming system for North Indian Ocean cyclones. It comes from a demon-like figure in the Persian epic Shahnameh, The Week reported. ### Key highlights

- Depression expected to become Cyclone Akvan on November 16, severe by November 17.
- Could become a very severe cyclonic storm by November 19, with gusts up to 145 kmph.
- Heavy to very heavy rain likely in coastal Andhra Pradesh on November 19 and 20.
- Puri, Ganjam and Gajapati in Odisha face heavy rain on November 18 and 19.
- Fishermen told not to go to sea from November 18 to 21.

## How Residents Can Prepare This Week

With landfall still a few days away and the track not yet fixed, people in coastal Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha should follow IMD and state disaster management updates closely, secure loose items and keep emergency supplies ready. Forecasts for cyclones can change quickly, so the next two IMD bulletins after the storm forms will be the most important to watch.
