European Union leaders have settled the question of who will speak for Europe in talks to end Russia's war in Ukraine. At the close of an informal summit in Dublin on Friday, November 13, European Council President Antonio Costa announced that France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the United Kingdom, together with EU institutions, will form Europe's negotiating team.
The five countries are known as the E5 within the Coalition of the Willing, the group of nations that has been discussing security support for Ukraine. The UK is not an EU member but has been one of Kyiv's strongest backers since 2022.

## How Dublin Moved From Division to a Deal

Ukraine was high on the agenda in Dublin after US President Donald Trump's envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Ireland, fresh from talks with Vladimir Putin in Moscow. As we reported earlier, <a href="/dublin-summit-europe-seat-ukraine-peace-talks/">EU leaders arrived in Dublin split over who should represent them</a>.

By the end of the day, leaders had agreed on a model that pairs the bloc's main institutions with five large European states. Costa said the team would act with full unity and in close coordination with all member states, according to <a href="https://aninews.in/news/world/europe/france-germany-italy-poland-uk-to-represent-europe-in-ukraine-peace-talks-alongside-eu-institutions20261114065159/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ANI</a>.

Under EU rules, Costa, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas represent the bloc on foreign and security policy, the German news agency dpa noted. Reports did not say which individual leaders will attend any future meeting with Putin, Zelenskyy and US President Donald Trump.

## What European Leaders Said

Costa set out Europe's position firmly. "Only Ukraine has the legitimacy to decide on matters concerning its territory," he said, according to <a href="https://kyivindependent.com/european-leaders-agree-on-joint-team-for-ukraine-peace-negotiations-costa-says/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Kyiv Independent</a>. He added that Europe's security begins with Ukraine's security, and that the coming weeks would test whether Russia is ready to end the war.

He said security guarantees for Ukraine should be discussed within the Coalition of the Willing in close coordination with NATO. He also said the EU must play a central role in Ukraine's reconstruction and economic recovery.

Other leaders welcomed the outcome:

- **Ursula von der Leyen** said a strong European presence matters and that the goal is to end the war with strong security guarantees and a plan for Ukraine's prosperity after the war.
- **Donald Tusk**, Poland's prime minister, wrote on X that peace in Ukraine and Poland's own security are at stake.
- **Friedrich Merz**, the German chancellor, said Germany would take part in possible new talks, which he expects in the coming weeks, dpa reported.
- **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** has said Kyiv wants European representatives to have an equal seat at the table.

## Russia's Response and Sticking Points

It is not clear that Moscow will accept the arrangement. Kushner said Putin was willing to accept European involvement, but Russia's Foreign Ministry said it was not aware of any such agreement, according to dpa.

Putin has also said that any foreign troops in Ukraine after a peace deal would be "absolutely unacceptable" to Russia, ANI reported. That position clashes directly with the security guarantees the Coalition of the Willing has been discussing.

Earlier in the week, two days of US-mediated talks between Ukrainian and Russian delegations in Abu Dhabi ended without a breakthrough. Some progress was reported on a possible prisoner exchange, which Zelenskyy has said could be the largest of the war, but major gaps remain on territory and security guarantees. European delegations from Germany, France, the UK, Italy, the EU and NATO were present in Abu Dhabi but did not take part directly in the talks, dpa said. ## Key Highlights

- EU leaders agreed on Europe's negotiating team in Dublin on November 13, 2026.
- The team is France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the UK, plus EU institutions.
- Costa said only Ukraine can decide on its territory and sovereignty.
- Russia's Foreign Ministry said it knew of no agreement on European involvement.
- Merz expects new talks in the coming weeks.

## What Comes Next for Europe and Ukraine

No date or venue has been announced for the next round of talks. On the European side, von der Leyen said the Commission will soon propose a 22nd package of sanctions on Russia, along with a plan to close Ukraine's financing gap for 2027. Leaders are expected to discuss Ukraine's funding needs for 2027 and beyond at the European Council meeting on November 26 and 27.

The new team gives Europe a clearer voice, but whether it wins a real seat at the table now depends on Washington and Moscow.
