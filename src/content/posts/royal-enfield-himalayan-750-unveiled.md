The Royal Enfield Himalayan 750 made its global debut at EICMA 2026 in Milan on 3 November. It uses a new 743cc air and oil cooled parallel twin making 55 PS and 63 Nm, and it is the first Royal Enfield with a six-axis IMU, which brings cornering ABS, lean sensitive traction control and cruise control. It weighs 224 kg in India spec. In the UK it costs GBP 6,999 to GBP 8,699 (about Rs 8.92 lakh to Rs 11.09 lakh at Autocar India's conversion). The India price has not been announced yet; evo India reports it will come at Motoverse in Goa from 27 to 29 November 2026.

## Update (24 November 2026)

Three days before Motoverse, Royal Enfield still has not published an Indian price. The festival dates and Goa venue are confirmed on the <a href="https://www.royalenfield.com/in/en/app/rides/events/mv-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Motoverse 2026 page</a>. evo India reported that Indian pricing will be announced at the festival and expects an ex-showroom figure close to Rs 5 lakh. That is the magazine's estimate, not an official figure. European bookings opened on 3 November through Royal Enfield's website and dealers.

## The 743cc Twin and How It Delivers Power

The engine is based on Royal Enfield's familiar 650cc twin, but the company says about 90 percent of the parts are new. Bore and stroke are 83.5 mm and 67.8 mm, and compression is 9.5:1. For touring riders the key figure is that roughly 57 Nm, or 90 percent of peak torque, is on tap from 3,500 rpm. That should mean fewer downshifts when overtaking with luggage on a highway. Power goes through a six speed gearbox with a slip and assist clutch.

## Himalayan 750 vs Himalayan 450: What Changes

Many Indian buyers will weigh the 750 against the Himalayan 450. The 450 figures below come from <a href="https://www.royalenfield.com/in/en/motorcycles/himalayan/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Royal Enfield's Himalayan 450 page</a> and Autocar India.

| | Himalayan 750 | Himalayan 450 |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 743cc twin, air and oil cooled | 452cc single |
| Power | 55 PS at 7,000 rpm | 40.02 PS at 8,000 rpm |
| Torque | 63 Nm at 5,000 rpm | 40 Nm at 5,500 rpm |
| Kerb weight | 224 kg | 196 kg |
| Front wheel | 19 inch | 21 inch |
| Suspension travel (front / rear) | 190 mm / 175 mm | 200 mm / 200 mm |
| Ground clearance | 205 mm | 230 mm |
| Seat height | 815 mm | 825 mm (adjustable to 845 mm) |
| Fuel tank | 17.8 litres | 17 litres |
| Rear brake | 255 mm disc, two piston caliper | 270 mm disc, single piston caliper |
| Starting price | GBP 6,999 in the UK; India price not announced | Rs 3.08 lakh ex-showroom in India |

The 750 has 15 PS and 23 Nm more than the 450, but it also weighs 28 kg more, runs a smaller 19 inch front wheel and gives up 25 mm of ground clearance and some suspension travel. The numbers point to a bike built for long highway days with luggage. On rocky trails the lighter 450 is likely to stay the easier bike. Its seat is 10 mm lower but not adjustable; accessory seats take it to 795 mm or 835 mm.

## Chassis, Brakes and Electronics

The 750 sits on a new box section steel spine frame with pressed steel outriggers. Showa supplies a 43 mm upside down fork with full adjustment and an adjustable monoshock. Tyres are 110/80 R19 at the front and 150/70 R17 at the rear, tubeless on both wheel types. Up front there are twin 280 mm discs, and dual channel cornering ABS is standard. The rear ABS can be switched off.

It is the most heavily equipped Royal Enfield so far:

- Five riding modes: Road, Road Sport, Rain, Trail and Custom
- Traction control with Low, Medium and High settings, or off
- Cruise control, ride by wire throttle and a key based immobiliser
- A 4 inch round TFT with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Google Maps navigation, plus a 4G and GPS connected system (Wingman 2.0)
- Tyre pressure monitoring, self cancelling indicators and an emergency brake lamp
- Two USB Type C ports, rated 27 W and 10 W

The screen looks like the 450's, but the 750 adds a built-in SIM, so connected features do not depend on a paired phone.

## Royal Enfield Himalayan 750: Common Questions

### What is the price of the Himalayan 750 in India?
Royal Enfield has not announced it yet. evo India reports that the price will be revealed at Motoverse in Goa from 27 to 29 November 2026. In the UK the bike costs GBP 6,999 to GBP 8,699.

### When will the Himalayan 750 launch in India?
Indian media expect the India debut and price announcement at Motoverse 2026, which runs from 27 to 29 November in Goa. Royal Enfield has not given an official delivery date.

### How powerful is the Himalayan 750?
Its 743cc twin makes 55 PS at 7,000 rpm and 63 Nm at 5,000 rpm. About 90 percent of that torque is available from 3,500 rpm.

### Is the Himalayan 750 heavier than the Himalayan 450?
Yes. The India spec 750 weighs 224 kg against 196 kg for the 450, a gap of 28 kg. The 750 also has less ground clearance, 205 mm against 230 mm.
