Indonesia's Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka has told President Prabowo Subianto that he intends to step down, following court rulings that questioned the school documents he used to run for office in 2024. State Secretary Prasetyo Hadi announced the development at the presidential palace in Jakarta on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. No formal resignation has been filed yet, and the government says Gibran remains vice president for now.

## What Was Announced at the Presidential Palace

Prasetyo told reporters that Gibran raised his plan during a morning meeting with Prabowo that lasted about two and a half hours. According to the Jakarta Globe, Prasetyo said Gibran had "expressed his intention to resign" in response to decisions by Indonesia's Constitutional Court and Supreme Court.

Prasetyo also denied reports that the president had turned down the offer. Instead, he said Prabowo gave Gibran advice and suggested that he continue to use legal routes to defend his academic documents, the Associated Press reported. Gibran has asked for a chance to prove the validity of his papers through proceedings at the State Administrative Court.

In a short statement, Gibran did not directly address the resignation offer. He said he respects public criticism and does not want the matter to turn into "a prolonged political controversy," according to AP.

## The Dispute Over Gibran's Education Documents

Gibran, 39, finished his secondary and university education abroad. To stand as a candidate, he had to show that his overseas qualifications were equivalent to Indonesian ones, including the minimum high school level required for the post.

Two court decisions have put that claim under pressure:

- **Supreme Court:** Ruling on an appeal filed by lawyer Subhan Palal, it annulled the certificate, issued by the then education ministry, that recognised Gibran's overseas schooling as equal to an Indonesian high school diploma, <a href="https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/11/02/supreme-court-ruling-renews-calls-for-gibran-to-step-down" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Jakarta Post reported</a>. Gibran studied at schools in Singapore and Australia before earning a degree in Singapore.
- **Constitutional Court:** It said it found no convincing evidence that he holds an overseas credential recognised as equal to a high school diploma, ANTARA reported.

These rulings led some lawmakers to call for his impeachment. Several parties in the House of Representatives, including some within Prabowo's ruling coalition, have pushed to use the legislature's right to formally state its position on the controversy, a step that The Jakarta Post said could open the way to impeachment proceedings.

## How Indonesia Would Choose a New Vice President

Yusril Ihza Mahendra, speaking for the government, said there is no vacancy in the vice presidency. "Since he has neither resigned nor been removed from office, he remains the Vice President," he said, as quoted by <a href="https://en.antaranews.com/news/441679/govt-assures-no-vice-presidential-vacancy-yusril" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Indonesia's state news agency ANTARA</a>. He added that neither court ruling ends Gibran's term automatically, and described the situation as a choice between resignation and removal.

If Gibran does step down, Yusril explained the steps involved:

1. A written resignation must be submitted to the People's Consultative Assembly (MPR).
2. The MPR informs the president.
3. Two vice presidential candidates are nominated.
4. The MPR holds a plenary session and elects a successor by vote.

## A Widening Rift Between Prabowo and Jokowi

The episode carries extra political weight because Gibran is the eldest son of former President Joko Widodo, widely known as Jokowi. Widodo backed Prabowo's run for the presidency after Prabowo picked Gibran as his running mate in 2024. According to AP, the two leaders now face a growing political rift.

That rift has deepened amid a separate investigation into an alleged wiretapping scheme targeting the president and other figures, which involves senior police officials. AP said the resignation offer appeared to be unrelated to that probe. Widodo himself has faced separate allegations about his own university diploma, which he denies.

## What to Watch in the Coming Days

The key questions now are whether Gibran submits a written resignation to the MPR, how the State Administrative Court handles his challenge, and whether lawmakers move ahead with steps that could lead to impeachment. Yusril urged the public to stay calm, saying, "let us wait and see what unfolds in the coming days." For now, Gibran formally keeps his post while Indonesia's political leaders weigh their next moves.
