<p><a href="https://www.apple.com/in/iphone/">Apple</a> has launched its <strong>most exciting iPhone line-up yet</strong>. At the "Awe Dropping" event on <strong>September 9, 2025</strong>, the company unveiled four new models: <a href="https://www.apple.com/in/iphone-air/"><strong>iPhone Air</strong></a>, <a href="https://www.apple.com/in/iphone-17/"><strong>iPhone 17</strong></a>,and <a href="https://www.apple.com/in/iphone-17-pro/"><strong>iPhone 17 Pro</strong></a>. Each model brings exciting upgrades, from thinner designs to powerful chips and dazzling cameras.</p><h4>Sleek and Strong: iPhone Air</h4>
<p>The <strong>iPhone Air</strong> stands out as the <strong>thinnest iPhone ever</strong>, measuring just <strong>5.6 mm</strong> in thickness.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>It features a <strong>6.5-inch Super Retina XDR display</strong> with <strong>ProMotion (120Hz)</strong> and a very bright <strong>peak brightness of 3,000 nits.</strong></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The body is made tough with a <strong>titanium frame</strong> protected by a ceramic shield.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Inside, it runs on the brand-new <strong>A19 Pro chip</strong>, the same processor used in the Pro models.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Connectivity has been improved with Apple’s own <strong>C1x modem</strong>, which is twice as fast as its predecessor.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The <strong>camera system</strong> includes a <strong>48 MP Fusion rear camera</strong>, a <strong>12 MP telephoto lens</strong>, and an <strong>18 MP front camera</strong> with "Center Stage" that automatically keeps you in frame during video calls.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It also includes modern features like <strong>Wi-Fi 7</strong>, <strong>Bluetooth 6</strong>, <strong>Thread</strong>, and an <strong>Action button</strong> for quick access.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Apple promises <strong>up to 40 hours of video playback</strong> on a single charge.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Price in India starts at <strong>₹1,19,900</strong> for <strong>256 GB</strong>, moving up to <strong>₹1,59,900</strong> for <strong>1 TB.</strong></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pre-orders begin <strong>September 12</strong>, with the phones available starting <strong>September 18.</strong></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/iphone-17-pro-price-1024x683.webp" alt="iphone 17 pro price" width="1024" height="683"></p>
<h4>The Everyday Champion: iPhone 17</h4>
<p>The standard <strong>iPhone 17</strong> is slightly larger but packed with fresh upgrades.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>It sports a <strong>6.3-inch display</strong> with <strong>120 Hz</strong>, <strong>3,000 nits brightness</strong>, and new <strong>Ceramic Shield 2</strong>, which improves resistance to scratches and cracks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It’s powered by the <strong>A19 chip</strong>, designed for future AI features.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The <strong>dual-fusion camera</strong>—a 48 MP main camera with 2× telephoto and a 48 MP ultra-wide camera—captures more detail, backed by an <strong>18 MP square front camera</strong> that’s great for landscape selfies.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Available storage options: <strong>256 GB</strong> (₹82,900) and <strong>512 GB</strong> (₹1,02,900).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Colors include lavender, mist blue, black, white, and sage.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pre-orders are open from <strong>September 12</strong>, with sales starting <strong>September 18</strong>.<br><br></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/iphone-17-pro-1024x683.webp" alt="iphone 17 pro" width="1024" height="683"></p>
<h4>High-Power Performance: iPhone 17 Pro</h4>
<p>Taking a step up, the <strong>iPhone 17 Pro</strong> adds premium features for power users.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>It packs a <strong>6.3-inch ProMotion 120 Hz display</strong> with Always-On and ultra-bright screen at 3,000 nits.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A <strong>Ceramic Shield 2</strong> on both sides and a <strong>7000-series aluminium unibody with vapor-chamber cooling</strong> give it both durability and better performance.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Inside, it uses the <strong>A19 Pro chip</strong> with a <strong>6-core CPU</strong>, <strong>6-core GPU</strong>, and <strong>16-core Neural Engine</strong>, plus the <strong>N1 wireless chip</strong> for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and Thread.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Camera enthusiasts will love the <strong>48 MP triple Fusion camera system</strong>, including a telephoto lens with <strong>8× optical zoom</strong>—the best zoom yet on an iPhone.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The front camera is <strong>18 MP</strong> with Center Stage, 4K HDR video, and stabilisation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It supports <strong>Dolby Vision HDR</strong>, <strong>ProRes RAW</strong>, <strong>Apple Log 2</strong>, and <strong>genlock</strong> for professional video workflows.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Runs <strong>iOS 26</strong>, introducing <strong>Apple Intelligence</strong>, Live Translation, offline AI models, and enhanced privacy tools.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Prices start at <strong>₹1,34,900</strong> (256 GB), going up to <strong>₹1,74,900</strong> (1 TB).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Available in silver, cosmic orange, and deep blue. Pre-orders from <strong>September 9</strong>, with sales on <strong>September 18</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h4>The Ultimate Powerhouse: iPhone 17 Pro Max</h4>
<p>For those wanting the best, the <strong>iPhone 17 Pro Max</strong> is Apple’s top tier offering.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>It features a massive <strong>6.9-inch ProMotion display</strong> with Always-On and 3,000 nits brightness.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Built with <strong>Ceramic Shield 2</strong> and aluminium unibody with vapor-chamber cooling for unmatched durability and performance.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Equipped with the <strong>A19 Pro chip</strong> plus enhanced cooling—delivering up to <strong>40% better sustained performance</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It packs the <strong>longest battery life yet</strong> in an iPhone and supports <strong>50% charge in just 20 minutes</strong> using a new <strong>40 W USB-C adapter</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Its <strong>48 MP triple camera</strong> offers <strong>8× optical zoom</strong> and up to <strong>40× digital zoom</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The front camera captures 4K HDR video with Center Stage and can record front and rear simultaneously.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Also includes <strong>Dolby Vision HDR</strong>, <strong>ProRes RAW</strong>, and genlock for creators.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It comes with <strong>N1 chip</strong> (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread) and runs <strong>iOS 26</strong> with Apple Intelligence features.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Price ranges from <strong>₹1,49,900</strong> (256 GB) to <strong>₹2,29,900</strong> (2 TB).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Sold in silver, cosmic orange, and deep blue. Pre-orders start <strong>September 9</strong>, shipping begins <strong>September 18</strong></p>
</li>
