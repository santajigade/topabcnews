Apple launched the iPhone 17 family in September 2025, with the iPhone 17 Pro starting at Rs 1,34,900 and the Pro Max at Rs 1,49,900 in India. A year later, right after the iPhone 18 Pro event in September 2026, Apple raised Indian prices of the models it kept on sale. The base **iPhone 17 (256GB) went from Rs 82,900 to Rs 99,900**, and the iPhone Air 1TB jumped by Rs 65,000. Here is what each model costs now compared with launch.

## Update (21 November 2026): Old vs New Prices

The new prices appeared on Apple's India online store around 10 September 2026, as reported by <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/iphone-17-iphone-air-17e-get-costlier-in-india-after-apple-event-check-new-prices-554418-2026-09-10" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Today</a> and Digit. Some retailers were still showing older prices at the time.

| Model and storage | Launch price | Price after Sept 2026 hike | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone 17e 256GB | Rs 64,900 | Rs 79,900 | Rs 15,000 |
| iPhone 17e 512GB | Rs 84,900 | Rs 1,04,900 | Rs 20,000 |
| iPhone 17 256GB | Rs 82,900 | Rs 99,900 | Rs 17,000 |
| iPhone 17 512GB | Rs 1,02,900 | Rs 1,24,900 | Rs 22,000 |
| iPhone Air 256GB | Rs 1,19,900 | Rs 1,49,900 | Rs 30,000 |
| iPhone Air 512GB | Rs 1,39,900 | Rs 1,74,900 | Rs 35,000 |
| iPhone Air 1TB | Rs 1,59,900 | Rs 2,24,900 | Rs 65,000 |

In percentage terms the iPhone 17 rose by about 21 percent and the Air 1TB by about 41 percent. The 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max did not appear in the revised lists, since the iPhone 18 Pro models replaced them at the top.

## What the iPhone 17 Family Launched With in 2025

Apple's <a href="https://www.apple.com/in/newsroom/2025/09/apple-unveils-iphone-17-pro-and-iphone-17-pro-max-the-most-powerful-and-advanced-pro-models-ever/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">September 2025 announcement</a> set these India details for the Pro models:

- **iPhone 17 Pro:** from Rs 1,34,900 for 256GB, with 512GB and 1TB options, in cosmic orange, deep blue and silver.
- **iPhone 17 Pro Max:** from Rs 1,49,900, and for the first time offered with up to 2TB of storage.
- **Pre-orders** opened in India on 12 September 2025 and phones went on sale on 19 September 2025.
- **iOS 26** arrived as a free update on 15 September 2025.

Apple also listed accessories in India, including the TechWoven case at Rs 5,900, the Clear case at Rs 4,900 and a 40W Dynamic Power Adapter at Rs 3,900.

## Why Apple Raised Prices in India

Apple did not give a reason. Analysts quoted by <a href="https://www.digit.in/features/mobile-phones/apple-hikes-iphone-prices-across-the-board-in-india-as-analysts-flag-growing-premium-gap.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Digit</a> pointed to three factors:

1. **Component and memory costs** rose globally through 2026, and Android brands had been raising prices since November 2025.
2. **The rupee weakened** by around 8 percent against the US dollar over the year. Techarc's Faisal Kawoosa estimated that roughly five percentage points of the hike came from currency alone.
3. **Brand strength:** Apple can pass on costs without losing its core buyers, according to the same analysts.

Kailash Lakhyani of the All India Mobile Retailers Association said this was the first time Apple had raised prices on existing models in this way.

## What This Means If You Are Buying Now

The hike changes the usual "buy last year's model cheaper" logic. A few practical points from the numbers above:

- **The new Pro costs more too.** The iPhone 18 Pro starts at Rs 1,64,900 and the 18 Pro Max at Rs 1,79,900, both about Rs 30,000 above the 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max launch prices.
- **The iPhone 17 at Rs 99,900** is Rs 17,000 above its own launch price, so check sale offers and bank discounts before paying list price.
- **The iPhone 17e at Rs 79,900** is now the cheapest new iPhone 17 family model.
- **Older stock** at some retailers may still carry the old price, so compare a few stores.
- If you are fine with Android, mid-range phones like the <a href="/samsung-galaxy-a56-5g-big-screen-long-battery/">Samsung Galaxy A56 5G</a> cost far less, though they are a different class of phone.

## iPhone 18 Pro and the Foldable iPhone Duo

The September 2026 event also brought the iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable, starting at Rs 2,99,900 and going up to Rs 4,49,900 in India. Counterpoint's Tarun Pathak told Digit the first Duo units are imported, not made in India, so they attract import duty plus 18 percent GST.

After the September 2026 revision, the iPhone 17 costs Rs 99,900 for 256GB and Rs 1,24,900 for 512GB on Apple's India store.

**What was the iPhone 17 launch price in India?**
