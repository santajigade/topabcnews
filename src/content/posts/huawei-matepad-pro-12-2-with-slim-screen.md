The Huawei MatePad Pro 12.2 (2025) is a 12.2-inch tablet with a Tandem OLED screen, a body 5.5 mm thick and a weight of about 508 g. Huawei announced it on 15 May 2025 and took it to Europe the same month at **849.99 euros for 12GB/256GB** and **999.99 euros for 12GB/512GB**. It has not gone on sale in India. When Huawei came back to the Indian tablet market in August 2026, it brought cheaper models, not this one.

## Update (20 November 2026)

When we first published this story in September 2025, it gave an Indian price of ₹62,999 and several specs that do not match Huawei's official listing. We have checked every figure again and corrected them. The main fixes:

- **India price:** we could not verify a ₹62,999 India launch. Huawei's Indian tablet comeback in 2026 covered the MatePad 11.5 and MatePad SE 11 only.
- **Connectivity:** GSMArena lists no cellular version, so there is no 5G. Huawei's spec sheet gives Bluetooth 5.2, not 5.3.
- **Cameras:** the 2025 model has a 50 MP main and an 8 MP wide-angle camera at the back, with an 8 MP front camera.
- **Software:** it ships with HarmonyOS 4.3, not 4.0.
- **Body:** it is 5.5 mm thick and weighs about 508 g, not 6.5 mm and 620 g. Huawei sells it in Green and Black.

## MatePad Pro 12.2 Specs Huawei Confirms

These figures come from <a href="https://consumer.huawei.com/ae-en/tablets/matepad-pro-12-2/specs/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Huawei's official specification page</a>, with refresh rate and charging from GSMArena.

| Spec | MatePad Pro 12.2 (2025) |
|---|---|
| Display | 12.2-inch Tandem OLED, 2800 x 1840, 274 ppi, 2000 nits peak |
| Refresh rate | 144Hz (GSMArena) |
| Processor | Kirin T92 A, octa-core |
| Memory | 12GB RAM with 256GB or 512GB storage |
| Rear cameras | 50 MP main (f/1.8) plus 8 MP wide-angle (f/2.2) |
| Front camera | 8 MP (f/2.0) |
| Battery | 10,100 mAh typical (rated 10,000 mAh) |
| Charging | 100W wired (GSMArena) |
| Software | HarmonyOS 4.3 |
| Size and weight | 271.25 x 182.53 x 5.5 mm, about 508 g (512 g PaperMatte edition) |
| Audio | 4 speakers, 4 microphones |
| In the box | Huawei Glide Keyboard |

The 512GB model comes with Huawei's PaperMatte finish, which cuts reflections. Ubergizmo also reports a side fingerprint reader and support for the third-generation M-Pencil.

## What Changed From the 2024 Model

The first MatePad Pro 12.2 launched globally in September 2024 at a Barcelona event, priced at 849 euros (700 pounds) for 256GB and 999 euros (800 pounds) for the 512GB PaperMatte version, GSMArena reported. The 2025 refresh launched at almost the same euro prices, so the changes are on the inside.

| Feature | 2024 model | 2025 model |
|---|---|---|
| Main rear camera | 13 MP | 50 MP |
| Software at launch | HarmonyOS 4.2 | HarmonyOS 4.3 |
| Processor | Kirin T92 A | Kirin T92 A |
| Charging | Up to 100W | 100W |
| Thickness and weight | 5.5 mm, about 508 g | 5.5 mm, about 508 g |

The 2024 figures are from <a href="https://consumer.huawei.com/uk/tablets/matepad-pro-12-2-2024/specs/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Huawei UK's 2024 spec sheet</a>. The biggest change is the main camera, which goes from 13 MP to 50 MP. The chip, body size and charging speed stay the same.

## Why the Pro 12.2 Is Not in Indian Shops

Huawei had not launched a smartphone in India since 2020, <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/huawei-tablet-launch-india-matepad-11-5-specs-126031600702_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard reported in March 2026</a>, when a Huawei teaser page appeared on Flipkart. Before 2026, the company's last tablet launch in India was the MatePad T8 in 2020, according to 91mobiles.

The comeback arrived in August 2026. Notebookcheck reported that the MatePad SE 11 (8GB/128GB) and MatePad 11.5 (8GB/256GB) went on sale on Flipkart at maximum retail prices of ₹25,999 and ₹45,999. None of the reports on the Indian launch mention the MatePad Pro 12.2.

There is a practical catch for Indian buyers. US export restrictions stop Huawei from licensing Google Mobile Services, so Huawei devices do not come with the Play Store, Gmail or YouTube in the usual way. 91mobiles notes that workarounds exist but are not always smooth. US export rules on chips are also behind other tech stories we track, such as the <a href="/us-remote-access-ai-chip-rule-india-impact/">new US rule on AI chips for overseas data centres</a>.

## MatePad Pro 12.2 Against the MatePad 11.5 Sold in India

If you are in India and like Huawei's hardware, the MatePad 11.5 is the model you can actually buy. Here is how it compares on paper. The 11.5 figures are from Business Standard and Notebookcheck.

| Feature | MatePad Pro 12.2 (2025) | MatePad 11.5 |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | 12.2-inch Tandem OLED, 144Hz | 11.5-inch LCD, up to 120Hz |
| RAM and storage | 12GB, 256GB or 512GB | 8GB, 256GB in India |
| Rear camera | 50 MP plus 8 MP | 13 MP |
| Battery | 10,100 mAh | 10,100 mAh |
| Thickness and weight | 5.5 mm, 508 g | 6.1 mm, 515 g |
| Software | HarmonyOS 4.3 | HarmonyOS 4.3 |
| Price | 849.99 euros in Europe | ₹45,999 MRP in India |

Battery size and software are the same on both. What the Pro adds is the OLED panel, more RAM, a better camera and a slightly thinner body. None of the reports we checked mention plans to sell the Pro 12.2 in India. For now you would have to buy it abroad, and it would come without Google apps.
