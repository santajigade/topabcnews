Indian Olympic Association (IOA) president PT Usha has lost the Sportspersons of Merit (SOM) election held on Monday, November 9, a result that leaves her path to a second term as IOA chief uncertain. Former badminton star Saina Nehwal, long jumper Anju Bobby George, ex-hockey captain Rani Rampal and former archer Dola Banerjee won the four women's seats, while Usha finished fifth. The vote matters because, under the IOA constitution, only an SOM or a General Assembly member can stand for president in the December 5 Executive Council elections.

## How the Women's SOM Vote Went

Six candidates contested four seats in the women's category. Voting took place virtually from 9 am to 5 pm, and all 80 SOMs on the electoral roll cast their votes, according to the result issued by Electoral Officer Sanjay Kumar Srivastava and reported by PTI.

| Candidate | Sport | Votes | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anju Bobby George | Athletics (long jump) | 53 | Elected |
| Rani Rampal | Hockey | 50 | Elected |
| Dola Banerjee | Archery | 45 | Elected |
| Saina Nehwal | Badminton | 43 | Elected |
| PT Usha | Athletics | 40 | Not elected |
| Anitha Paul Durai | Not stated | 6 | Not elected |

Usha missed the fourth seat by three votes. The result is a sharp turn for the athletics legend, who reached the IOA's top post through the same SOM route four years ago.

## Men's Seats Filled Without a Contest

The men's side was settled two days earlier. On Saturday, November 7, shooter Gagan Narang, Vijender Singh, Yogeshwar Dutt and Kamlesh Mehta were elected unopposed after eight other candidates pulled out.

Those who withdrew included World Athletics vice president Adille Sumariwalla, Achanta Sharath Kamal and B Sai Praneeth. PTI described the withdrawals as happening in "inexplicable circumstances." Several women candidates, including Ashwini Nachappa and Ragina Kiro, also stepped aside.

The field had started with 27 contestants for eight SOM seats. It was trimmed to 23 on November 5 before the weekend withdrawals.

### Key highlights

- **Election date:** November 9, 2026 (women's SOM category)
- **Seats:** Four women's seats and four men's seats
- **Voters:** All 80 SOMs, 40 men and 40 women
- **Winners:** Saina Nehwal, Anju Bobby George, Dola Banerjee, Rani Rampal (women); Gagan Narang, Vijender Singh, Yogeshwar Dutt, Kamlesh Mehta (men)
- **Next step:** IOA Executive Council polls on December 5

## Complaints Over Campaigning

The vote was marked by complaints about the Model Code of Conduct. Usha wrote to the Electoral Officer alleging that Dola Banerjee had broken the rules. Her complaint centred on a WhatsApp message, sent from an account in Banerjee's name, that backed a "New Bharat Panel" and listed four candidates along with their ballot serial and roll numbers.

On the other side, Commonwealth Games weightlifting gold medallist Sathish Sivalingam, himself an SOM voter, filed his own complaint. He alleged an organised effort to sway electoral roll members in favour of a candidate who was also the IOA president, and claimed IOA resources were being used for it. He did not name Usha directly.

No ruling on either complaint had been reported at the time of writing.

## What the Result Means for Usha's Presidency

Usha made history in 2022 when she was <a href="https://www.espn.com/olympics/story/_/id/35223920/pt-usha-elected-unopposed-becomes-first-woman-ioa-indian-olympic-association-president" target="_blank" rel="noopener">elected unopposed as the first woman president of the IOA</a>. She entered the electoral college then as a Sportsperson of Merit.

Without an SOM seat this time, her only remaining route is a nomination to the General Assembly by a National Sports Federation. Reports suggest that is unlikely, as she is not expected to win one of the two representative seats from the Athletics Federation of India.

The eight newly elected SOMs now join the General Assembly. They can vote and contest in the December 5 polls, and one man and one woman from the group will also take seats on the Executive Council. PTI reported that Gagan Narang, the London 2012 Olympic bronze medallist in 10m air rifle, is seen as a strong contender for the presidency.

## Why the IOA Election Matters for Indian Sport

The IOA is India's national Olympic body and a member of the <a href="https://anocolympic.org/nocs-directory/indian-olympic-association/16379" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Association of National Olympic Committees</a>. It selects and sends Indian teams to the Olympics, Asian Games and Commonwealth Games, and works with national federations on athlete preparation.

Its leadership has a direct effect on how India plans for major multi-sport events. Indian athletes are already competing on the global stage this season, as seen in our report on <a href="/youth-olympics-2026-india-medal-tally-taniya-silver/">India's medal run at the Youth Olympics 2026</a>.

The SOM system was designed to give former top athletes a stronger say in running Indian sport. This year's winners, from badminton, athletics, hockey, archery, shooting and other sports, will give well-known former athletes a strong voice in the new General Assembly.

## The Road to the December 5 Polls

All eyes now turn to the Executive Council elections on December 5, when the IOA will choose its next president and office bearers. Key questions include whether any federation nominates Usha, how the pending complaints are handled, and whether a consensus candidate emerges from the new group of athlete members. For now, Monday's vote has opened the race for the top job in Indian Olympic sport.
