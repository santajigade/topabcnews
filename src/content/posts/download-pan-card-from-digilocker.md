You can get a verified digital copy of your PAN in DigiLocker by fetching the "PAN Verification Record" issued by the Income Tax Department. All you need is a DigiLocker account, your PAN number and your gender as recorded with the department. Documents pushed into DigiLocker by the issuing department are treated as equal to the original paper document under Rule 9A of the Digital Locker rules, notified in February 2017.

If you need a PDF of the e-PAN itself instead, there are two other official routes: the Income Tax e-Filing portal, which is free for people who got their PAN through Instant e-PAN, and Protean eGov Technologies, where the free download covers PANs allotted or changed in the last 30 days.

## Three Official Ways to Get a Digital PAN

| Route | Who can use it | What you need | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| DigiLocker app or website | Any PAN holder with a DigiLocker account | DigiLocker login, PAN number, gender | PAN Verification Record in "Issued Documents" |
| Income Tax e-Filing portal | People whose PAN came through Instant e-PAN | 12-digit Aadhaar and OTP on the Aadhaar-linked mobile | e-PAN to view or download |
| Protean | PANs allotted or changed in the last 30 days (free link) | Acknowledgement number or PAN, date of birth, OTP | e-PAN PDF |

## How to Fetch Your PAN in the DigiLocker App

First make sure you have a DigiLocker account. It is linked to your Aadhaar number, and new users sign up with the mobile number linked to Aadhaar and an OTP. Keep your 6-digit DigiLocker security PIN handy too, since the Income Tax e-PAN flow asks for it. Then:

1. Open the DigiLocker app and log in.
2. Tap the **Search** icon at the bottom right of the home screen.
3. Search for **Income Tax Department, Govt. of India**.
4. Select **PAN Verification Record**.
5. Enter your PAN and select your gender.
6. Tick the consent box and tap **Get Document**.
7. The record is saved under **Issued Documents**, and you can share it from there.

DigiLocker's own guidance says the name on a document must match the name on your Aadhaar. If the fetch fails, check that your PAN and Aadhaar records carry the same spelling of your name.

## Download e-PAN from the Income Tax Portal

The <a href="https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/Instant%20e-PAN-faq?mobile-app=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Income Tax Department's Instant e-PAN service</a> is free, and you can check status and download the e-PAN without logging in:

1. Go to the e-Filing portal and click **Instant e-PAN** under Quick Links.
2. On the **Check Status / Download e-PAN** tile, click **Continue**.
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and click **Continue**.
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile and click **Validate Aadhaar OTP and Continue**.
5. If the e-PAN has been allotted, click **View e-PAN** or **Download e-PAN**.

If you already have an e-Filing account, log in and go to **Services**, then **View / Download e-PAN**, and verify with an Aadhaar OTP. The same e-Filing account is what you use for returns; see our guide to the <a href="/itr-filing-deadline-2025-26/">ITR filing deadline and late fees</a>.

Instant e-PAN is also how adults without a PAN can get one quickly. The portal says it is for people with a valid Aadhaar, an Aadhaar-linked mobile number and DigiLocker access. It is not open to minors, to anyone who already holds a PAN, or to representative assessees.

## Download e-PAN from Protean

1. On the Protean website, choose **Download e-PAN** under PAN services.
2. Pick the option that matches when your PAN was allotted: within the last 30 days or earlier.
3. Enter your 15-digit acknowledgement number (or your PAN, Aadhaar and date of birth), plus the captcha.
4. Choose OTP on email, mobile or both, enter it and click **Validate**.
5. Click **Download PDF**.

The PDF is password protected. The password is your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, so a birthday of 15 August 1990 becomes 15081990.

If you want a printed card instead, <a href="https://onlineservices.proteantech.in/paam/ReprintEPan.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Protean's reprint page</a> charges Rs 50 for dispatch within India and Rs 959 for dispatch abroad, including taxes. The card goes to the communication address in the Income Tax Department's latest records. Reprints are open to people whose last PAN application was processed by Protean and to those who got their PAN through Instant e-PAN.

## Is a DigiLocker PAN as Good as the Card?

This is where most confusion lies, so it helps to know the difference between DigiLocker's two sections.

- **Issued Documents** hold digitally signed files sent directly by the issuing department, such as the PAN Verification Record. A January 2018 <a href="https://cdn.digilocker.gov.in/assets/img/circulars/Letter-to-All-State-Governments-UTs.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MeitY letter to all states</a> says such documents are deemed at par with original physical documents under Rule 9A.
- **Uploaded Documents** are your own scans, stored in personal space. A scanned PAN you upload yourself is not treated as an authentic issued document, and DigiLocker recommends using Issued Documents for official work.

DigiLocker has also admitted, in answers to user questions, that some officials still refuse digital copies and that wider acceptance is ongoing work. Keep the e-PAN PDF or the physical card handy when you deal with an office you have not used before.

DigiLocker itself was launched on 1 July 2015 under the Digital India programme, and gives each user 1 GB for uploaded files, up from 100 MB at launch.

## PAN Card Download: Common Questions

**Is a PAN downloaded from DigiLocker valid?**
Yes. A PAN fetched into Issued Documents comes from the Income Tax Department and is treated as equal to the original under Rule 9A. A scan you upload yourself does not carry that status.

**What is the password to open the e-PAN PDF from Protean?**
Your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, for example 15081990 for 15 August 1990.

**Is Instant e-PAN free?**
Yes. The Income Tax Department's FAQ says the service is free of cost.

**Can I get Instant e-PAN if I already have a PAN?**
No. It is only for people who have not been allotted a PAN, are not minors and have an Aadhaar linked to an active mobile number.
