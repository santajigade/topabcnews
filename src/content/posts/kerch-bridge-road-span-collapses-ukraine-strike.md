Ukraine says it has brought down part of the road section of the Kerch Bridge, the 19 km crossing that links mainland Russia to occupied Crimea. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on Tuesday, November 17, called the strike an "unprecedented" operation carried out with drones and missiles over Monday night. Ukraine's General Staff said an arch and a span of the road section collapsed, cutting the road link, while the rail line next to it was still standing with a damaged arch. Russia says the bridge will be repaired. The attack comes two days before US President Donald Trump is due to meet Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in China, where the war is expected to be discussed.

## What Ukraine Says It Hit, Step by Step

According to the Associated Press, Zelenskyy described a seven stage operation that ran over several weeks. Ukrainian forces first went after Russian air defence systems, radars and electronic warfare equipment around the crossing before the final overnight strike.

He gave a breakdown of the weapons used in that last wave:

- **Road section:** long range drones built in Ukraine.
- **Rail section:** Neptune missiles and other drones.
- **The "final blow":** Flamingo precision guided missiles, a Ukrainian made cruise missile.

Zelenskyy did not give details of the damage himself. The General Staff said the "symbol of the occupation has cracked." Footage he shared, as reported by <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/ukrainian-drone-attack-kills-two-in-crimea-as-kyiv-targets-kerch-bridge" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera</a>, showed the main road arch and part of the roadway down. Zelenskyy said the strike was a reply to Russian attacks on Ukraine's own infrastructure.

This was the second night in a row the bridge was targeted. Ukrainian outlet Militarnyi reported that a strike on Sunday night had already tilted the arch over the shipping channel, and road traffic was briefly suspended then.

## What Still Works and What Russia Says

The two sides describe the damage very differently, and full details have not been independently confirmed.

| Question | What has been reported |
|---|---|
| Road traffic | Halted, said Crimea's Russian installed governor Sergei Aksyonov |
| Rail traffic | Restored by Tuesday afternoon, Aksyonov said, without mentioning damage |
| Russian official line | Investigative Committee said the bridge "sustained damage" from drones |
| Kremlin | Spokesman Dmitry Peskov said there is "no doubt" the bridge will be restored |
| Casualties in Crimea | At least two people killed in the drone attack, Aksyonov said on Telegram |
| Shipping channel | Rubble has fallen into the strait's only navigation channel, The Maritime Executive reported |

The rail line matters because it carries heavy freight. After the first major attack in 2022, the operator had to cut the load the rail span could take, according to The Maritime Executive. The same outlet notes that repeated Ukrainian strikes on the nearby ferry crossing had already made the bridge more important to Russia for moving supplies.

## Four Attacks on One Bridge: How This Strike Compares

The Kerch Bridge was <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/10/9/crimea-bridge-resumes-traffic-after-blast" target="_blank" rel="noopener">opened by Putin in 2018</a>, four years after Russia annexed Crimea, and carries both cars and trains across the strait between the Black Sea and the Sea of Azov. It is the only bridge connecting southern Russia to the peninsula. That is why Ukraine has gone after it repeatedly. Here is how the attacks since the full scale invasion compare, based on reports from Al Jazeera, Euronews and AP:

| Date | Method | Reported result |
|---|---|---|
| October 8, 2022 | Truck bomb on the road deck | Three killed, part of the road fell into the sea, fuel tankers on a train caught fire |
| July 2023 | Naval drones hit support pillars | Damaged but stayed in use; repairs took about 10 months |
| June 3, 2025 | About 1,100 kg of underwater explosives planted by Ukraine's SBU security service | Underwater supports damaged, bridge briefly closed |
| November 16 and 17, 2026 | Weeks of strikes on air defences, then drones plus Neptune and Flamingo missiles | Road arch and span collapsed, road link cut; rail arch damaged |

The difference this time is the method. Earlier attacks relied on a single blast or sabotage. This one, by Ukraine's account, was an open military operation that first blinded the defences around what has been described as one of Russia's most heavily protected sites, and then used home made missiles to finish the job.

## Why the Bridge Matters Beyond the Battlefield

For readers trying to make sense of the headlines, three points explain the weight placed on this crossing.

**Supply line.** AP describes the bridge as a key route for both military and civilian supplies into Crimea and on to Russian troops in southern Ukraine. With the road cut, more load falls on the rail line, the ferries and the land route through occupied Ukrainian territory.

**Shipping.** The bridge spans the only navigable channel into the Sea of Azov. Since 2018 its low arch has limited the size of ships that can pass, which gave Russia a way to restrict traffic to Ukrainian Azov ports. If debris blocks the channel, all ships using that route are affected until it is cleared.

**Symbol.** The bridge was built as a symbol of the 2014 annexation, and Putin personally led the first convoy across it. Damage to it carries political weight in both capitals.

## Part of a Wider Escalation Before Thursday's Summit

The strike came during one of the heaviest exchanges of long range fire in the war. Russia's Defence Ministry said it downed 1,441 Ukrainian drones on Monday night, after a record 1,336 the night before. In Kyiv, Zelenskyy said seven Russian ballistic missiles hit two of the city's main bridges, and Mayor Vitali Klitschko said the North and South bridges were closed while experts inspected them. AP reported at least two people killed in the capital.

On the diplomatic track, Ukrainian, US and European negotiators held a video call on Monday, according to Ukraine's Rustem Umerov. Trump plans to raise the war with Xi and Putin at the meeting in China on Thursday, November 19. ## What to Watch Next

The key questions now are how long road repairs will take and whether the shipping channel is cleared quickly. After the 2023 strike, Russia needed roughly 10 months to fully fix the damage, and this time a whole road arch has come down. How each side frames the strike at Thursday's summit will also show whether it hardens positions or adds pressure for a deal.
