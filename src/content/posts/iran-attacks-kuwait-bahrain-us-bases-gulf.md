Iran attacked the Ali Al Salem air base in Kuwait, which hosts US forces, early on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, and launched several more attacks on Bahrain, home of the US Navy's 5th Fleet. Iranian state broadcasting said the military fired at air defence systems and drone and missile launch platforms. The strikes followed a US jet strike a day earlier on a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island, which itself came after Iran targeted a US radar in Bahrain.

According to the [Associated Press](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/iran-widens-gulf-attacks-by-targeting-u-s-air-base-in-kuwait-and-sites-in-bahrain), these were the first such attacks in about two months. The war began on February 28, 2026, when Israel and the US attacked Iran, and it had been in a relative lull. Earlier this month, President Donald Trump described the war as [effectively over](/trump-iran-war-effectively-over-qatar-talks/).

## What Each Side Says

Iran says its recent attacks answer US drone surveillance and a strike on Iranian communications towers. Kuwait's Foreign Ministry condemned the repeated attacks as criminal and said they breach international law and UN Security Council Resolution 2817. Bahrain's Interior Ministry issued two shelter alerts on Wednesday before giving the all clear.

The Gulf Cooperation Council's Secretary-General Jasem Albudaiwi condemned the attacks and asked the UN Security Council to act, [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-war-iran-attacks-bahrain-kuwait-drone-threats-keep-gulf-on-high-alert-as-us-hits-irans-qeshm-1.500751444). Saudi Arabia and the UAE also condemned the strikes. In Washington, Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao told senators he could not give a full damage assessment in Bahrain because it was "a nonpermissive area for us to go."

The US signals have been mixed. AP reported that Trump said new strikes were highly unlikely, while also sending a third aircraft carrier and thousands more troops to the region. The US keeps up a naval blockade of Iran and escorts tankers through the Strait of Hormuz.

## Why Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Base Matters

Ali Al Salem has been one of the most frequent targets of the war. An AP analysis found Iran struck the base at least 30 times, damaging or destroying at least 15 aircraft hangars, many warehouses and 10 buildings near a residential area. Through August, AP counted more than 115 drone or missile strikes, claimed by Iran or groups it backs, that damaged structures on 26 military bases and other sites holding US equipment.

Analyst Danny Citrinowicz told AP that Iran seems to be escalating in a measured, deliberate way, and that calm would need either a negotiated deal or an end to the US blockade.

## What It Means for Indian Seafarers and Travellers

India has a direct stake because so many Indians crew ships in these waters. The Tara Gas, a Panama-flagged LPG tanker, had 24 Indian seafarers aboard. India's Ministry of External Affairs said 21 were brought ashore at Khasab, two were taken to hospital, and one is still missing while Omani authorities search, according to gCaptain and [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/middle-east/2026/11/17/indian-sailor-missing-strait-of-hormuz.html). The MEA called the attacks "deeply worrisome" and asked for free navigation under international law. Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal said officials are working with the ship manager and regional rescue agencies.

The wider toll is heavy. MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal has said 22 Indian nationals have been killed in the West Asia conflict since February, 14 of them seafarers. We reported last week on [an LNG tanker hitting a mine in Hormuz](/hormuz-lng-tanker-hits-mine-irgc-says/).

For travellers, Gulf carriers have cut routes into Saudi Arabia, where Houthi missiles are also targeting Riyadh:

- **flydubai:** Riyadh and Abha flights cancelled until November 27, reduced Dammam service.
- **Etihad:** Dammam and Riyadh flights cancelled until November 30.

Indians flying through Gulf hubs to Saudi cities should check with their airline before leaving home. The fighting in Yemen is a separate front; we covered the [Houthi advance towards Aden](/houthis-advance-60-km-from-aden-yemen/) earlier this week.

## Wider Front Lines

The Kuwait and Bahrain strikes came as Saudi-led coalition jets hit Houthi targets in Yemen on Wednesday. The Houthi health ministry said at least 17 people were killed, including 15 at a sponge factory in Hodeidah. Coalition air defences also intercepted two Houthi cruise missiles fired at Riyadh. Iran's judiciary chief said this week that the country is better equipped than when the war began, while CENTCOM commander Adm. Brad Cooper met Israeli military and intelligence leaders in Tel Aviv on Wednesday.
