In mid October 2025, gold in India touched about **Rs 1.28 lakh per 10 grams** as global prices crossed $4,250 an ounce, the peak this post first reported. Prices kept climbing well past that level into 2026, then turned lower in November 2026. On 17 November 2026, 24K gold was quoted at about **Rs 1,34,802 per 10 grams** before GST, down 8.7 percent in a month but still 9.7 percent higher than a year earlier.

## Update (21 November 2026)

Gold has fallen fast this month. Between 7 and 17 November 2026, 24K gold dropped from Rs 1,42,860 to Rs 1,34,802, a fall of about Rs 8,000 or 5.6 percent. Silver fell harder, from Rs 1,85,630 to Rs 1,70,210 per kg, and was down nearly 20 percent in a month. On Comex, gold traded near $3,873 an ounce on 17 November, below the $4,000 plus levels of November 2025. Our report on <a href="/gold-price-14-month-low-fed-minutes/">gold falling to a 14 month low after the Fed minutes</a> explains the latest trigger, a stronger dollar and fears of US rate hikes.

## Why Gold Rose So Much in 2025

When gold was setting records in late 2025, the drivers were global. <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/markets/commodities/gold-prices-slip-10-silver-down-100-yellow-metal-trading-at-1-22-010-125111000049_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Standard</a> reported on 10 November 2025 that traders expected another US Federal Reserve rate cut in December 2025, and weak US data had raised worries about global growth. Lower interest rates make gold, which pays no interest, more attractive. Spot gold was near $4,028 an ounce that day.

Samco's analysis put the long run move in perspective: 24K gold was about Rs 7,638 per 10 grams in 2005 and above Rs 1,25,000 by September 2025, a rise of roughly 1,200 percent in 20 years, with gains in 16 of those 20 years.

## Why It Is Falling Now

The 2026 fall has the opposite cause. When the dollar strengthens and markets expect US rates to stay high or rise, gold loses its shine for global investors. Indian prices follow the global price, adjusted for the rupee. That is why Indian rates moved with Comex on 17 November, though by a smaller percentage.

Making charges vary from jeweller to jeweller, so ask for them in writing. Compared with 7 November, a 10 gram 22K purchase on 17 November cost about Rs 7,400 less before charges. Compared with mid November 2025, it costs about Rs 12,000 more.

## Points to Check Before You Buy

- **Check the purity mark.** Hallmarked jewellery shows the BIS mark and purity grade.
- **Compare the day's rate.** City rates differ slightly; on 18 November 2025, Chennai was the costliest of seven big cities and Delhi the cheapest.
- **Ask for a breakup** of gold value, making charges and GST on the bill.
- **Do not chase a single day.** Gold swung by thousands of rupees within a week in November 2026.
