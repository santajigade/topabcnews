Russian cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone came down inside southeast Poland early on Friday, November 20, during one of Russia's heaviest overnight attacks on Ukraine. Nobody was hurt. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it a deliberate act, because the weapons fell not far from where thousands of European troops were holding drills to prepare for a possible post-war mission in Ukraine. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte was due in Warsaw on Saturday, November 21.

Poland has not asked for formal NATO consultations under Article 4, and analysts quoted by Al Jazeera do not expect the alliance's mutual defence clause, Article 5, to be triggered.

## What Fell, and Where

Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said Poland was investigating three sites in the Lublin and Subcarpathian regions:

| Site | Object reported | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Nowy Lipowiec, Bilgoraj County | Kh-101 cruise missile | Fell in fields near a forest and caught fire |
| Rudnik nad Sanem | Second missile | Named by the defence minister; AFP described it as suspected |
| Molodycz | Jet-powered drone | Named by the defence minister |

The Kyiv Independent reported that Poland's operational command switched on air defences at around 3:30 am local time, and air raid alerts went out in the two border regions. A resident near Nowy Lipowiec told broadcaster TVN her house shook at about 6:30 am when the missile hit, AFP reported.

The Kh-101 is a long-range, air-launched Russian cruise missile in service since 2012 and designed to fly under radar, the Kyiv Independent noted. AFP said one Kh-101 had been confirmed, while officials were still checking the other two objects.

The strike on Ukraine that night was large. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia fired more than 400 drones and at least 110 missiles, 27 of them ballistic, according to <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/russian-missiles-hit-poland-what-happened-and-could-nato-respond" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera's explainer</a>. At least eight civilians were killed in Ukraine.

## Why Warsaw Calls It Deliberate

Poland's case rests on timing. Since November 9, troops from 11 countries, including France, the UK, Germany, Spain, Sweden and Turkey, have been taking part in a two-week "Coalition of the Willing" exercise in southeast Poland, with activity around Nowa Deba. Kosiniak-Kamysz said the presence of visiting defence ministers and eight chiefs of staff at the time meant the overlap was no accident.

Tusk told reporters that three Russian objects had entered Polish airspace and that one exploded. He said Warsaw was certain the weapons were Russian and described the incident as "an intentional provocation", AFP reported. He also said Poland would give the United States access to the debris.

Poland asked allies for AWACS surveillance aircraft and F-35 fighters. Two Swedish Gripen jets and a Dutch tanker aircraft were deployed, the Kyiv Independent said. The Kremlin had not commented by Friday afternoon, according to AFP.

## How NATO and Washington Responded

Rutte called the incident a "grave violation" of Polish airspace and said NATO jets had been scrambled. He was expected to give Tusk recommendations on strengthening the eastern flank during his Warsaw visit.

The reaction from the White House was different. A day before the strike, President Donald Trump told Fox News that Putin was "landing drones in Poland" and sending "a couple of little missiles, by mistake", the Kyiv Independent reported. Tusk replied on X with a sharp line asking God to protect Poland from such friends.

Denmark expelled Russia's defence attache the same day over an alleged plot to kill a Ukrainian rapper near Copenhagen. Estonia had already said it will [shut its road crossings with Russia from December 1](/estonia-closes-russia-border-crossings-december-1/).

## Article 4 vs Article 5: What Each One Does

Both articles of the <a href="https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/official_texts_17120.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">North Atlantic Treaty</a> come up whenever a weapon lands on NATO soil, but they work differently.

| | Article 4 | Article 5 |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A request for consultations among allies | Collective defence: an attack on one is an attack on all |
| Who can trigger it | Any member that feels its security is threatened | A political decision needing agreement of all 32 members |
| Used by Poland before? | Yes, in September 2025 after Russian drones entered its airspace | No |
| Used this time? | Not invoked so far | Analysts say probably not |

Instead of Article 4, Poland is asking for more support through NATO's existing Eastern Sentry mission, Al Jazeera reported. Analyst Harry Nedelcu of Rasmussen Global told the outlet Warsaw had stopped short of formal consultations. Article 5 would need every ally to agree, and the analysts Al Jazeera spoke to did not expect it to be triggered.

## How This Compares With Przewodow in 2022

Russian weapons, or weapons first blamed on Russia, have strayed into Poland before. The best known case came on November 15, 2022.

| | Przewodow, November 2022 | Southeast Poland, November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| What fell | One Russian-made missile, as first reported | Two Kh-101 cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone, per Poland's defence minister |
| Casualties | Two people killed at a grain-drying site | No injuries |
| Context | Russia fired at least 85 missiles at Ukraine that day, Zelenskyy said | More than 400 drones and at least 110 missiles, Zelenskyy said |
| Russia's response | Defence ministry denied striking near the border | No Kremlin comment by Friday afternoon |
| Poland's line | Summoned the Russian ambassador and demanded explanations | Called it an intentional provocation tied to troop drills |

The <a href="https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/poland-says-russian-made-missile-fell-on-its-soil-killing-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AP report from 2022</a> described that blast as the first time in the war that Russian weapons had come down on a NATO country. This time Warsaw is treating the incident as a provocation timed to the European drills, not as stray fire.

## What to Watch Next

Three things will shape what happens after Rutte's visit: whether forensic work on the debris confirms both missiles as Russian, whether Poland moves from Eastern Sentry requests to a formal Article 4 call, and whether Moscow responds at all. Putin has also pledged retaliation for Ukraine's strike on the [Kerch Bridge](/kerch-bridge-road-span-collapses-ukraine-strike/).
