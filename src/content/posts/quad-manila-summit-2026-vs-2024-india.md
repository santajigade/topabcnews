The leaders of India, the United States, Japan and Australia met in Manila on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, holding their first Quad summit in more than two years. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, President Donald Trump, Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese talked for about 90 minutes on the last day of the ASEAN summit and issued a joint press release. The meeting launched a new maritime surveillance effort and a business forum, but the bigger story is what the four leaders chose to leave out compared with their last summit.

## Why This Meeting Took So Long

The previous leaders' summit was held in Wilmington, Delaware, on September 21, 2024, hosted by then President Joe Biden, according to the <a href="https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/statements-releases/2024/09/12/statement-by-white-house-press-secretary-karine-jean-pierre-on-the-2024-quad-leaders-summit" target="_blank" rel="noopener">White House statement on the 2024 summit</a>. India was due to host the next one in 2025, but it never took place. <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/26/as-trump-woos-china-the-quad-alliance-drifts-toward-irrelevance" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Al Jazeera reported in May</a> that the New Delhi summit failed to happen amid diplomatic tensions and competing priorities, even though Modi had personally invited Trump in June 2025.

So the Manila meeting was partly about showing the group still works. It was also the first time the four leaders gathered in a country that is not a Quad member, according to The Wire. Fittingly, Manila is where Quad officials first met in 2007 and where the dialogue was revived in 2017.

## What Was Announced in Manila

The joint release, as reported by ETV Bharat and HNGN, listed these steps:

- **Indo-Pacific Maritime Surveillance Collaboration**: a new effort to share information and improve awareness of what moves at sea.
- **First Quad air patrol exercise in 2027**: P-8 and P-3C maritime patrol aircraft from all four countries will fly together in a Quad setting for the first time. India operates the P-8I.
- **Quad Business Leaders' Forum**: the first edition is planned for early 2027, bringing together companies and officials from the four countries.
- **Quad AI Accelerator**: progress was welcomed on this plan to fund trusted AI access and technical help in the region. Trump said members had put in at least $85 million.
- **Enhanced cybersecurity cooperation**, in response to a sharp rise in cyber incidents in the region.
- **Cable Connectivity and Resilience Partnership**: Australia's contribution now includes electricity cables, and undersea cable projects are planned in the Indian Ocean.

Trump also said he had signed new critical minerals and rare earths deals, but he did not name the partners or the minerals. The leaders pledged to cut dependence on a single supplier for minerals used in chips, electric vehicles, batteries and defence equipment.

## Wilmington 2024 vs Manila 2026, Side by Side

Comparing the two statements, using the wording reported by The Wire and HNGN, shows how the group's tone has shifted under Trump:

| Issue | Wilmington 2024 | Manila 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| South China Sea | "Seriously concerned" about East and South China Seas; criticised militarisation and coast guard tactics | No specific mention; general opposition to changing the status quo "by force or coercion" |
| 2016 arbitral award | Called a "significant milestone" | Dropped |
| Terrorism | Named 26/11 Mumbai and Pathankot attacks | Condemns cross-border terrorism, no attacks named |
| Democracy and shared values | Central theme | Not mentioned |
| Health, climate, education | Part of the agenda | Not included |
| New focus | Broad public goods agenda | Supply chains, critical minerals, AI, maritime security |

The 2026 release also voiced "grave concerns" about economic coercion through arbitrary export restrictions, which The Wire read as an implicit reference to China. In short, the Quad has moved from a values-led club to a more transactional group built around supply chains and security hardware.

## What It Means for India

For New Delhi, several of these shifts are convenient. India has long been wary of the Quad looking like an anti-China military alliance, given its long land border with China. A statement that avoids naming China and focuses on minerals, sea lanes and technology lets India deepen cooperation without being seen as part of a pact.

There are also concrete gains. The 2027 patrol aircraft exercise gives the Indian Navy's P-8I fleet a new framework to train with three partners. Indian Ocean undersea cable projects touch India's own region directly. The minerals push lines up with India's wish to reduce reliance on Chinese rare earths for its electric vehicle and electronics makers.

What India lost is the explicit naming of the Mumbai and Pathankot attacks, which New Delhi had valued as a signal on terrorism from Pakistan. The general reference to cross-border terrorism remains.

Modi called the Quad a "constructive and trusted partnership" and said the meeting should set concrete goals for the next phase. According to The Wire, he also said India is keen to host in the first quarter of 2027, though no date or venue for the next leaders' summit was announced.
