India has pledged Rs 15,000 crore (NPR 24,000 crore) to Nepal, the centrepiece of Prime Minister Balendra Shah's state visit to New Delhi on November 22 and 23. Rs 3,500 crore is a grant for rebuilding after this year's disasters and for new infrastructure. The other Rs 11,500 crore is a rupee-denominated line of credit for energy projects. Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri called it the "largest-ever financial assistance package for Nepal". It was Shah's first visit to India since taking office, and his first bilateral trip abroad as prime minister.

## How the Money Splits

| Component | Indian rupees | Nepali rupees | What it pays for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grant | Rs 3,500 crore | NPR 5,600 crore | Post-disaster reconstruction and new infrastructure projects |
| Line of credit (rupee-denominated) | Rs 11,500 crore | NPR 18,400 crore | Energy sector projects |
| Total | Rs 15,000 crore | NPR 24,000 crore | Specific projects to be chosen jointly |

More than three quarters of the package is credit for energy work rather than grant money. Misri described the credit line as concessional. Only the smaller grant goes to reconstruction, and the <a href="https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/joint-press-release-on-the-state-visit-of-the-prime-minister-of-nepal-to-india/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">joint press release issued by the Prime Minister's Office</a> says the actual projects and terms are still to be worked out between the two governments. The release links the package to the August flood in Nepal's Rasuwa-Bhotekoshi area. Bangladesh's The Business Standard, which reported the visit as a flood package, put the total at about $1.62 billion.

Shah called India a "first responder" for Nepal, according to The Business Standard, and invited Indian investors to the country. He also invited Modi to Nepal. Modi accepted, and dates will be set through diplomatic channels.

## The Eight MoUs, Grouped by What They Do

The two sides exchanged eight memoranda of understanding. The Tribune's ANI report counts 19 outcomes in all, though neither it nor the official release lists them as 19 items.

| Area | Agreement |
|---|---|
| Policing and security training | National Forensic Sciences University (Gandhinagar) with the Nepal Police Crime Investigation Department; Rashtriya Raksha University with Nepal's National Police Academy |
| Civil service | National Centre for Good Governance with Nepal Administrative Staff College, to train Nepali civil servants |
| Health | Health ministries of both countries on health and medicine; a separate pact on Ayurveda and other traditional systems of medicine |
| Space | ISRO with Nepal's Ministry of Information and Communication, for a SATCOM network using the South Asia Satellite |
| Cities and farms | Technical cooperation on sustainable urban development; livestock and fisheries development |

Leaders also adopted a joint vision document titled "Viksit Bharat and Samunnat Nepal", covering trade, connectivity, energy, water, climate, disaster management, start-ups, digital links and space.

## What Changes for Travellers and Traders

Several of the announcements affect people who cross the border, not just the two governments.

- New train to Janakpur: The two prime ministers flagged off the Sarayu-Kamala Express between Ayodhya and Janakpur. <a href="https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/world/india-nepal-announce-19-outcomes-during-pm-balen-shahs-two-day-visit-unveil-rs-15000-crore-package-rail-links" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Tribune's report</a> describes it as a weekly service running from Ayodhya Cantt to Janakpur Dham, serving Gorakhpur, Basti, Raxaul, Sitamarhi, Madhubani and Jaynagar on the way. No timetable has been published in the reports we read.
- Two-way flights on B345: The air route from Kathmandu to Lucknow via Bhairahawa, which currently works in one direction only, will become bidirectional after technical talks.
- Sea access for Nepali cargo: Nepal gets transit trade access to Deendayal Port at Kandla in Gujarat, and later to Paradip Port in Odisha once infrastructure there is ready.
- Banking and phones: Both sides will make it easier for citizens living in the other country to get bank accounts and telecom services. The release gives no timeline.
- Border posts and a bridge: A motorable bridge between Dharchula in Uttarakhand and Darchula in Nepal was opened, and the foundation stone for an integrated check post at Banbasa was laid virtually. Check posts at Bhairahawa, Sunauli and Dodhara-Chandani are under construction, in addition to three already working.

For the planned Raxaul-Kathmandu electrified broad-gauge line, the survey has been finalised. India will also work with Nepal on the East-West Railway, and both sides are exploring rail links for Buddhist and Shiv-Shakti pilgrimage circuits.

## Energy: Lower Arun, Pipelines and Power Lines

The largest single project is the 669 MW Lower Arun hydroelectric project, whose foundation stone was laid virtually during the visit. Other energy items:

- Phase II of the Motihari-Amlekhgunj petroleum pipeline was dedicated, and a foundation stone was laid for a petroleum terminal in Jhapa.
- An early start is planned on a Siliguri-Jhapa pipeline, and gas pipelines will be studied.
- Joint ventures for two cross-border transmission lines, Inaruwa to New Purnea and Dododhara to Bareilly, are progressing.
- The detailed project report for Pancheshwar is to be finalised, and reports for the Saptakoshi and Sunkoshi multipurpose projects are to be speeded up.

The Rs 11,500 crore credit line is reserved for energy projects, but the release does not say which of these it will fund.

## An Offshore IIT Campus and an Agro Park

India will set up an offshore campus of an Indian Institute of Technology in Nepal, the joint release says. Misri put it more cautiously, saying India "will work on establishing" it. No location, intake or start date has been announced. The leaders also welcomed plans for an agro-industrial park in Chitwan, built with Indian grant money.

## What to Watch Next

The size of the package is now set. What it will fund is still open: the grant and the credit line both depend on a list of projects the two governments have yet to agree. Foreign secretaries have also been told to keep working on outstanding boundary issues. Readers planning to use the new Janakpur train or the two-way Lucknow flights should wait for official timetables before booking.
