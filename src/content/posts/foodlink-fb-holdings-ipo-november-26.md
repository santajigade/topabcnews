Foodlink F&B Holdings (India), the Mumbai caterer that runs the Art of Dum, India Bistro, China Bistro and Glocal restaurant brands, opens its IPO on Thursday, November 26. The price band is Rs 181 to Rs 191, one lot is 78 shares, and bidding ends on Monday, November 30. The issue raises up to Rs 360.76 crore, but less than Rs 200 crore of that goes to the company. The rest goes to shareholders who are selling.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Price band | Rs 181 to Rs 191 (face value Rs 2) |
| Lot size | 78 shares |
| One lot at upper band | Rs 14,898 |
| Total issue | Up to Rs 360.76 crore (1,88,87,738 shares) |
| Fresh issue | Up to Rs 192 crore (1,00,52,356 shares) |
| Offer for sale | 88,35,382 shares, up to Rs 168.76 crore |
| Bidding | November 26 to November 30 |
| Allotment | December 1 (tentative) |
| Refunds and demat credit | December 2 |
| Listing | NSE and BSE, December 3 (tentative) |

## What One Lot Costs at Each End of the Band

A retail investor can apply for between 1 and 13 lots; above that the bid moves into the small HNI category. The table works out the cost at both ends of the band, using the 78-share lot and the category limits listed on <a href="https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/foodlink-fb-ipo/2493/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Chittorgarh's IPO page</a>.

| Bid | Shares | At Rs 181 | At Rs 191 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retail minimum, 1 lot | 78 | Rs 14,118 | Rs 14,898 |
| Retail maximum, 13 lots | 1,014 | Rs 1,83,534 | Rs 1,93,674 |
| Small HNI minimum, 14 lots | 1,092 | Rs 1,97,652 | Rs 2,08,572 |
| Small HNI maximum, 67 lots | 5,226 | Rs 9,45,906 | Rs 9,98,166 |

If you bid at the cut-off price, plan for the Rs 191 column. Retail bidders get no more than 10% of the offer. At least 75% is for qualified institutional buyers and up to 15% for non-institutional investors.

## How Much Money Reaches the Company

Of the Rs 360.76 crore, the Rs 168.76 crore offer for sale goes straight to the shareholders who are selling. They include promoter Sanjay Manohar Vazirani, who is selling 22,86,446 shares worth about Rs 43.67 crore, along with investors such as Arpit Khandelwal and V'Ocean Investments, according to <a href="https://upstox.com/news/market-news/ipo/foodlink-f-and-b-holdings-ipo-price-band-set-at-181-191-per-share-check-lot-size-and-key-dates/article-203832/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Upstox</a> and Chittorgarh. The company keeps only the Rs 192 crore fresh issue.

The IPO details set out how that fresh money will be used:

- Rs 50.81 crore into its material subsidiary, Foodlink Global Restaurants & Catering Services, to open four new casual dining restaurants
- Rs 41.50 crore for two new banquet venues
- Rs 21.40 crore for two new centralised kitchens
- Rs 21 crore to repay or prepay loans
- An unspecified sum for general corporate purposes

The promoter group's holding drops from 63.40% before the issue to 49.51% after it, so the public will own just over half the company.

## The Business Behind the Brands

Foodlink was set up in 2003 and began with event and banquet catering for weddings, corporate events and conferences. It also runs Jade Luxury Banquets. As of September 30, 2026, it had 28 casual dining restaurants and cloud kitchens: 15 at 10 locations in two Indian cities and 13 at seven locations in two UAE cities. It has five centralised kitchens in India, in Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Mohali, Hyderabad and New Delhi, and 1,481 employees. Upstox lists Hardcastle Restaurants, Greenply and Zee Entertainment among its event catering clients.

## Profit Swings in the Last Three Years

Restated figures from the IPO details:

| Period | Total income | Profit after tax | Net worth |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY24 (to March 2024) | Rs 380.17 crore | Loss of Rs 15.35 crore | Rs 79.59 crore |
| FY25 (to March 2025) | Rs 455.40 crore | Rs 14.26 crore | Rs 106.22 crore |
| FY26 (to March 2026) | Rs 572.26 crore | Rs 29.11 crore | Rs 229.35 crore |
| Period to Sept 30, 2026 | Rs 222.18 crore | Loss of Rs 16.15 crore | Rs 216.01 crore |

Income rose 26% in FY26 and profit roughly doubled. The latest period, though, shows a loss of Rs 16.15 crore, and the red herring prospectus is the place to check what drove it. Total borrowings were Rs 24.29 crore at the end of March 2026.

At Rs 191, the company is valued at about Rs 1,190.90 crore after the issue. Chittorgarh puts the price-to-earnings multiple at 34.29 times, based on pre-IPO earnings per share of Rs 5.57.

Equirus Capital and JM Financial are the lead managers and MUFG Intime India is the registrar. Allotment status will be on the registrar's site and on the <a href="https://www.bseindia.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BSE</a> and NSE websites once the basis of allotment is fixed on December 1. ## Foodlink IPO: Common Questions

### What is the minimum amount needed to apply for the Foodlink IPO?
One lot of 78 shares. At the upper band of Rs 191 that is Rs 14,898.

### When will Foodlink IPO shares be allotted and listed?
The basis of allotment is expected on December 1, 2026, refunds and demat credit on December 2, and listing on NSE and BSE on December 3. These dates are tentative.

### How much of the Foodlink IPO money goes to the company?
Up to Rs 192 crore from the fresh issue. The Rs 168.76 crore offer for sale goes to the selling shareholders.

### What is the retail quota in the Foodlink IPO?
Retail investors get no more than 10% of the offer.
