The Philippines has won the first ever Eurovision Song Contest Asia. Singer Carmelle, 19, took the title in Bangkok on Saturday, November 14, 2026, with her song "Sobra-Sobra", finishing on 211 points and topping both the jury vote and the public vote. Host nation Thailand came second and Vietnam third in a contest that brought the long running <a href="https://www.eurovision.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Eurovision Song Contest</a> format to Asia for the first time.

The grand final featured 11 countries singing in 10 local languages and English, under the Eurovision theme "United by Music".

## How Carmelle Won Eurovision Asia

According to the <a href="https://www.ebu.ch/news/2026/11/winner-inaugural-eurovision-song-contest-asia-2026-in-bangkok" target="_blank" rel="noopener">European Broadcasting Union (EBU)</a>, which organises Eurovision, Carmelle received 93 points from the national juries and 118 points from viewers. Leading both halves of the vote gave her a clear 41 point margin over the runner up.

The scoring followed the familiar Eurovision model. Juries from all 11 countries counted for half the result and the audience vote for the other half. A "Rest of the World" vote, open to viewers outside the competing countries, added points worth the same as one participating country.

"Sobra-Sobra", whose title translates as "Too Much", was described by The Thaiger as a women's empowerment anthem, and her performance included a rap section. The song was written by Gianina Camille del Rosario and Mart Sam Emmanuel Olavides. Reports said Carmelle was the youngest performer in the field.

## Who Is Carmelle?

Carmelle's full name is Carmelle Allyza San Juan Collado. She was born in 2007 in Sipocot, in the Camarines Sur province on the island of Luzon. She earned her Eurovision ticket by winning the Philippine Selection Night, where she beat nine other acts in the final.

Her career started early. Eurovoix reports that in 2019, at the age of 12, she won seven gold medals at the World Championships of Performing Arts in California, and later that year finished second in season four of The Voice Kids Philippines. In January 2025 she won the talent contest Tawag ng Tanghalan: School Showdown, and she later spent 120 days on Pinoy Big Brother: Celebrity Edition.

In May 2026 she released "Morena", her first original solo single, followed by a self titled debut album. She is also a Tourism student at the University of Nueva Caceres.

## A Night in Bangkok

The final was staged at One Bangkok Forum and hosted by Thai television personalities Woody Milintachinda and Cindy Bishop. The special guests were Jeff Satur and DARA, the singer who won the 70th Eurovision Song Contest for Bulgaria and handed over the trophy.

Key highlights of the show:

- 11 countries took part, from South Korea and Mongolia to Bhutan and Bangladesh
- Songs were performed in 10 local languages plus English
- The show aired in all competing countries and on the official Eurovision YouTube channel
- EBU members in Israel, Australia, Poland and Sweden also carried the broadcast
- The event was produced by Voxovation and S20 Productions, with ZOOP as sponsor

The Thaiger noted that the venue was busy but not completely full, and that the crowd began thinning soon after the trophy was handed over.

## South Asia's Debut and the India Question

For readers in India, one notable feature of the first edition was the presence of three South Asian neighbours. Bhutan's Nyendra finished a strong sixth, Nepal's Pratik Himal Rai came ninth and Bangladesh's Beatmosphere placed eleventh. India was not among the 11 participating countries, so the contest went ahead without the region's largest music market.
