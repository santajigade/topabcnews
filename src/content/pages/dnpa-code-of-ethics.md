<p>At <strong>Top ABC News</strong>, we are deeply committed to upholding ethical journalism and responsible reporting in alignment with the Digital News Publishers Association (DNPA) Code of Ethics.</p>
<h4>1. Purpose and Integrity</h4>
<p>We uphold the highest ethical standards in all aspects of our operations. Every member of our team conducts themselves with integrity, compliance, and transparency—never motivated by personal gain.</p>
<h4>2. Gifts, Favors &amp; Payments</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Any gift, favor, or payment given by Top ABC News must align with ethical business practices, be of modest value, comply with the law, and not embarrass our organization if disclosed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Offering payments, commissions, or incentives to customers or their associates without a formal written agreement is strictly prohibited.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>3. Accepting Gifts or Benefits</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Team members must not solicit or accept gifts, favors, entertainment, or payments from entities doing business or seeking to with Top ABC News unless there is a clear, legitimate business purpose.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Reasonable exceptions include customary business courtesies, such as modest tokens (e.g., calendars, pens), provided they meet our internal guidelines and do not influence editorial decisions.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>4. Conflicts of Interest</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Associates must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with those of the organization.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Any potential conflict—such as ownership in outside enterprises, dual roles, or affiliations with competitors must be promptly disclosed to management.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>5. Confidential Information</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Disclosure or misuse of confidential information such as internal data, unpublished reports, or personal employee data is strictly forbidden. Violations may result in disciplinary actions, including termination.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>6. Compliance &amp; Reporting</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Policy violations will result in appropriate consequences.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>All staff members are expected to report any known or suspected breaches. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>7. Social Media &amp; Public Conduct</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Be Mindful of Your Posts</strong>: Your online activity reflects on our brand. Always use discretion and act professionally.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Clarity of Voice</strong>: When opinions are personal and shared online, they must be clearly separated from the organization’s viewpoints.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Stay Respectful</strong>: Avoid offensive, harassing, or misleading content. Uphold courtesy and respect privacy at all times.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Factually Accurate</strong>: If a post contains an error, correct it promptly and transparently.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Legal Compliance &amp; Confidentiality</strong>: Respect copyright, privacy, and confidentiality laws in all online activity.</p>
</li>
</ul>
