<p>Top ABC News publishes short, easy to read news updates on technology, finance, health, education, government schemes (yojana), sports, automobiles and entertainment.</p>

<h2>How our stories are produced</h2>
<p>Our newsroom uses AI assisted research and writing tools. Every story is built from publicly available reports by established news organisations, official government websites, company announcements and regulatory filings. The sources used for a story are listed at the end of the article.</p>

<h2>Accuracy and corrections</h2>
<p>We aim to report facts accurately and to separate facts from opinion. If you find an error, please write to us through the <a href="/contact/">contact page</a>. Verified corrections are made promptly and the article's updated date is changed.</p>

<h2>Health and finance content</h2>
<p>Articles on health, money and government schemes are for general information only. They are not medical, financial or legal advice. Always check official notifications and consult a qualified professional before acting.</p>

<h2>Independence</h2>
<p>Advertising does not influence our news coverage. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labelled.</p>
